# Comparisons

Where this project sits next to the things it is most likely to be confused
with, and when to use one of them instead.

## The official framework

**[Government Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework](https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/)**
— Government Digital Service. The authority.

|  | Official framework | This project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status | Authoritative | Unofficial, community-built |
| Covers | Roles, levels, skills | The same, plus eight more documents per level |
| Documents per role level | 1 | 9 |
| Format | A website, plus a skills download | 1,845 markdown files, and a website |
| Reuse | OGL v3.0 | OGL v3.0 |
| Use it for | Pay, grading, recruitment, promotion, anything that carries weight | Reading, preparing, and self-assessment |

**Use the official framework whenever the answer matters.** It is maintained by
the people who own it, it is correct by definition, and it has a
[roadmap](https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/roadmap) and a
feedback route. This project restates it and cannot be more current than the
day its summaries were written.

What this adds is the material the framework does not set out to provide: what
to learn next, what to practise, what to ask yourself, and a form to take to a
development conversation. Those are AI-generated and unvalidated — see
[AI_STATEMENT.md](AI_STATEMENT.md) — which is the trade.

## SFIA

**[SFIA](https://sfia-online.org/)** — the Skills Framework for the Information
Age. A global skills framework, in commercial and public sector use worldwide.

The two are complements, not competitors, and the history says so: the UK
Cabinet Office was involved in SFIA's original development, and the SFIA
Foundation publishes
[an illustrative mapping of SFIA skills onto the UK government DDaT roles](https://sfia-online.org/en/tools-and-resources/standard-profiles/uk-government-ddat-roles),
building on mapping work done by the Ministry of Defence, HMRC, and DWP.

The vocabularies already meet: SFIA's proficiency levels — awareness, working,
practitioner, and expert — are the same four words the framework uses, and the
same ones the rating scale in every skills gap form here is built on.

**Reach for SFIA** if you need a skills language that travels outside UK
government, or one with a licensing and accreditation ecosystem behind it. SFIA
is licensed, not open; this project and the framework it restates are OGL.

## Other public sector frameworks

- **[Government Science and Engineering career framework](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/government-science-and-engineering-profession)**
  — the sibling profession framework. Different profession, same idea.
- **[DIRECT framework](https://directframework.com/)** — digital research
  competencies, aimed at research software and data roles in academia. Overlaps
  in the technical skills, diverges on everything about grade and career path.
- **[Curated collections of career frameworks](https://github.com/laurikoobas/data)**
  — one open catalogue that compares public career frameworks and their
  licences, and which rates the DDaT framework as the deepest public source of
  its kind. Useful if you are choosing a framework rather than working inside
  one.

## Other ways to read the framework

- **The framework's own [skills download](https://ddat-capability-framework.service.gov.uk/download)**
  gives every skill and its proficiency-level definitions as a table. If all you
  need is the skills, take it from the source rather than from here.
- **Scraping the site yourself** is the other obvious route, and this project
  exists partly so nobody has to. The difference is the shape: one file per role
  level, a stable slug that addresses the same level across five document sets,
  and a plain-text summary format a parser can read with string prefixes and no
  markdown library.

## What this project is not

- **Not a government service**, and not endorsed by one.
- **Not an assessment instrument.** The practice assessments are not normed, not
  validated, and not fit for making decisions about people.
- **Not an HR system.** The skills gap forms are documents. Nothing is submitted
  anywhere, and answers stay in the reader's own browser.
- **Not a job board.** For roles, see
  [Civil Service Jobs](https://www.civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk/).
