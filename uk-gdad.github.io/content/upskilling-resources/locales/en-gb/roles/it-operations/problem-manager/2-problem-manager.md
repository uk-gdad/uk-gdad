# Problem manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of problem manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you take feeds from numerous sources, including the service desk, and put continual improvements in place to stop incidents such as regular service outages recurring. You resolve emerging and recurring problems, perform root cause analysis, and work closely with major incident managers. The resources below emphasise trend and pattern investigation, consulting specialists, determining preventative measures, and making improvements that hold.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- [Improved Root Cause Analysis Supporting Resilient Production Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278612522001273): 14 challenges and 17 enablers, including expertise gaps, employee bias, poor data quality, and the tooling that helps.

- [Difficulties in Implementing Effective Problem Management](https://www.ijser.org/paper/Difficulties-in-Implementing-Effective-Problem-Management.html): The benefits of effective problem management and the barriers that prevent it.

- [Improving the Software Problem Management Process: A Case Study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11908562_5): A case study of the challenges in a real problem management process.

- [Failures and Fixes: A Study of Software System Incident Response](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.11192): An empirical study of incident response, including where fixes fail to prevent recurrence.

- [Root Cause Analysis in Quality Problem Solving: A Case Study](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325207104_Root_cause_analysis_in_quality_problem_solving_of_research_information_systems_A_case_study): Identifying underlying causes and preventing recurrence in a real system.

- [A Survey of Time Series Anomaly Detection Methods in the AIOps Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00393): How monitored KPIs become detectable anomalies, which is one of your feeds.

- [AIOps for log anomaly detection in the era of LLMs: A systematic literature review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2667305325001346): Where log anomaly detection has genuinely reached, which matters when tooling is proposed as the answer.

- [Enhancing reliability in AI inference services: An empirical study on real production incidents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.07424): 156 high-impact production incidents analysed, with lessons that generalise.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework, directly usable as your improvement programme.

## Professional development tips

- Analyse patterns across incidents rather than investigating each one in isolation
- Distinguish a cause you can evidence from a cause that merely sounds convincing
- Consult specialists early and record what they told you, so the knowledge outlives the investigation
- Track whether your preventative measures actually reduced recurrence, and publish the answer
- Work with major incident managers before the next crisis, not during it
- Make the cost of recurring problems visible in business terms, since that is what funds prevention
- Own an issue until it genuinely has a new owner, and say clearly who that is
