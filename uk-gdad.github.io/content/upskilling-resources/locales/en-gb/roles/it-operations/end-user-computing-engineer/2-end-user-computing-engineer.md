# End user computing engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of end user computing engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you work on site, maintain hardware and fix technical problems quickly. You provide first-time resolution by troubleshooting and diagnosing or escalating faults, and you coach associates and share knowledge with colleagues. The resources below emphasise endpoint management, diagnosis, asset and configuration accuracy, and the reporting and coaching that come with the level.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

## Research articles

- [Evaluation of User Support: Factors That Affect User Satisfaction With Helpdesks and Helplines](https://www.academia.edu/17926413/Evaluation_of_User_Support_Factors_That_Affect_User_Satisfaction_With_Helpdesks_and_Helplines): Finds solution quality, consultation experience, and tangibles as the real dimensions.

- [An investigation of technical support issues influencing user satisfaction](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc2528/): Finds support method and technician experience level to be the significant factors.

- [Evaluating User Satisfaction of IT Services Through Service Quality Approach](https://www.iieta.org/journals/isi/paper/10.18280/isi.290225): A study finding tangibles and reliability contribute most to positive satisfaction.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical ITSM research from 2012 to 2021.

- [Ensuring Efficient IT Service Management to Increase Information Systems Availability](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/337847919_Ensuring_Efficient_IT_Service_Management_to_Increase_Information_Systems_Availability): Service management practice connected to measured availability outcomes.

- [Improving the Software Problem Management Process: A Case Study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11908562_5): The real difficulties in a problem management process, from a case study.

- [A Comprehensive Survey on Root Cause Analysis in (Micro) Services](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00803v1): Root cause analysis techniques, useful when a device problem turns out not to be a device problem.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework for service operations.

## Professional development tips

- Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, since it is expected at this role level
- Aim for first-time resolution, but escalate quickly rather than exhausting the user's patience
- Automate device build and configuration rather than repeating manual steps across a fleet
- Verify asset records physically when you are on site, because that is when it is cheapest to do
- Coach associates by explaining your reasoning, not only your fix
- Write and update knowledge articles, since your value compounds through what others can then do
- Test your support of assistive technology with the actual tools, not from documentation alone
