# Senior infrastructure engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of senior infrastructure engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you build, administer, support and maintain solutions, ensure services are integrated and operated as required, work with technical architects to translate designs into operations, and provide input into workforce, budget and technology roadmaps. The resources below emphasise systems design at medium risk and complexity, integration, reviewing others' designs, and defining test conditions.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Terraform Masterclass: From Beginner to Advanced](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/packt-terraform-masterclass-from-beginner-to-advanced): Project-based Terraform through to advanced module and state design, which is where infrastructure code starts to need architecture.

- [DevOps Pro: Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and IaC](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/devops-linux-docker-kubernetes-ci-cd-iac): Every layer of the modern stack with production-like labs, useful for filling gaps across a broad remit.

- [Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) Principles](https://www.coursera.org/learn/site-reliability-engineering-principles): Reliability measurement, automation, and incident response, which is the operational half of translating a design.

## Udemy courses

- [The Complete Terraform with Ansible Bootcamp](https://www.udemy.com/course/terraform-and-ansible/): Provisioning and configuration together, including multi-cloud and post-deployment automation.

- [DevOps MasterClass: Terraform Kubernetes Ansible Docker](https://www.udemy.com/course/devops-training/): Broad coverage across the toolchain, useful when you are reviewing designs that span several tools.

- [Secure by Design Principles, UK Government Security](https://www.security.gov.uk/policy-and-guidance/secure-by-design/principles/): Mandatory for departments and arm's length bodies, and directly relevant to design review.

## Research articles

- [Strategies and Challenges in Cloud-to-Cloud Migration Using Infrastructure as Code](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-78392-0_1): A migration case study with honest treatment of the obstacles and their mitigations.

- [Leveraging Infrastructure-as-Code Automation to Establish Standardized, Reliable, and Reproducible Cloud Infrastructure](https://zenodo.org/records/19452393): Configuration drift, deployment variability, and traceability, which are design problems.

- [Infrastructure as Code: Technology Review and Research Challenges](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/389406746_Infrastructure_as_Code_-Technology_Review_and_Research_Challenges): A review of the field and its unresolved problems.

- [A Comprehensive Survey on Root Cause Analysis in (Micro) Services](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00803v1): Root cause analysis across metrics, traces, logs, and multi-model data.

- [Improved Root Cause Analysis Supporting Resilient Production Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278612522001273): Challenges and enablers in root cause analysis, most of which are addressable at senior level.

- [An empirical study on performance comparisons of different types of DevOps team formations](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2025.1554299/full): Team structure against delivery performance, relevant to your workforce input.

- [Enhancing reliability in AI inference services: An empirical study on real production incidents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.07424): 156 high-impact production incidents analysed, with lessons that generalise.

- [Capacity and availability management by quantitative project management in the IT service industry](https://www.emerald.com/ajq/article-abstract/13/2/163/60850/Capacity-and-availability-management-by): A quantitative approach, useful for the budget and capacity input expected at this level.

- [Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and Observations on SRE Process to Make Tasks Easier](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01926v1): Practitioner observations on process friction.

## Professional development tips

- Review others' designs seriously, because it is the fastest way to raise the standard across the team
- Select design standards and methods explicitly, then check they are actually applied
- Define the integration build and co-ordinate build activity across systems rather than assuming it will converge
- Translate architectural designs into operational reality, and tell architects early when a design will not operate
- Define test conditions from requirements, and be explicit about non-functional coverage
- Contribute to roadmaps with evidence about capacity, cost, and risk rather than preference
- Consult specialists early and record what they told you, so the knowledge outlives the ticket
