# Principal infrastructure engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of principal infrastructure engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are an accomplished technical leader. You develop strategic and tactical engineering roadmaps for your technologies and services, may own operational relationships with suppliers, work with technical architects to operationalise designs, and may lead infrastructure specialist teams. The resources below emphasise design at high risk and complexity, enterprise design practice, procurement and implementation strategy, and future-proofing without chasing fashion.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Strategic Technology Management](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/technology-management): A University of Illinois specialisation on the strategic view of technology and innovation, which is the frame for an engineering roadmap.

- [Real-World Engineering Management](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/real-world-engineering-management): Business, technical and leadership skills for engineering leadership at scale.

- [Developing a Google SRE Culture](https://www.coursera.org/learn/developing-a-google-sre-culture): How cultural and technical practices rather than tooling produce collaboration between the business and engineering.

## Udemy courses

- [IT Outsourcing, Procurement and Vendor Management](https://www.udemy.com/course/it-outsourcing-and-supplier-management/): Structuring and governing supplier arrangements, which is central if you own operational supplier relationships.

- [Procurement Basics: Supplier Relationship Management](https://www.udemy.com/course/procurement-basics-supplier-relationship-management/): Assessing supplier capability and coordinating vendor interactions, which supports procurement strategy.

- [Terraform: Hands-On Infrastructure as Code Development](https://www.udemy.com/course/terraform-hands-on-infrastructure-as-code-development/): Development practice for infrastructure code, useful for keeping technical credibility and for baseline repair work.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [16.842 Fundamentals of Systems Engineering](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/16-842-fundamentals-of-systems-engineering-fall-2015/): Stakeholder analysis, architecture, trade-space exploration, interface management, verification, validation and commissioning. ## Blog posts

- [Secure design principles, NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/cyber-security-design-principles): The principles you should be embedding as enterprise design practice.

- [The cloud security principles, NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/cloud/the-cloud-security-principles): The assessment framework for cloud providers, which is a procurement instrument as much as a security one.

- [Secure by Design Principles, UK Government Security](https://www.security.gov.uk/policy-and-guidance/secure-by-design/principles/): Mandatory for departments and arm's length bodies, and applicable across the whole life cycle you own.

- [Relaunching the NCSC's Cloud security guidance collection](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/blog-post/relaunching-the-ncscs-cloud-security-guidance-collection): Context on how the national guidance has evolved, which matters when your standards cite it.

- [Why we've decided to decommission GOV.UK PaaS](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2022/07/12/why-weve-decided-to-decommission-gov-uk-paas-platform-as-a-service/): A public account of a platform strategy being reversed. The most useful cautionary reading available for roadmap work.

- [Why IaaS? Government Digital Service](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2012/09/25/why-iaas/): The original reasoning behind a strategic infrastructure decision, worth reading alongside its later reversal.

- [The Digital, Data and Technology Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook): Commissioning guidance that shapes your procurement strategy.

- [Google SRE Book, table of contents](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/): The full set of essays, including capacity planning and managing operational load. Worth reading whole at this level.

- [Embracing Risk, Google SRE Book chapter 3](https://sre.google/sre-book/embracing-risk/): Error budgets, which turn reliability targets into a negotiated business decision.

- [GOV.UK Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard): The governing frame for any technology strategy in UK government.

## Research articles

- [Organizational design of IT supplier relationship management: a multiple case study of five client companies](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/jit.2011.30): Strategy, structure and process for supplier relationship management at organisational level.

- [A Review of the IT Outsourcing Empirical Literature and Future Research Directions](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1057/jit.2010.21): Twenty years of research on outsourcing outcomes, capabilities and governance.

- [Relational dynamics in information technology outsourcing: An integrative review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/ijmr.12347): 127 empirical studies on what makes outsourcing relationships succeed.

- [Strategies and Challenges in Cloud-to-Cloud Migration Using Infrastructure as Code](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-78392-0_1): A migration case study, relevant to any roadmap that involves moving platforms.

- [Infrastructure as Code: Technology Review and Research Challenges](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/389406746_Infrastructure_as_Code_-Technology_Review_and_Research_Challenges): The field and its unresolved problems, useful for judging where the technology actually is.

- [Leveraging Infrastructure-as-Code Automation to Establish Standardized, Reliable, and Reproducible Cloud Infrastructure](https://zenodo.org/records/19452393): Configuration drift and traceability as governance problems.

- [Artificial Intelligence for IT Operations (AIOps) Workshop White Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06054): The AIOps landscape, useful for separating capability from marketing in a roadmap.

- [Capacity and availability management by quantitative project management in the IT service industry](https://www.emerald.com/ajq/article-abstract/13/2/163/60850/Capacity-and-availability-management-by): A quantitative approach, applicable to capacity planning across an estate.

- [A Reassessment of Risk Management in Software Projects](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/978-3-319-05915-0_20.pdf): What research actually shows about risk management and performance, including adoption barriers.

- [Enhancing reliability in AI inference services: An empirical study on real production incidents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.07424): 156 high-impact production incidents analysed, with implications for platform governance.

## Books on Amazon

- [Designing Data-Intensive Applications](https://www.amazon.com/Designing-Data-Intensive-Applications-Reliable-Maintainable/dp/1449373321): By Martin Kleppmann. The fundamentals behind most high-complexity design arguments. Essential at this level.

- [Infrastructure as Code: Designing and Delivering Dynamic Systems for the Cloud Age](https://www.amazon.com/Infrastructure-Code-Designing-Delivering-Dynamic/dp/109815035X): By Kief Morris. Patterns and governance for infrastructure code across an organisation.

- [Team Topologies: Organizing Business and Technology Teams for Fast Flow](https://www.amazon.com/Team-Topologies-Organizing-Business-Technology/dp/1942788819): By Skelton and Pais. Team structure and cognitive load, which determines whether a roadmap survives delivery.

- [Accelerate: The Science of Lean Software and DevOps](https://www.amazon.com/Accelerate-Software-Performing-Technology-Organizations/dp/1942788339): By Forsgren, Humble and Kim. The strongest available evidence for the practices you are asking an organisation to fund.

- [The Practice of Cloud System Administration, Volume 2](https://www.amazon.com/Practice-Cloud-System-Administration-Practices-ebook/dp/B00N7N2CRQ): By Limoncelli, Chalup and Hogan. Operations at scale with case studies from organisations that had to solve it.

- [Release It! Design and Deploy Production-Ready Software](https://www.amazon.com/Release-Design-Deploy-Production-Ready-Software-ebook/dp/B0DGX43D9B): By Michael Nygard. Stability and capacity patterns, updated for virtualised systems and chaos engineering.

- [The Staff Engineer's Path: A Guide for Individual Contributors Navigating Growth and Change](https://www.amazon.com/Staff-Engineers-Path-Individual-Contributors/dp/1098118731): By Tanya Reilly. Strategic thinking and setting technical standards without line authority.

- [An Elegant Puzzle: Systems of Engineering Management](https://www.amazon.com/Elegant-Puzzle-Systems-Engineering-Management-ebook/dp/B07QYCHJ7V): By Will Larson. Systems thinking about organisations, including sizing teams and managing technical migrations.

- [Chaos Engineering: System Resiliency in Practice](https://www.amazon.com/Chaos-Engineering-System-Resiliency-Practice/dp/1492043869): By Rosenthal and Jones. Deliberately discovering weakness, which is what future-proofing should actually mean.

- [The Site Reliability Workbook: Practical Ways to Implement SRE](https://www.amazon.com/Site-Reliability-Workbook-Practical-Implement/dp/1492029505): By Beyer, Murphy and Rensin. Introducing these practices where operational commitments already exist.

## Professional development tips

- Write roadmaps that name what you will stop doing, not only what you will start
- Judge future technologies against the estate you actually have, not the one a vendor demonstrates
- Control design practice through review and standards, since a policy nobody reads changes nothing
- Develop procurement and implementation strategies together, because a procurement that ignores implementation fails late
- Read the GOV.UK PaaS decommissioning post before committing to any platform strategy
- Own supplier relationships on evidence and outcomes, not on relationship warmth
- Keep writing and reading code, since baseline repair capability is what makes your technical judgement credible
