# Lead infrastructure engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead infrastructure engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you manage third party provision of infrastructure services and the provision of expertise to develop architectural solutions throughout the service product life cycle. You oversee programmes and projects, work with technical architects to operationalise designs, and may lead infrastructure specialist teams. The resources below emphasise supplier management, architecture and design at scale, security engineering, and leading technical work.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- [Terraform Masterclass: From Beginner to Advanced](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/packt-terraform-masterclass-from-beginner-to-advanced): Advanced module and state design, which is where infrastructure code becomes an architecture question.

## Professional development tips

- Manage third parties on architecture and measured outcomes, not on effort reported
- Require designs to include security controls engineered for specific threats, rather than security appended
- Support architects by telling them early what will not operate, and offering the alternative
- Set standards for definition, security and integrity of objects, then check conformity rather than assuming it
- Provide authoritative test planning advice, particularly on the non-functional requirements that fail in production
- Lead others in applying modern development standards, since standards you do not model are ignored
- Keep enough hands-on depth to tell a good technical answer from a confident one
