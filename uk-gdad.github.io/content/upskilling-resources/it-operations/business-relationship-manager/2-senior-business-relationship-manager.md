# Senior business relationship manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of senior business relationship manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you develop relationships across the organisation to understand business ambitions and ensure digital products and services support them. You explore shared needs and encourage re-use and commoditisation, lead activities that improve relationship maturity, and support strategic demands such as spending reviews. The resources below emphasise leading investigative work, defining strategy and policy, and championing user-centred approaches.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

## Coursera courses

- [Advanced Negotiation and Conflict Resolution for Leaders](https://www.coursera.org/learn/advanced-negotiation-and-conflict-resolution-for-leaders): Frameworks for multi-party negotiation and building systematic influence capability, aimed at people navigating complex organisational dynamics.

- [Negotiation skills: Negotiate and resolve conflict](https://www.coursera.org/learn/negotiation-skills-conflict): Conceptualising and measuring power and politics, and developing strategies for influencing stakeholders with integrity.

- [Strategic Technology Management](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/technology-management): A University of Illinois specialisation on the strategic view of technology and innovation, which is the frame for judging whether investment adds value.

## Udemy courses

- [Stakeholders Relationships Management](https://www.udemy.com/course/stakeholders-relationships-management/): Influencing, negotiating and communicating with stakeholders, and sustaining relationships rather than transactions.

- [Business Partnerships: Strategy, Negotiation and Management](https://www.udemy.com/course/business-partnerships-strategy-negotiation-management/): Choosing partners, negotiating agreements that work for both sides, and building the communication systems that prevent conflict.

- [Master Course: Developing Business Partnerships 2.0](https://www.udemy.com/course/master-course-developing-business-partnerships/): A framework for identifying, structuring and managing alliances, including the financial and communication dimensions.

## Blog posts

- [The Four Stages of the Business Relationship Organizational Maturity Model, Pink Elephant](https://blog.pinkelephant.com/blog/blog/the-four-stages-of-the-business-relationship-organizational-maturity-model): A maturity model for both the role and the organisation, with the actions needed to advance a level. Directly relevant to leading relationship maturity work.

- [ITIL 4 Relationship Management: Guide to Building Strategic IT-Business Partnerships](https://itsm.tools/relationship-management/): The ITIL 4 relationship management practice and how it supports strategic objectives.

- [Shifting from Business Relationship Management to Relationship Management in ITIL 4](https://www.beyond20.com/blog/relationship-management-in-itil-4/): On trust and value being built across many conversations, and what that implies for how you plan engagement.

- [GOV.UK Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard): The standard digital investment is assessed against, and the vocabulary your strategic guidance should use.

- [Solve a whole problem for users, GOV.UK Service Standard point 2](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-2-solve-a-whole-problem): The strongest available argument for shared needs and cross-organisational re-use.

- [Use and contribute to open standards, common components and patterns, GOV.UK Service Standard point 13](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-13-use-common-standards-components-patterns): The standard behind re-use, central management, and commoditisation.

- [The Digital, Data and Technology Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook): How digital and technology work should be commissioned in government, which is where strategic demand meets delivery reality.

- [Performance Review of Digital Spend: Enabling Strategic Investment and Innovation](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/performance-review-of-digital-spend/performance-review-of-digital-spend-enabling-strategic-investment-and-innovation): A government review of how digital spend is judged, useful preparation for spending review conversations.

- [Getting approval to spend money on a service, GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/getting-approval-to-spend-money-on-a-service): Digital and technology spend controls were replaced on 1 April 2026. Departments now make most of their own digital and technology spending decisions through their own assurance team, with HM Treasury approval still required above the department's spend limit or for proposals that are novel, contentious or repercussive.

- [Iterate and improve frequently, GOV.UK Service Standard point 8](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-8-iterate-and-improve-frequently): Useful when arguing for sustained investment rather than one-off funding.

## Research articles

- [Strategic IT alignment: twenty-five years on](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/jit.2014.35): A retrospective on what alignment research has established and where it has stalled. Read this before adopting an alignment framework.

- [IT-Business Alignment: A Systematic Literature Review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349523359_IT-Business_Alignment_A_Systematic_Literature_Review): A synthesis of the alignment literature, including the recurring measurement problems.

- [Strategic Alignment: A Model for Organizational Transformation via Information Technology](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/38009338_Strategic_alignment_A_model_for_organizational_transformation_via_information_technology): The Henderson and Venkatraman model that most later work responds to.

- [Effectiveness of IT Service Management Capability: Value Co-Creation and Value Facilitation Mechanisms](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333777833_Effectiveness_of_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Value_Co-Creation_and_Value_Facilitation_Mechanisms): A study of 256 organisations finding capability works through alignment, which is evidence for the relationship investment you are asking for.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical implementation research from 2012 to 2021.

- [Investigating business-IT alignment through multi-disciplinary goal concepts](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00766-009-0081-0): Connects business goals to system requirements through explicit goal modelling.

- [Strategic Stakeholder Alignment for Organizational Success](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/399827322_Strategic_Stakeholder_Alignment_for_Organizational_Success): Treating stakeholder alignment as a deliberate strategic activity.

- [Organizational design of IT supplier relationship management: a multiple case study of five client companies](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/jit.2011.30): Strategy, structure, and process for managing supplier relationships, which shares most of its mechanics with internal relationship work.

- [Measuring IT Service Management Capability: Scale Development and Empirical Validation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/299616426_Measuring_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Scale_Development_and_Empirical_Validation): A validated instrument, better than a self-assessed maturity score for demonstrating improvement.

- [An IT Service Management Literature Review: Challenges, Benefits, Opportunities and Implementation Practices](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349857851_An_IT_Service_Management_Literature_Review_Challenges_Benefits_Opportunities_and_Implementation_Practices): Benefits, challenges, and practices extracted across 47 studies.

## Professional development tips

- Demonstrate knowledge of the ITIL life cycle or capability elements, since it is expected at this role level
- Look for shared needs across teams before accepting a bespoke request, and make the re-use case with evidence
- Prepare for spending reviews continuously, so the narrative already exists when the deadline arrives
- Lead investigative work by framing the question well, then let the evidence contradict you
- Absorb conflicting information without resolving it prematurely, since the conflict is usually the finding
- Measure relationship maturity rather than asserting it, and publish the measure
- Explain the difference between what users need and what stakeholders want, repeatedly and without heat
