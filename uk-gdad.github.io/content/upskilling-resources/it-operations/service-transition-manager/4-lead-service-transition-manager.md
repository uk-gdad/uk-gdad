# Lead service transition manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead service transition manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are responsible for planning and co-ordinating the resources that get services effectively into service operation, and you are solely responsible for co-ordination across projects, suppliers and service teams. The resources below emphasise cross-organisational co-ordination, high-impact change, capacity and availability standards, and continuity and runbook discipline.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework for the process you own.

## Professional development tips

- Publish the transition plan where projects, suppliers and service teams can all see it, since co-ordination fails silently
- Agree acceptance criteria with the receiving teams before the project hears them, so they are defensible
- Plan resources for early-life support explicitly, because it is the first thing cut and the first thing missed
- Test the continuity runbooks before handover rather than after the first incident
- Manage suppliers on evidence and defined interfaces rather than on assurance
- Read the DORA evidence before adding co-ordination process, since more gates are not more safety
- Review each transition against what actually happened afterwards, and change the criteria accordingly
