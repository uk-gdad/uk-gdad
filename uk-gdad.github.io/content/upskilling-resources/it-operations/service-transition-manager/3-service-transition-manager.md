# Service transition manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of service transition manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you accept products, determine whether they are fit for purpose and assess readiness against agreed service acceptance criteria. You make recommendations on go-live, early-life support and service acceptance, and ensure the acceptance criteria are understood across the wider IT operations. The resources below emphasise the go-live decision, early-life support, and communicating criteria so they are met rather than argued about.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

## Professional development tips

- Make the acceptance criteria visible and agreed early, since criteria introduced late read as obstruction
- Recommend on evidence, and be explicit about what has not been demonstrated
- Treat monitoring, runbooks and briefed support teams as hard criteria, not nice-to-haves
- Plan early-life support as a funded, staffed phase rather than an expectation of goodwill
- Be willing to recommend against go-live, and make the conditions for a yes concrete
- Demonstrate knowledge of the ITIL life cycle or capability elements, since it is expected at this role level
- Review your own transition decisions against what actually happened after go-live
