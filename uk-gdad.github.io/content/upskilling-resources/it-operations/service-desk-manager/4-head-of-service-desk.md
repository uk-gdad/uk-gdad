# Head of service desk - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of head of service desk.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you manage a team providing first and second line technical support, ensure support for existing and emerging ICT services, provide technical advice to project teams, ensure the team develops so it can advise on future projects, and manage and monitor service desk service level agreements and performance. The resources below emphasise capability building, performance management, experience measurement and advising delivery.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Research articles

- [Sources of Customer Satisfaction and Dissatisfaction with Information Technology Help Desks](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1009794622860): What actually drives satisfaction, which should shape what your performance measures capture.

- [Evaluation of User Support: Factors That Affect User Satisfaction With Helpdesks and Helplines](https://www.academia.edu/17926413/Evaluation_of_User_Support_Factors_That_Affect_User_Satisfaction_With_Helpdesks_and_Helplines): Finds three quality dimensions rather than the standard SERVQUAL set, which should change your survey design.

- [Management of perceptions of information technology service quality](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296308002270): How perception of quality forms, which matters when your SLA is green and stakeholders are unhappy.

- [Evaluating User Satisfaction of IT Services Through Service Quality Approach](https://www.iieta.org/journals/isi/paper/10.18280/isi.290225): Finds tangibles and reliability contribute most to satisfaction.

- [An empirical study of factors that contribute to the emotional and physical well-being of call centre agents](https://www.academia.edu/68702512/An_empirical_study_of_factors_that_contribute_to_the_emotional_and_physical_well_being_of_call_centre_agents): Lower job control and higher uncertainty as measured stressors, which is a management responsibility.

- [How Employees' Emotional Labor Promotes Perceived Service Quality: A Dual-Pathway Model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12649272/): Why deep acting produces better service than surface acting, which has staffing and training implications.

- [Effectiveness of IT Service Management Capability: Value Co-Creation and Value Facilitation Mechanisms](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333777833_Effectiveness_of_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Value_Co-Creation_and_Value_Facilitation_Mechanisms): A study of 256 organisations finding capability works through business alignment.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical implementation research from 2012 to 2021.

- [Measuring IT Service Management Capability: Scale Development and Empirical Validation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/299616426_Measuring_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Scale_Development_and_Empirical_Validation): A validated capability measure for baselining and reporting improvement.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework you can run across the function.

## Professional development tips

- Report on experience alongside service level attainment, since a green dashboard and an unhappy user are both real
- Get your team into project conversations early, because support cost is decided at design time
- Develop the team deliberately towards advising rather than only resolving, since that is where the value is
- Manage performance on demand patterns and outcomes, not on activity counts
- Take the well-being evidence about support work seriously when setting staffing and rotation
- Make your technical advice to projects specific and written down, so it survives the meeting
- Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, since it is expected at this role level
