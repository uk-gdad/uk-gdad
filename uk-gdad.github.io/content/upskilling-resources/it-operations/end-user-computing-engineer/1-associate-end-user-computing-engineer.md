# Associate end user computing engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of associate end user computing engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are a trainee working inside an established team, under the direction of end user computing engineers. The resources below emphasise support fundamentals, registering incidents properly, collating data and understanding what a key performance indicator is for, and the user focus that separates support from ticket closure.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Technical Support Fundamentals](https://www.coursera.org/learn/technical-support-fundamentals): Computer hardware, the internet, software, troubleshooting and customer service. The standard first course for this career path.

- [Google IT Support Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-it-support): Troubleshooting and customer service, networking, operating systems, system administration, automation and security, designed for people new to the industry.

- [ITSM Foundations: Optimizing IT Service Management](https://www.coursera.org/learn/itsm-foundations-optimizing-it-service-management): How service requests, incidents and problems are handled in a real service management platform.

## Research articles

- [Sources of Customer Satisfaction and Dissatisfaction with Information Technology Help Desks](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1009794622860): Empirical work on what actually drives satisfaction with IT support, which is rarely what teams assume.

- [Evaluation of User Support: Factors That Affect User Satisfaction With Helpdesks and Helplines](https://www.academia.edu/17926413/Evaluation_of_User_Support_Factors_That_Affect_User_Satisfaction_With_Helpdesks_and_Helplines): Finds three quality dimensions, solution quality, consultation experience, and tangibles, rather than the standard SERVQUAL set.

- [An investigation of technical support issues influencing user satisfaction](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc2528/): Finds the support method and the technician's experience level to be the significant variables.

- [Evaluating User Satisfaction of IT Services Through Service Quality Approach](https://www.iieta.org/journals/isi/paper/10.18280/isi.290225): A SERVQUAL-based study finding tangibles and reliability contribute most to satisfaction.

- [Managing IT service quality as perceived by the customer: The service oriented IT SERVQUAL](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228903894_Managing_IT_service_quality_as_perceived_by_the_customer_The_service_oriented_IT_SERVQUAL): Adapting service quality measurement to IT services specifically.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical ITSM research from 2012 to 2021.

- [An IT Service Management Literature Review: Challenges, Benefits, Opportunities and Implementation Practices](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349857851_An_IT_Service_Management_Literature_Review_Challenges_Benefits_Opportunities_and_Implementation_Practices): Benefits, challenges, and practices extracted across 47 studies.

- [Improving the Software Problem Management Process: A Case Study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11908562_5): A case study of the real difficulties in a problem management process.

- [Difficulties in Implementing Effective Problem Management](https://www.ijser.org/paper/Difficulties-in-Implementing-Effective-Problem-Management.html): The barriers that stop problem management working in practice.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework for service operations.

## Professional development tips

- Learn what each key performance indicator you report actually measures, and what it fails to capture
- Register incidents with enough detail that nobody has to come back to you for the basics
- Write knowledge articles as you learn things, since knowledge management is part of the role rather than an extra
- Ask users what they were trying to do, not only what error they saw
- Learn how assistive technology works, because supporting it badly excludes people from their work
- Escalate promptly and clearly, since passing a problem on well is a skill at this level
- Shadow the engineers you support, and ask why they chose one diagnostic path over another
