# Lead infrastructure operations engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead infrastructure operations engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you ensure the service meets the contractual and regulatory requirements of the organisation. You manage operational support, the workload and development of a team of specialists, third party provision, expertise to programmes and projects, and the development of architectural solutions across the service life cycle. The resources below emphasise supplier management, standards and documentation, capability building, and high-impact change.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

## Coursera courses

- [Real-World Engineering Management](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/real-world-engineering-management): Business, technical and leadership skills for engineering leadership, including managing workload and development.

- [Developing a Google SRE Culture](https://www.coursera.org/learn/developing-a-google-sre-culture): Aligning incentives between development and operations, which is the recurring problem when supporting programmes.

- [Advanced ITSM with ServiceNow: Optimizing IT Operations](https://www.coursera.org/learn/advanced-itsm-with-servicenow-optimizing-it-operations): Incident, problem, change and configuration management at the level where you own the standards.

## Udemy courses

- [IT Outsourcing, Procurement and Vendor Management](https://www.udemy.com/course/it-outsourcing-and-supplier-management/): Written for both sides of an outsourcing relationship. The most directly relevant course for managing third party provision.

- [Procurement Basics: Supplier Relationship Management](https://www.udemy.com/course/procurement-basics-supplier-relationship-management/): Assessing supplier capability and deciding which activities to place with which vendor.

- [Root Cause Analysis: A Leader's Guide to Problem Solving](https://www.udemy.com/course/root-cause-analysis-a-leaders-guide-to-problem-solving/): Root cause analysis and the 8D method for people directing investigation rather than performing it.

- [Improved Root Cause Analysis Supporting Resilient Production Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278612522001273): The challenges and enablers of root cause analysis, most of which a lead can address.

## Professional development tips

- Turn contractual and regulatory requirements into checkable standards, since a requirement nobody can test is not met
- Manage third parties on architecture and measured outcomes rather than on effort reported
- Draft and maintain procedures deliberately, and make maintaining them somebody's actual job
- Set standards for definition, security and integrity of objects, then check conformity rather than assuming it
- Build capability by giving people real ownership, not only training days
- Demonstrate knowledge of the ITIL life cycle or capability elements, since it is expected at this role level
- Provide authoritative test planning advice, particularly on non-functional coverage
