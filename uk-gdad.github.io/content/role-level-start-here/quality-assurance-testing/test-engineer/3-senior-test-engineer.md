# Senior test engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior test engineer level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior test engineer does what a test engineer does, but at greater scale
and complexity, and with less of a safety net. You are expected to spot risk
in automation results before it becomes a production incident, bring in new
test frameworks where the existing ones fall short, hold your own across
several coding languages even where you are only truly expert in one, and
mentor other engineers on how to think about testing, not just how to write
it. The step up from test engineer is less about doing more of the same thing
and more about being the person a team turns to when the testing problem in
front of them doesn't have an obvious answer.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, the most useful question to ask an AI tool is often not
"how do I do this" but "what am I missing" — use it to pressure-test your own
judgement, not just to fill gaps in your knowledge.

- [ ] **Broaden your technical range across languages and frameworks**

  You are expected to be proficient in a range of coding languages, even if
  you are a genuine expert in only one, and to be the person who identifies
  when an existing test framework has stopped fitting the problem and knows
  what to replace it with.

  Ask AI: "I'm strong in one programming language and need to become
  proficient enough in others to review and contribute to test code written
  in them. What's an efficient way to build that range?"

- [ ] **Lead test analysis across more than one team**

  Rather than analysing one piece of work, you are expected to lead
  cross-team work to determine which quality characteristics genuinely add
  value, judge when a testing approach needs to change based on effort and
  risk, and use several analysis techniques together to make sense of complex
  or ambiguous information.

  Ask AI: "How does leading test analysis across multiple teams differ from
  doing it for a single team? What coordination problems come up that don't
  exist at smaller scale?"

- [ ] **Shape testing strategy beyond your own team**

  You are expected to help teams build and implement quality testing
  approaches, contribute to strategy at the organisational level, and put in
  place ways of capturing data that drive continuous improvement, rather than
  relying on strategies staying fixed once written.

  Ask AI: "What kind of data should a testing strategy capture to show
  whether it's actually working, and how would a team act on that data over
  time?"

- [ ] **Set, not just follow, standards for designing and executing tests**

  Influence and guide which test types and techniques get used to catch risk
  early, lead others through designing and executing tests, and contribute to
  the standards the wider organisation uses — then keep improving those
  standards through a structured process rather than ad hoc changes.

  Ask AI: "What does a good, structured process for improving test standards
  over time look like, as opposed to standards that just accumulate one-off
  exceptions?"

- [ ] **Develop and extend the frameworks and tools other people rely on**

  Build, standardise and extend reusable frameworks and tools that support a
  range of testing activities, coach others in keeping tests comprehensive
  and reliable, research what testing will need next, and keep continuous
  integration and deployment pipelines adapted to changing needs.

  Ask AI: "What makes a test automation framework genuinely reusable across
  teams, rather than something that only works well for the team that built
  it?"

- [ ] **Turn defect patterns into recommendations, not just reports**

  Contribute to standards for how defects are managed, manage and escalate
  dependencies and risk across teams, and use patterns and trends in defects
  — not single incidents — to recommend changes to testing and quality
  approaches, with support.

  Ask AI: "How would a tester spot a pattern across many individual defects
  that points to a deeper process or design problem, rather than treating
  each defect as a one-off?"

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations with more senior stakeholders**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of a wider range of stakeholders, manage
  both the communication you start and the communication that lands on you
  unplanned, and support or host difficult discussions involving diverse
  senior stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "Give me a scenario where two senior stakeholders disagree about
  release risk based on the same test results, and suggest how a senior
  tester might facilitate that conversation toward a decision."

Ask your manager or a principal or lead colleague which of these matters most
in your organisation right now, and start there.
