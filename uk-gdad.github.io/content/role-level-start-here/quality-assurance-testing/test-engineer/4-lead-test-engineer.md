# Lead test engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead test engineer level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this repository
has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A lead test engineer writes new automation test frameworks from project
requirements, but the bigger shift at this level is outward: you are expected
to have a presence in external test communities and share what you learn,
work with leads across the whole Government Digital and Data profession to
champion quality, and manage the communication between projects that keeps
integration and dependencies under control rather than discovered too late.
Where a senior test engineer is the person a team turns to, a lead test
engineer is often the person other teams' leads turn to. The standards, tools
and behaviours you establish here tend to outlast any single project — which
is exactly the point.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is most useful as a sounding board for
decisions that will outlive you in the organisation — use it to stress-test
the standards and strategies you're setting, not to hand you the answer.

- [ ] **Set, and keep setting, engineering practices and standards**

  Establish and lead test engineering practices, standards and behaviours;
  influence which testing technologies and tools an organisation adopts; and
  advocate for solutions that genuinely align with organisational goals and
  quality objectives, including, where the framework calls for it, promoting
  the use of open source tools.

  Ask AI: "How does a lead decide when it's worth changing an organisation's
  established testing standards, versus when it's better to leave a working
  standard alone even if a newer approach exists?"

- [ ] **Guide test analysis and quality planning across many teams**

  Lead and guide multiple teams in test analysis so it happens early in the
  life cycle, set standards and principles for test analysis across the whole
  organisation, and create and manage multiple quality testing plans and
  strategies at once — while advocating for quality to be involved early in
  organisational delivery processes, not bolted on afterwards.

  Ask AI: "What tends to go wrong when an organisation tries to apply one
  quality testing strategy across many teams with different delivery
  approaches, and how would a lead handle that variation?"

- [ ] **Make strategic calls on test types, techniques and tools**

  Set standards and influence organisational decisions on test types,
  techniques, design and execution; advocate for continuous improvement of
  them; and make the strategic decisions about new or improved approaches
  used in your area, rather than leaving that to individual teams.

  Ask AI: "How should a lead weigh the cost of standardising a test technique
  across an organisation against the value of letting individual teams choose
  what fits their own context?"

- [ ] **Turn defect and test data into organisational recommendations**

  Lead and coach others in improving test and defect management processes,
  support colleagues in assessing the most complex and challenging defects
  across the organisation, and use defect patterns and trends to make both
  tactical and strategic recommendations that influence wider quality
  processes.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a tactical recommendation and a
  strategic recommendation arising from the same defect data, and how would a
  lead decide which one a given pattern deserves?"

- [ ] **Represent testing to people who don't report to you**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships across teams; manage
  stakeholder expectations and moderate difficult, high-risk discussions even
  under tight timescales; and speak on behalf of, and represent, the testing
  community to large audiences inside and outside the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead prepare to represent their team's quality
  position to a large, mostly non-technical audience, including handling
  pushback on a decision they didn't personally make?"

- [ ] **Build a presence beyond your own organisation**

  Maintain a presence in external test communities, share what you learn
  regularly rather than occasionally, and work with leads across the wider
  Government Digital and Data profession — including managing the
  communication between projects that keeps integration and dependencies
  under control.

  Ask AI: "What's a sustainable way for someone in a technical leadership role
  to keep contributing to external communities without it becoming another
  full-time job on top of their actual one?"

Ask peers in other leadership roles, inside or outside your organisation, which
of these is hardest for people at this level to get right — that conversation
is often more useful than any single resource.
