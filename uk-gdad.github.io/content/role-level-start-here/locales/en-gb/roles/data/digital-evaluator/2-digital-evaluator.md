Welcome. If you are working toward the digital evaluator level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A digital evaluator supports the evaluation of products and services, with
a good understanding of the role and some practical experience. You work
with a more senior evaluator to plan and deliver evaluations, manage the
day-to-day development of monitoring and analytical tools, support
designing quantitative and qualitative research, help with research
activities and data collection, report on product and service performance,
and communicate findings through written reports while still receiving
guidance to develop your skills. Most named skills grow at this level,
because you're now producing real evaluation work, not just building
foundational awareness.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own evaluation work.

- [ ] **Translate stakeholder needs into evaluation approaches**

  Work with stakeholders to understand their needs, translate them into
  potential research questions and evaluation approaches, present
  monitoring and evaluation options, and influence decisions on evaluation
  plans with support.

  Ask AI: "How would a digital evaluator turn a vague stakeholder need
  into a specific, answerable research question?"

- [ ] **Develop a theory of change, with support**

  Develop a theory of change for a product or service through discussion
  with stakeholders, with support, identify, and present options for
  measuring indicators, turn some identified indicators into measurement
  frameworks, and support building a performance dashboard.

  Ask AI: "What makes a theory of change genuinely useful for guiding an
  evaluation, rather than a diagram nobody refers back to?"

- [ ] **Manage evaluation delivery across product life cycle phases**

  Make sure the team is measuring performance appropriate for the relevant
  service standard phase, manage delivering evaluation of services or
  products at different phases, and plan and look ahead at data
  requirements for future phases.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a digital evaluator that the team's performance
  measurement needs to change because the product has moved to a new
  phase?"

- [ ] **Take ownership of preparing data, and peer-review outputs**

  Identify appropriate ways to collect, collate and prepare data, decide
  whether data and analysis are accurate and fit for purpose, perform data
  preparation and cleansing with limited guidance, undertake quality
  assurance processes, and peer-review colleagues' analysis outputs.

  Ask AI: "What should a digital evaluator look for when peer-reviewing a
  colleague's data analysis, beyond checking the numbers add up?"

- [ ] **Choose the right medium, and give tactical recommendations**

  Choose the appropriate medium to communicate findings, shape
  communications for the audience, present analysis, and visualisations
  clearly for both specialist and non-specialist audiences with support,
  and give tactical recommendations to inform decisions.

  Ask AI: "How would a digital evaluator decide whether a finding should
  be a written report, a presentation, or a short conversation?"

- [ ] **Apply data ethics and privacy principles, and share risks**

  Apply fundamental principles of data ethics and privacy in your work
  under supervision, and share ethics and privacy risks through the
  appropriate channels.

  Ask AI: "What's an example of a data ethics or privacy risk a digital
  evaluator might spot in a real dataset, and how would they raise it?"

- [ ] **Keep evaluating interventions with support**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from junior level:
  evaluating interventions with support using recognised techniques, and
  using some analytical approaches such as descriptive and inferential
  analysis.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely more independent evaluation practice add
  beyond what's written here?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
