# Head of content design - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of content design level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of content design is an expert practitioner with broad industry
experience who defines and assures best practice while influencing, leading
and mentoring others — the top of this profession's line. You develop a
strategy for content that meets the organisation's objectives, lead a team
capable of executing it, work with senior stakeholders to influence
organisational strategy, prioritise and collaborate with counterparts
across government, and champion good content design practice within
government and industry. Every named skill carries the same wording as
lead level here — at the top of this profession's ladder, the real
differentiation is owning the organisation's content strategy and
representing the discipline externally, not a longer skill description for
each named capability.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Own content strategy and life cycle management across the organisation**

  Direct the approach to content life cycle management so content is
  regularly reviewed, oversee teams' work, demonstrate extensive
  experience across multiple channels, encourage continuous process
  evaluation, take accountability for high-quality output, and identify
  capability gaps across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of content design build a content strategy
  that survives changes in political priority and leadership?"

- [ ] **Represent the organisation in the hardest stakeholder relationships**

  Manage long-term strategic relationships, direct the strategic approach
  to stakeholder relationships, act as the ultimate escalation point when
  relationships become challenging, influence the most senior
  stakeholders, and facilitate discussions across high-risk, complex areas
  under constrained timelines.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of content design represent the discipline's
  interests to a senior stakeholder who sees content as a formatting
  exercise, not a strategic function?"

- [ ] **Set direction for user needs across the whole organisation**

  Give direction on which tools or methods teams should use, demonstrate
  extensive experience meeting user needs across channels, direct
  solutions within complex user journeys, and bring insight into how user
  needs have changed over time across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of content design keep organisation-wide user
  insight current, rather than relying on research that's grown stale?"

- [ ] **Build capability and lead Agile and Lean practice across teams**

  Coach and lead teams in Agile and Lean practices, evaluate and iterate
  approaches through a project's life, think of innovative ways of
  working, act as a recognised expert and advocate, and regularly assess
  capability across teams.

  Ask AI: "What does building content design capability across an entire
  organisation look like, beyond developing it well within one team?"

- [ ] **Lead the organisation's strategic content improvement priorities**

  Help lead strategy design and implementation, evaluating impact against
  business objectives and user needs, and direct strategic content
  improvement projects focused on the organisation's highest priorities.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of content design decide which strategic
  content priority deserves the organisation's limited attention first?"

- [ ] **Champion prototyping practice across the organisation**

  This skill's wording here matches the earlier, more foundational levels
  of this role — showing awareness of prototyping and working
  collaboratively. That's the framework's own choice, not a gap in your
  practice; the depth expected of you at this level shows up in owning
  strategy and representing the discipline, not in your own hands-on
  prototyping.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill reads more simply than my other
  responsibilities, what would genuinely head-of-role prototyping
  leadership add if I pushed beyond what's written here?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
