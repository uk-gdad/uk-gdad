# Junior content designer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the junior content designer level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A junior content designer is typically a graduate with a relevant degree,
or someone with some relevant work experience in content creation. You
explain your content decisions rather than just making them, work
collaboratively, work independently once a more senior content designer
has given direction, independently identify user issues and needs, support
content team administration and publishing processes, and engage with the
cross-government content community. Only strategic thinking genuinely
deepens in the framework's own wording at this level — the rest of the
growth here is in the independence and judgement you're trusted with, not a
longer skill description.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own organisation's content.

- [ ] **Contribute to strategy, and improve processes**

  Support creating and developing strategies and policies, contribute to
  iterating and improving processes and guidelines, and understand how
  your activities meet strategic goals.

  Ask AI: "How would a junior content designer find out how a specific
  piece of content connects to a bigger strategic goal, if nobody's
  explained it directly?"

- [ ] **Keep building basic editorial and style standards**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from associate level:
  demonstrating decent editorial standards, showing awareness of style
  standards, and working well under supervision.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since associate
  level, what would genuinely more independent editorial judgement look
  like at the next level?"

- [ ] **Keep learning what prototyping is for**

  This skill also plateaus: showing awareness of prototyping, explaining
  why and when to use it, and working collaboratively.

  Ask AI: "What would taking more ownership of a prototyping exercise,
  rather than just understanding why it's used, look like for a junior
  content designer?"

- [ ] **Keep building a sense of who you're writing for**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: showing an understanding of user
  experience analysis, and seeing the purpose of user stories.

  Ask AI: "What's a good next step, beyond seeing the purpose of user
  stories, that a junior content designer could actually practise?"

- [ ] **Keep learning who your stakeholders are**

  This skill also plateaus: understanding who your stakeholders are, and
  what evidence is relevant to them.

  Ask AI: "What would independently identifying stakeholders for a piece
  of work, rather than being told who they are, look like at this level?"

- [ ] **Keep building a working knowledge of Agile practices**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: demonstrating experience in Agile
  teams, being aware of Agile tools, and being responsive to feedback.

  Ask AI: "What would working independently within an Agile team, rather
  than following direction closely, look like for a junior content
  designer?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
