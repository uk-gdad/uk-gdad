# Senior content designer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior content designer level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior content designer is an expert practitioner who develops content
strategy and solutions for large-scale problems and high-profile events.
You take responsibility for content quality, manage small teams and mentor
colleagues, write and map user stories, review others' work to assure
quality, lead on cross-government content projects, and engage with the
content community. Most named skills grow at this level, because you're
now accountable for other people's content and for problems bigger than
one service.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is often
most useful for pressure-testing a content strategy decision — describe
the actual trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Assure content quality across a team, and coach junior colleagues**

  Show a deep understanding of end-to-end journeys and how content is
  affected within them, identify where fixes are needed, take
  responsibility for assuring junior colleagues' content quality, coach
  and guide them, ensure content is regularly reviewed, and deliver
  through others by directing and critiquing their work.

  Ask AI: "What should a senior content designer look for when assuring a
  junior colleague's content, beyond checking it reads well?"

- [ ] **Champion user research, and put accessibility at the centre**

  Collaborate with researchers and performance analysts to advocate for
  users, understand different research and evidence at each product
  stage, formulate hypotheses and explain your decisions clearly, evaluate
  data to prioritise approaches, champion research for all users, and put
  accessibility at the heart of content design.

  Ask AI: "What does putting accessibility 'at the heart' of a content
  approach look like, as opposed to checking for it at the end?"

- [ ] **Lead strategic content improvement projects**

  Help lead the design and implementation of strategies, evaluating their
  impact against business objectives and user needs, and lead and direct
  strategic content improvement projects focused on the highest-priority
  areas.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior content designer decide which content
  improvement project deserves priority when several are equally
  plausible?"

- [ ] **Negotiate with stakeholders, and remove blockers**

  Negotiate with and influence stakeholders, manage relationships
  effectively, deal with challenging situations and remove blockers, and
  build long-term strategic relationships that support mutual needs while
  focusing on users.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior content designer negotiate with a
  stakeholder who wants content that doesn't serve user needs well?"

- [ ] **Build and combine prototypes for real stakeholder conversations**

  Build a variety of prototypes from paper sketches to coded versions,
  choose the most appropriate option for different audiences, combine
  prototypes with other content and design outputs to test with users and
  show stakeholders, and establish, test and iterate content patterns.

  Ask AI: "What makes a prototype genuinely persuasive to a stakeholder,
  as opposed to accurate but hard for them to picture in context?"

- [ ] **Keep applying an Agile mindset to real, fast-moving work**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from content designer
  level: applying an Agile mindset, working iteratively, taking
  considered risks, and keeping situational awareness of the team's work.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level Agile judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

Ask a lead content designer, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
