# User researcher - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the user researcher level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A user researcher is usually embedded in a multidisciplinary team and
responsible for planning and carrying out user research activities. At this
level, you're expected to work independently within a team, rather than in
combination with someone more senior. Most named skills grow at this level,
because independence means defining your own research's scope, standards
and stakeholder relationships, not just running sessions someone else has
set up.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own research.

- [ ] **Define research scope, and build your own good practice**

  Make sure research has a clearly defined scope and purpose, get advice
  from someone more experienced when you need help defining it before it
  starts, build your understanding of good practice standards, and improve
  your practice through reflection and feedback.

  Ask AI: "What questions should a user researcher ask to make sure a
  research project's scope is genuinely clear before it starts?"

- [ ] **Build stakeholder relationships, and reach consensus**

  Identify important stakeholders and communicate with them clearly and
  regularly, tailor communication to their needs, build and reach
  consensus, and use evidence to explain decisions.

  Ask AI: "How would a user researcher build genuine consensus with a
  stakeholder who disagrees with a research finding, rather than just
  getting their sign-off?"

- [ ] **Design research that fits an Agile workflow**

  Understand and explain the benefits and limitations of Agile working,
  design and conduct research so findings can be embedded into an Agile
  workflow, and understand and work with different roles in a
  multidisciplinary team to plan and do research.

  Ask AI: "How would a user researcher design a research activity so its
  findings arrive in time to actually influence an Agile team's next
  sprint?"

- [ ] **Include a genuinely diverse range of users**

  Understand the diversity of users of government services and why
  services need to be usable and accessible for everyone, and work with
  colleagues to include many kinds of users in appropriate research
  activities.

  Ask AI: "What practical steps would a user researcher take to recruit a
  genuinely diverse group of participants, rather than whoever is easiest
  to reach?"

- [ ] **Use and advocate for user-centred practices**

  Describe and use a range of user-centred practices, and advocate for the
  user by sharing research insights on user needs and changing behaviour
  with your team.

  Ask AI: "How would a user researcher advocate for a user need that
  conflicts with what the team originally planned to build?"

- [ ] **Keep applying basic analysis techniques**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from junior level:
  applying basic techniques for analysis and synthesis, involving your
  team, and presenting clear findings.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely independent-level analysis judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep planning and conducting research, with support**

  This skill also plateaus: planning and conducting research projects with
  support, and applying basic methods correctly.

  Ask AI: "What would planning and conducting research fully
  independently, rather than with support, look like at the next level?"

Ask a senior user researcher, or an equivalent lead colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
