# Lead technical writer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead technical writer level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead technical writer is an expert practitioner who directs a team of
technical writers, assuring the quality of technical writing across teams
and its alignment to strategy — the top of this profession's line. You
standardise tools, ways of communicating and processes, work closely with
service managers, technology leaders and senior stakeholders to resource
teams and resolve problems, promote the discipline of technical writing,
engage with the cross-government community, and keep up to date with
industry change. Stakeholder relationship management and user-centred
content design both sharpen at this level, because you're now directing
teams and standardising practice, not producing documentation yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Direct content life cycle management across teams**

  Direct the approach to content life cycle management so content is
  regularly reviewed by teams, oversee teams' work to make sure the right
  content gets produced, demonstrate extensive experience across multiple
  channels, encourage teams to evaluate their processes, take
  accountability for high-quality output, and identify capability gaps to
  help teams grow.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead technical writer identify a documentation
  capability gap across several teams, before it shows up as inconsistent
  quality?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and
  negotiate with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable
  progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior technology
  leader who has the authority to block a documentation standard but no
  obligation to compromise?"

- [ ] **Keep giving direction on meeting technical users' needs**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from technical writer
  level: giving direction on tools and methods, meeting user needs across
  channels, and applying strategic thinking to the end user's experience.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level judgement about technical
  readers add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep demonstrating technical knowledge, and collaborating across teams**

  This skill also plateaus: demonstrating knowledge of the technologies
  used to build and operate digital services, and collaborating closely
  with different digital disciplines.

  Ask AI: "What would leading technical credibility across a whole
  writing team add, beyond having strong technical knowledge yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep leading the design and implementation of strategy**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: leading strategy design and
  implementation, and directing the evaluation of strategies and policies.

  Ask AI: "What would leading documentation strategy across multiple
  teams add, beyond leading it well for one?"

- [ ] **Keep helping teams choose the best delivery approach**

  This skill also plateaus: identifying and comparing the best delivery
  methods, and helping teams manage outcomes and priorities.

  Ask AI: "What would standardising delivery approach decisions across
  several teams add, beyond helping one team choose well?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding when prototyping earns its place**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: understanding when to use a
  prototyping technique, and showing the team its value.

  Ask AI: "What would setting a standard for when teams should prototype
  documentation add, beyond judging it well for your own work?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
