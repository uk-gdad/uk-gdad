# Principal solution architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal solution architect level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal solution architect can be responsible for a large programme or
group of solution architecture projects, or a single, very complex or
critical business area — the top of this profession's line. You lead teams
of more junior solution architects, lead multiple projects or programmes,
build relationships with senior stakeholders across the organisation and
its partners, stay proactive in spotting improvement opportunities for both,
work with technology partners to inform their roadmaps, take a leading role
in the overall direction of business and digital capabilities, and inspire
other architects to understand how to meet organisational goals. Community
collaboration jumps sharply in scope at this level — from team-level
dynamics to resolving conflict at the highest level across an organisation
— which is the clearest sign of what separates this level from lead.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Resolve conflict at the highest level, and grow the community**

  Solve and unblock issues between teams or departments at the highest
  level, and coach the organisation on team dynamics and conflict
  resolution, while also building and growing the wider community.

  Ask AI: "What separates a principal architect who genuinely resolves
  organisational conflict from one who just mediates a truce that
  reappears later?"

- [ ] **Assess societal and industry trends, and build resilience against them**

  Assess how trends in society and industry practices might affect the
  organisation, work with people outside it to inform policies, strategies
  and standards, anticipate policy changes and build resilience through
  your architectural work, and coach others in identifying important
  trends.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal solution architect distinguish a societal
  trend worth building resilience against from one that's interesting but
  not yet relevant?"

- [ ] **Lead architectural governance at the highest levels of risk**

  Make and guide architectural decisions of high risk and complexity,
  identify and address risks across the organisation or wider government,
  lead and evolve architectural governance and assurance, and represent it
  as part of wider governance such as legal or commercial.

  Ask AI: "What does representing architectural governance as part of
  wider legal or commercial governance actually involve in practice?"

- [ ] **Connect strategy across the organisation, or across government**

  Define and connect strategies or visions across the organisation or
  wider government, and enable their implementation by advocating for
  resources and removing blockers.

  Ask AI: "What does 'connecting' strategies across an organisation
  actually involve, as distinct from defining several separate strategies
  that happen to coexist?"

- [ ] **Keep mediating difficult architecture discussions**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  communicating with stakeholders at every level, mediating difficult
  architectural discussions, and coaching others in architecture
  communication.

  Ask AI: "What would leading architecture communication for an entire
  organisation add, beyond mediating individual difficult discussions
  well?"

- [ ] **Keep choosing contractual frameworks, and selecting suppliers**

  This skill also plateaus: identifying appropriate contractual
  frameworks, and evaluating and selecting suppliers.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide commercial strategy add, beyond
  selecting the right framework and supplier for one programme?"

- [ ] **Keep leading framing the hardest problems**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: leading the framing of
  high-complexity problems, coaching others in defining problems, and
  helping challenge requirements and assumptions.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem framing across the whole
  organisation add, beyond doing it well for individual high-complexity
  problems?"

- [ ] **Keep leading technical design at high levels of risk**

  This skill also plateaus: creating technical designs of high risk and
  complexity, leading others in creating designs, and refining design
  standards throughout the life cycle.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide technical design standards add,
  beyond leading design well for individual high-risk projects?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
