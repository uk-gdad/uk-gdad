# Senior solution architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior solution architect level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A senior solution architect is responsible for a single, more complex
solution, usually where risk is moderate, and may lead and coach other
solution architects. You build relationships with senior stakeholders
across different business or technical areas, stay proactive in spotting
opportunities to improve the organisation, support multiple architecture
projects at once, find and use emerging technologies, and support others in
following best practice. Two named skills sharpen clearly at this level —
community collaboration and technical design throughout the life cycle —
because moderate risk means your own judgement now has to hold up without
someone senior checking it first.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
design decision — describe the actual trade-off and ask what you might be
missing.

- [ ] **Build genuine collaboration across your team**

  Work collaboratively, actively networking with others, adapt feedback so
  it's effective and lasting, use team health checks to spot dynamic
  issues, and help stimulate the right response to them.

  Ask AI: "What makes feedback 'effective and lasting' rather than
  something a colleague hears once and forgets?"

- [ ] **Maintain design quality and coherence as things change**

  Create technical designs of medium risk, impact and complexity, maintain
  appropriate quality and architectural coherence as a design responds to
  change, and use feedback to optimise and refine designs throughout the
  life cycle.

  Ask AI: "What does 'architectural coherence' actually mean when a design
  has already had to change several times during delivery?"

- [ ] **Keep leading communication of complex architecture topics**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from solution architect
  level: leading communication of complex or risky topics, communicating
  with senior stakeholders, and adapting your message to the audience.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level architecture communication add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep seeing beyond your own solution to the wider organisation**

  This skill also plateaus: aligning your work with other architects,
  tracking emerging issues and technologies, and identifying how other
  teams contribute to outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What would leading awareness of the wider organisation across
  several solutions add, beyond doing it well for one?"

- [ ] **Keep making architectural decisions of managed risk**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: making decisions of managed risk and
  complexity, identifying risks relevant to your team or domain, and
  engaging with governance with support.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level decision-making judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep framing problems, and giving teams a shared view**

  This skill also plateaus: framing problems of medium complexity,
  producing shared architectural representations, and describing options
  for delivery.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem framing across multiple projects
  add, beyond framing problems well for one?"

- [ ] **Keep supporting strategy development and implementation**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: supporting strategy or vision
  development, challenging requirements and assumptions, and supporting
  implementation through a roadmap.

  Ask AI: "What would contributing to strategy at a genuinely senior level
  add, beyond supporting its development well?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding the commercial context you work within**

  This skill also plateaus: understanding commercial processes, internal
  contacts, and sourcing strategies.

  Ask AI: "What would deepening commercial awareness, beyond understanding
  processes and contacts, look like at the next level?"

Ask a lead solution architect, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
