# Lead security architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead security architect level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A lead security architect undertakes complex work at a high risk level,
often working across several projects at once. You interact with senior
stakeholders across departments, reach and influence a wide range of people
across larger teams and communities, research and apply innovative security
solutions to new or existing problems while justifying and communicating
your design decisions, develop vision, principles and strategy for security
architects on a project or technology, work out subtle security needs,
balance requirements and understand the impact of decisions, produce
patterns and support quality assurance, act as the escalation point for
architects in lower grade roles, and lead the technical design of systems
and services.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is often
most useful for pressure-testing a design or risk decision — describe the
actual trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Monitor and reuse analysis, and feed back on policy**

  Monitor the analysis of a technical solution and make sure it's reused
  for similar problem sets, review solutions to identify areas for change,
  drive the collection of information that's analysed, and feed back on
  policy and requirements.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead security architect make sure good analysis
  from one project actually gets reused on a similar one, rather than
  redone from scratch?"

- [ ] **Develop patterns and principles, not just apply them**

  Design and review system architectures by developing patterns and
  principles, rather than only applying ones that already exist.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between applying an existing security
  pattern and developing a new one, and when does a lead architect need to
  do the second?"

- [ ] **Advise on higher-impact and more complex risk**

  Work with higher-impact or more complex risks, advising on their impact
  and whether they sit within risk tolerance, and apply different risk
  methodologies in proportion to the risk involved.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead security architect judge that a risk is
  complex enough to need a different methodology than a routine one?"

- [ ] **Start influencing policy on security implications of transformation**

  Interpret and apply your understanding across a genuinely complex area,
  and start influencing policy and process, business architecture, and
  legal and political implications rather than just responding to them.

  Ask AI: "What does 'starting to influence policy' actually look like for
  a lead security architect, as distinct from a policy team's role?"

- [ ] **Explain complex architectures, and how easily they could be exploited**

  Explain complex system architectures, identify and explain how easy or
  difficult it would be to exploit vulnerabilities in them, and lead and
  influence security technology in the wider security industry.

  Ask AI: "What makes an explanation of exploitability genuinely useful to
  a non-specialist decision maker, rather than technically accurate but
  unclear?"

- [ ] **Contribute to security technology developments, and engage the community**

  Contribute to and inform developments in security properties in
  technology, identify new technologies and design their use across the
  organisation, and engage with the broader security community.

  Ask AI: "What does genuine engagement with the broader security
  community look like for a lead architect, beyond reading what others
  publish?"

- [ ] **Keep communicating security and risk clearly**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from security architect
  level: demonstrating a deep understanding of security concepts,
  translating implications for stakeholders, and managing expectations to
  reach consensus.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level communication judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

Ask a principal security architect which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
