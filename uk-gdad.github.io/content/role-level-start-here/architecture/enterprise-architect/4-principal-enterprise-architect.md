# Principal enterprise architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal enterprise architect
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal enterprise architect leads at the highest level and is
responsible for making sure strategy is developed, agreed and followed —
the top of this profession's line. You network with senior stakeholders
across enterprises, actively seek improvement, support multiple communities
and teams at once, find and use best practice and emerging approaches,
inspire other enterprise architects, horizon scan for external influences,
support the long-term strategy, take responsibility for the overall
direction of business and digital capabilities, and own the creation,
maintenance and use of a digital twin — a live, structured representation of
the organisation's systems and processes. Community collaboration jumps
sharply in scope at this level — from team-level dynamics to resolving
conflict at the highest level across an organisation — which is the
clearest sign of what separates this level from lead.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Resolve conflict at the highest level, and grow the community**

  Solve and unblock issues between teams or departments at the highest
  level, and coach the organisation on team dynamics and conflict
  resolution, while also building and growing the wider community.

  Ask AI: "What separates a principal architect who genuinely resolves
  organisational conflict from one who just mediates a truce that
  reappears later?"

- [ ] **Lead architecture design, and ensure it aligns organisation-wide**

  Lead others in designing architectures that define the technology,
  people, process and other elements needed for organisational objectives,
  work with people beyond the organisation to influence structures and
  processes, and make sure organisation-wide changes align with the
  architectural strategy.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal architect make sure organisation-wide
  changes stay aligned with architectural strategy, when many of those
  changes are led by people who don't report to architecture at all?"

- [ ] **Lead architectural governance at the highest levels of risk**

  Make and guide architectural decisions of high risk and complexity,
  identify and address risks across the organisation or wider government,
  lead and evolve architectural governance and assurance, and represent it
  as part of wider governance such as legal or commercial.

  Ask AI: "What does representing architectural governance as part of
  wider legal or commercial governance actually involve in practice?"

- [ ] **Keep assessing societal and industry trends**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level: assessing
  how trends might affect the organisation, working with people outside it
  to inform strategy, and coaching others in identifying trends.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely principal-level horizon scanning add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep mediating difficult architecture discussions**

  This skill also plateaus: communicating with stakeholders at every
  level, mediating difficult architectural discussions, and coaching
  others in architecture communication.

  Ask AI: "What would leading architecture communication for an entire
  organisation add, beyond mediating individual difficult discussions
  well?"

- [ ] **Keep choosing contractual frameworks, and selecting suppliers**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: identifying appropriate contractual
  frameworks, and evaluating and selecting suppliers.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide commercial strategy add, beyond
  selecting the right framework and supplier for one piece of work?"

- [ ] **Keep leading framing the hardest problems**

  This skill also plateaus: leading the framing of high-complexity
  problems, coaching others in defining problems, and helping challenge
  requirements and assumptions.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem framing across the whole
  organisation add, beyond doing it well for individual high-complexity
  problems?"

- [ ] **Keep connecting strategy across the organisation and government**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: defining and connecting strategies
  across the organisation or wider government, and enabling their
  implementation.

  Ask AI: "What would genuinely principal-level strategic ownership add,
  beyond connecting strategies well across the organisation?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
