# Lead enterprise architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead enterprise architect level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A lead enterprise architect plays a high-level role in making sure strategy
is developed, agreed and followed. You network and communicate with senior
stakeholders across enterprises, seek opportunities for improvement, support
at least one community or team, find and use best practice and emerging
approaches, lead teams including other enterprise architects, horizon scan
for influences or risks, support delivery of the long-term strategy, and
take a leading role in the overall direction of business and digital
capabilities. Several named skills sharpen at this level — seeing the
whole context, architecture communication, commercial perspective, problem
definition, strategy design — because you're now shaping direction beyond
your own organisation, not just within it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a strategic call — describe the trade-off
you're weighing across the organisation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Assess societal and industry trends, and build resilience against them**

  Assess how trends in society and industry practices might affect the
  organisation, work with people outside it to inform policies, strategies
  and standards, anticipate policy changes and build resilience through
  your architectural work, and coach others in identifying important
  trends.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead enterprise architect distinguish a societal
  trend worth building resilience against from one that's interesting but
  not yet relevant?"

- [ ] **Mediate difficult architecture discussions, and coach others in it**

  Communicate with technical and non-technical stakeholders at every
  level and across organisations, mediate between people in difficult
  architectural discussions, gain support for high-risk, high-impact
  topics, and coach others in architecture communication.

  Ask AI: "What approach would help a lead architect mediate a genuine
  disagreement between two senior stakeholders about an architectural
  direction?"

- [ ] **Choose contractual frameworks, and select suppliers**

  Identify appropriate contractual frameworks and approaches, and
  identify, evaluate and select appropriate suppliers.

  Ask AI: "What should a lead enterprise architect weigh when selecting a
  contractual framework for a major piece of architectural work?"

- [ ] **Lead framing the hardest problems, and coach others in it**

  Lead framing a problem of high complexity, complication or risk so a
  solution can be created, coach others in defining problems and
  describing options, and help others challenge requirements and
  assumptions to spot opportunities.

  Ask AI: "What separates a well-framed high-complexity problem from one
  that just feels overwhelming because nobody has structured it yet?"

- [ ] **Connect strategy across the organisation, or across government**

  Define and connect strategies or visions across the organisation or
  wider government, and enable their implementation by advocating for
  resources and removing blockers.

  Ask AI: "What does 'connecting' strategies across an organisation
  actually involve, as distinct from defining several separate strategies
  that happen to coexist?"

- [ ] **Keep building genuine collaboration across your team**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior enterprise
  architect level: networking actively, adapting feedback so it lasts, and
  using team health checks to spot and address issues.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level collaboration add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep designing architecture, and making sure it gets used**

  This skill also plateaus: designing architecture that defines the
  elements needed for organisational objectives, and working with others
  to make sure it's actually used.

  Ask AI: "What would leading enterprise architecture design across
  several teams add, beyond designing well for one?"

- [ ] **Keep making architectural decisions of medium risk**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: making decisions of medium risk and
  complexity, addressing risks affecting multiple teams, and contributing
  to governance.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level decision-making judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

Ask a principal enterprise architect which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
