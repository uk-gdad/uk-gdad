# Associate business architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the associate business architect level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

An associate business architect uses their knowledge and experience to
address real business scenarios, while continuing to get support and
training for the role. You apply architectural methods following direction
and wider strategy to produce outputs, break down complexity into clear
outputs, explain complex business problems in plain language and artefacts,
bring together disconnected information to inform decisions, and recognise
issues that cut across different parts of the organisation. A new skill
appears here — stakeholder relationship management — because producing real
outputs means the people who'll use them start mattering as much as the
outputs themselves.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own organisation.

- [ ] **Help support the adoption of business architecture**

  Help support the adoption of business architecture, communicate its
  frameworks, principles and techniques clearly, support producing a
  multidimensional view of your organisation, and assess the impact of
  strategies, highlighting risks and issues.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate business architect help colleagues who
  are sceptical of business architecture see its value in a real piece of
  work?"

- [ ] **Select the right methods and tools for a project**

  Select and use the most appropriate tools and techniques to support
  planning, analysis, development, testing, implementation and improvement
  of systems and services within a project.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate business architect choose between
  several plausible tools or techniques for a specific project, rather
  than defaulting to the one they know best?"

- [ ] **Make and recommend decisions as risk and complexity grow**

  Make decisions characterised by managed levels of risk and complexity,
  recommend decisions as risk and complexity increase further, and resolve
  technical disputes between wider peers and indirect stakeholders,
  considering all views.

  Ask AI: "How should an associate business architect resolve a technical
  disagreement between two peers where both have a reasonable case?"

- [ ] **Advise as a specialist, not just apply guidance**

  Provide advice, guidance and recommendations based on your specialist
  knowledge and experience, propose methodologies and approaches to
  implementation, and frame problems so they're easy to understand and
  troubleshoot.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between giving a stakeholder an answer and
  giving them consultancy, and when does an associate business architect
  need to do the second?"

- [ ] **Learn to identify and communicate with stakeholders**

  Identify important stakeholders and communicate with them clearly and
  regularly, tailor communication to their needs, build and reach
  consensus, and use evidence to explain decisions.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate business architect work out who the real
  stakeholders are for a piece of work, beyond the obvious people in the
  room?"

- [ ] **Manage stakeholder expectations, not just communicate information**

  Work collaboratively and build relationships with others, identify
  issues through team health checks, manage stakeholder expectations, and
  host or moderate difficult discussions with the team or senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What does 'managing stakeholder expectations' actually involve
  day to day for a business architect, beyond just answering their
  questions?"

- [ ] **Connect your work to strategic goals, and contribute to strategy**

  Work within a strategic context and communicate how your activities meet
  strategic goals, and start contributing to developing strategy and
  policy rather than only implementing it.

  Ask AI: "What would contributing to strategy development actually
  involve for an associate business architect, as distinct from a policy
  team's role?"

Ask your manager or a more senior architect which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
