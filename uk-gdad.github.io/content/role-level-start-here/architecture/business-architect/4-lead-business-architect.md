# Lead business architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead business architect level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead business architect uses expert knowledge and experience to direct
work addressing high-level, complex business scenarios, supporting and
leading other business architects. You're accountable for the outputs the
whole business architecture team produces, lead and direct how architectural
principles and methods get applied, form a view of the whole enterprise that
stakeholders can genuinely understand, work at executive level, provide
overall direction for business architecture in support of strategy, and
develop the team's capability through coaching, mentoring and training.
Business architecture, methods and tools, and stakeholder relationship
management all sharpen clearly at this level, because you're now
responsible for the practice, not just your own output within it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is little level above lead in
this role, so use an AI tool as a sounding board rather than a teacher:
describe a real strategic call you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Build business architecture maturity across the organisation**

  Simplify complex business architecture frameworks into clear actions,
  develop maturity through something like a community of practice, lead or
  guide others in industry best practice, and identify strategic
  misalignment with recommendations for improvement.

  Ask AI: "What does 'business architecture maturity' actually look like
  for an organisation, and how would a lead architect help build it over
  time rather than all at once?"

- [ ] **Set direction for methods and tools across the organisation**

  Set direction and give others the means and authority to advise on the
  best methodologies and tools, ensuring consistent adoption, and assess
  the effectiveness of different approaches to recommend what supports
  collaboration and builds capability.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead architect assess whether an organisation's
  current methods and tools are genuinely working, rather than just
  familiar?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and
  negotiate with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable
  progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior stakeholder
  who has the authority to block a decision but no obligation to
  compromise?"

- [ ] **Deepen your understanding of business change**

  Show a strong understanding of business issues, events and activities
  and their long-term impact, define — rather than just support defining —
  principles, patterns, standards, policies, roadmaps and vision
  statements, and keep the focus on outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What separates a genuinely strong understanding of business
  change from a good one, in a way that shows up in the recommendations a
  lead architect makes?"

- [ ] **Keep making and recommending decisions, and building consensus**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from business architect
  level: making decisions of medium risk and complexity, recommending
  decisions as they increase, and building consensus between services or
  independent stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since business
  architect level, what would genuinely lead-level judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep leading guidance across the organisation**

  This skill also plateaus: providing advice based on significant
  specialist knowledge, and leading the definition of guidance that shapes
  how the organisation approaches delivery.

  Ask AI: "What would leading guidance across the whole architecture
  practice add, beyond leading it for one business area?"

- [ ] **Keep representing the architecture community to large audiences**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: mediating between people, managing
  expectations under time pressure, and speaking on behalf of the
  community to large audiences.

  Ask AI: "What's different about representing business architecture as a
  practice, compared with representing one team's work?"

- [ ] **Keep defining strategy, and evaluating whether it's working**

  This skill also plateaus: defining strategies and policies, providing
  guidance to others, and evaluating current strategies against business
  requirements.

  Ask AI: "What would leading strategy definition across the organisation
  add, beyond defining it well for one area?"

Ask a peer lead business architect, in your organisation or another part of
government, which of these has taken them longest to get right — that
conversation will teach you more than any general reading on architecture
leadership.
