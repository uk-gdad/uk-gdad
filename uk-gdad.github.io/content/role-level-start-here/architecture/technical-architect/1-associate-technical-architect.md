# Associate technical architect - start here

Welcome. If you are starting out as an associate technical architect, this
page is for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for,
one step at a time.

## Purpose

A technical architect provides technical leadership and architectural
design. As an associate, you support technical architects in putting
forward designs as solutions to technology challenges, usually under
supervision, working closely with developers when designing solutions and
building an understanding of the overall strategy and how your work fits
into it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Create technical designs of managed risk, with support**

  Create technical designs of managed risk, impact and complexity, provide
  guidance and support to teams using them throughout the life cycle, adapt
  a design during delivery if needed, and work with well-understood
  technology, identifying appropriate patterns.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to technical architecture. Explain, simply, what a
  technical design actually needs to contain to be usable by a delivery
  team, not just accurate."

- [ ] **Describe your reasoning behind architectural decisions**

  Describe the reasoning behind architectural design decisions, gather
  information to inform them, and understand the architectural governance
  and assurance relevant to your work.

  Ask AI: "What makes an architectural decision's reasoning genuinely
  convincing, rather than just a conclusion stated confidently?"

- [ ] **Understand how strategy links to organisational objectives**

  Explain how organisational objectives link to designing strategy, and
  describe the purpose and use of strategy, standards, patterns, policies,
  roadmaps, vision and mission statements.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, how an organisation's high-level
  objective actually turns into a technical strategy that shapes real
  designs."

- [ ] **Represent architecture clearly for a limited audience**

  Show an awareness of different ways of creating architecture
  representations for a limited audience, including technical and
  non-technical stakeholders, and gather and explain the information those
  representations use.

  Ask AI: "What makes an architecture representation genuinely clear to a
  non-technical stakeholder, rather than accurate but hard to follow?"

- [ ] **Build awareness of the wider context your work sits in**

  Identify relevant information that could inform your work — strategies,
  roadmaps, policies and technical trends — and understand how your work
  supports the team in enabling change.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate technical architect find the strategies
  and policies relevant to a piece of work, if nobody's pointed them out?"

- [ ] **Understand your team's dynamics and the value of feedback**

  Understand the work of others and why team dynamics, collaboration and
  feedback matter, even before you're expected to shape any of them
  yourself.

  Ask AI: "What does good team collaboration actually look like day to day
  on a small architecture team, beyond just being friendly to colleagues?"

You do not need to master all of this before you start. Ask your manager or
a more senior architect which of these your team leans on most, and start
there.
