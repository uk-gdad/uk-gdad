# Lead data governance manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead data governance manager level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A lead data governance manager undertakes complex work in the principles
and practice of data governance, co-ordinating with data owners to lead
wider data management competencies. You lead the formation, implementation
and management of governance frameworks, policies and standards, establish
wider data management competencies such as quality assurance and master
data management, and take responsibility for understanding and managing the
organisation's data risks, co-ordinating with data owners to accept or
resolve them. Every named skill grows at this level, because leading now
matters as much as doing.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is often
most useful for pressure-testing a governance or risk decision — describe
the actual trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Implement governance at scale, and gain accreditation**

  Implement and monitor data governance using standard methodology
  throughout the data life cycle within a large organisation, meet the
  requirements for relevant governance accreditation, and take
  responsibility for specific parts of a governance framework.

  Ask AI: "What changes about implementing data governance once it's
  applied across a genuinely large organisation, rather than one team?"

- [ ] **Optimise governance across the whole data life cycle**

  Understand and apply governance structures and principles over the data
  life cycle, demonstrate knowledge of industry standards, design and
  implement continuous improvements, and build organisational capacity and
  capability in data governance with stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What does 'building organisational capacity' in data governance
  actually involve, beyond applying good governance yourself?"

- [ ] **Set data standards senior leaders actually understand**

  Create data standards for different subjects, ensuring senior leaders
  understand them, work with subject matter experts across the
  organisation on best practice, monitor compliance organisation-wide, and
  recommend how breaches should be resolved.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager explain a technical
  data standard to a senior leader in a way that makes them genuinely care
  about it?"

- [ ] **Lead improving data maturity across the organisation**

  Lead implementing data best practice, communicate opportunities to
  improve data maturity, and lead monitoring and improving areas of
  maturity.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager prioritise which area
  of data maturity to improve first, when several are equally weak?"

- [ ] **Advocate for governance, and improve practices in your team**

  Advocate for effective data governance and management policies within
  your team and across the organisation, monitor and improve practices in
  your team, and help develop and support the tools and methods that
  comply with governance policy.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager make the case for a
  governance improvement to colleagues who see it as extra process?"

- [ ] **Recommend data literacy as a valuable skill across the organisation**

  Recommend data literacy as valuable at every level of the organisation,
  and identify training and other opportunities to build data literacy
  skills and a data-driven culture.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager make the case for data
  literacy training to a team that doesn't see data as central to their
  work?"

- [ ] **Judge when to adopt data innovation, and when to wait**

  Identify areas of innovation in data tools and techniques, and recognise
  the appropriate timing for adopting them.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager decide an emerging
  data governance tool is ready for adoption, rather than still too
  immature to depend on?"

- [ ] **Advise on higher-impact and more complex risk**

  Work with higher-impact or more complex risks, advising on their impact
  and whether they sit within risk tolerance, and apply different risk
  methodologies in proportion to the risk involved.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager judge that a risk is
  complex enough to need a different methodology than a routine one?"

- [ ] **Define strategy, and evaluate whether it's working**

  Define strategies and policies, providing guidance to others on working
  in the strategic context, and evaluate current strategies to make sure
  business requirements are being met and exceeded where possible.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager evaluate whether an
  existing governance strategy is still meeting business requirements,
  rather than assuming it still is?"

- [ ] **Own stakeholder strategy, not just individual relationships**

  Work with your team to develop and maintain a shared understanding of
  stakeholders, develop and implement stakeholder communications
  strategies, identify and resolve issues, and build long-term strategic
  relationships.

  Ask AI: "What does a genuine stakeholder communications strategy contain
  for data governance work, beyond a list of who to update and how often?"

- [ ] **Turn complex data into compelling, actionable stories**

  Turn complex data into compelling, clear and actionable stories, share
  data communication skills with your team and across government, present
  complex messages clearly, communicate both negative and positive
  information honestly, and give recommendations at both tactical and
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data story genuinely actionable, rather than just
  clear and well presented?"

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations with senior stakeholders**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of technical and non-technical
  stakeholders, manage active and reactive communication, and support or
  host difficult discussions with the team or with diverse senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data governance manager prepare for a
  difficult conversation with a senior stakeholder who disagrees with a
  governance decision?"

- [ ] **Address ethical and privacy concerns, and challenge policy**

  Work with stakeholders to identify and address ethical and privacy
  concerns, communicate how ethical issues fit into the wider
  organisational context, research developments in data ethics, and
  constructively challenge proposed data ethics policies.

  Ask AI: "What does 'constructively challenging' a proposed data ethics
  policy look like in practice, without it coming across as obstructive?"

Ask a head of data governance, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
