# Lead data scientist - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead data scientist level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead data scientist is a leader, coach and champion of data science, with
responsibility for managing and developing teams, building organisational
capability, and setting and communicating direction. You have experience
identifying opportunities to deploy data science techniques and challenging
the status quo, design and implement scalable outputs while driving
collaboration in support of organisational objectives, select the right
tools and technologies, navigate ethical and data challenges, communicate
and present effectively, and challenge delivery plans and priorities. Data
ethics, delivering business impact, developing data science capability and
understanding product delivery all sharpen further at this level, because
you're now setting organisational direction, not just delivering within it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for an organisational decision — describe the
trade-off you're weighing and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Own compliance and culture for data ethics across the organisation**

  Oversee compliance with data ethics standards and legislation across the
  organisation, develop and continuously improve an ethical framework,
  build a data ethics culture that influences how others actually act, and
  make sure ethics is applied appropriately when policies and programmes
  are formulated, implemented and evaluated.

  Ask AI: "What builds a genuine data ethics culture across a data science
  function, rather than one that only shows up in compliance checklists?"

- [ ] **Champion the role of data science, and take responsibility for products**

  Champion the role of data science within the organisation, understand
  and champion user research, identify and create opportunities to
  develop products in support of meaningful goals, and take responsibility
  for delivering scalable products with proper maintenance support.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data scientist make the case that data science
  should shape an organisational decision, to stakeholders who don't see
  it as central to their work?"

- [ ] **Lead capability building, and develop deep specialism**

  Act as a leader or technical specialist providing detailed support and
  guidance, set the direction of continuous professional development
  within your team, keep up to date with new developments and match them
  to organisational opportunities, and demonstrate in-depth understanding
  of a wide range of techniques plus deep knowledge of at least one
  specialism.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data scientist decide which technique is worth
  developing genuine specialist depth in, rather than staying broad across
  everything?"

- [ ] **Lead a team through the whole product delivery life cycle**

  Understand delivery method differences well enough to set out how your
  team should use and adapt them, lead a team through the phases of the
  product delivery life cycle, collaborate with the product manager to
  influence direction, involve the right teams to deliver smoothly, and
  keep oversight of any data science features implemented in products.

  Ask AI: "What does 'having oversight of data science features across
  products' actually involve for a lead data scientist, beyond building
  the features themselves?"

- [ ] **Keep using advanced techniques through the whole data life cycle**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from principal level:
  identifying opportunities for statistical insight, critiquing analyses,
  and using techniques through the whole data life cycle.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since principal
  level, what would genuinely lead-level statistical judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep identifying data engineering requirements**

  This skill also plateaus: helping identify engineering requirements
  while working with data engineers, and understanding a broad range of
  architectures and tools.

  Ask AI: "What would leading data engineering collaboration across
  several teams add, beyond doing it well for one product?"

- [ ] **Keep leading data science innovation, and coaching curiosity**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: being a leader in the data science
  space, coaching curiosity and creativity in others, and challenging the
  status quo.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide innovation leadership add, beyond
  coaching curiosity well within your own team?"

- [ ] **Keep writing complex programs, and setting coding standards**

  This skill also plateaus: writing complex programs, supervising junior
  analysts, setting coding standards, and performing user testing before
  launch.

  Ask AI: "What would setting coding standards across several teams add,
  beyond setting them well for your own?"

Ask a head of data science, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
