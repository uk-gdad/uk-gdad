# Trainee data scientist - start here

Welcome. If you are starting out as a trainee data scientist, this page is
for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for,
one step at a time.

## Purpose

Data science is a broad, fast-moving field spanning maths, statistics,
software engineering and communication, and data scientists usually work as
part of a multidisciplinary team to use data and analytics in service of
organisational goals. As a trainee, what matters most is being inquisitive:
curious about data, keen to learn, willing to ask questions and find out
what there is to know. You're building a basic understanding of software
development and analytical methods, learning to interpret statistical
output, becoming aware of how techniques like machine learning get used,
working as part of a team, preparing and manipulating data, presenting
effectively, and staying aware of ethical considerations and the
technologies involved.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation's data, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Write simple scripts under supervision**

  Show a basic understanding of software development principles, and
  write simple scripts under supervision.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to data science. Explain, simply, what separates
  writing a one-off script from writing code that a team can maintain
  later."

- [ ] **Interpret statistical output accurately**

  Show an understanding of the benefits of applied mathematics and
  statistics, carry out general analysis techniques for inspecting,
  exploring and visualising data, interpret statistical output accurately,
  and show an awareness of different accuracy metrics.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what a performance or accuracy metric
  is actually measuring, with a simple example."

- [ ] **Understand why data engineering support matters**

  Explain why you need engineering support to design and provide products,
  why data needs cleansing and preparing before use, and explain different
  architectures and data manipulation tools at a basic level.

  Ask AI: "Explain, simply, what a data engineer does that a data
  scientist typically doesn't, and why that division of labour exists."

- [ ] **Develop data science products as part of a team**

  Show an understanding of the organisation and the benefits of data
  science, develop products as part of a team, create basic visuals and
  presentations, and discuss how data and analysis align to user needs.

  Ask AI: "What does 'aligning data and analysis to user needs' actually
  mean for a trainee data scientist working on a real product?"

- [ ] **Start building your own development plan**

  Show an awareness of the various sources of training and self-directed
  learning available, create a continuous professional development plan
  with your manager's support, and build a basic understanding of key
  techniques such as machine learning.

  Ask AI: "What would a sensible first-year learning plan look like for a
  trainee data scientist, balancing breadth against depth?"

- [ ] **Manage your contribution to the wider team's work**

  Manage your contribution to tasks so it fits with the work of your wider
  team, rather than working in isolation from what everyone else is doing.

  Ask AI: "What questions should a trainee data scientist ask to make sure
  their piece of work fits into what the rest of the team is delivering?"

- [ ] **Stay curious, and keep asking questions**

  Adopt an inquisitive and curious approach to data, stay receptive to
  learning about data science and its techniques, and don't be afraid to
  ask questions and find out what there is to learn.

  Ask AI: "What are good questions a trainee data scientist should be
  asking their team regularly, even if they feel basic?"

- [ ] **Understand why data ethics and privacy matter**

  Explain the importance of using data ethics and privacy in your work,
  and identify the right channels to discuss ethical issues, with support.

  Ask AI: "What's an example of a data ethics concern a trainee data
  scientist might notice in their day-to-day work, and who should they
  raise it with?"

You do not need to master all of this before you start. Ask your manager or
a more senior colleague which of these your team leans on most, and start
there.
