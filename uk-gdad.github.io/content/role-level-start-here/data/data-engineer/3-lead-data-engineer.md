# Lead data engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead data engineer level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead data engineer is responsible for the design and implementation of
numerous complex data flows connecting operational systems to analytics and
business intelligence systems. You recognise and share opportunities to
reuse existing flows between teams, take responsibility for building
data-streaming systems, co-ordinate teams and set best practice and
standards, apply your knowledge of systems integration, and champion data
engineering across government. Most named skills grow again at this level,
because you're now setting standards other engineers and teams follow, not
just applying your own.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a standards or scale decision — describe the
actual trade-off you're weighing and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Establish enterprise-scale data integration procedures**

  Establish enterprise-scale data integration procedures across the whole
  data development life cycle, make sure teams adhere to them, and manage
  resources so data services work effectively at enterprise level.

  Ask AI: "What does establishing a procedure 'across the data development
  life cycle' mean in practice, and what tends to go wrong if a step gets
  skipped?"

- [ ] **Set and maintain data integration standards**

  Establish data integration standards, keep them up to date, ensure teams
  adhere to them, and keep abreast of best practice in industry and across
  government.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data engineer keep an integration standard
  relevant as technology and best practice keep changing, rather than
  letting it go stale?"

- [ ] **Recognise reuse opportunities across government and industry**

  Understand the concepts and principles of data modelling well enough to
  produce relevant models, work across government and industry to
  recognise opportunities for reuse and alignment, and design the method
  to categorise data models within your organisation.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a lead data engineer that a data model from
  another government organisation is genuinely reusable, rather than
  superficially similar?"

- [ ] **Bring multiple data sources together for analysis**

  Help teams apply a range of data profiling techniques, carry out source
  system analysis from a complex single source, and bring multiple data
  sources together in a conformed model for analysis.

  Ask AI: "What makes combining several data sources into one conformed
  model genuinely harder than modelling one source well?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to problems, not just solve them**

  Make sure the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate problems,
  implement solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to a data problem
  involve, beyond diagnosing and fixing it yourself?"

- [ ] **Judge when to adopt data innovation, and when to wait**

  Identify areas of innovation in data tools and techniques, and recognise
  the appropriate timing for adopting them.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data engineer decide an emerging data tool is
  ready for adoption, rather than still too immature to depend on?"

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations with senior stakeholders**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of technical and non-technical
  stakeholders, manage active and reactive communication, and support or
  host difficult discussions with the team or with diverse senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead data engineer prepare for a difficult
  conversation with a senior stakeholder who disagrees with an
  architecture decision?"

- [ ] **Keep designing and improving metadata repositories**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior data engineer
  level: designing appropriate repositories, suggesting improvements, and
  advising less experienced team members.

  Ask AI: "What would leading metadata strategy across several teams add,
  beyond designing and improving one repository well?"

- [ ] **Keep designing and building programs of real complexity**

  This skill also plateaus: designing, coding and documenting
  moderate-to-complex programs from agreed specifications, and reviewing
  specifications with others.

  Ask AI: "What would leading programming standards across a team add,
  beyond writing moderate-to-complex programs well yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep defining test conditions, and analysing results**

  This skill's wording plateaus too, unchanged throughout this role:
  reviewing requirements to define test conditions, identifying issues
  and risks, and analysing test results.

  Ask AI: "What would leading testing practice across a team add, beyond
  defining and analysing test conditions well yourself?"

Ask a head of data engineering, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
