# Head of data engineering - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of data engineering level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of data engineering leads multi-functional delivery teams to deliver
robust data services for their department, other government departments and
private sector partners — the top of this profession's line. You inspire
best practice for data products and services within your teams, build
community capability through technical leadership and career development,
and work with other senior team members to identify, plan, develop and
deliver data services.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Represent the community to large audiences**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships, adopt the right
  communication method for stakeholders at every level, moderate difficult
  discussions about high-risk, complex topics under real time pressure,
  and speak on behalf of the community to large audiences inside and
  outside the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data engineering prepare to represent
  their community to a large, mostly non-technical audience?"

- [ ] **Horizon-scan for the organisation, and introduce new ways of working**

  Investigate emerging trends in data-related approaches, perform
  horizon-scanning for the organisation, and introduce genuinely
  innovative ways of working.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data engineering distinguish a trend worth
  introducing organisation-wide from one that's interesting but not yet
  proven?"

- [ ] **Make metadata repositories a strategic asset**

  Identify how metadata repositories can support different areas of the
  organisation, communicate their value, and set up robust governance
  processes to keep them up to date.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data engineering make the case that
  investment in metadata repositories is worthwhile, to a stakeholder who
  sees it as overhead?"

- [ ] **Anticipate problems, and build problem-solving capability in others**

  Anticipate problems and defend against them at the right time,
  understand how a problem fits into the larger picture, help others
  describe problems clearly, and build problem-solving capability across
  the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What separates anticipating a data engineering problem before
  it happens from just responding well once it does?"

- [ ] **Set programming standards, and take technical responsibility**

  Set local or team-based standards for programming tools and techniques,
  select appropriate development methods, advise on applying standards and
  ensure compliance, and take technical responsibility for all stages of a
  software development project, advising project stakeholders directly.

  Ask AI: "What makes a programming standard get adopted consistently
  across a whole team, rather than followed inconsistently?"

- [ ] **Keep establishing enterprise-scale data integration procedures**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  establishing procedures across the data development life cycle, and
  managing resources so data services work effectively at enterprise
  scale.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely head-of-role process leadership add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep setting and maintaining data integration standards**

  This skill also plateaus: establishing standards, keeping them current,
  and staying abreast of best practice across industry and government.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide integration standards ownership
  add, beyond setting standards well for one area?"

- [ ] **Keep bringing multiple data sources together for analysis**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: helping teams apply profiling
  techniques, analysing complex single sources, and bringing multiple
  sources into a conformed model.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide data analysis leadership add,
  beyond bringing sources together well for one analysis?"

- [ ] **Build and maintain data models for real organisational needs**

  This skill's wording here matches the entry level of this role rather
  than the more advanced versions used at senior and lead level —
  explaining data modelling concepts, producing and maintaining models,
  and reverse-engineering them from live systems. That's the framework's
  own choice, not a gap in your practice; the leadership expected of you
  at this level shows up clearly in the skills above.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill reads more simply than my other
  responsibilities, what would genuinely head-of-role data modelling
  judgement look like if I pushed beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep defining test conditions, and analysing results**

  This skill's wording plateaus throughout this whole role: reviewing
  requirements to define test conditions, identifying issues and risks,
  and analysing test results.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide testing standards add, beyond
  defining and analysing test conditions well for one team?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
