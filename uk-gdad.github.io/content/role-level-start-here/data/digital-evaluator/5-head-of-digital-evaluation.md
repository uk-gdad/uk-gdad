# Head of digital evaluation - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of digital evaluation level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of digital evaluation leads the digital evaluation community and
acts as the point of contact between the community and the rest of the
organisation — the top of this profession's line. You remove blockers and
act as an escalation point for important issues, set the digital evaluation
strategy with input from lead and senior evaluators, define a framework for
quality assurance within evaluation, advocate for digital evaluation across
government and other analytical professions, and manage the organisation's
digital evaluation capability through business planning, development and
recruitment. Evaluation delivery, evaluation planning and monitoring across
the product life cycle all shift at this level from doing the work yourself
to guiding, coaching and setting standards for the people who do it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Guide evaluation technique, and coach the wider organisation**

  Provide guidance on evaluation techniques to evaluators, assure the
  evaluation work of digital evaluator teams, define best practice and
  methodologies for evaluation and make sure they're shared across teams,
  and coach people across the organisation to use appropriate evaluation
  techniques.

  Ask AI: "What does defining evaluation best practice that genuinely gets
  shared and used across teams involve, beyond writing it down once?"

- [ ] **Secure organisation-wide resources, and set quality standards**

  Influence the organisation's senior stakeholders to secure resources for
  evaluation projects, provide guidance on evaluation strategy for a group
  of products and services, make sure evaluation plans are delivered
  effectively and meet quality standards, and coach teams on creating
  their own plans and strategies.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of digital evaluation make the case for
  evaluation resourcing at an organisational level, rather than
  project by project?"

- [ ] **Lead best practice through the product life cycle, and manage risk**

  Lead and champion best practice, identify improvements to current
  approaches for monitoring and evaluation through the product life cycle,
  identify and raise evaluation or product risks at any stage, and coach,
  guide and support teams moving through its phases.

  Ask AI: "What early warning signs would a head of digital evaluation
  look for that suggest a product's evaluation approach needs to change
  before a phase transition?"

- [ ] **Keep communicating compelling, complex recommendations**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  communicating relevant, compelling stories, and making complex
  recommendations at both tactical and strategic level.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely head-of-role communication add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep owning compliance and culture for data ethics**

  This skill also plateaus: overseeing compliance with data ethics
  standards, developing an ethical framework, and building a data ethics
  culture.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide data ethics leadership for
  evaluation add, beyond owning it well for one area?"

- [ ] **Keep getting senior support for theories of change**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: getting senior stakeholder support
  for theories of change, providing guidance on developing them, and
  defining best practice for measuring indicators.

  Ask AI: "What would setting the standard for theories of change across
  the whole organisation add, beyond guiding it well for one product?"

- [ ] **Keep setting up multi-team quality assurance**

  This skill also plateaus: setting up tools and systems for assuring data
  and analysis across multiple teams, and coaching others in it.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide data quality assurance ownership
  add, beyond setting it up well across several teams?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
