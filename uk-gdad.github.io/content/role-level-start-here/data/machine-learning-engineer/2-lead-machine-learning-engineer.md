# Lead machine learning engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead machine learning engineer
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead machine learning engineer leads the technical development and
deployment of machine learning models — the top of this profession's line.
You lead the most complex technical work needed to develop models,
co-ordinate moving a model from research and development into production,
define ways of working across the whole machine learning life cycle,
identify training needs for engineers and related roles, help your team
work well with other teams and disciplines, assure the effectiveness of
models in use across the organisation, and define and communicate software
standards and guidelines covering ethics, risk and security. Programming,
systems integration, data science innovation and communication all
sharpen at this level, because you're now setting the standard other
engineers work to, not just meeting it yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real technical or organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to
argue the case you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Take technical responsibility for a whole project, not just your part**

  Advise on the right way to apply standards and methods to ensure
  compliance, maintain technical responsibility for all the stages and
  iterations of a software development project, and provide technical
  advice to stakeholders while setting team-based standards for
  programming tools and techniques.

  Ask AI: "What does 'maintaining technical responsibility for all stages
  of a project' actually involve for a machine learning system, beyond
  writing the model code well?"

- [ ] **Establish integration standards across a whole product life cycle**

  Establish standards and procedures across a service's product life
  cycle, including its development life cycle, and manage the resources
  that keep systems integration working effectively.

  Ask AI: "What does managing systems integration resources look like for
  machine learning models deployed across several products, rather than
  one?"

- [ ] **Lead data science innovation, and coach others in curiosity**

  Be a leader in the data science space, demonstrate in-depth knowledge to
  solve problems creatively and create opportunities for your team, act
  as a coach inspiring curiosity and creativity in others, keep up to date
  with the industry, and challenge the status quo.

  Ask AI: "What does 'coaching curiosity' in a machine learning team
  actually look like, as distinct from just assigning interesting
  problems?"

- [ ] **Represent the community to large audiences**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships, adopt the right
  communication method for stakeholders at every level, moderate difficult
  discussions about high-risk, complex topics under real time pressure,
  and speak on behalf of the community to large audiences inside and
  outside the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead machine learning engineer prepare to represent
  their team's model risk decisions to a large, mostly non-technical
  audience?"

- [ ] **Keep applying quantitative techniques to build and embed models**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level: applying
  quantitative techniques such as time series analysis and simulation, and
  using the statistical tools available.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely lead-level modelling judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep addressing ethical and privacy concerns**

  This skill also plateaus: working with stakeholders on ethical and
  privacy concerns, researching developments, and constructively
  challenging proposed policies.

  Ask AI: "What would leading data ethics standards for machine learning
  across the organisation add, beyond addressing concerns well for your
  own models?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
