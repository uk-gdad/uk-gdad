# Principal data analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal data analyst level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal data analyst leads teams or significant areas of work in data
analysis, determining and implementing the standards, processes and
structures that make sure data is prepared, managed and used effectively
across the organisation — the top of this profession's line. You influence
organisational decision making on data analysis and data use, oversee
continuous improvement of the organisation's data practices, and represent
the data analyst community inside and outside the organisation. Almost
every named skill grows again at this level; only data ethics and privacy
plateaus, because senior level already asked for genuine organisational
judgement on it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Champion and lead data governance across the organisation**

  Champion and lead data management and governance processes within your
  team and across the organisation, define and lead implementing data
  management responsibilities, define tools and training requirements, and
  enable continuous improvement of practices and compliance.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal data analyst get data governance
  genuinely embedded across an organisation, rather than followed only
  where it's actively enforced?"

- [ ] **Set data quality standards, and mentor others in them**

  Develop analytical data models using a deep understanding of data
  sources, make sure models are implemented in line with modelling
  standards, set data quality standards across the organisation or beyond,
  and mentor others in data quality and modelling.

  Ask AI: "What does setting a data quality standard for an entire
  organisation involve, beyond writing down what 'good' looks like?"

- [ ] **Influence stakeholders with statistical and analytical outputs**

  Identify opportunities to develop statistical insights that support
  organisational objectives, develop and guide others in a range of
  techniques, oversee quality assurance while continually innovating
  practice, and use outputs to influence stakeholders across and beyond the
  organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal data analyst use a statistical finding to
  genuinely influence a decision at the most senior level, rather than
  just inform it?"

- [ ] **Set visualisation standards, and promote their adoption**

  Translate business requirements into goals for data visualisations, set
  and advocate for new standards in inclusive, accessible visualisation,
  identify capability gaps and implement training, and promote the value
  and adoption of visualisation across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal data analyst get a new visualisation
  standard adopted across teams that already have their own established
  habits?"

- [ ] **Advise on problem statements, and lead stakeholder relationships**

  Advise on problem statement development, ensuring standards are met,
  oversee and advise on planning and implementing the project life cycle
  across varied projects, share good practice in adapting ways of working,
  and lead and develop stakeholder relationships for your team's projects.

  Ask AI: "What would advising on problem statement quality across many
  varied data projects look like, beyond writing good problem statements
  yourself?"

- [ ] **Champion the role of data across the organisation**

  Champion the role of data across the organisation, strategically
  communicate its business impact, lead others in delivering and
  identifying opportunities for scalable data products, and partner with
  other professions to decide when to pivot.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal data analyst make the case that data
  should shape a strategic decision, to stakeholders who don't see data as
  central to their work?"

- [ ] **Set coding standards, and ensure reproducibility across the organisation**

  Oversee and promote implementing coding standards on existing and new
  analytical outcomes, contribute to coding standards across the
  organisation, and ensure code and its documentation are reproducible
  organisation-wide.

  Ask AI: "What does 'reproducibility across the organisation' actually
  require, beyond one team's code being reproducible internally?"

- [ ] **Represent the community to large audiences**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships, adopt the right
  communication method for stakeholders at every level, moderate difficult
  discussions about high-risk, complex topics under real time pressure,
  and speak on behalf of the community to large audiences inside and
  outside the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal data analyst prepare to represent their
  community to a large, mostly non-technical audience?"

- [ ] **Keep addressing ethical and privacy concerns**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level: working
  with stakeholders on ethical and privacy concerns, researching
  developments, and constructively challenging proposed policies.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely principal-level data ethics judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
