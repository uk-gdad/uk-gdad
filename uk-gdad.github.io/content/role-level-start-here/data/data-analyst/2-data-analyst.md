# Data analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the data analyst level, this page is for
you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this repository
has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A data analyst contributes to the planning, analysis and implementation of
data, working to appropriate standards to make sure data is used to achieve
the team's goals. You prepare and manage data, apply the right techniques,
tools and processes to analyse it effectively, and communicate data to
stakeholders with different needs. Every named skill grows at this level,
because you're now producing real outputs rather than developing skills
under close supervision.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own data.

- [ ] **Design and test code for real research questions**

  Design the analytical approach and the code needed to address simple
  research questions, explain why testing code matters, review and improve
  analytical approaches under supervision, and produce documentation that
  describes the code you wrote.

  Ask AI: "Why does testing analytical code matter as much as testing
  production software, when it's often just producing a report?"

- [ ] **Plan a data model, and resolve data quality issues**

  Support planning a data model based on appropriate data sources, help
  make sure models and pipelines can be reused or reproduced, identify and
  resolve data quality issues, and prepare and cleanse data so it's fit
  for purpose.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data model 'reusable' by someone else later,
  rather than something only its original author understands?"

- [ ] **Answer research questions with the right techniques**

  Contribute to developing statistical and analytical insights and
  reports, apply appropriate techniques under supervision to answer
  research questions, follow and apply quality assurance standards, and
  respond to stakeholder questions about your methods.

  Ask AI: "How would a data analyst choose the right statistical technique
  for a research question, rather than defaulting to the one they're most
  comfortable with?"

- [ ] **Develop visualisations that tell a focused, inclusive story**

  Use the most appropriate visualisation to tell a focused story relevant
  to the team's goals, develop inclusive and accessible visualisations,
  and work with a multidisciplinary team to make sure a visualisation
  provides the information they actually need.

  Ask AI: "How would a data analyst check that a visualisation actually
  answers the question a multidisciplinary colleague needs answered?"

- [ ] **Contribute to defining a data project's problem and life cycle**

  Contribute to defining problem statements and gathering project
  requirements, explain the importance of different stages of the data
  project life cycle and contribute to planning them, and adapt your ways
  of working to deliver different outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What questions should a data analyst ask to help define a
  vague problem statement into something a project can actually deliver
  against?"

- [ ] **Deliver data products that align to business priorities**

  Explain how your work aligns to organisational priorities and delivers
  business impact, help identify business requirements, identify the data
  needed to address them, and develop and deliver data products with some
  support.

  Ask AI: "How would a data analyst check that a data product they're
  building genuinely aligns with an organisational priority, rather than
  just being technically sound?"

- [ ] **Follow governance, and use data management tools with some support**

  Follow organisational data governance, including policies on access,
  sharing, dissemination and protection, participate in or deliver data
  management across services or products, and use the right tools and
  methods with some support.

  Ask AI: "What data governance policies would a data analyst most need to
  understand day to day, beyond knowing they exist?"

- [ ] **Apply data ethics and privacy principles, and share risks**

  Apply fundamental principles of data ethics and privacy in your work
  under supervision, and share ethics and privacy risks through the
  appropriate channels.

  Ask AI: "What's an example of a data ethics or privacy risk a data
  analyst might spot in a real dataset, and how would they raise it?"

- [ ] **Communicate effectively across a multidisciplinary team**

  Communicate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders,
  support discussions within a multidisciplinary team that may have
  difficult dynamics, advocate for your team externally, and manage
  differing stakeholder perspectives.

  Ask AI: "What should a data analyst focus on when explaining a technical
  finding to a non-technical stakeholder, so it actually lands?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
