# Senior data analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior data analyst level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior data analyst leads data analysis in a team or across an area,
making sure data is appropriately prepared, managed and used, and that
analysis outcomes align with the organisation's goals. You guide others in
good practice for preparing, managing and using data, lead improving the
processes and ways of working, and lead communicating data and data
projects with stakeholders, adapting to their needs. Every named skill
grows again at this level, because leading now matters as much as doing —
other people's practice depends on your judgement, not just your own
output.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
standards or team decision — describe the actual situation and ask what you
might be missing.

- [ ] **Design and lead review of analytical code**

  Design and implement the analytical approach, including code, using
  appropriate standards and tests, lead collaboration to review and
  improve analytical approaches, including shared code, and guide others
  in producing analytical documentation.

  Ask AI: "What does leading a code review culture for analytical work
  actually involve, beyond reviewing your own team's code well?"

- [ ] **Plan the framework for an analytical data model**

  Plan the framework of an analytical data model based on appropriate
  data sources, support implementing it using the right tools and systems,
  and make sure your team understands why data quality and modelling
  matter.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between planning a data model's framework
  and implementing it, and where does a senior analyst's responsibility
  for each begin and end?"

- [ ] **Lead statistical insight development, and build stakeholder trust**

  Lead developing valuable statistical insights and reports, identify and
  apply a range of techniques while supporting others in applying them,
  quality assure statistical work across a team, and engage stakeholders
  to increase their understanding and trust.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data analyst build genuine stakeholder trust
  in a statistical technique they don't fully understand themselves?"

- [ ] **Simplify complex stories, and improve visualisation standards**

  Use a range of visualisations to simplify a complex story, adapt it to
  the needs of multidisciplinary stakeholders, advise others on inclusive,
  accessible visualisation, and suggest improvements to visualisation
  processes and standards.

  Ask AI: "What makes a complex story genuinely simplified by a
  visualisation, rather than just compressed into a smaller chart?"

- [ ] **Lead defining a data project's problem, and its stages**

  Identify and define a problem statement, apply and share project
  management methodologies, plan and implement stages of the data project
  life cycle, communicate progress with stakeholders, and adapt teams' ways
  of working.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data analyst turn a genuinely ambiguous
  business problem into a clear, well-scoped problem statement?"

- [ ] **Lead others in delivering business impact through data**

  Lead and support others in using data to deliver business impact, define
  and communicate the scope of that impact so its value is clear, identify
  opportunities to develop new data products, and develop and deliver
  scalable ones.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data product genuinely 'scalable', as opposed to
  one that works well for the specific case it was built for?"

- [ ] **Advocate for data governance, and improve practices in your team**

  Advocate for effective data governance and management policies within
  your team and across the organisation, monitor and improve practices in
  your team, and help develop and support the tools and methods that
  comply with governance policy.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data analyst make the case for a data
  governance improvement to colleagues who see it as extra process?"

- [ ] **Address ethical and privacy concerns, and challenge policy**

  Work with stakeholders to identify and address ethical and privacy
  concerns, communicate how ethical issues fit into the wider
  organisational context, research developments in data ethics, and
  constructively challenge proposed data ethics policies.

  Ask AI: "What does 'constructively challenging' a proposed data ethics
  policy look like in practice, without it coming across as obstructive?"

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations with senior stakeholders**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of technical and non-technical
  stakeholders, manage active and reactive communication, and support or
  host difficult discussions with the team or with diverse senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data analyst prepare for a difficult
  conversation with a senior stakeholder who disagrees with an analytical
  finding?"

Ask a principal data analyst which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
