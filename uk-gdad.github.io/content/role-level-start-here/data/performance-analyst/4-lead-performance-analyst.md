# Lead performance analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead performance analyst level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead performance analyst manages junior performance analysts at any
level, co-ordinating teams or groups of related analysts. You assist, take
forward and influence the strategy set by the head of performance analysis,
including data implementation and architecture, remove blockers and act as
an escalation point, act as an ambassador for analytics across communities,
departments and government, stay responsible for the accuracy and quality
of data and analysis, proactively share knowledge and promote a
data-driven culture as a community leader, and coach and mentor performance
analysts at every level below you. Every named skill grows at this level,
because leading now matters as much as doing.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a strategic or coaching decision — describe
the actual situation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Oversee performance measurement frameworks across projects**

  Oversee the development and consistency of performance measurement
  frameworks across multiple projects, and make sure they're aligned to
  business needs and strategy.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead performance analyst keep measurement
  frameworks consistent across several projects without forcing a
  one-size-fits-all approach on genuinely different work?"

- [ ] **Set up multi-team quality assurance, and coach others in it**

  Set up tools and systems for effectively assuring data and analysis
  across multiple teams, use appropriate approaches for verifying and
  validating data, influence senior stakeholders over data and analysis
  approaches, and coach and mentor others in assuring data and analysis.

  Ask AI: "What does multi-team data quality assurance actually require,
  beyond doing quality checks well within one team?"

- [ ] **Lead teams through the whole product life cycle, and manage risk**

  Develop sustainable support models, identify and deal with risks across
  or between all stages of the product life cycle, coach others,
  contribute to assessing other teams as they move through the phases, and
  lead and champion best practice.

  Ask AI: "What does a 'sustainable support model' for a product mean in
  practice, as opposed to support that works only while the original team
  is still around?"

- [ ] **Bring strategic and political awareness to user-centred analysis**

  Give direction on which tools or methods to use, bring insight into how
  user needs have changed over time, apply strategic thinking to
  delivering the best service for the end user, and consider the
  political strategy and implications of any analysis.

  Ask AI: "What does 'considering the political implications' of an
  analysis mean in a government context, without it compromising the
  analysis itself?"

- [ ] **Teach and supervise a wide range of analysis techniques**

  Understand, teach and supervise a wide range of analysis techniques,
  help organisations create and learn from strategic insights, and apply
  innovative approaches to resolve problems.

  Ask AI: "What does teaching an analysis technique well to a colleague
  actually involve, beyond demonstrating it once yourself?"

- [ ] **Identify opportunities in new digital technologies**

  Identify opportunities to use new digital technologies to enhance
  analytical capability, in alignment with business goals.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead performance analyst evaluate whether a new
  digital technology is worth adopting for analytical work, rather than
  just interesting in the abstract?"

- [ ] **Communicate compelling, complex recommendations**

  Communicate relevant, compelling stories using the most appropriate
  medium, and make complex recommendations at both a tactical and a
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead performance analyst tailor the same complex
  recommendation differently for a tactical audience and a strategic
  one?"

Ask a head of performance analysis, or an equivalent principal colleague,
which of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start
there.
