# Head of performance analysis - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of performance analysis level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

The head of performance analysis leads the performance analysis community,
acting as the point of contact between the community and the business —
the top of this profession's line. You remove blockers and act as an
escalation point for important issues, set the performance analysis
strategy with input from lead and senior performance analysts, take
responsibility for defining a framework for quality assurance within
performance analysis, champion performance analysis and performance
analysts across government and other analytical professions, and manage
the organisation's performance analysis capability through business
planning, development and recruitment. Every named skill carries the same
wording as lead level here — at the top of this profession's ladder, the
real differentiation is owning the strategy and the community, not a
longer skill description for each named capability.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Own performance measurement strategy across the organisation**

  Oversee the development and consistency of performance measurement
  frameworks across multiple projects, and make sure they're aligned to
  business needs and strategy.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of performance analysis make sure measurement
  frameworks stay consistent across an organisation with genuinely
  different kinds of projects?"

- [ ] **Define the quality assurance framework for the whole community**

  Set up tools and systems for effectively assuring data and analysis
  across multiple teams, use appropriate approaches for verifying and
  validating data, influence senior stakeholders over data and analysis
  approaches, and coach and mentor others in assuring data and analysis.

  Ask AI: "What does defining a quality assurance framework for an entire
  professional community involve, beyond setting it up well for one
  programme?"

- [ ] **Lead best practice through the product life cycle, organisation-wide**

  Develop sustainable support models, identify and deal with risks across
  all stages of the product life cycle, coach others, contribute to
  assessing other teams, and lead and champion best practice across the
  organisation.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide leadership of product life cycle
  best practice add, beyond leading it well for individual teams?"

- [ ] **Bring strategic and political awareness to user-centred analysis**

  Give direction on which tools or methods to use, bring insight into how
  user needs have changed over time, apply strategic thinking to
  delivering the best service for the end user, and consider the
  political strategy and implications of any analysis.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of performance analysis balance political
  sensitivity against analytical honesty when the two pull in different
  directions?"

- [ ] **Build teaching and supervision capability across the community**

  Understand, teach and supervise a wide range of analysis techniques,
  help organisations create and learn from strategic insights, and apply
  innovative approaches to resolve problems.

  Ask AI: "What would building teaching capability across a whole
  performance analysis community add, beyond teaching techniques well
  yourself?"

- [ ] **Set direction on emerging digital technology**

  Identify opportunities to use new digital technologies to enhance
  analytical capability, in alignment with business goals.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of performance analysis decide which emerging
  digital technology is worth investing organisational resources in?"

- [ ] **Represent the community with compelling, complex recommendations**

  Communicate relevant, compelling stories using the most appropriate
  medium, and make complex recommendations at both a tactical and a
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of performance analysis represent their
  community's recommendations to senior leaders who ultimately control
  the resources it needs?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
