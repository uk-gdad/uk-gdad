# Head of data ethics - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of data ethics level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

The head of data ethics leads the development and implementation of
organisational data ethics policies — the top of this profession's line.
You provide research and expertise on data ethics, enable people across the
organisation to implement best practice, communicate to raise awareness and
mediate across the organisation, and help people ask questions and discuss
genuine ethical dilemmas. Five named skills sharpen clearly beyond what a
data ethics lead is asked for — data ethics and privacy, analysis and
synthesis, applied social sciences, product ownership, and stakeholder
relationship management — because you now own organisation-wide policy, not
just support for it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Own compliance and culture for data ethics across the organisation**

  Oversee compliance with data ethics standards and legislation across the
  organisation, develop and continuously improve an ethical framework,
  build a data ethics culture that influences how others actually act, and
  make sure ethics is applied appropriately when policies and programmes
  are formulated, implemented and evaluated.

  Ask AI: "What builds a genuine data ethics culture across an
  organisation, rather than one that only shows up in compliance
  checklists?"

- [ ] **Turn findings into decisions for the whole organisation**

  Expertly draw together and evaluate data, distil complex documents,
  turn research into findings that inform data ethics decisions for the
  entire organisation, build the organisation's capacity for analysis and
  synthesis, and help it continually assure and improve its practices.

  Ask AI: "What does building an organisation's own capacity for ethical
  analysis look like, beyond producing good findings yourself each time?"

- [ ] **Apply social science theory to strategic oversight**

  Explain complex concepts across the social sciences, apply their
  theories to the strategic oversight of data projects and policies as
  well as to evaluate assumptions, identify and use best practice in data
  ethics, and work with academics and external researchers to publish
  research.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between applying social science theory to
  one project and applying it to strategic oversight of an organisation's
  entire data ethics practice?"

- [ ] **Own organisation-wide data ethics tools and practice**

  Define and create organisation-wide data ethics tools that translate
  theoretical principles into practice, demonstrate expertise in product
  management, set new ways of working, capture user needs into
  deliverables, and demonstrate expertise in feedback and evaluation.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide ownership of data ethics tooling
  look like, beyond building a good tool for one team?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and
  negotiate with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable
  progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior stakeholder
  who has the authority to block an ethics decision but no obligation to
  compromise?"

- [ ] **Keep translating technical concepts for non-technical audiences**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from data ethics lead
  level: expertly translating technical concepts, understanding how
  products get built, and supporting data scientists and engineers in
  implementing ethics.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely head-of-role communication add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep handling harder conversations across the organisation**

  This skill also plateaus: listening to and interpreting stakeholder
  needs, managing communication, and hosting difficult discussions with
  diverse senior stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What would leading organisation-wide stakeholder communication
  add, beyond handling individual difficult conversations well?"

- [ ] **Keep working with complex risk, and building consensus**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: working with consequential or complex
  risks, building consensus between stakeholders, and applying risk
  methodologies in proportion.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide risk ownership for data ethics
  add, beyond working with complex individual risks well?"

- [ ] **Keep bringing underrepresented perspectives into your work**

  This skill also plateaus: incorporating views from underrepresented
  groups, engaging with the diversity and inclusion plan, and drawing on
  a multidisciplinary understanding of bias.

  Ask AI: "What would setting the standard for inclusive consultation
  across the organisation add, beyond practising it well yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep coordinating the team's response to ethical problems**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: ensuring the right actions are taken
  to investigate and resolve problems, and coordinating the team's
  response.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide problem management for data
  ethics add, beyond coordinating your own team's response well?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
