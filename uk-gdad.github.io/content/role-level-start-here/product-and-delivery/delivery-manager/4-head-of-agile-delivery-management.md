# Head of (Agile) delivery management - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of (Agile) delivery management
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything
else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it
names.

## Purpose

A head of (Agile) delivery management is an experienced practitioner who
exemplifies what good looks like across the delivery roles. You represent
and champion the role within your department, across government and in
industry, lead the community of practice and build its capability, take
responsibility for recruiting the right people into the right teams, support
professional development and continuous improvement across the community,
work with other heads of role to promote effective cross-functional
delivery, and stay credible and influential across departments. Team
dynamics and collaboration jumps sharply in scope at this level — from
shaping one team's dynamic to shaping how an entire organisation resolves
conflict — which is the clearest sign of what separates this level from
senior.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Lead organisational change and resolve the hardest conflicts**

  Change organisational structures towards sustainable designs, lead
  strategy for the entire organisation by joining up business needs with
  innovative analysis, make and justify high-risk and high-complexity
  decisions, build consensus between highly independent or diverse
  stakeholders, solve and unblock issues at the highest level, and coach
  the organisation on team dynamics and conflict resolution.

  Ask AI: "What separates a head of delivery who genuinely resolves
  organisational conflict from one who just mediates a truce that
  reappears later?"

- [ ] **Own escalation for the hardest commercial issues**

  Act as the escalation point for large or high-risk commercial management
  issues, and coach others in handling commercial management appropriately.

  Ask AI: "What makes a commercial issue serious enough to need a head of
  delivery's direct involvement, rather than being resolved at senior
  delivery manager level?"

- [ ] **Lead teams through the life cycle, and support the people using it**

  Lead and coach teams through different phases of the life cycle, ensure
  effective support for the product or service and its users, predict,
  prevent, mitigate and manage risks at different phases, and contribute to
  assessing other teams with guidance and support.

  Ask AI: "What does 'predicting and preventing' a life cycle risk look
  like in practice, as opposed to reacting well once it's already
  happened?"

- [ ] **Keep leading Agile and Lean practice as a recognised expert**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level: coaching
  and leading teams in Agile and Lean good practice, tailoring the approach
  through the life cycle, and experimenting with new ways of working across
  the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What would leading Agile and Lean practice for an entire
  professional community add, beyond being a recognised expert for
  individual teams?"

- [ ] **Keep leading planning in a genuinely complex environment**

  This skill also plateaus: leading a continual planning process in a very
  complex environment, planning beyond product delivery, and coaching other
  teams as the central point of expertise.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide planning leadership add, beyond
  coordinating dependencies across a range of services?"

- [ ] **Keep tackling the most complicated risks and dependencies**

  This skill plateaus too: optimising delivery flow, addressing the most
  complicated risks with no clear ownership, and finding innovative ways to
  unblock them.

  Ask AI: "What would it mean to build organisational capability for
  handling ownerless risks, rather than personally unblocking each one?"

- [ ] **Keep representing the delivery community to large audiences**

  This skill's wording plateaus as well: mediating between people,
  managing expectations and difficult discussions under time pressure, and
  speaking on behalf of the community to large audiences.

  Ask AI: "What's different about representing delivery management as a
  profession, compared with representing one senior delivery manager's
  programme?"

- [ ] **Keep negotiating and setting budgets, and writing business cases**

  This skill also plateaus: negotiating and setting budgets in complex
  environments, and writing or contributing to business cases with clear
  business-value propositions.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of delivery make the business case for
  investing in the delivery management community itself, not just in
  individual programmes?"

- [ ] **Keep challenging organisational process**

  This skill's wording plateaus too, unchanged since delivery manager
  level: identifying and challenging organisationally complex processes,
  and coaching the organisation to inspect and adapt them.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since delivery
  manager level, what would genuinely head-of-role process leadership add
  beyond what's written here?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
