# Delivery manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the delivery manager level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A delivery manager is accountable for the performance of the team — not just
the process, the actual outcomes. You build and maintain teams that are
motivated, collaborating and working well, identify obstacles and help the
team overcome them, focus the team on what genuinely matters to delivery,
encourage continuous improvement, and coach both team members and others in
applying the right Agile and Lean tools and techniques. The complexity and
breadth of what you're responsible for will vary by context, but every
named skill grows at this level, because the step up from associate is real
accountability, not just competence.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own team.

- [ ] **Build and empower a genuinely motivated team**

  Bring people together to form a motivated team, help create the right
  environment for them to work in and empower them to deliver, recognise
  and deal with issues, and help create the best team makeup for the
  situation.

  Ask AI: "What early signs would tell a delivery manager a team isn't
  genuinely motivated, even if everyone seems to be getting on fine?"

- [ ] **Challenge organisational process, not just team process**

  Identify and challenge organisational processes that are increasingly
  complex or unnecessarily complicated, add value by coaching the
  organisation to inspect and adapt them, and guide teams through
  implementing a new process.

  Ask AI: "How would a delivery manager challenge an organisational process
  that's slowing teams down, without just being seen as difficult?"

- [ ] **Choose and adapt Agile and Lean approaches deliberately**

  Compare and select the most appropriate delivery methods and processes,
  recognise and adapt when something isn't working, use a blended approach
  where it fits the context, and help teams measure, evaluate and visualise
  their outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would a delivery manager decide a delivery approach isn't
  working, rather than assuming it just needs more time?"

- [ ] **Plan with real data, and manage complex dependencies**

  Understand the environment and prioritise the highest-value tasks, use
  data to inform planning, manage complex internal and external
  dependencies, provide delivery confidence, remove blockers, and make sure
  teams plan appropriately for their own capacity.

  Ask AI: "What data would genuinely improve a delivery manager's planning
  decisions, as opposed to data that just looks reassuring?"

- [ ] **Keep the delivery flow moving, including risks outside your control**

  Facilitate the delivery flow of a team, managing pace and tempo, and
  actively address internal and external risks, issues and dependencies —
  including ones owned outside the team.

  Ask AI: "How should a delivery manager approach a risk they can see but
  don't own, and can't force someone else to fix?"

- [ ] **Manage delivery through the life cycle, with some support**

  Manage the delivery of products and services that add value through
  different life cycle phases, with some support, identify whether and how
  a product or service should move to the next phase, and ensure it's
  developed in line with the right service standards.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a delivery manager a product or service is
  genuinely ready to move to the next life cycle phase, rather than just
  due to move by the calendar?"

- [ ] **Manage stakeholder communication actively, not just clearly**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of technical and non-technical
  stakeholders, manage both active and reactive communication, and support
  or host difficult discussions with the team or with diverse senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between active and reactive communication
  for a delivery manager, and how would they balance the two in a busy
  week?"

- [ ] **Take on real financial accountability**

  Balance cost against value, consider the impact of user needs on that
  balance, report on financial delivery, monitor cost and budget, and know
  how and when to escalate financial issues.

  Ask AI: "How would a delivery manager explain a cost-versus-value
  trade-off to a stakeholder who only wants to hear about cost?"

- [ ] **Keep the basics of commercial management**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from associate level: being
  the point of contact for contracted suppliers, understanding the right
  internal contacts, and knowing when to bring third parties into a
  project.

  Ask AI: "What would taking more ownership of a supplier relationship,
  beyond being a point of contact, look like for a delivery manager?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
