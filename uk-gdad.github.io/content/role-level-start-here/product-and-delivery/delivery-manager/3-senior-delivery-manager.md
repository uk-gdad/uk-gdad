# Senior delivery manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior delivery manager level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior delivery manager is accountable for the effective delivery of
complex, high-risk products and services. The role is similar to a delivery
manager's, but you bring more experience across a range of products and
services, throughout the entire product life cycle, and carry greater
responsibility as the main point of escalation when things go wrong. You
have strong communication skills and engage senior stakeholders directly,
and you coach and mentor other delivery managers. Almost every named skill
grows at this level, because the step up is genuinely about scale and
stakes, not a different job.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing an
escalation decision — describe the actual situation and ask what you might
be missing.

- [ ] **Guide products and services through the entire life cycle**

  Work and consult with the right people at the right time to move through
  the life cycle and deliver value, use evidence to decide whether a team
  should continue, change direction or stop, identify the tools and
  techniques each phase needs, and guide colleagues and stakeholders
  through it.

  Ask AI: "What evidence would genuinely justify stopping a product or
  service, as opposed to evidence that just makes stopping feel
  justified?"

- [ ] **Become a recognised expert in Agile and Lean approaches**

  Coach and lead teams in Agile and Lean good practice, create and tailor
  the right approach for a team through the whole life cycle, experiment
  with new ways of working to improve delivery across the organisation, and
  act as a recognised expert and advocate.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior delivery manager introduce a new way of
  working across the organisation without it feeling imposed on teams that
  weren't consulted?"

- [ ] **Lead planning in a genuinely complex environment**

  Lead a continual planning process in a very complex environment, plan
  beyond product delivery, identify dependencies across services and
  co-ordinate delivery, and coach other teams as the central point of
  expertise.

  Ask AI: "What does 'planning beyond product delivery' actually mean for a
  senior delivery manager, compared with planning one product's roadmap?"

- [ ] **Tackle the most complicated risks and dependencies**

  Optimise the delivery flow of teams, actively address the most
  complicated risks, issues and dependencies — including ones with no clear
  ownership at all — and identify innovative ways to unblock them.

  Ask AI: "How should a senior delivery manager approach a risk that nobody
  in the organisation clearly owns?"

- [ ] **Accelerate team development, and choose feedback deliberately**

  Identify and rectify problems in team dynamics, use team health checks to
  spot issues, engage in different types of feedback and choose the right
  one for the moment so decisions actually stick, and accelerate a team's
  development cycle.

  Ask AI: "What different types of feedback should a senior delivery
  manager have in their toolkit, and how would they choose between them in
  a specific moment?"

- [ ] **Take responsibility for complex supplier relationships**

  Take responsibility for complex relationships with contracted suppliers,
  identify appropriate contractual frameworks and suppliers, negotiate with
  them, and get good value out of contracts and suppliers.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach would a senior delivery manager use
  with a supplier who has more leverage in the relationship than the
  organisation does?"

- [ ] **Set budgets, and write the business case**

  Negotiate, influence or set budgets in complex environments, and write or
  input into business cases while communicating business-value
  propositions clearly.

  Ask AI: "What makes a business-value proposition genuinely persuasive to
  a senior stakeholder who's seen dozens of them?"

- [ ] **Represent the delivery community to large audiences**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships, adopt the right
  communication method for stakeholders at every level, manage expectations
  and moderate difficult discussions about high-risk topics under real time
  pressure, and speak on behalf of the community to large audiences inside
  and outside the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior delivery manager prepare to represent their
  delivery community to a large, mostly non-technical audience?"

- [ ] **Keep challenging organisational process**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from delivery manager
  level: identifying and challenging organisationally complex processes,
  coaching the organisation to inspect and adapt, and guiding teams through
  new process implementation.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since delivery
  manager level, what would genuinely senior-level process leadership add
  beyond what's written here?"

Ask a head of delivery, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of these
matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
