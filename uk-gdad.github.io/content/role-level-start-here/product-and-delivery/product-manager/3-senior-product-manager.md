# Senior product manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior product manager level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior product manager can work on a group of products that share a value
proposition, or a single product that's complex, high risk or sensitive.
You're responsible for product quality and value, working with teams to
identify, implement and improve strategies and tactics, and you might also
take part in programme management activity. At this level you lead products
through the full life cycle, make decisions based on the overall quality and
value of your products, manage associate product managers and product
managers, and get involved in recruiting them. Most named skills grow at
this level, because you're now accountable for other people's product
decisions, not only your own.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
strategic call — describe the actual trade-off and ask what you might be
missing.

- [ ] **Set strategy and vision, and plan across multiple teams**

  Set the strategy and vision for your products collaborating with others,
  create a prioritised roadmap and use it to discuss strategy at all
  levels, plan prioritised work across multiple teams while minimising
  dependencies and risks, support multiple teams in delivering quality
  outcomes, and coach and mentor others in product management.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior product manager plan work across several
  teams so their dependencies don't quietly become each other's blockers?"

- [ ] **Guide products through the entire life cycle**

  Work and consult with the right people at the right time to move through
  the life cycle and deliver value, use evidence to decide whether a team
  should continue, change direction or stop, identify the tools and
  techniques each phase needs, and guide colleagues and stakeholders
  through it.

  Ask AI: "What evidence would genuinely justify stopping a product, as
  opposed to evidence that just makes stopping feel justified?"

- [ ] **Lead outcome measurement, and champion data-driven decisions**

  Lead product teams in creating effective methods for measuring outcomes,
  advocate for data-driven decision making, help others prioritise better,
  use data to drive continuous improvement, and find opportunities to
  share and connect product data.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior product manager help another product
  manager make a data-driven decision they're currently avoiding?"

- [ ] **Make and justify value-based decisions at organisational scale**

  Advocate for user needs while balancing value against cost for the
  organisation, determine and persuade others of a product's benefit, write
  or contribute to business cases, communicate business-value
  propositions, and help others make value-based decisions.

  Ask AI: "What makes a business case genuinely persuasive to a senior
  stakeholder who's seen dozens of them, beyond just listing the
  benefits?"

- [ ] **Represent product management to senior stakeholders**

  Seek opportunities for reuse and learning across your organisation,
  represent product management in conversations with senior stakeholders,
  protect teams from unnecessary distractions, coach and manage others in
  product management, and lead some community activities.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior product manager represent their area's
  product decisions convincingly to senior stakeholders who weren't
  involved in making them?"

- [ ] **Ensure strategy delivers its intended benefits**

  Make sure vision and strategy deliver the intended benefits and policy
  intent, using evidence to challenge them when needed, work with
  stakeholders to build agreement and trust, and proactively review and
  iterate the strategy based on insight.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a senior product manager that a strategy isn't
  delivering its intended benefits, even while individual features keep
  shipping successfully?"

- [ ] **Keep managing stakeholder relationships across your team**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from product manager
  level: developing a shared understanding of stakeholders, implementing
  communications strategies, and building long-term relationships.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since product
  manager level, what would genuinely senior-level stakeholder judgement
  add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep representing users in internal discussions**

  This skill also plateaus: using user insights to represent users
  internally, advocating for research with all user types, and
  recommending the right tools for insight.

  Ask AI: "What would leading user-centred practice across several product
  teams add, beyond representing users well in one team's discussions?"

- [ ] **Keep choosing and adapting Agile and Lean approaches**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: comparing and selecting delivery
  methods, adapting when something isn't working, and helping teams
  measure and visualise outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What would leading Agile and Lean practice across several
  product teams add, beyond applying it well within one?"

Ask a lead product manager, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
