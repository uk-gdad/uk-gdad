# Digital portfolio analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are starting out as a digital portfolio analyst, this page
is for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for,
one step at a time. Some organisations call this level "associate digital
portfolio manager" — it's the same role.

## Purpose

A digital portfolio manager co-ordinates and manages a group, or
"portfolio", of digital projects, using an iterative and flexible approach
to help define how that portfolio is governed. As a digital portfolio
analyst, you support the tracking, analysis and delivery of outcomes across
that portfolio while you're still learning basic portfolio management
skills, reporting to a more senior portfolio manager rather than owning
governance decisions yourself. That reporting line is deliberate: portfolio
decisions affect many projects at once, and it takes time to build the
judgement to make them well.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation's portfolio, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Learn why planning and forecasting matter**

  Understand the importance of planning and forecasting, and show an
  awareness of the different ways a plan can be developed.

  Ask AI: "Explain, simply, why a portfolio of projects needs planning and
  forecasting beyond what each individual project already does."

- [ ] **Understand why governance and assurance exist**

  Explain why governance and assurance matter, and follow the relevant
  processes under supervision.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what 'governance and assurance' means
  for a portfolio of digital projects, with a concrete example of what it
  actually checks."

- [ ] **Learn to identify and communicate with stakeholders**

  Identify important stakeholders and communicate with them clearly and
  regularly, tailor communication to their needs, build and reach
  consensus, and use evidence to explain decisions.

  Ask AI: "How would a digital portfolio analyst work out who the real
  stakeholders are for a portfolio, beyond the obvious project leads?"

- [ ] **Practise communicating analysis clearly, with support**

  Choose the right medium to communicate findings, shape communications for
  the audience, present analysis and visualisations clearly for both
  specialist and non-specialist audiences with support, and give tactical
  recommendations based on your analysis.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data visualisation genuinely clear to a
  non-specialist audience, rather than just accurate?"

- [ ] **Start connecting your work to the wider strategy**

  Explain the strategic context of your work and why it matters, and
  support strategic planning in an administrative capacity.

  Ask AI: "How would a digital portfolio analyst find out the strategic
  context behind the portfolio they're working on, if nobody's explained
  it to them?"

- [ ] **Build a working knowledge of Agile methodology**

  Show an awareness of Agile methodology and how to apply its principles in
  practice, take an open-minded approach, explain why iteration matters,
  and iterate quickly yourself.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to portfolio management. Explain, simply, how Agile
  principles apply at portfolio level, as distinct from within one
  project."

You do not need to master all of this before you start. Ask your manager or
a more senior portfolio manager which of these your team leans on most, and
start there.
