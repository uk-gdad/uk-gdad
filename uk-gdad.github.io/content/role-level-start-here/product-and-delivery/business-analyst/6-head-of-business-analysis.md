# Head of business analysis - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of business analysis level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

The head of business analysis leads the business analysis function across
the whole enterprise area and builds business analysis capability for the
organisation — the top of this profession's line. Only two of the eleven
named skills grow further from lead level here: adapting to delivery
methodologies, and consultancy. That's not a gap in the framework; at the
top of a specialist ladder, the real differentiation is organisational
reach and the standard you set for the function, not a longer skill
description for every named capability.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Set and evolve delivery methodology standards for the whole function**

  Set and iterate standards for working within different delivery
  methodologies, actively promote collaboration so the team delivers value
  at the right time, experiment with new and innovative ways of working,
  and research emerging methodologies to work out how business analysis
  would fit within them.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of business analysis evaluate whether a new
  delivery methodology is worth experimenting with across the function,
  rather than adopting it because it's fashionable?"

- [ ] **Lead consultancy at organisational scale**

  Provide advice and recommendations to stakeholders based on significant
  specialist knowledge and experience, and lead defining the guidance that
  shapes how the organisation approaches delivery or uses digital and data
  skills more effectively.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between advising one team well and setting
  guidance an entire organisation follows, and what changes about how you
  communicate?"

- [ ] **Keep translating business goals into operating models**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  translating business drivers, goals and constraints into objectives, and
  helping define capabilities and operating models that meet them.

  Ask AI: "What would enterprise-wide ownership of operating model design
  add, beyond translating goals for one part of the organisation?"

- [ ] **Keep advising on options analysis at strategic scale**

  This skill also plateaus: advising on approaches to options analysis and
  impact assessment, ensuring work aligns to strategic context, and guiding
  tactical decisions that influence strategic plans.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of business analysis make sure options-analysis
  best practice is actually followed across a whole organisation, not just
  known about?"

- [ ] **Keep leading large-scale process improvement**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: leading the identification of
  large-scale improvement opportunities, setting direction for how they get
  designed and tested, and coaching others in doing the same.

  Ask AI: "What would leading process improvement capability for an entire
  organisation look like, beyond setting direction for individual
  initiatives?"

- [ ] **Keep directing the approach to modelling complex programmes**

  This skill also plateaus: determining scope and methodology for
  representing complex programmes, and documenting conceptual models that
  support strategic planning.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of business analysis make sure modelling
  standards stay consistent across many concurrent programmes?"

- [ ] **Keep directing the approach to requirements across the organisation**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: directing the approach to requirements
  management, enabling others to apply the right methods, and ensuring
  proposed solutions align with organisational strategy.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide oversight of requirements practice
  add, beyond directing it for individual complex programmes?"

- [ ] **Keep setting direction for systems analysis across the organisation**

  This skill also plateaus: setting direction for identifying IT system
  capabilities, identifying gaps, and bridging business and technical
  perspectives into shared recommendations.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of business analysis make sure business and
  technical perspectives stay genuinely connected across a large
  organisation, not just within individual project teams?"

- [ ] **Keep directing stakeholder strategy at the highest level**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: directing stakeholder relationship
  strategy, ensuring objectives are set and supported, and negotiating with
  senior stakeholders to resolve issues.

  Ask AI: "What changes about stakeholder strategy when you're accountable
  for the whole function's relationships, rather than one programme's?"

- [ ] **Keep recommending and validating the testing approach**

  This skill's wording plateaus as well, unchanged since senior level:
  recommending the value of testing, advising on scenarios and acceptance
  criteria, and reviewing test plans and outcomes.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely organisation-wide testing judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep advising on user experience approach**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: advising on analysing, prioritising
  and validating user experience needs, and articulating how findings
  affect system design.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of business analysis make sure user experience
  evidence carries weight in strategic decisions across the whole
  organisation?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
