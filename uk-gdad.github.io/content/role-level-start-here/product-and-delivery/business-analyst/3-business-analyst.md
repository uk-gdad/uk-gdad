# Business analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the business analyst level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A business analyst leads small to medium size projects and supports larger,
more complex ones — the step up from junior is real independence: you manage
stakeholder relationships yourself, work independently, build a good
understanding of your work area, and start developing the business analysis
community by sharing best practice and mentoring others. Every skill grows
noticeably at this level, because the shift isn't learning new techniques so
much as being trusted to choose and apply the ones you already know without
someone checking your working.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own organisation's context.

- [ ] **Own requirements, don't just capture them**

  Identify, analyse, challenge and validate business and user requirements
  yourself, work under limited supervision to co-ordinate and review their
  prioritisation, and use the requirements management life cycle that fits
  the project.

  Ask AI: "What does it mean to 'challenge' a requirement rather than just
  capturing it, and when should a business analyst actually push back?"

- [ ] **Lead your own approach to understanding a problem**

  Plan and use the most appropriate analysis techniques to understand
  context, problem and root causes, communicate how your work supports
  strategic goals, define problem statements, and formulate recommendations
  considering feasibility, benefits and impact.

  Ask AI: "How would a business analyst choose between several plausible
  analysis techniques for a given problem, rather than defaulting to the
  one they know best?"

- [ ] **Model business situations with limited direction**

  Model various elements of the business with limited direction, and
  understand how proposed changes affect processes, systems, data, roles
  and responsibilities together.

  Ask AI: "What's a good way to check that a business model actually
  captures how changes ripple through processes, data and roles, not just
  how the process looks today?"

- [ ] **Take more ownership of digital and data systems analysis**

  Identify and analyse IT system capabilities, and develop models and
  system requirements for bespoke systems or software with minimal
  supervision.

  Ask AI: "What questions should a business analyst ask to properly
  understand an existing IT system's capabilities before proposing
  requirements for changing it?"

- [ ] **Take ownership of testing input, not just support it**

  Support the use of relevant testing tools and techniques, identify
  business scenarios and develop acceptance criteria that trace requirements
  to functionality, and review prototypes, test plans and test outcome
  reporting.

  Ask AI: "What makes a good acceptance criterion, as distinct from a vague
  description of what 'done' looks like?"

- [ ] **Apply and present user experience analysis**

  Apply basic techniques to analyse, validate and prioritise user
  experience needs, and present findings accessibly to support
  data-informed decisions based on user research.

  Ask AI: "What makes a presentation of user research findings genuinely
  accessible to stakeholders who weren't in the research sessions?"

- [ ] **Lead process improvement work, with some support**

  Analyse and evaluate business processes to identify problems and
  opportunities, design, prioritise and facilitate improvements with some
  support, and validate them through testing.

  Ask AI: "How would a business analyst decide which process improvement
  opportunity to prioritise when several are equally plausible?"

- [ ] **Manage stakeholder relationships, and build genuine consensus**

  Identify important stakeholders and communicate with them clearly and
  regularly, tailor communication to their needs, build and reach
  consensus, and use evidence to explain decisions and improve
  relationships over time.

  Ask AI: "What does building genuine consensus with a stakeholder look
  like, as distinct from just getting their sign-off?"

- [ ] **Adapt how you and your team work to the delivery methodology**

  Explain how products and services are delivered through different
  methodologies, follow the approach that fits your team's methodology, and
  adapt how you work with other roles in a multidisciplinary team
  accordingly.

  Ask AI: "How would a business analyst's day-to-day work change if their
  team switched delivery methodology partway through a project?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
