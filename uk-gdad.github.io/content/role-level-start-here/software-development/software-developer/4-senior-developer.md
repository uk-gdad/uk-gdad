# Senior developer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior developer level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

There are two different roles at this level in the framework: a technical
one, covered here, and a management one. Both carry the same title and the
same named skills; if the day-to-day work you're aiming at is people
management rather than deep technical delivery, the
[senior developer - management](https://uk-gdad.github.io/roles/software-development/software-developer/5-senior-developer-management/)
page describes the difference in practice.

## Purpose

A senior developer delivers and integrates software to form a complete
service. You plan and lead development on sets of related stories, hold an
understanding of the whole system and take responsibility for teaching it to
others, work with other disciplines to understand what actually needs
building, coach and mentor junior colleagues, operate the production
services you build, and find ways to improve system robustness, resilience
and stability. Set the review standard for the people you influence, and
make sure it survives your absence — if good practice only holds up while
you're in the room, you've built a dependency rather than a habit.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
design decision — describe the actual trade-off you're weighing and ask
what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Take ownership of finding and fixing faults**

  Identify, locate and fix service faults yourself — a step up from
  following an agreed procedure into being the one accountable for solving
  the problem.

  Ask AI: "What diagnostic approach would help a senior developer locate
  the actual cause of a service fault efficiently, rather than guessing at
  fixes?"

- [ ] **Design systems of real complexity, and review other people's designs too**

  Design systems characterised by medium levels of risk, impact and
  business or technical complexity, select and apply the right design
  standards, and review the systems designs of others to check the
  technology choice and integration are sound.

  Ask AI: "What should a senior developer look for when reviewing someone
  else's system design, beyond just checking it will technically work?"

- [ ] **Coordinate integration across systems, not just build one interface**

  Define the integration build, co-ordinate build activities across
  systems, and understand how to support integration testing across the
  whole thing rather than one interface at a time.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating integration across several systems
  actually involve day to day, compared with building and testing one
  interface?"

- [ ] **Keep contributing to process optimisation under guidance**

  This skill's wording here is simpler than the level below it — working
  under guidance to identify process optimisation opportunities and
  contribute to implementing proposed solutions. That's the framework's own
  choice, not a gap in your practice; the growth expected of you at this
  level shows up more clearly in the skills above.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill reads more simply than my other
  responsibilities, what would genuinely leading process optimisation look
  like if I chose to push beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep managing service components against business needs and KPIs**

  This skill plateaus here, unchanged from the level below: manage service
  components to ensure they meet business needs and key performance
  indicators.

  Ask AI: "What would taking availability and capacity management from
  'meeting the KPIs' to genuinely owning them look like at a senior level?"

- [ ] **Keep designing security in from the start**

  This skill also plateaus: designing solutions and services with security
  controls included, specifically engineered to mitigate the relevant
  threats.

  Ask AI: "What security decisions at the design stage have the biggest
  long-term consequences if they're got wrong at a senior level?"

- [ ] **Apply development standards, and help others apply them too**

  This skill plateaus too: applying modern development standards and
  supporting others in applying them.

  Ask AI: "What's an effective way for a senior developer to help a
  colleague apply a development standard they're struggling with, without
  just doing it for them?"

- [ ] **Keep building your programming and build practice**

  This skill's wording plateaus as well: collaborating on specifications,
  then designing, coding, testing and documenting programs of
  medium-to-high complexity to the right standards. It's worth being
  deliberately good at this rather than assuming it looks after itself.

  Ask AI: "What separates genuinely strong code review and documentation
  habits from merely adequate ones at a senior level?"

- [ ] **Keep treating prototyping as a team activity**

  This skill's wording also plateaus: approaching prototyping as something
  the team does together, establishing and iterating design patterns, and
  choosing the right method from several.

  Ask AI: "What might a senior developer do differently in a prototyping
  session compared with a more junior one, even if the described skill
  looks the same on paper?"

- [ ] **Keep leading on user focus**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: collaborating with user researchers,
  representing users internally, explaining the difference between user
  needs and desires, championing research that covers all users, and
  guiding others through the user story.

  Ask AI: "What's the practical difference between what users need and
  what users say they want, and how would a senior developer explain that
  difference to a colleague who's confusing the two?"

Ask your manager or a lead colleague which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
