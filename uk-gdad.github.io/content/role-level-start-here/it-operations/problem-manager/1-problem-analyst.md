# Problem analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the problem analyst level, this page is
for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

Problem managers aim to resolve and control the root causes of incidents
caused by errors within the IT infrastructure, working to prevent
recurrence rather than firefighting the same fault repeatedly. As a
problem analyst, you are responsible for maintaining software systems once
they are up and running and, where necessary, support the problem manager
in co-ordinating the resolution of problems — the foundations of tracking
what's broken, why, and what stops it happening again.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a foundation for this role,
described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a specific
question rather than searching for a generic guide.

- [ ] **Track and protect assets and components**

  Track, log and correct information to protect assets and components,
  keeping records accurate as things change.

  Ask AI: "Why does keeping asset information accurate matter for
  diagnosing a recurring problem quickly?"

- [ ] **Manage service components against business needs**

  Manage service components to make sure they meet business needs and key
  performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "What key performance indicators would a problem analyst
  typically be checking service components against?"

- [ ] **Implement change requests, under supervision**

  Implement change requests, and apply change control procedures under
  supervision.

  Ask AI: "Why do organisations use a formal change control procedure
  instead of just making a fix when it's needed?"

- [ ] **Contribute to a well-functioning team**

  Contribute to the work of others, help motivate and empower the team,
  help create the right environment for the team to work in, and recognise
  and deal with issues.

  Ask AI: "What does good team collaboration actually look like day to
  day, beyond just being friendly to colleagues?"

- [ ] **Understand the basics of IT continuity management**

  Explain the basic principles of IT standard continuity management
  processes and procedures.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what 'IT continuity management' means,
  and why it's treated separately from problem management."

- [ ] **Diagnose incidents, and find their resolution**

  Diagnose and prioritise incidents, investigate their causes, and find
  resolutions rather than just recording and routing them.

  Ask AI: "What's a structured way to diagnose the root cause of an
  incident, rather than fixing the first plausible symptom?"

- [ ] **Own an issue until it's genuinely resolved**

  Own an issue until a new owner has been found, or the problem has been
  mitigated or resolved — rather than passing it on the moment it gets
  difficult.

  Ask AI: "What does 'owning an issue' mean in practice for a problem
  analyst, when the actual fix might belong to someone else's team?"

- [ ] **Investigate patterns behind problems, and consult specialists**

  Initiate and monitor actions to investigate patterns and trends behind
  problems, consult specialists where needed, determine the right
  resolution and help implement it, and identify preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between fixing a problem and preventing
  the same class of problem from recurring, and how would a problem
  analyst approach the second?"

- [ ] **Build coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  Take inputs and establish coherent frameworks that work, rather than
  leaving problem information scattered across different sources.

  Ask AI: "What does 'establishing a coherent framework' mean in practice
  for someone tracking problems across several services?"

- [ ] **Build service management framework knowledge**

  Work towards a Level 3 service management framework qualification, so
  your day-to-day practice is grounded in a recognised standard.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what a service management framework
  qualification actually covers, and why organisations value it."

- [ ] **Turn user evidence into decisions**

  Identify and engage with users or stakeholders to collate evidence of
  their needs, understand and define research that actually fits those
  needs, and use both quantitative and qualitative data to turn user
  focus into real outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would a problem analyst use user evidence to decide which
  of several recurring problems to prioritise?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
