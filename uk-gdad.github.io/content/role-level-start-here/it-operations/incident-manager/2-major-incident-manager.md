# Major incident manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the major incident manager level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A major incident manager manages significant outages and crises, reporting
issues to problem management — the top of this profession's line. You work
closely with workplace services, infrastructure, applications operators
and communication managers, and in some departments you may also be the
head of service. Most named skills grow at this level, because managing a
genuine crisis asks for more than diagnosing and resolving individual
incidents well.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real crisis-response decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Lead the investigation and resolution of incidents**

  Lead the investigation and resolution of incidents, rather than
  diagnosing and resolving them individually.

  Ask AI: "What does leading a major incident investigation involve, that
  diagnosing and resolving a routine one doesn't?"

- [ ] **Manage runbooks, and test service continuity**

  Manage the runbooks and processes around service continuity, and test
  those runbooks to make sure service availability is genuinely
  maintained, not just documented.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a runbook that reads well and one
  that's actually been tested to work during a real outage?"

- [ ] **Analyse the impact of change before it happens**

  Analyse and assess the impact of change, document change requests, and
  action changes from approved requests.

  Ask AI: "What should a major incident manager look for when assessing
  the potential impact of a change made during or after a crisis, beyond
  whether it technically works?"

- [ ] **Take accountability, and search proactively for problems**

  Take accountability for issues that occur, be proactive in searching for
  potential problems before they surface, and achieve excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proactively searching for potential problems' look
  like in practice, rather than waiting for the next major incident to
  reveal one?"

- [ ] **Manage service components against business needs**

  Manage service components to make sure they meet business needs and key
  performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "What key performance indicators would a major incident manager
  typically be accountable for after resolving a significant outage?"

- [ ] **Lead on user focus, not just gather it**

  Collaborate with user researchers and represent users internally,
  explain the difference between user needs and desires, champion user
  research so it covers all users, and guide others in understanding the
  user story.

  Ask AI: "How would a major incident manager balance user needs against
  technical urgency when deciding how to communicate during an outage?"

- [ ] **See the bigger picture across groups of services**

  Take groups of services and investigate how to get the best out of the
  underlying services that support them, rather than optimising one
  service at a time.

  Ask AI: "What would a major incident manager look for across a group of
  related services that wouldn't be visible looking at any one service in
  isolation?"

- [ ] **Build genuine collaboration across your team**

  Work collaboratively, actively networking with others, adapt feedback so
  it's effective and lasting, use team health checks to spot dynamic
  issues, and help stimulate the right response to them.

  Ask AI: "What makes feedback 'effective and lasting' rather than
  something a colleague hears once and forgets, especially after a
  stressful major incident?"

- [ ] **Keep tracking and protecting assets and components**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from incident manager
  level: tracking, logging and correcting information to protect assets
  and components.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely major-incident-level asset management
  judgement add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep investigating patterns behind problems**

  This skill also plateaus: investigating patterns and trends, consulting
  specialists, and determining preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem management strategy across major
  incidents add, beyond investigating patterns well yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep building service management framework knowledge**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: holding a Level 3 service management
  framework qualification.

  Ask AI: "What would deepening service management framework knowledge,
  beyond the base qualification, look like for a major incident manager?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
