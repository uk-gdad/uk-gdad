# Senior business relationship manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior business relationship
manager level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior business relationship manager develops relationships across the
organisation to understand business ambitions and, where appropriate,
makes sure digital products and services support them. You explore shared
needs, encouraging reuse, central management and commoditisation aligned to
digital, data, technology and security strategies, lead activities that
improve relationship maturity and value, ensure strategic demands such as
spending reviews are supported, partner with others on strategic guidance,
promote sustained investment in user-centred approaches, and make sure
complex needs and challenges are understood, prioritised and communicated.
Business analysis, ownership and initiative, service management framework
knowledge, strategic thinking and user focus all grow at this level,
because leading improvement work asks for more than maintaining consistent
engagement.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is often
most useful for pressure-testing a strategic or investment case — describe
the actual trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Lead investigative work, and make sense of conflicting information**

  Lead investigative work into problems and opportunities in existing
  processes, lead collecting information and creating recommendations for
  improvements, and absorb large amounts of conflicting information to
  produce workable solutions.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior business relationship manager make sense of
  genuinely conflicting information from different stakeholders before
  recommending a way forward?"

- [ ] **Define strategy, and evaluate whether it's working**

  Define strategies and policies, providing guidance to others on working
  in the strategic context, and evaluate current strategies to make sure
  business requirements are being met and exceeded where possible.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior business relationship manager evaluate
  whether an existing digital investment strategy is still meeting
  business requirements, rather than assuming it still is?"

- [ ] **Lead on user focus, not just gather it**

  Collaborate with user researchers and represent users internally,
  explain the difference between user needs and desires, champion user
  research so it covers all users, and guide others in understanding the
  user story.

  Ask AI: "What's the practical difference between what a business area
  needs and what it says it wants, and how would a senior manager explain
  that difference to a stakeholder who's confusing the two?"

- [ ] **Take accountability, and search proactively for problems**

  Take accountability for issues that occur, be proactive in searching for
  potential problems before they surface, and achieve excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proactively searching for potential problems'
  look like in practice for a business relationship manager, rather than
  waiting for a stakeholder to raise a concern?"

- [ ] **Build ITIL knowledge alongside your qualification**

  Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, and
  demonstrate knowledge of the life cycle or capability elements of ITIL
  (Information Technology Infrastructure Library).

  Ask AI: "Explain, for someone who already has a service management
  qualification, what ITIL's life cycle and capability elements add for
  someone managing business relationships rather than delivering
  services directly."

- [ ] **Keep building coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from the level below:
  taking inputs and establishing coherent frameworks that work.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level framework-building judgement
  add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep negotiating with stakeholders, and removing blockers**

  This skill also plateaus: negotiating with and influencing stakeholders,
  and building long-term strategic relationships.

  Ask AI: "What would leading stakeholder negotiation across several
  business areas add, beyond negotiating well with one at a time?"

Ask a lead business relationship manager, or an equivalent principal
colleague, which of these matters most in your organisation right now, and
start there.
