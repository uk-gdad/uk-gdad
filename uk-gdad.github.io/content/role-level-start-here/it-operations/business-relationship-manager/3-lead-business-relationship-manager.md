# Lead business relationship manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead business relationship manager
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead business relationship manager leads engagement with internal and
external stakeholders to make sure complex needs and challenges are
understood, prioritised and communicated to the right audiences, and may
lead a team of business relationship managers — the top of this
profession's line. You make sure strategic demands such as spending
reviews and services strategy commitments are met and communicated,
promote ways of working that support meaningful connections aligned to the
organisation's changing needs, set the conditions for a culture of
positive impact, and enable others to improve relationship maturity.
Stakeholder relationship management, business analysis, user focus and
service focus all sharpen at this level, because you're now directing
strategy and escalation for a whole team, not managing your own
relationships well.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Manage strategic relationships, and arbitrate escalated blockers**

  Manage long-term strategic relationships, identifying where new
  connections need making or nurturing, direct the strategic approach to
  stakeholder relationships, act as an escalation point when relationships
  become challenging for junior team members, influence senior
  stakeholders, and facilitate discussions across high-risk, complex areas
  under constrained timelines.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead business relationship manager step in when a
  junior colleague's stakeholder relationship has broken down, without
  undermining that colleague's authority going forward?"

- [ ] **Direct business analysis, and communicate recommendations persuasively**

  Direct business analysis, including collecting information and
  identifying problems and opportunities, make sure recommendations fit
  strategic business objectives, communicate recommendations persuasively
  to important stakeholders, and identify the simplest of several
  approaches.

  Ask AI: "What makes a business case genuinely persuasive to senior
  stakeholders who've seen dozens of them, beyond listing the benefits?"

- [ ] **Set direction on meeting user needs across channels**

  Give direction on which tools or methods to use, demonstrate experience
  meeting user needs across a variety of channels, bring insight into how
  user needs have changed over time, and apply strategic thinking to
  provide the best service for the end user.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead business relationship manager use insight
  about how business needs have changed over time to shape organisational
  strategy?"

- [ ] **See the bigger picture across groups of services**

  Take groups of services and investigate how to get the best out of the
  underlying services that support them, rather than optimising one
  relationship at a time.

  Ask AI: "What would a lead business relationship manager look for
  across a group of related services that wouldn't be visible looking at
  any one relationship in isolation?"

- [ ] **Keep taking accountability, and searching proactively for problems**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level: taking
  accountability for issues, searching proactively for problems, and
  achieving excellent user outcomes.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely lead-level accountability add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep building ITIL knowledge alongside your qualification**

  This skill also plateaus: holding a Level 3 service management
  framework qualification, and demonstrating knowledge of ITIL's life
  cycle and capability elements.

  Ask AI: "What would leading service management framework practice
  across a team add, beyond holding the qualification yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep defining strategy, and evaluating whether it's working**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: defining strategies and policies, and
  evaluating current strategies against business requirements.

  Ask AI: "What would leading strategy definition across several business
  areas add, beyond defining it well for one?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
