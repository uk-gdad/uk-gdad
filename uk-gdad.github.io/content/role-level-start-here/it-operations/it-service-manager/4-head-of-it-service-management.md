# Head of IT service management - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of IT service management
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of IT service management is responsible for setting the vision and
strategy for service management, ensuring processes are owned and
maturing — the top of this role's line. You lead a team, ensuring adequate
resources and capacity, represent the function at a senior level, and act
as an escalation point for business stakeholders. Most named skills grow
at this level, because setting vision and strategy asks for more than
managing maturity across platforms.

The framework does not carry a Service focus skill at this level, though it
names one at every level below. That is the framework's own choice, not a
gap in your practice: the seeing-the-bigger-picture judgement that skill
described becomes assumed rather than separately assessed once you are
setting the strategy those services sit within.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real strategic decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case you
haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Manage configuration for the whole organisation**

  Manage configuration management for the organisation, ensuring value for
  the business and adherence to company policies, ensure changes to assets
  are recorded and controlled appropriately, and advocate organisational
  commitment to asset control.

  Ask AI: "What does advocating organisational commitment to asset control
  involve, beyond managing configuration well within one service?"

- [ ] **Lead the design and implementation of strategy**

  Lead the design and implementation of strategy, directing the evaluation
  of strategies and policies to ensure business requirements are being
  met.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between defining a strategy and leading
  its design and implementation across a whole function?"

- [ ] **Manage strategic stakeholder relationships, and escalations**

  Manage long-term strategic relationships with stakeholders, direct the
  strategic approach to those relationships, act as a point of escalation
  when relationships become challenging for more junior team members,
  influence senior stakeholders, and facilitate discussions across
  high-risk, complex or time-constrained areas.

  Ask AI: "What does it mean to 'direct the strategic approach' for
  stakeholder relationships, rather than managing individual relationships
  well?"

- [ ] **Demonstrate industry understanding of the framework**

  Show you have an expert certificate in the service management
  framework qualification, and demonstrate an industry understanding of
  how to implement the framework into numerous environments.

  Ask AI: "What does 'implementing a service management framework across
  numerous environments' involve, that implementing it well in one
  organisation doesn't?"

- [ ] **Keep leading investigative work, and absorbing complexity**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level: leading
  investigative work into problems and opportunities, leading the
  collection of information and creation of recommendations, and
  absorbing large amounts of conflicting information to produce
  solutions.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely head-of-level investigative judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep using data analytics to enhance business performance**

  This skill also plateaus: using data analytics skills to make decisions
  that enhance business performance.

  Ask AI: "What would sponsoring data-driven decision-making across a
  whole function add, beyond making good data-driven decisions yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep taking accountability, and searching proactively for problems**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: taking accountability for issues,
  searching proactively for problems, and achieving excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide accountability for user outcomes
  add, beyond taking accountability well for your own team's incidents?"

- [ ] **Keep leading on user focus**

  This skill also plateaus: giving direction on tools and methods,
  demonstrating experience meeting user needs across channels, and
  applying strategic thinking to provide the best service for the end
  user.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of IT service management make sure user focus
  stays central to strategic decisions, not just delivery-level ones?"

Ask a peer head of IT service management, in your organisation or another
part of government, which of these has taken them longest to get right —
that conversation will teach you more than any general reading on
technical leadership.
