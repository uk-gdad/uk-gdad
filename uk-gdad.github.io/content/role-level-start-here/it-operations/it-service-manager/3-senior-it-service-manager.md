Welcome. If you are working toward the senior IT service manager level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything
else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it
names.

## Purpose

A senior IT service manager is responsible for service management across
platforms, multiple channels and services, to ensure service availability,
performance and process maturity. All nine named skills grow at this
level, because owning service maturity across platforms asks for more
than managing service level agreements within one.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Where you want to go deeper, ask an
AI tool a specific question rather than searching for a generic guide —
you'll get an answer shaped around your own organisation's services.

- [ ] **Manage configuration items, and service compliance**

  Manage configuration items and related information, and manage service
  compliance and risk.

  Ask AI: "What does managing service compliance and risk add, beyond
  keeping configuration information accurate?"

- [ ] **Lead investigative work, and absorb conflicting information**

  Lead investigative work into problems and opportunities in existing
  processes, lead the collection of information and creation of
  recommendations, and absorb large amounts of conflicting information to
  produce solutions.

  Ask AI: "What's a structured way to absorb conflicting information from
  several sources and turn it into a clear recommendation?"

- [ ] **Use data analytics to enhance business performance**

  Use data analytics skills to make decisions that enhance business
  performance, rather than producing reports for others to interpret.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between producing a report on service
  performance and using that data to make a decision that changes
  business outcomes?"

- [ ] **Take accountability, and search proactively for problems**

  Take accountability for issues that occur, be proactive in searching for
  potential problems before they surface, and achieve excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proactively searching for potential problems' look
  like in practice for a senior IT service manager, rather than waiting
  for an incident to reveal one?"

- [ ] **Keep seeing the bigger picture across groups of services**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from IT service manager
  level: seeing the bigger picture by taking groups of services and
  investigating how to get the best out of the underlying services that
  support them.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level service focus judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Hold an expert service management framework certificate**

  Show you have an expert certificate in the service management framework
  qualification.

  Ask AI: "What does an expert-level service management framework
  certificate cover, beyond a Level 3 qualification and ITIL knowledge?"

- [ ] **Negotiate with stakeholders, and remove blockers**

  Negotiate with and influence stakeholders, manage relationships
  effectively, influence decisions, deal with challenging situations, and
  remove blockers.

  Ask AI: "What's a good approach for a senior IT service manager to
  negotiate with a stakeholder who disagrees with a recommended course of
  action?"

- [ ] **Define strategies, and evaluate them against requirements**

  Define strategies and policies, provide guidance to others on working in
  the strategic context, and evaluate current strategies to ensure
  business requirements are being met and exceeded where possible.

  Ask AI: "What makes an existing strategy worth evaluating and revising,
  rather than leaving it as it is?"

- [ ] **Lead on user focus, not just gather it**

  Collaborate with user researchers and represent users internally,
  explain the difference between user needs and desires, champion user
  research so it covers all users, and guide others in understanding the
  user story.

  Ask AI: "What's the practical difference between what users need and
  what they say they want, and how would a senior manager explain that
  difference to a colleague who's confusing the two?"

Ask a head of IT service management which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
