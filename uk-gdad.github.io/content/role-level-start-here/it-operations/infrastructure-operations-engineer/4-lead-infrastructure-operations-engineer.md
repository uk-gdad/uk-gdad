# Lead infrastructure operations engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead infrastructure operations
engineer level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead infrastructure operations engineer ensures the service and the
contractual and regulatory requirements of the organisation are met,
managing operational support of IT solutions, the workload, performance
and development of a team of IT operations specialists, third party
provision of IT operations services, and the development of architectural
solutions throughout the service life cycle. Most named skills grow at
this level, because managing the service and the people who run it asks
for more than acting as escalated support for it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is often
most useful for pressure-testing a management or vendor decision — describe
the actual trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Manage complex change, and enforce release discipline**

  Manage high-impact, complex change requests, and make sure release
  policies, procedures and processes are actually applied.

  Ask AI: "What makes a change request genuinely high-impact for
  infrastructure operations, and how should that change how a lead
  engineer manages it?"

- [ ] **Interact with, read and write code**

  Interact with, read and write code, and carry out baseline repair
  activities.

  Ask AI: "What kind of code does an infrastructure operations lead
  typically need to read and write, if their job isn't primarily software
  development?"

- [ ] **Lead the investigation and resolution of incidents**

  Lead the investigation and resolution of incidents, rather than
  diagnosing and resolving them individually.

  Ask AI: "What does leading an incident investigation involve, that
  diagnosing and resolving one individually doesn't?"

- [ ] **Take accountability, and search proactively for problems**

  Take accountability for issues that occur, be proactive in searching for
  potential problems before they surface, and achieve excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proactively searching for potential problems' look
  like in practice for infrastructure operations, rather than waiting for
  an incident to reveal one?"

- [ ] **Build ITIL knowledge alongside your qualification**

  Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, and
  demonstrate knowledge of the life cycle or capability elements of ITIL
  (Information Technology Infrastructure Library).

  Ask AI: "Explain, for someone who already has a service management
  qualification, what ITIL's life cycle and capability elements add on
  top of it."

- [ ] **Set standards for objects, and maintain documentation**

  Draft and maintain procedures and documentation, and set standards for
  the definition, security and integrity of objects, ensuring conformity
  to those standards.

  Ask AI: "What makes documentation genuinely useful to the next engineer
  who reads it, rather than technically complete but hard to act on?"

- [ ] **Set the strategic direction for test planning**

  Manage the planning of system and acceptance tests, co-ordinating
  functional and non-functional specifications, provide authoritative
  advice on test planning, and identify process improvements contributing
  to best practice.

  Ask AI: "What does authoritative, organisation-wide test planning advice
  look like, as opposed to planning tests well for one rollout?"

- [ ] **Lead on user focus, not just gather it**

  Collaborate with user researchers and represent users internally,
  explain the difference between user needs and desires, champion user
  research so it covers all users, and guide others on the best tools and
  methods for understanding the user story.

  Ask AI: "What's the practical difference between what end users need and
  what they say they want, and how would a lead engineer explain that
  difference to a colleague who's confusing the two?"

- [ ] **Keep secure configuration maintained, and IT assets tracked**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  maintaining secure configuration, controlling IT assets, and verifying
  their location and state.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level asset management judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep managing service components against business needs**

  This skill also plateaus: managing service components to make sure they
  meet business needs and key performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "What would setting availability and capacity standards across
  an organisation add, beyond meeting them well within your own team?"

- [ ] **Keep investigating patterns behind problems**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: investigating patterns and trends,
  consulting specialists, and determining preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem management strategy across
  infrastructure operations add, beyond investigating patterns well
  yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep building coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  This skill also plateaus: taking inputs and establishing coherent
  frameworks that work.

  Ask AI: "What would setting the standard for how frameworks get built
  across a team add, beyond building good ones yourself?"

Ask a principal infrastructure operations engineer which of these has taken
them longest to get right, and start there.
