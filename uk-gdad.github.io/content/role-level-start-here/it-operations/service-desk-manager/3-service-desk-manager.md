# Service desk manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the service desk manager level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A service desk manager is responsible for managing the first- and
second-line technical support for all departmental IT applications and
services across sites, identifying emerging issues, acting as a knowledge
expert, and taking responsibility for resourcing and developing the team,
including preparing and co-ordinating service transition activities. Most
named skills grow at this level, because owning continual improvement
across the desk asks for more than supporting a team through calls.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI
tool a specific question rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll
get an answer shaped around your own service desk.

- [ ] **Turn lessons learnt into continual service improvement**

  Translate lessons learnt into continual service improvement, oversee
  the full range of customer service functions including reviewing
  customer satisfaction, and investigate which processes can be
  automated and how.

  Ask AI: "What does 'translating lessons learnt into continual service
  improvement' look like in practice, rather than just recording what
  went wrong?"

- [ ] **Use data analytics to enhance business performance**

  Use data analytics skills to make decisions that enhance business
  performance, rather than producing reports for others to interpret.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between producing a report on service
  desk performance and using that data to make a decision that changes
  business outcomes?"

- [ ] **Take accountability, and search proactively for problems**

  Take accountability for issues that occur, be proactive in searching for
  potential problems before they surface, and achieve excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proactively searching for potential problems' look
  like in practice for a service desk manager, rather than waiting for a
  complaint to reveal one?"

- [ ] **Investigate patterns behind problems, and consult specialists**

  Initiate and monitor actions to investigate patterns and trends behind
  problems, consult specialists where needed, determine the right
  resolution and help implement it, and identify preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between fixing a recurring fault and
  preventing the pattern behind it from continuing?"

- [ ] **Build ITIL knowledge alongside your qualification**

  Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, and
  demonstrate knowledge of the life cycle or capability elements of ITIL
  (Information Technology Infrastructure Library).

  Ask AI: "Explain, for someone who already has a service management
  qualification, what ITIL's life cycle and capability elements add on
  top of it."

- [ ] **Lead on user focus, not just gather it**

  Collaborate with user researchers and represent users internally,
  explain the difference between user needs and desires, champion user
  research so it covers all users, and guide others on the best tools and
  methods to understand the user story.

  Ask AI: "What's the practical difference between what users need and
  what they say they want, and how would a service desk manager explain
  that difference to a colleague who's confusing the two?"

- [ ] **Keep tracking and protecting assets and components**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior service desk
  analyst level: tracking, logging and correcting information to protect
  assets and components.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely manager-level asset management judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep building coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  This skill also plateaus: taking inputs and establishing coherent
  frameworks that work.

  Ask AI: "What would setting the standard for how frameworks get built
  across a service desk add, beyond building good ones yourself?"

Ask a head of service desk which of these matters most in your organisation
right now, and start there.
