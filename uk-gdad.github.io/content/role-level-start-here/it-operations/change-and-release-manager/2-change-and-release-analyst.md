# Change and release analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the change and release analyst level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A change and release analyst plans and rolls out software, designing and
carrying out procedures for distributing and installing changes to
systems and services. You undertake analysis after a release and set the
plan for upcoming changes, liaising with DevOps and service designers
along the way. Only change management genuinely deepens in the framework's
own wording at this level — the rest of the growth here is in what you're
actually rolling out and planning for, not a longer skill description.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own release process.

- [ ] **Analyse the impact of change before it happens**

  Analyse and assess the impact of change, document change requests, and
  action changes from approved requests.

  Ask AI: "What should a change and release analyst look for when
  assessing the potential impact of a release, beyond whether it
  technically works?"

- [ ] **Keep configuration secure, and IT assets accounted for**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from configuration
  analyst level: maintaining secure configuration, controlling IT assets,
  and verifying their location and state.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely more independent asset management judgement
  add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep identifying and registering incidents accurately**

  This skill also plateaus: identifying and registering incidents, and
  allocating them to the right channel.

  Ask AI: "What would taking more ownership of incident handling during a
  release, rather than just registering incidents, look like at this
  level?"

- [ ] **Keep investigating problems at the right level**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: investigating problems with an
  understanding of their level, and contributing to remedies.

  Ask AI: "What would taking more ownership of problem investigation
  during a release cycle, beyond contributing to remedies, look like?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding problem resolution, and passing on what you can't solve**

  This skill also plateaus: being aware of problem resolution processes,
  and passing problems on to your team.

  Ask AI: "What would a change and release analyst's first attempt at
  resolving a problem themselves, rather than passing it on, look like?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding your team's dynamics and the value of feedback**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: contributing to the work of others,
  and helping create the right team environment.

  Ask AI: "What would actively shaping team dynamics during a release,
  rather than just contributing to the work of others, look like?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding the basics of IT continuity management**

  This skill also plateaus: explaining the basic principles of IT
  continuity management processes.

  Ask AI: "What would applying continuity management principles during a
  release, rather than just explaining them, look like at this level?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding availability and capacity management**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: explaining availability and capacity
  management processes.

  Ask AI: "What would considering availability and capacity impact when
  planning a release, rather than just explaining the concepts, look
  like?"

- [ ] **Keep building awareness of different products and services**

  This skill also plateaus: showing awareness of the different products
  and services your organisation runs.

  Ask AI: "How would a change and release analyst use awareness of the
  organisation's products and services to plan a release more safely?"

- [ ] **Keep building service management framework knowledge**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: holding a Level 3 service management
  framework qualification.

  Ask AI: "What would applying service management framework knowledge
  directly to release planning look like, beyond holding the
  qualification?"

- [ ] **Keep building a basic sense of who you're supporting**

  This skill also plateaus: showing awareness of user experience analysis,
  and the purpose of user stories.

  Ask AI: "How would understanding a user story change how a change and
  release analyst schedules or communicates a release?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
