# Lead infrastructure engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead infrastructure engineer level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead infrastructure engineer manages third party provision of
infrastructure services and provides expertise to develop architectural
solutions for infrastructure services throughout the service product life
cycle, overseeing programmes and projects and working with technical
architects to translate designs into operations. Most named skills grow at
this level, because leading teams and vendors asks for more than designing
and implementing solutions yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is often most
useful for pressure-testing a vendor or design decision — describe the
actual trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Interact with, read and write code**

  Interact with, read and write code, and carry out baseline repair
  activities.

  Ask AI: "What kind of code does an infrastructure lead typically need to
  read and write, if their job isn't primarily software development?"

- [ ] **Lead teams in designing and supporting infrastructure**

  Lead teams and departments in the design, implementation, administration
  and support of infrastructure solutions and services.

  Ask AI: "What changes about designing infrastructure when you're leading
  a team through it, rather than doing the design work yourself?"

- [ ] **Design solutions with security engineered in**

  Design solutions and services with security controls included,
  specifically engineered to mitigate security threats.

  Ask AI: "What does 'engineering in' a security control mean, as opposed
  to adding one after a design is already finished?"

- [ ] **Apply modern development standards to complex programmes**

  Identify and apply modern development standards to support complex
  projects and programmes, and lead others in applying them.

  Ask AI: "What makes a project or programme genuinely complex enough to
  need a lead engineer's judgement on which development standards apply?"

- [ ] **Take accountability, and search proactively for problems**

  Take accountability for issues that occur, be proactive in searching for
  potential problems before they surface, and achieve excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proactively searching for potential problems' look
  like in practice for infrastructure, rather than waiting for an incident
  to reveal one?"

- [ ] **Establish standards across the service product life cycle**

  Establish standards and procedures across a service product life cycle,
  including the development product life cycle, and ensure practitioners
  adhere to them, managing resources so the systems integration function
  works effectively.

  Ask AI: "What does it mean to establish standards across a whole service
  product life cycle, rather than for one integration project?"

- [ ] **Manage test planning, and give authoritative advice**

  Manage the planning of system and acceptance tests, co-ordinating
  functional and non-functional specifications, provide authoritative
  advice on test planning, and identify process improvements contributing
  to best practice.

  Ask AI: "What does authoritative, organisation-wide test planning advice
  look like, as opposed to planning tests well for one project?"

- [ ] **Use lateral thinking to diagnose root causes**

  Use lateral thinking to break a problem down into its component parts to
  identify and diagnose root causes, troubleshooting across technology
  capabilities including computing, storage, networking, physical
  infrastructure, software, commercial-off-the-shelf software and open
  source software.

  Ask AI: "What does 'lateral thinking' add to root cause analysis, beyond
  a purely linear, step-by-step approach?"

- [ ] **Keep designing systems with medium risk and complexity**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  designing systems characterised by medium levels of risk, impact, and
  business or technical complexity, and selecting appropriate design
  standards.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level design judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep investigating patterns behind problems**

  This skill also plateaus: initiating and monitoring actions to
  investigate patterns and trends, consulting specialists, determining the
  right resolution, and identifying preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem management strategy across
  infrastructure add, beyond investigating patterns well yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep building coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: taking inputs and establishing
  coherent frameworks that work.

  Ask AI: "What would setting the standard for how frameworks get built
  across a team add, beyond building good ones yourself?"

Ask a principal infrastructure engineer which of these has taken them
longest to get right, and start there.
