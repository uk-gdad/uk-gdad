# Operations analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are starting out as an operations analyst, this page is
for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for,
one step at a time.

## Purpose

Command and control centre managers proactively monitor live services and
performance trends, identifying potential problems or areas for
improvement that can then be investigated. As an operations analyst, you
provide administrative support to your manager and alert support staff
when necessary — learning to spot what matters in a stream of monitoring
data, and knowing who to tell when something needs attention.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation's monitoring setup, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Track and protect assets and components**

  Track, log and correct information to protect assets and components,
  keeping records accurate as things change.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to IT operations. Explain, simply, why tracking and
  correcting asset information matters beyond just having a record."

- [ ] **Identify and register incidents accurately**

  Identify and register incidents, gathering the information needed and
  allocating them to the right channel.

  Ask AI: "What information does a good incident record need to contain
  so the next person picking it up doesn't have to ask basic questions
  first?"

- [ ] **Investigate problems at the right level**

  Investigate problems in systems, processes and services with an
  understanding of what level a problem sits at — strategic, tactical or
  operational — and contribute to implementing remedies and preventative
  measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a strategic, a tactical and an
  operational problem, with an example of each?"

- [ ] **Manage service components against business needs**

  Manage service components to make sure they meet business needs and key
  performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "What key performance indicators would an operations analyst
  typically be monitoring day to day?"

- [ ] **Implement changes under supervision**

  Implement change requests, and apply change control procedures under
  supervision.

  Ask AI: "Why do organisations use a formal change control procedure
  instead of just making a fix when it's needed?"

- [ ] **Understand the basics of IT continuity management**

  Explain the basic principles of IT standard continuity management
  processes and procedures.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what 'IT continuity management'
  means, and why it's treated separately from incident management."

- [ ] **Own an issue until it's genuinely resolved**

  Own an issue until a new owner has been found, or the problem has been
  mitigated or resolved — rather than passing it on the moment it gets
  difficult.

  Ask AI: "What does 'owning an issue' mean in practice for an operations
  analyst monitoring live services, when the actual fix belongs to
  someone else?"

- [ ] **Build coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  Take inputs and establish coherent frameworks that work, rather than
  leaving monitoring information scattered across different sources.

  Ask AI: "What does 'establishing a coherent framework' mean in practice
  for someone monitoring several live services at once?"

- [ ] **Build service management framework knowledge**

  Work towards a Level 3 service management framework qualification, so
  your day-to-day practice is grounded in a recognised standard.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what a service management framework
  qualification actually covers, and why organisations value it."

- [ ] **Turn user evidence into decisions**

  Identify and engage with users or stakeholders to collate evidence of
  their needs, understand and define research that actually fits those
  needs, and use both quantitative and qualitative data to turn user
  focus into real outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would an operations analyst use user evidence to decide
  which monitoring alert matters most, when several fire at once?"

- [ ] **Understand your team's dynamics and the value of feedback**

  Contribute to the work of others, help motivate and empower the team,
  help create the right environment for the team to work in, and
  recognise and deal with issues.

  Ask AI: "What does good team collaboration actually look like day to
  day on a command and control team, beyond just being friendly to
  colleagues?"

You do not need to master all of this before you start. Ask your manager or
a more senior colleague which of these your team leans on most, and start
there.
