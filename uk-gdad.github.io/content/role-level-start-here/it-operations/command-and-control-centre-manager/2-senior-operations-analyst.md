# Senior operations analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior operations analyst level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A senior operations analyst prioritises incoming messages for the team and
makes sure information flows correctly, with proper record keeping and data
capture. You detect notifications and conduct monitor checks to make sure
systems are running correctly, be on call outside working hours during
emergencies to check events logged as not functioning, and in some
organisations oversee the virtual environment. Asset and configuration
management, incident management and problem management all grow at this
level, because prioritising and acting on what the monitoring surfaces
asks for more than logging it accurately.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own monitoring setup.

- [ ] **Keep configuration secure, and IT assets accounted for**

  Maintain secure configuration and accurate information, control IT
  assets in one or more areas, and verify the location and state of those
  assets.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior operations analyst decide an asset record
  needs correcting, rather than just noting the discrepancy?"

- [ ] **Diagnose incidents, and find their resolution**

  Diagnose and prioritise incidents, investigate their causes, and find
  resolutions rather than just recording and routing them.

  Ask AI: "What's a structured way to diagnose the root cause of an
  incident flagged by monitoring, rather than fixing the first plausible
  symptom?"

- [ ] **Investigate patterns behind problems, and consult specialists**

  Initiate and monitor actions to investigate patterns and trends behind
  problems, consult specialists where needed, determine the right
  resolution and help implement it, and identify preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between fixing a problem an alert
  flagged and preventing the same class of problem from recurring?"

- [ ] **Keep managing service components against business needs**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from operations analyst
  level: managing service components to meet business needs and key
  performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level judgement about service
  components add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep analysing the impact of change before it happens**

  This skill also plateaus: analysing and assessing the impact of change,
  documenting change requests, and actioning approved changes.

  Ask AI: "What would taking more ownership of change impact assessment,
  beyond documenting and actioning it, look like at the next level?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding your team's dynamics and the value of feedback**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: contributing to the work of others,
  and helping create the right team environment.

  Ask AI: "What would actively shaping team dynamics during an incident,
  rather than just contributing to the work of others, look like?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding the basics of IT continuity management**

  This skill also plateaus: explaining the basic principles of IT
  continuity management processes.

  Ask AI: "What would applying continuity management principles during an
  on-call shift, rather than just explaining them, look like?"

- [ ] **Keep owning an issue until it's genuinely resolved**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: owning an issue until a new owner is
  found or it's mitigated or resolved.

  Ask AI: "What would taking accountability for an issue, rather than
  just owning it until resolved, look like at the next level?"

- [ ] **Keep building coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  This skill also plateaus: taking inputs and establishing coherent
  frameworks that work.

  Ask AI: "What would leading how a team builds coherent monitoring
  frameworks add, beyond building good ones yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep building service management framework knowledge**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: holding a Level 3 service management
  framework qualification.

  Ask AI: "What would deepening service management framework knowledge,
  beyond the base qualification, look like for a senior analyst?"

- [ ] **Keep turning user evidence into decisions**

  This skill also plateaus: collating user evidence, defining fitting
  research, and using data to turn focus into outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior operations analyst use user evidence to
  prioritise which of several competing alerts genuinely matters most?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
