# Serial comma: lines read and declined

`bin/serial-comma` cannot always tell a list of three from a pair hung off a
sentence. Where its rules propose a comma and a person has read the line and
decided against it, the line goes here and the script leaves it alone.

Each entry was read in context. Most are two-item pairs whose preceding comma
is a clause boundary rather than a separator — *items 1 and 2*, *Respond and
Recover*, *tested and documented to standard* — and a few are wording quoted
from the framework, where the punctuation is not this repository's to change.

The list also carries the lines `--held` used to report: those where an item
either side of the comma is long enough that the script will not judge them at
all. They were read once, during the sweep that adopted the rule, and declined.
Recording them here is what keeps `--held` meaningful — what it prints is what
nobody has looked at yet, which today is nothing.

To decline a line, add it here exactly as it appears in the source.
