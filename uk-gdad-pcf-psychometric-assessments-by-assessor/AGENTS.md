# Assessments

Assessor-administered psychometric assessment material per role level: an
assessor presents these items to a candidate, holds the answer key, and scores
the result. Not a validated assessment instrument — see the advisory in every
file.

Part of the [UK GDAD PCF repository](../AGENTS.md). The repository-wide rules —
taxonomy, slugs, the role index, writing style, licensing — live there and in
[`spec/index.md`](../spec/index.md). Do not restate them here.
