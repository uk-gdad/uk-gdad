Welcome. If you are working toward the network architect level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A network architect has responsibility for network designs and
specifications that support business strategy, using common tools. You
research new technologies and solutions and make sure they're assured to an
appropriate level, plan, direct and co-ordinate activities while engaging
stakeholders to manage and implement a programme, agree service level
agreements and engage with third-party vendors, understand most of the
common network technologies rather than just one or two, and coach and
mentor associate network architects where appropriate. Every named skill
grows at this level, because the step up from associate is real ownership,
not just competence.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own network.

- [ ] **Design systems of real complexity, and review other people's designs too**

  Design systems characterised by medium levels of risk, impact and
  business or technical complexity, select and apply the right design
  standards, and review the systems designs of others to check the
  technology choice and integration are sound.

  Ask AI: "What should a network architect look for when reviewing
  someone else's network design, beyond just checking it will technically
  work?"

- [ ] **Coordinate integration across systems, not just build one interface**

  Define the integration build, co-ordinate build activities across
  systems, and understand how to support integration testing across the
  whole thing rather than one interface at a time.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating network integration across several
  systems actually involve day to day, compared with building and testing
  one interface?"

- [ ] **Design and implement infrastructure, not just support it**

  Design and implement infrastructure solutions and services — networking
  and physical infrastructure, off-the-shelf software, open source
  packages, or virtual and cloud computing.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between designing an infrastructure
  solution and implementing it, and where does a network architect's
  responsibility for each begin and end?"

- [ ] **Diagnose root causes, not just symptoms**

  Break a problem down into its component parts to identify and diagnose
  root causes, and troubleshoot problems across different technology
  capabilities.

  Ask AI: "What's a structured way to break down a network problem into
  component parts, so the actual root cause surfaces rather than the most
  obvious symptom?"

- [ ] **Develop and improve governance processes, not just follow them**

  Develop appropriate governance and assurance processes, identify and
  recommend improvements to existing ones, and support others in following
  or applying them.

  Ask AI: "How would a network architect tell that a governance process
  has become an obstacle rather than a safeguard, and needs changing?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
