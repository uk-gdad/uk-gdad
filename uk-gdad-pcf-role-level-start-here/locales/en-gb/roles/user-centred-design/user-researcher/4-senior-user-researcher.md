Welcome. If you are working toward the senior user researcher level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior user researcher is an experienced practitioner who can plan and
lead user research activities in larger teams and on more complex services.
You build user-centred practices in new teams, align research activities
with wider plans that inform a service's proposition, and supervise and
develop other user researchers to assure and improve research practice.
Most named skills grow at this level, because leading and assuring other
people's research asks more than doing strong research yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is often
most useful for pressure-testing a research or standards decision —
describe the actual situation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Evaluate research quality, and give feedback to other researchers**

  Evaluate the quality of research against accepted professional
  standards, explain what good user research practice involves, and give
  constructive feedback to other user researchers to make sure their work
  meets those standards.

  Ask AI: "What should a senior researcher look for when evaluating a
  colleague's research against professional standards, beyond whether the
  session went smoothly?"

- [ ] **Help teams apply a range of methods, and critique findings**

  Understand and help teams apply a range of methods to analyse data and
  synthesise findings, effectively engage sceptical colleagues in the
  process, and advise on choosing and applying techniques while critiquing
  colleagues' findings to assure best practice.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior researcher engage a sceptical colleague who
  doesn't trust qualitative research findings?"

- [ ] **Plan research for challenging needs, and advise on method choice**

  Use a wide range of user research methods and help teams adopt them,
  plan research for services with challenging user needs and complex
  journeys, and advise colleagues on choosing and applying methods to
  assure best practice.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior researcher choose the right method for a
  service with a genuinely complex, multi-step user journey?"

- [ ] **Advocate for inclusive practice, and help teams design accessibly**

  Help teams understand the diversity of users of government services,
  effectively include all kinds of users in research activities, and
  advocate for inclusive practices that help teams design and deliver
  accessible services.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior researcher make the case for including
  harder-to-reach user groups, to a team focused on hitting a delivery
  deadline?"

- [ ] **Advocate for user research with sceptical stakeholders**

  Evaluate and choose approaches for building your team's understanding of
  the user, advocate for user research with sceptical colleagues and
  stakeholders, help inexperienced teams adopt user-centred practices,
  identify important challenges and opportunities, and align research
  plans to team priorities.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior researcher make the case for user research
  to a stakeholder who sees it as slowing delivery down?"

- [ ] **Keep advocating for Agile working, and adapting research to complexity**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from user researcher
  level: advocating for Agile working, adapting research to product
  complexity, and understanding the team's strategic decisions.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level Agile research judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep building stakeholder relationships, and reaching consensus**

  This skill also plateaus: working with your team to understand
  stakeholders, developing communications strategies, and building
  long-term relationships.

  Ask AI: "What would leading stakeholder strategy across several
  research projects add, beyond building relationships well for one?"

Ask a lead user researcher, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
