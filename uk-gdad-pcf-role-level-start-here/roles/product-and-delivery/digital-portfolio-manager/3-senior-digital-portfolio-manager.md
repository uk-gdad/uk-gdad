# Senior digital portfolio manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior digital portfolio manager
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything
else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it
asks for.

## Purpose

A senior digital portfolio manager is responsible for co-ordinating and
managing a portfolio of digital projects — not just helping with it. You
track, analyse and help deliver outcomes across the portfolio, give guidance
and deal with issues so teams can deliver faster, manage governance and
assurance processes and make sure standards are actually met, and provide
stakeholders with real information on performance and risk rather than
reassurance. Most named skills grow noticeably at this level, because you're
now accountable for the portfolio's outcomes, not just contributing to them.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
prioritisation or risk call — describe the actual trade-off and ask what you
might be missing.

- [ ] **Plan with real data, and manage complex dependencies**

  Understand the environment and prioritise the highest-value tasks, use
  data to inform planning, manage complex internal and external
  dependencies, provide delivery confidence, remove blockers, and make sure
  teams plan appropriately for their own capacity.

  Ask AI: "What data would genuinely improve a portfolio manager's
  prioritisation decisions, as opposed to data that just looks
  reassuring?"

- [ ] **Develop and improve governance processes, not just follow them**

  Develop appropriate governance and assurance processes, identify and
  recommend improvements to existing ones, and support others in following
  or applying them.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior portfolio manager tell that a governance
  process has become an obstacle rather than a safeguard, and needs
  changing?"

- [ ] **Own stakeholder strategy, not just individual relationships**

  Work with your team to develop and maintain a shared understanding of
  stakeholders, develop and implement stakeholder communications
  strategies, identify and resolve issues, influence stakeholders, and
  build long-term strategic relationships.

  Ask AI: "What does a genuine stakeholder communications strategy contain
  for a portfolio, beyond a list of who to update and how often?"

- [ ] **Turn complex data into compelling, actionable stories**

  Turn complex data into compelling, clear and actionable stories, share
  data communication skills with your team and across government, present
  complex messages clearly, communicate both negative and positive
  information honestly, and give recommendations at both tactical and
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data story genuinely actionable, rather than just
  clear and well presented?"

- [ ] **Contribute to shaping strategy, not just working within it**

  Work within a strategic context and communicate how your activities meet
  strategic goals, and contribute to developing strategy and policy rather
  than only implementing it.

  Ask AI: "What would contributing to strategy development actually
  involve for a senior portfolio manager, as distinct from a policy team's
  role?"

- [ ] **Keep applying Agile methodology at portfolio level**

  This skill's wording is unchanged here, as it is across every level of
  this role: showing awareness of Agile methodology, taking an
  open-minded approach, and iterating quickly.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill doesn't change wording across the whole role,
  what would genuinely senior-level Agile judgement at portfolio scale add
  beyond what's written here?"

Ask a head of portfolio, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of these
matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
