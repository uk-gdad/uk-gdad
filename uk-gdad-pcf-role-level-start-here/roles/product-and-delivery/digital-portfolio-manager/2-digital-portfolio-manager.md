# Digital portfolio manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the digital portfolio manager level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A digital portfolio manager helps co-ordinate and manage a portfolio of
digital projects. The step up from analyst is scope, more than a change in
named skill: you help create environments that enable teams to deliver,
support prioritisation across a group of projects and programmes rather than
one, help teams follow governance and delivery processes, help stakeholders
understand the performance of work and its risks, and support more senior
portfolio managers and leadership in making improvements. Only governance
and assurance genuinely deepens in the framework's own wording at this
level — the rest of the growth here is in the breadth of what you're
trusted to help with, not a longer skill description.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own portfolio.

- [ ] **Contribute to deciding what governance a project actually needs**

  Explain how governance and assurance work, follow the appropriate
  processes, and contribute to deciding what level of governance and
  assurance a product or service actually requires — rather than applying
  the same process regardless of context.

  Ask AI: "How would a portfolio manager decide how much governance a
  specific project genuinely needs, rather than applying a one-size-fits-
  all process?"

- [ ] **Keep planning with an awareness of different approaches**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from analyst level:
  understanding the importance of planning and forecasting, and being aware
  of different ways to build a plan.

  Ask AI: "What's a realistic next step, beyond being aware of different
  planning approaches, that a portfolio manager could actually practise
  across several projects?"

- [ ] **Keep building stakeholder relationships across a group of projects**

  This skill also plateaus: identifying stakeholders, tailoring
  communication to their needs, building consensus, and using evidence to
  explain decisions.

  Ask AI: "How does managing stakeholder relationships change once you're
  helping coordinate several projects at once, rather than one?"

- [ ] **Keep communicating analysis clearly, with support**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: choosing the right medium, shaping
  communications for the audience, presenting analysis clearly with
  support, and giving tactical recommendations.

  Ask AI: "What would taking more ownership of presenting portfolio-level
  analysis, beyond doing it with support, look like at the next level?"

- [ ] **Keep connecting your work to the wider strategy**

  This skill plateaus as well: explaining the strategic context of your
  work and supporting strategic planning in an administrative capacity.

  Ask AI: "What would contributing to strategic planning, rather than just
  supporting it administratively, involve for a portfolio manager?"

- [ ] **Keep applying Agile methodology at portfolio level**

  This skill's wording is unchanged too, across every level of this role:
  showing awareness of Agile methodology, taking an open-minded approach,
  and iterating quickly.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill doesn't change wording across the whole role,
  what would genuinely deepening Agile practice at portfolio level look
  like in your own organisation?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
