# Head of portfolio - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of portfolio level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of portfolio leads a team of digital portfolio managers, making sure
the portfolio of digital projects delivers the best possible outcomes. You
lead the tracking and governing of digital projects, make sure the right
controls are used, address risks and deal with or escalate issues, give
leaders the insight into the portfolio's future they need to make informed
decisions, build and lead a diverse, skilled and motivated team of portfolio
managers, make sure that team meets the organisation's needs, and support
the professional development of the portfolio community — including line
management and coaching. Strategic thinking and stakeholder relationship
management sharpen clearly at this level, because you're now setting
direction and negotiating on behalf of a whole team, not just your own
portfolio.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Define strategy, and guide others in working within it**

  Define strategies and policies, providing guidance to others on working
  within the strategic context, and evaluate current strategies to make
  sure business requirements are being met and exceeded where possible.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of portfolio evaluate whether an existing
  strategy is still meeting business requirements, rather than assuming it
  still is?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and negotiate
  with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior stakeholder
  who has the authority to block a portfolio decision but no obligation to
  compromise?"

- [ ] **Communicate compelling, complex recommendations**

  Communicate relevant, compelling stories using the most appropriate
  medium, and make complex recommendations at both a tactical and a
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of portfolio tailor the same complex
  recommendation differently for a tactical audience and a strategic one?"

- [ ] **Keep developing and improving governance processes**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  developing appropriate governance and assurance processes, identifying
  improvements, and supporting others in applying them.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide governance leadership add, beyond
  developing and improving processes for one portfolio?"

- [ ] **Keep planning with real data across complex dependencies**

  This skill also plateaus: understanding the environment, using data to
  inform planning, managing complex dependencies, and providing delivery
  confidence.

  Ask AI: "What would leading planning capability across an entire
  portfolio team add, beyond doing the planning well yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep applying Agile methodology at portfolio level**

  This skill's wording is unchanged here too, as it is across every level
  of this role: showing awareness of Agile methodology, taking an
  open-minded approach, and iterating quickly.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill doesn't change wording across the whole role,
  what would genuinely head-of-portfolio Agile leadership add beyond what's
  written here?"

Beyond these named skills, this level is also about people: building,
leading and developing a whole team of portfolio managers. Ask a peer head
of portfolio, in your organisation or another part of government, how they
built that side of the role — that conversation will teach you more than any
general reading on team leadership.
