# Lead business analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead business analyst level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A lead business analyst has a good understanding of the enterprise arena and
works in highly complex problem spaces, typically spanning multiple products
or services. You're a leader in the business analysis community across
government and externally, hold functional or people management
accountabilities, actively share knowledge as a go-to person in the field,
mentor others, and own the main stakeholder relationships for your area. Two
skills appear for the first time here — consultancy and enterprise and
business architecture — because working across multiple products means
advising on approach as much as doing analysis yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is most useful as a sounding board for a
strategic recommendation — describe the trade-off you're weighing across
products or services and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Translate business goals into operating models**

  Work with limited direction to translate business drivers, goals and
  constraints into business objectives, and help define required
  capabilities and support organisational changes to create operating
  models that meet those objectives.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a business objective and an
  operating model built to achieve it, and how would a lead analyst
  translate one into the other?"

- [ ] **Advise as a specialist, not just deliver analysis**

  Provide advice, guidance and recommendations based on your specialist
  knowledge and experience, propose methodologies and approaches to
  implementation, and frame problems so they're easy to understand and
  troubleshoot.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between giving a stakeholder an analysis
  and giving them consultancy, and when does a lead analyst need to do the
  second?"

- [ ] **Advise on options analysis at strategic scale**

  Advise on approaches to options analysis, feasibility and impact
  assessments to ensure best practice is followed, make sure all work
  aligns to the organisation's strategic context, advise on how the
  organisation can use business analysis skills to add value, and guide
  tactical decisions while influencing strategic plans.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead analyst advise on best practice for options
  analysis across teams that have never worked together before?"

- [ ] **Lead large-scale process improvement, and coach others in it**

  Lead the identification of large-scale opportunities to improve business
  performance, set the direction for how improvements and their tests get
  designed and evaluated, and coach others in doing the same.

  Ask AI: "What does 'setting direction' for process improvement across an
  organisation involve, as distinct from leading one improvement project?"

- [ ] **Determine the approach to modelling complex programmes**

  Determine the scope and methodology for representing complex programmes
  across an organisation, and document conceptual models that support
  strategic planning and decision making.

  Ask AI: "What makes a conceptual model useful for strategic planning,
  rather than just an accurate diagram of what already exists?"

- [ ] **Direct the approach to requirements across complex programmes**

  Direct the approach to requirements management, enable others to select
  and apply the right life cycle methods, influence prioritisation of
  complex requirement sets to balance long-term strategy and short-term
  fixes, and ensure proposed solutions align with organisational strategy.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead analyst balance a long-term strategic
  requirement against an urgent short-term fix competing for the same
  resource?"

- [ ] **Set direction for systems analysis, and bridge business and technical views**

  Set the direction for identifying and analysing IT system capabilities,
  identify gaps in functionality and enable others to explore their effect,
  and bring together business perspectives and technical concepts into
  recommendations that users, development teams and external suppliers can
  all understand.

  Ask AI: "What does it take to communicate the same technical gap clearly
  to a user, a developer and an external supplier, each of whom needs a
  different level of detail?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and negotiate
  with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior stakeholder
  who has the authority to block progress but no obligation to compromise?"

- [ ] **Keep recommending and validating the testing approach**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  recommending the value of testing, advising on scenarios and acceptance
  criteria, and reviewing test plans and outcomes.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely lead-level testing judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep advising on user experience approach**

  This skill also plateaus: advising on the approach to user experience
  analysis, recommending techniques, and articulating how findings affect
  system design.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead analyst make user experience findings carry
  weight in a strategic decision, rather than being treated as a nice-to-
  have?"

- [ ] **Keep determining your team's approach within the delivery methodology**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: determining your role's approach
  within the team's methodology, agreeing scope and priorities, and
  communicating the value of iterative delivery.

  Ask AI: "What would leading delivery methodology decisions across
  multiple teams add, beyond determining your own role's approach within
  one?"

Ask a head of business analysis, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these has taken them longest to get right — that conversation will teach
you more than any general reading on business analysis leadership.
