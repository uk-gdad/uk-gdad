# Senior business analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior business analyst level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior business analyst has a good understanding of strategic arenas and
leads large or complex projects. You'll have functional or people management
responsibilities, or a combination of both, mentor others, develop best
practice for the wider team, own stakeholder relationships outright, and
manage community of practice activities within your business area. Every
named skill grows into leading the activity rather than just doing it well —
the through-line at this level is that other analysts start looking to you
for the approach, not just the output.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
recommendation before you take it to stakeholders — describe the actual
trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Lead process improvement, not just contribute to it**

  Lead process analysis and evaluation to define business performance
  problems and opportunities, lead the design, testing, implementation and
  assessment of improvements, and manage the execution and assessment of
  business process tests.

  Ask AI: "How does leading a process improvement initiative differ from
  contributing strong analysis to one someone else is leading?"

- [ ] **Lead the approach to understanding a problem, and gain support for it**

  Lead the approach to understanding context, problem and root causes, set
  goals for a piece of work that align with the organisation's strategic
  context, and lead developing and evaluating options while advocating for
  recommendations to gain support.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between formulating a good recommendation
  and successfully gaining support for it, and where do senior analysts
  usually need to invest more effort?"

- [ ] **Lead complex business modelling across a project or programme**

  Lead modelling of business processes, systems and data at varying levels
  of complexity across a project or programme, and manage the impact of
  proposed changes.

  Ask AI: "What changes about business modelling when it spans a whole
  programme rather than a single project?"

- [ ] **Lead digital and data systems analysis, and bridge disciplines**

  Lead identifying and analysing IT system capabilities, use systems
  analysis to deepen understanding of how processes and systems work and
  identify gaps, and work with other digital professionals to design
  interfaces and assess the effect of proposed changes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'bridging' business analysis and technical disciplines
  actually look like in a real project, beyond attending the same
  meetings?"

- [ ] **Advise on requirements strategy across a project or programme**

  Advise on the approach to requirements management, define the most
  appropriate life cycle methods to ensure traceability through design,
  build, test and evaluation, and co-ordinate and negotiate prioritisation
  to meet programme objectives.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior analyst decide which requirements management
  approach fits a programme with several interdependent projects?"

- [ ] **Own stakeholder strategy, not just individual relationships**

  Work with your team to develop and maintain a shared understanding of
  stakeholders, develop and implement stakeholder communications
  strategies, identify and resolve issues, and build long-term strategic
  relationships.

  Ask AI: "What does a genuine stakeholder communications strategy contain,
  beyond a list of who to update and how often?"

- [ ] **Advise on user experience approach across a project**

  Advise on the approach to analysing, prioritising and validating user
  experience needs, recommend techniques to analyse the experience
  properly, and articulate how user experience needs affect system design.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior analyst make the case that a user experience
  finding should change a system design decision, to stakeholders focused
  on cost and timeline?"

- [ ] **Recommend and validate testing approach**

  Recommend the value of testing and apply the relevant tools and
  techniques to ensure test validity, advise on business scenarios and
  acceptance criteria, and review prototypes, test plans and test outcome
  reporting.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior analyst judge whether a test plan actually
  validates the requirements it claims to, rather than just running
  through motions?"

- [ ] **Determine your team's approach within the delivery methodology**

  Determine the approach for your role within the team's delivery
  methodology, define and agree the scope of delivery, negotiate delivery
  priorities with stakeholders, and communicate the value of iterative
  delivery and realising benefits early.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior analyst make the case for iterative delivery
  to a stakeholder who wants everything specified up front?"

Ask a lead colleague which of these matters most in your organisation right
now, and start there.
