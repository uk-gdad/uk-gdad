# Junior business analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the junior business analyst level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A junior business analyst still receives direction from more senior
colleagues, but is now responsible for the output of specific tasks rather
than just building general skill. You'll have limited skills and still work
with others or under supervision, but you're expected to support stakeholder
relationship management directly rather than just observing it. Two new
skills appear here — business modelling and user experience analysis —
because understanding a business problem properly means being able to
represent it visually and understanding what users actually experience, not
just what a document says they need.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own organisation's context.

- [ ] **Keep supporting requirements capture within a defined scope**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from trainee level:
  supporting identification, analysis, capture and validation of business
  and user requirements, and managing them under supervision within a
  defined scope.

  Ask AI: "What's a realistic next step, beyond supporting requirements
  capture, that a junior analyst could actually practise?"

- [ ] **Learn to represent a business situation visually**

  Explain basic business modelling techniques, support representing
  business situations within a defined task, and visualise business
  processes.

  Ask AI: "What is business modelling in practice, and what's a simple way
  a junior analyst could start visualising a process they're analysing?"

- [ ] **Start connecting user research to what gets built**

  Understand the importance of making data-informed decisions based on user
  research findings, and support analysing and prioritising user experience
  needs, tying them to system, product or service requirements.

  Ask AI: "How does a business analyst use user research findings, as
  distinct from the job a dedicated user researcher does?"

- [ ] **Keep building the techniques for understanding a problem**

  This skill's wording plateaus here too: describing analysis techniques
  for understanding context, problem and options, and contributing to
  identifying and assessing them under supervision.

  Ask AI: "What's a good next step, beyond describing analysis techniques,
  that a junior analyst could actually practise on a real piece of work?"

- [ ] **Keep supporting digital and data systems analysis**

  This skill also plateaus: supporting the analysis of IT system
  capabilities and the development of specifications and models under
  supervision.

  Ask AI: "What would taking more ownership of systems analysis, beyond
  supporting it under supervision, look like for a junior analyst?"

- [ ] **Keep building your understanding of testing**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: understanding the stages and purpose
  of testing, and supporting the representation of business scenarios under
  supervision.

  Ask AI: "What would a junior analyst need to learn to move from
  supporting testing activities to leading them?"

- [ ] **Keep contributing to business process improvement**

  This skill plateaus as well: contributing to analysing, designing and
  testing business process improvements.

  Ask AI: "What makes a business process improvement genuinely stick, as
  opposed to reverting once attention moves elsewhere?"

- [ ] **Keep learning who your stakeholders are**

  This skill's wording is unchanged too: describing who your stakeholders
  are and what they find important.

  Ask AI: "What would supporting stakeholder relationship management
  directly, rather than just observing it, involve for a junior analyst?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding how delivery methodology shapes your work**

  This skill's wording plateaus here as well: explaining the differences
  between delivery methodologies and how roles work together to support
  delivery.

  Ask AI: "What would adapting your own approach to a specific delivery
  methodology, rather than just explaining the differences, look like?"

Ask your manager or a more senior analyst which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
