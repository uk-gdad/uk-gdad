# Trainee business analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are starting out as a trainee business analyst, this page is
for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for,
one step at a time.

## Purpose

Business analysts help teams understand a business problem or opportunity
properly before anyone builds anything: researching how a business area
actually works, identifying where it could improve, exploring feasible
options, and making sure whatever gets built genuinely meets business and
user needs rather than an assumption about them. As a trainee, you receive
direction from more senior business analysts and work with others, or under
supervision, to build your skills — the point of this level is developing
judgement you don't have yet, not proving judgement you already do.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation's way of working, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Support identifying and capturing requirements**

  Support identifying, analysing, capturing and validating business and
  user requirements, work under supervision to manage them and support
  their prioritisation within a defined scope, and provide distinct
  task-based outputs as directed, using a requirements management life
  cycle someone else has set.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to business analysis. Explain, simply, what a business
  requirement is, how it's different from a user requirement, and why
  capturing both matters."

- [ ] **Learn the techniques for understanding a problem before solving it**

  Describe the analysis techniques you could use to understand the
  context, problem and options that meet business and user needs, explain
  why internal and external factors influence an organisation, and
  contribute to identifying and assessing options under supervision.

  Ask AI: "What are the main techniques business analysts use to understand
  a problem properly before jumping to a solution, explained simply?"

- [ ] **Get comfortable with the digital and data side of analysis**

  Support the analysis of IT system capabilities, work under supervision to
  identify and specify system requirements, and support developing
  specifications and models for systems or software.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what 'systems analysis' means for a
  business analyst, and how it differs from what a developer does."

- [ ] **Understand what testing means for a business analyst**

  Understand the stages and purpose of testing, have an awareness of the
  tools and techniques used, and work under supervision to support
  representing business scenarios and tracing requirements to functionality.

  Ask AI: "What role does a business analyst play in testing, as distinct
  from the job a dedicated tester does?"

- [ ] **Contribute to improving business processes**

  Contribute to analysing and evaluating business processes to identify
  problems and opportunities, contribute to designing and implementing
  improvements, and contribute to testing and evaluating their usability.

  Ask AI: "What does it mean to 'evaluate a business process' in practice,
  and what kind of problem would a business analyst be looking for?"

- [ ] **Learn who your stakeholders are, and why that matters**

  Describe who your stakeholders are and why managing relationships with
  them matters, and explain what they find important and why.

  Ask AI: "How would a trainee business analyst work out who the real
  stakeholders are for a piece of work, beyond the obvious people already
  in the room?"

- [ ] **Understand how delivery methodology shapes your work**

  Explain the differences between delivery methodologies, how your approach
  would change based on the one your team uses, why iteration matters, and
  how roles in a multidisciplinary team work together to support delivery.

  Ask AI: "Explain, simply, how a business analyst's day-to-day work
  differs depending on the delivery methodology their team uses."

You do not need to master all of this before you start. Ask your manager or a
more senior analyst which of these your team leans on most, and start there.
