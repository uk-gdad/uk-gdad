# Programme delivery manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the programme delivery manager role, this
page is for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names. The
framework publishes this as a single level rather than a ladder, and its
named skills read at a genuinely senior register throughout — this is not a
role most people arrive at without delivery management experience already
behind them.

## Purpose

A programme delivery manager is accountable for the delivery of complex
products and services delivered by multiple teams, or that carry high
technical or political risk. You manage dependencies of varying complexity,
potentially feeding into larger programmes and portfolios, remove blockers
and manage risk, commercial relationships, budgets and people together,
balance objectives and redeploy resources as priorities shift, and bring an
in-depth knowledge of Agile and other methodologies to bear. You're
responsible for understanding, managing and communicating with complex
stakeholder groups, act as the programme's initial escalation point, keep
an awareness of the bigger picture beyond your own programme, support the
programme director in delivering their vision, and support and coach
delivery managers under you.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for an escalation or resourcing decision —
describe the actual situation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Lead planning in a genuinely complex environment**

  Lead a continual planning process in a very complex environment, plan
  beyond product delivery, identify dependencies across services and
  co-ordinate delivery, and coach other teams as the central point of
  expertise.

  Ask AI: "What does 'planning beyond product delivery' actually mean for
  a programme delivery manager, compared with planning one product's
  roadmap?"

- [ ] **Lead teams through the life cycle, and manage risk across it**

  Lead and coach teams through different phases of the life cycle, ensure
  effective support for the product or service and its users, predict,
  prevent, mitigate and manage risks at different phases, and contribute
  to assessing other teams with guidance and support.

  Ask AI: "What does 'predicting and preventing' a life cycle risk look
  like in practice, as opposed to reacting well once it's already
  happened?"

- [ ] **Tackle the most complicated risks and dependencies**

  Optimise the delivery flow of teams, actively address the most
  complicated risks, issues and dependencies — including ones with no
  clear ownership at all — and identify innovative ways to unblock them.

  Ask AI: "How should a programme delivery manager approach a risk that
  nobody in the organisation clearly owns?"

- [ ] **Resolve conflict at the highest level, and grow the community**

  Solve and unblock issues between teams or departments at the highest
  level, and coach the organisation on team dynamics and conflict
  resolution, while also building and growing the wider community.

  Ask AI: "What separates a programme delivery manager who genuinely
  resolves cross-department conflict from one who just mediates a truce
  that reappears later?"

- [ ] **Represent the delivery community to large audiences**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships, adopt the right
  communication method for stakeholders at every level, manage
  expectations and moderate difficult discussions about high-risk topics
  under real time pressure, and speak on behalf of the community to large
  audiences inside and outside the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a programme delivery manager prepare to represent
  their programme to a large, mostly non-technical audience?"

- [ ] **Own escalation for the hardest commercial issues**

  Act as the escalation point for large or high-risk commercial management
  issues, and coach others in handling commercial management
  appropriately.

  Ask AI: "What makes a commercial issue serious enough to need a
  programme delivery manager's direct involvement, rather than being
  resolved at delivery manager level?"

- [ ] **Influence complex budgets, and make the budget you're given work**

  Influence or create complex budgets across an organisation, programme or
  product view, and manage the budget you're given so it actually works
  for the programme.

  Ask AI: "How would a programme delivery manager make a fixed budget work
  when priorities shift partway through the programme?"

- [ ] **Keep leading Agile and Lean practice as a recognised expert**

  Coach and lead teams in Agile and Lean good practice, tailor the
  approach for a team through the whole life cycle, experiment with new
  ways of working across the organisation, and act as a recognised expert
  and advocate.

  Ask AI: "How would a programme delivery manager introduce a new way of
  working across several teams without it feeling imposed on the ones
  that weren't consulted?"

- [ ] **Keep challenging organisational process**

  Identify and challenge organisationally complex or unnecessarily
  complicated processes, coach the organisation to inspect and adapt them,
  and guide teams through implementing a new process.

  Ask AI: "How would a programme delivery manager challenge an
  organisational process that's slowing several teams down, without just
  being seen as difficult?"

Ask a programme director, or an equivalent senior colleague, which of these
matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
