# Head of product - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of product level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of product provides support to every product manager in the
organisation and to the product community across government, leads on
product strategy working closely with lead product managers and their
business units, and is responsible for setting and raising standards for
product management in the organisation. You coach others in product
management, work with organisation management and other communities to
represent product community interests, oversee every member of the
community — their role, assignments, development, pay and performance —
manage recruitment, development, moves and exits across civil servants and
contractors, and line manage senior and lead product managers. Three named
skills sharpen clearly at this level: creating value for money, managing
product outcomes and product leadership, each moving from doing the thing
well to building the organisation's capacity to do it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Own the organisation's approach to value, and coach others in it**

  Develop and own a business case and iterate it throughout the life
  cycle, coach others on identifying benefits while balancing user and
  business needs, communicate the delivery of incremental value, build the
  case for an organisational approach to value through product strategy,
  and coach and empower teams to take a value-for-money approach.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of product build a consistent value-for-money
  culture across many product teams that currently define value
  differently?"

- [ ] **Use data at organisational scale, and challenge teams to do better**

  Use data to make prioritisation decisions for multiple or complex
  projects, products or services, use data to influence the priorities of
  senior stakeholders, guide and challenge teams to make better use of
  data, guide and challenge teams to demonstrate they've achieved their
  outcomes, and find opportunities to share and connect product data
  across government.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of product challenge a team that's using data
  to justify a decision they'd already made, rather than to inform it?"

- [ ] **Lead and represent product management across government**

  Seek opportunities for reuse and learning across government, co-ordinate
  the product management community, lead coaching and mentoring for
  product management in your organisation, lead activities in cross-
  government communities to build relationships across the profession, and
  represent product management in conversations with senior stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What does building a genuine cross-government product
  management community look like, beyond attending the same conferences as
  other heads of product?"

- [ ] **Keep leading Agile and Lean practice as a recognised expert**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level: coaching
  and leading teams in Agile and Lean good practice, and acting as a
  recognised expert and advocate.

  Ask AI: "What would leading Agile and Lean practice for the whole
  product community add, beyond being a recognised expert for individual
  portfolios?"

- [ ] **Keep championing user-centred practice across the organisation**

  This skill also plateaus: advocating for effective research approaches,
  coaching others in user-centred decisions, and using insight for
  strategic decisions.

  Ask AI: "What would embedding user-centred practice as a standard for
  the whole product community add, beyond championing it in individual
  portfolios?"

- [ ] **Keep leading teams through the life cycle**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: leading and coaching teams through
  life cycle phases, ensuring effective support, and predicting and
  preventing risks.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide life cycle standards add, beyond
  leading individual teams through their phases well?"

- [ ] **Keep setting strategy and planning across multiple teams**

  This skill also plateaus: setting strategy and vision for products,
  planning prioritised work across multiple teams, and coaching others in
  product management.

  Ask AI: "What would setting the standard for product strategy across the
  whole organisation add, beyond doing it well for one portfolio?"

- [ ] **Keep directing stakeholder strategy at senior level**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: directing stakeholder relationship
  strategy, ensuring objectives are set and supported, and negotiating with
  senior stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What changes about stakeholder strategy when you're accountable
  for the whole product community's relationships, rather than one
  portfolio's?"

- [ ] **Keep coaching others in building and delivering strategy**

  This skill also plateaus: supporting and coaching others in creating
  long-term strategy, influencing stakeholders, and ensuring strategic
  alignment across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What would building organisation-wide strategic capability add,
  beyond coaching individual lead product managers well?"

Beyond these named skills, this level is also about people: recruitment,
development, pay and performance across a whole community. Ask a peer head
of product, in your organisation or another part of government, how they
built that side of the role — that conversation will teach you more than any
general reading on people leadership.
