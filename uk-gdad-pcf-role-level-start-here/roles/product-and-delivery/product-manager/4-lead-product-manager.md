# Lead product manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead product manager level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A lead product manager is responsible for a product portfolio or an
end-to-end service, and for the delivery of product quality and value
across it. You work with teams to identify, implement and improve
strategies and tactics, sit on the management team for your product area,
and provide product leadership and direction across multiple teams. You
manage people and products together, maintain the high-level roadmap, take
part in programme management activity relevant to your portfolio, get
involved in recruiting product managers at every level and contractors, and
provide leadership across your programmes and the local community, working
closely with a head of product where one exists.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is most useful as a sounding board for a
strategic call — describe the trade-off you're weighing across a portfolio
and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Become a recognised expert in Agile and Lean approaches**

  Coach and lead teams in Agile and Lean good practice, create and tailor
  the right approach for a team through the whole life cycle, experiment
  with new ways of working to improve delivery across the organisation, and
  act as a recognised expert and advocate.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead product manager introduce a new way of working
  across a portfolio without it feeling imposed on teams that weren't
  consulted?"

- [ ] **Champion user-centred practice across the organisation**

  Advocate for a range of effective research approaches across the
  organisation, coach others in making decisions that meet user needs
  across every channel, advocate for the continuous use of user insight,
  and use it to make strategic decisions about user experience.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead product manager make user-centred practice
  something teams do by habit, rather than something they're reminded to
  do?"

- [ ] **Lead teams through the life cycle, and manage risk across it**

  Lead and coach teams through different phases of the life cycle, ensure
  effective support for products and their users, predict, prevent,
  mitigate and manage risks at different phases, and contribute to
  assessing other teams with guidance and support.

  Ask AI: "What does 'predicting and preventing' a life cycle risk look
  like in practice, as opposed to reacting well once it's already
  happened?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and negotiate
  with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior stakeholder
  who has the authority to block a portfolio decision but no obligation to
  compromise?"

- [ ] **Coach others in building and delivering strategy**

  Support and coach others in creating and implementing a long-term
  strategy and tactical approach that others genuinely agree with,
  influence and persuade stakeholders to support delivery of the strategy,
  and ensure strategic alignment across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between coaching someone to build their
  own strategy and just handing them one you've already decided?"

- [ ] **Keep making and justifying value-based decisions**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  advocating for user needs balanced against cost, writing business cases,
  and helping others make value-based decisions.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely lead-level value-for-money judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep leading outcome measurement across teams**

  This skill also plateaus: leading teams in creating measurement methods,
  advocating for data-driven decisions, and finding opportunities to share
  and connect product data.

  Ask AI: "What would leading outcome measurement across a whole
  portfolio add, beyond doing it well for one product?"

- [ ] **Keep representing product management to senior stakeholders**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: seeking reuse and learning across the
  organisation, representing product management to senior stakeholders,
  and coaching others.

  Ask AI: "How does representing product management change when you're
  speaking for a whole portfolio, rather than one product?"

- [ ] **Keep setting strategy and planning across multiple teams**

  This skill also plateaus: setting the strategy and vision for your
  products, planning prioritised work across multiple teams, and coaching
  others in product management.

  Ask AI: "What would maintaining a high-level roadmap across a whole
  portfolio add, beyond planning one product's roadmap well?"

Ask a head of product, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of these
matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
