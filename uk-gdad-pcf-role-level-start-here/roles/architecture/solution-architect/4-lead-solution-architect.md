# Lead solution architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead solution architect level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A lead solution architect is responsible for a group of solution
architecture projects, or a single more complex area, and may lead teams of
more junior solution architects. You build relationships with senior
stakeholders across multiple business or technical areas, stay proactive in
spotting opportunities to improve the organisation, support multiple
architecture projects or programmes, find and use emerging technologies,
and develop best practice for solution architecture. Six named skills grow
at this level — seeing the whole context, architecture communication,
commercial perspective, problem definition, strategy design and technical
design — because you're now shaping direction across several projects, not
delivering one.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a strategic call — describe the trade-off
you're weighing across projects and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **See beyond your own projects to the whole organisation**

  Work to support wider organisational objectives beyond your immediate
  goals, track emerging internal and external issues that could affect
  teams across the organisation, and take action to solve or mitigate
  problems by influencing colleagues elsewhere.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead solution architect notice an emerging issue
  that affects several of their projects, when day-to-day focus is
  naturally on delivery?"

- [ ] **Mediate difficult architecture discussions, and coach others in it**

  Communicate with technical and non-technical stakeholders at every
  level and across organisations, mediate between people in difficult
  architectural discussions, gain support for high-risk, high-impact
  topics, and coach others in architecture communication.

  Ask AI: "What approach would help a lead solution architect mediate a
  genuine disagreement between two senior stakeholders about an
  architectural direction?"

- [ ] **Choose contractual frameworks, and select suppliers**

  Identify appropriate contractual frameworks and approaches, and
  identify, evaluate and select appropriate suppliers.

  Ask AI: "What should a lead solution architect weigh when selecting a
  contractual framework for a major piece of architectural work?"

- [ ] **Lead framing the hardest problems, and coach others in it**

  Lead framing a problem of high complexity, complication or risk so a
  solution can be created, coach others in defining problems and
  describing options, and help others challenge requirements and
  assumptions.

  Ask AI: "What separates a well-framed high-complexity problem from one
  that just feels overwhelming because nobody has structured it yet?"

- [ ] **Define strategy across teams, and direct its implementation**

  Define strategies or visions across teams that align with organisational
  objectives, direct implementation through roadmaps or plans, define
  architectural principles and patterns, and develop or maintain strategy
  in response to feedback.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between supporting a strategy's
  development and genuinely defining it across several teams?"

- [ ] **Lead technical design at high levels of risk**

  Create technical designs of high risk, impact and complexity, lead and
  guide others in creating designs that achieve organisational objectives,
  and use feedback to optimise and refine design standards throughout the
  life cycle.

  Ask AI: "What changes about technical design leadership when the risk
  and impact are genuinely high, rather than moderate?"

- [ ] **Keep building genuine collaboration across your team**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior solution
  architect level: networking actively, adapting feedback so it lasts,
  and using team health checks to spot and address issues.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level collaboration add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep making architectural decisions of medium risk**

  This skill also plateaus: making decisions of medium risk and
  complexity, addressing risks affecting multiple teams, and contributing
  to governance.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level decision-making judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

Ask a principal solution architect which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
