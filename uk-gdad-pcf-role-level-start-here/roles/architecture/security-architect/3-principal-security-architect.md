# Principal security architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal security architect level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal security architect works on services of high complexity and
risk, making decisions that enable the business to achieve its needs — the
top of this profession's line. You work on projects with high strategic
impact, setting a strategy usable for the long term and across the breadth
of the organisation, communicate with a broad range of senior stakeholders,
define the vision, principles and strategy for security architects,
recommend security design across several projects or technologies up to an
organisational or inter-organisational level, hold deep and evolving
technical expertise as an exemplar, make and influence important business
and architectural decisions, research and adopt new technologies and
methodologies, act as a recognised expert solving unprecedented problems,
and further the profession by sharing best practice within and outside the
organisation.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real strategic or risk decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the
case you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Direct analysis, and revisit decisions as the environment changes**

  Provide direction and lead change regarding the factors that feed into
  analysis, monitor changes in the technical environment to check risks
  are still acceptable or previous decisions need revisiting, and direct
  and influence others on best practice and policy.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a principal security architect that a past risk
  decision needs revisiting because the technical environment has moved
  on?"

- [ ] **Communicate security at the most senior levels, under real pressure**

  Demonstrate expert understanding of security concepts at the highest
  levels of risk and complexity, communicate implications accurately at
  the most senior levels, respond successfully to challenges, and manage
  expectations across high risk, complexity or constrained timescales.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal security architect communicate a
  genuinely high-risk security decision to the most senior stakeholders,
  under real time pressure?"

- [ ] **Lead design for the hardest problems, and set precedent**

  Lead designing and reviewing solutions to complex problems by defining
  and challenging patterns and principles, and create precedents that set
  direction for others to follow.

  Ask AI: "What separates a security pattern that becomes a genuine
  precedent others follow from one that's just used once and forgotten?"

- [ ] **Be the trusted point of escalation for the hardest risk decisions**

  Act as a point of escalation, be trusted by senior risk owners as an
  expert in security, and apply risk methodologies at the most complex
  levels of risk.

  Ask AI: "What builds the kind of trust that makes senior risk owners
  escalate a decision to a principal security architect rather than
  deciding it themselves?"

- [ ] **Lead changes to policy on security implications of transformation**

  Challenge and lead changes to policy and processes to support business
  outcomes, business architecture, and legal and political implications.

  Ask AI: "What does 'leading changes to policy' actually involve for a
  principal security architect, as distinct from advising on existing
  policy?"

- [ ] **Keep contributing to security technology developments**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  contributing to developments in security technology, identifying new
  technologies for the organisation, and engaging the broader security
  community.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely principal-level engagement with the security
  community add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep explaining complex architectures and exploitability**

  This skill also plateaus: explaining complex system architectures,
  identifying how easily vulnerabilities could be exploited, and leading
  security technology influence in the wider industry.

  Ask AI: "What would leading security technology direction for the whole
  organisation add, beyond explaining exploitability well for individual
  systems?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
