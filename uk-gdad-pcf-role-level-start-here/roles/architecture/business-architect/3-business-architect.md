# Business architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the business architect level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A business architect uses their knowledge and experience to understand
business scenarios, communicate the issues clearly and recommend next
steps — and supports and guides other business architects rather than only
producing outputs themselves. You support executive-level work and help
manage the business architecture practice, guide others in the right tools
and methods, create business architecture artefacts and know when to apply
them, and develop standard, scalable models for collecting and making sense
of complex information. A new skill appears here — strategic design and
business change — because at this level you're expected to shape how the
organisation changes, not just describe how it works today.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
recommendation before you take it to stakeholders — describe the actual
trade-off and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Shape strategic design, focused on outcomes**

  Show a good understanding of business issues, events and activities and
  their short- to long-term impact, help define principles, patterns,
  standards, policies, roadmaps and vision statements, focus on outcomes
  rather than solutions, and help develop or update strategy in response
  to feedback.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between designing a solution and shaping
  a strategic outcome, and how would a business architect keep a
  conversation focused on the latter?"

- [ ] **Apply and develop business architecture practice**

  Apply and develop business architecture frameworks, principles and
  techniques in your organisation, advise and guide the organisation on
  implementing its strategy, share the value of business architecture, and
  produce clear multidimensional views to support decision making.

  Ask AI: "How would a business architect make the case for business
  architecture's value to a stakeholder who's never seen it used well?"

- [ ] **Build organisational capability in methods and tools**

  Build capability in methods and tools, making sure they're adopted
  consistently and used effectively across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What makes a method or tool get adopted consistently across an
  organisation, rather than used well by one team and ignored elsewhere?"

- [ ] **Make and recommend decisions, and build consensus between services**

  Make decisions characterised by medium levels of risk and complexity,
  recommend decisions as risk and complexity increase, and build consensus
  between different services or independent stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a business architect build consensus between two
  services that both have valid but conflicting priorities?"

- [ ] **Lead guidance, not just give advice**

  Provide advice and recommendations based on significant specialist
  knowledge and experience, and lead the definition of guidance that
  informs how the organisation approaches delivery or uses its digital and
  data skills.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between advising one team well and
  setting guidance the whole organisation follows?"

- [ ] **Own stakeholder strategy, not just individual relationships**

  Work with your team to develop and maintain a shared understanding of
  stakeholders, develop and implement stakeholder communications
  strategies, identify and resolve issues, and build long-term strategic
  relationships.

  Ask AI: "What does a genuine stakeholder communications strategy
  contain, beyond a list of who to update and how often?"

- [ ] **Represent the architecture community to large audiences**

  Mediate between people and mend relationships, communicate with
  stakeholders at every level, manage expectations and host or moderate
  discussions about high-risk, complex topics under real time pressure,
  and speak on behalf of the community to large audiences inside and
  outside government.

  Ask AI: "How would a business architect prepare to represent their
  practice to a large, mostly non-technical audience?"

- [ ] **Define strategy, and evaluate whether it's working**

  Define strategies and policies, providing guidance to others on working
  in the strategic context, and evaluate current strategies to make sure
  business requirements are being met and exceeded where possible.

  Ask AI: "How would a business architect evaluate whether an existing
  strategy is still meeting business requirements, rather than assuming it
  still is?"

Ask a lead business architect, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
