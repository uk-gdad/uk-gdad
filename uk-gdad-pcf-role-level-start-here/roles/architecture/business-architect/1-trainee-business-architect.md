# Trainee business architect - start here

Welcome. If you are starting out as a trainee business architect, this page
is for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for,
one step at a time.

## Purpose

A business architect develops an integrated view of an enterprise — a
business area or a whole organisation — and uses that view to help the
organisation put its strategy into practice. As a trainee, you support more
senior business architects while receiving training on the tasks you're
helping with, so you can eventually work on your own. You're developing your
knowledge of business architecture frameworks, practice and principles,
working with other architects to help produce clear outputs, and supporting
in workshops and stakeholder meetings rather than leading them. That support
role is exactly right for now — business architecture is as much judgement
as technique, and judgement takes time in the room to build.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Build awareness of business architecture frameworks**

  Show an awareness of business architecture frameworks, principles and
  techniques, and support applying them to business change under guidance.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to business architecture. Explain, simply, what a
  business architecture framework actually is, and give an example of how
  one might be applied."

- [ ] **Learn to apply methods and tools with guidance**

  Follow advice to apply the most appropriate tools and techniques to
  support planning, analysis, development, testing, implementation and
  improvement of systems and services.

  Ask AI: "What are the main categories of tools a business architect might
  use, explained simply enough for someone new to the field?"

- [ ] **Practise recommending, and explaining your reasoning**

  Recommend decisions and describe the reasoning behind them, and identify
  and articulate technical disputes between direct peers and local
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What makes a recommendation's reasoning genuinely convincing,
  rather than just a conclusion stated confidently?"

- [ ] **Understand what consultancy means for this role**

  Show an understanding of why consulting matters in this role and what
  it's for, work under guidance to identify what advice might be
  appropriate — usually by referring to previous work — and understand how
  to frame a problem that needs solving.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between giving someone an answer and
  consulting with them properly, and why does a business architect need to
  do the second?"

- [ ] **Practise listening and joining stakeholder discussions**

  Listen to the needs of design and business stakeholders and interpret
  what they're telling you, and take part in discussions within a
  multidisciplinary team.

  Ask AI: "What should someone new to business architecture focus on when
  joining a stakeholder discussion, so they contribute usefully rather
  than staying silent out of uncertainty?"

- [ ] **Start connecting your work to the wider strategy**

  Explain the strategic context of your work and why it matters, and
  support strategic planning in an administrative capacity.

  Ask AI: "How would a trainee business architect find out the strategic
  context behind a piece of work, if nobody's explained it to them?"

You do not need to master all of this before you start. Ask your manager or
a more senior architect which of these your team leans on most, and start
there.
