# Chief data architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the chief data architect level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A chief data architect sets the vision for the organisation's use of data,
as directed by the appropriate governance body — the top of this
profession's line. You oversee the design of multiple data models with a
broad understanding of how each fulfils the organisation's needs, are
accountable for supporting and aligning to the organisation's data
strategy, champion data architecture both internally and at the most senior
levels across government, set the standards and ways of working for the
whole data architecture community, are accountable for assuring data models
at project or enterprise level, advise project teams and oversee the full
data product life cycle, and are responsible for making sure the
organisation's systems are designed in line with the enterprise data
architecture.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Define data strategy, and evaluate whether it's working**

  Define strategies and policies, providing guidance to others on working
  in the strategic context, and evaluate current strategies to make sure
  business requirements are being met and exceeded where possible.

  Ask AI: "How would a chief data architect evaluate whether the
  organisation's data strategy is genuinely being met by day-to-day
  decisions, rather than existing separately from them?"

- [ ] **Align data governance with the organisation's wider governance**

  Make sure data governance supports changes to organisational strategy,
  align it with wider governance such as budget, and assure corporate
  services by understanding key risks and providing mitigation through
  assurance mechanisms.

  Ask AI: "What does aligning data governance with an organisation's
  budget governance actually involve in practice?"

- [ ] **Set data standards across the organisation and government**

  Create data standards for the organisation, advocate for and oversee
  compliance with them, and decide where standards need setting across the
  organisation and in the wider context of government.

  Ask AI: "How would a chief data architect decide when a data standard
  should be set at organisational level versus pushed for adoption across
  government?"

- [ ] **Recognise reuse opportunities across government and industry**

  Work across government and industry, recognising opportunities to reuse
  and align data models between organisations, and design the method to
  categorise data models within your own.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a chief data architect that a data model from
  another government organisation is genuinely reusable, rather than
  superficially similar?"

- [ ] **Make metadata repositories a strategic asset**

  Identify how metadata repositories can support different areas of the
  organisation, communicate their value, and set up robust governance
  processes to keep them up to date.

  Ask AI: "How would a chief data architect make the case that investment
  in metadata repositories is worthwhile, to a stakeholder who sees it as
  overhead?"

- [ ] **Anticipate problems, and build problem-solving capability in others**

  Anticipate problems and defend against them at the right time,
  understand how a problem fits into the larger picture, help others
  describe problems clearly, and build problem-solving capability across
  the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What separates anticipating a data problem before it happens
  from just responding well once it does?"

- [ ] **Turn complex data into clear, actionable solutions**

  Turn complex data into clear, well-understood solutions that can be
  acted on, share data communication skills across the team and
  organisation, and communicate different options honestly, including
  their risks and uncertainties.

  Ask AI: "What makes a communicated data option genuinely honest about
  its risks and uncertainties, rather than presenting only the case for
  it?"

- [ ] **Judge when to adopt data innovation, and when to wait**

  Identify areas of innovation in data tools and techniques, and recognise
  the appropriate timing for adopting them.

  Ask AI: "How would a chief data architect decide an emerging data
  technology is ready for adoption, rather than still too immature for a
  government organisation to depend on?"

- [ ] **Keep handling harder conversations with senior stakeholders**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior data architect
  level: listening to and interpreting stakeholder needs, and supporting
  or hosting difficult discussions with diverse senior stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior data
  architect level, what would genuinely chief-level stakeholder judgement
  add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep profiling data and presenting clear insight**

  This skill's wording also plateaus, unchanged since data architect
  level: undertaking data profiling and source system analysis, and
  presenting clear insight to support how data gets used.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide data analysis leadership add,
  beyond profiling data and presenting insight well yourself?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
