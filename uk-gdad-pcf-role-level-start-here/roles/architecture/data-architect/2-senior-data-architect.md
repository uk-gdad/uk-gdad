# Senior data architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior data architect level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A senior data architect delivers the vision for the organisation as set by
the chief data architect — turning organisational intent into models,
standards and oversight that other architects can actually work to. You
design data models and metadata systems, help chief data architects
interpret the organisation's needs, provide oversight and advice to other
data architects producing data artefacts, design and support the management
of data dictionaries, make sure your teams work to the standards the chief
data architect has set, and work with technical architects so systems are
designed in line with the right data architecture. Nearly every named skill
grows at this level, because you're now responsible for other architects'
work, not just your own.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
modelling or standards decision — describe the actual trade-off and ask
what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Produce and compare data models across subject areas**

  Produce relevant data models across multiple subject areas, explain
  which models suit which purpose, understand industry-recognised
  modelling patterns and standards and when to apply them, and compare and
  align different data models.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data architect decide whether two data
  models from different subject areas can be aligned, or should genuinely
  stay separate?"

- [ ] **Evolve data governance, not just apply it**

  Evolve and define data governance, take responsibility for supporting
  and collaborating on wider governance, assure and integrate data
  services to meet multiple business services' needs, and work proactively
  so architecture decisions consider data properly.

  Ask AI: "What does 'evolving' data governance actually involve, as
  distinct from applying an existing governance framework well?"

- [ ] **Set data standards senior leaders actually understand**

  Create data standards for different subjects, ensuring senior leaders
  understand them, work with subject matter experts across the
  organisation on best practice, monitor compliance organisation-wide, and
  recommend how breaches should be resolved.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data architect explain a technical data
  standard to a senior leader in a way that makes them genuinely care
  about it?"

- [ ] **Design and improve metadata repositories**

  Design an appropriate metadata repository, suggest improvements to
  existing ones, understand a range of tools for storing and working with
  metadata, and advise less experienced team members.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a senior data architect that an existing
  metadata repository needs redesigning, rather than incremental
  improvement?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to problems, not just solve them**

  Make sure the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate, implement
  solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to a data problem
  involve, beyond diagnosing and fixing it yourself?"

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations with senior stakeholders**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of technical and non-technical
  stakeholders, manage both active and reactive communication, and support
  or host difficult discussions with the team or with diverse senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data architect prepare for a difficult
  conversation with a senior stakeholder who disagrees with a data
  architecture decision?"

- [ ] **Choose the right medium, and shape it for the audience**

  Understand the appropriate media to communicate findings, and shape
  communications specifically for the audience receiving them.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data architect decide whether a finding
  should be a written report, a presentation, or a short conversation?"

- [ ] **Understand the organisational impact of emerging data trends**

  Understand the impact on the organisation of emerging trends in data
  tools, analysis techniques and data usage — a step up from simply being
  aware of them.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior data architect judge whether an emerging
  data trend is genuinely relevant to their organisation, rather than
  interesting in the abstract?"

- [ ] **Contribute to strategy, not just work within it**

  Work within a strategic context and communicate how your activities meet
  strategic goals, and start contributing to developing strategy and
  policy.

  Ask AI: "What would contributing to data strategy actually involve for a
  senior data architect, as distinct from a chief data architect's role?"

- [ ] **Keep profiling data and presenting clear insight**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from data architect level:
  undertaking data profiling and source system analysis, and presenting
  clear insight to support how data gets used.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since data
  architect level, what would genuinely senior-level analysis judgement
  add beyond what's written here?"

Ask a chief data architect, or an equivalent principal colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
