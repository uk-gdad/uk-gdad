# Principal technical architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal technical architect
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal technical architect leads at the highest level and is
responsible for making sure the strategy is agreed and followed — the top
of this profession's line. You network and communicate with senior
stakeholders across organisations, proactively seek opportunities for
digital transformation, support multiple teams using best practice and
emerging technologies, inspire other architects to understand how to
deliver the organisation's goals, and take responsibility for governance,
solving complex or high-risk issues, or delivering architecture design
directly. Making architectural decisions and strategy design are the two
skills whose wording sharpens further at this level — everything else has
already reached the ceiling the framework describes for this role.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Lead architectural governance at the highest levels of risk**

  Make and guide architectural decisions of high risk and complexity,
  identify and address risks across the organisation or wider government,
  lead and evolve architectural governance and assurance, and represent it
  as part of wider governance such as legal or commercial.

  Ask AI: "What does representing architectural governance as part of
  wider legal or commercial governance actually involve in practice?"

- [ ] **Connect strategy across the organisation, or across government**

  Define and connect strategies or visions across the organisation or
  wider government, and enable their implementation by advocating for
  resources and removing blockers.

  Ask AI: "What does 'connecting' strategies across an organisation
  actually involve, as distinct from defining several separate strategies
  that happen to coexist?"

- [ ] **Keep seeing beyond your own projects to the whole organisation**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level: supporting
  wider organisational objectives, tracking emerging issues, and
  influencing colleagues across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely principal-level awareness of the organisation add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep mediating difficult architecture discussions**

  This skill also plateaus: communicating with stakeholders at every
  level, mediating difficult architectural discussions, and coaching
  others in architecture communication.

  Ask AI: "What would leading architecture communication for an entire
  organisation add, beyond mediating individual difficult discussions
  well?"

- [ ] **Keep building genuine collaboration across your team**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: networking actively, adapting
  feedback so it lasts, and using team health checks to spot and address
  issues.

  Ask AI: "What would principal-level collaboration leadership add, beyond
  building it well within one team?"

- [ ] **Keep leading technical design at high levels of risk**

  This skill also plateaus: creating technical designs of high risk and
  complexity, leading others in creating designs, and refining design
  standards throughout the life cycle.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide technical design standards add,
  beyond leading design well for individual high-risk projects?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
