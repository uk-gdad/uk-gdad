# Lead technical architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead technical architect level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A lead technical architect works with multiple projects or teams on
problems that require broad architectural thinking. You're responsible for
leading the technical design of systems and services, justifying and
communicating design decisions, assuring other services and system quality
so technical work fits into the broader strategy for government, exploring
the benefits of cross-government alignment, mentoring within teams, and
providing leadership to other architects. Almost every named skill grows at
this level, because you're now shaping direction across multiple projects,
not delivering one.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a strategic call — describe the trade-off
you're weighing across projects and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Lead technical design at high levels of risk**

  Create technical designs of high risk, impact and complexity, lead and
  guide others in creating designs that achieve organisational objectives,
  and use feedback to optimise and refine design standards throughout the
  life cycle.

  Ask AI: "What changes about technical design leadership when the risk
  and impact are genuinely high, rather than moderate?"

- [ ] **Mediate difficult architecture discussions, and coach others in it**

  Communicate with technical and non-technical stakeholders at every
  level and across organisations, mediate between people in difficult
  architectural discussions, gain support for high-risk, high-impact
  topics, and coach others in architecture communication.

  Ask AI: "What approach would help a lead technical architect mediate a
  genuine disagreement between two senior stakeholders about an
  architectural direction?"

- [ ] **See beyond your own projects to the whole organisation**

  Work to support wider organisational objectives beyond your immediate
  goals, track emerging internal and external issues that could affect
  teams across the organisation, and take action to solve or mitigate
  problems by influencing colleagues elsewhere.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead technical architect notice an emerging issue
  that affects several of their projects, when day-to-day focus is
  naturally on delivery?"

- [ ] **Make architectural decisions that affect multiple teams**

  Make and guide architectural design decisions of medium risk and
  complexity, identify and address risks affecting multiple teams or
  domains, use governance to make decisions and manage risk at the right
  level, and contribute to developing that governance.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead technical architect decide a risk affects
  multiple teams and genuinely needs escalating, rather than staying with
  one?"

- [ ] **Define strategy across teams, and direct its implementation**

  Define strategies or visions across teams that align with organisational
  objectives, direct implementation through roadmaps or plans, define
  architectural principles and patterns, and develop or maintain strategy
  in response to feedback.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between supporting a strategy's
  development and genuinely defining it across several teams?"

- [ ] **Keep building genuine collaboration across your team**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior technical
  architect level: networking actively, adapting feedback so it lasts,
  and using team health checks to spot and address issues.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level collaboration add beyond what's
  written here?"

Ask a principal technical architect which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
