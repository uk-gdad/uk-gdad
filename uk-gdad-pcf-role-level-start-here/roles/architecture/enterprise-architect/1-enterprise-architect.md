# Enterprise architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the enterprise architect level, this
page is for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

Enterprise architects are leaders working across different levels within an
organisation to translate business strategy into business change and
technical delivery. At this level, you support other, more senior architects
in developing that strategy: networking and communicating with stakeholders
across domains, using best practice and emerging technologies and
approaches, learning to identify influences or risks, and playing your part
in the team's objectives. This is a role that asks a lot even at its first
level — you're expected to see beyond your immediate goals to the wider
organisation from the start, because that's what enterprise architecture
actually is.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Turn organisational goals into architecture, with support**

  Turn an organisation's goals into clear objectives with limited
  direction, and design an architecture that defines the technology,
  people, process and other elements needed to achieve them, with support.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to enterprise architecture. Explain, simply, what
  turning an organisational goal into a clear objective actually involves,
  with an example."

- [ ] **Make architectural decisions of managed risk, with support**

  Work with others to make architectural design decisions of managed risk
  and complexity, identify and address architectural risks relevant to
  your team or domain — business, data or security — and engage with
  architectural governance and assurance with support.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between an architectural risk relevant to
  my own team and one that would need escalating to architectural
  governance?"

- [ ] **Help frame problems so a solution can be created**

  Help frame a problem of managed complexity, complication or risk so a
  solution can actually be created, and help create options for solving it
  at an appropriate level of detail.

  Ask AI: "What makes a problem well-framed for architecture work, as
  opposed to a vague statement of what's wrong?"

- [ ] **Support developing and implementing a strategy**

  Support developing a strategy or vision that aligns with organisational
  objectives, challenge requirements and assumptions to spot opportunities,
  support implementing it through a roadmap or plan, and use architectural
  principles and patterns when appropriate.

  Ask AI: "What does 'challenging requirements and assumptions' actually
  look like in a real strategy conversation, without it coming across as
  obstructive?"

- [ ] **Contribute to a healthy, well-empowered team**

  Contribute to the work of others, help motivate and empower the team,
  help create the right environment for the team to work in, and recognise
  and deal with issues.

  Ask AI: "What are some early signs of an architecture team's dynamic
  going wrong, that a team member might notice before it's raised
  formally?"

- [ ] **See beyond your own goals to the wider organisation**

  Work to support wider organisational objectives beyond your immediate
  goals, track emerging internal and external issues that could affect
  teams across the organisation, and take action to solve or mitigate
  problems by influencing colleagues elsewhere.

  Ask AI: "How would an enterprise architect notice an emerging issue that
  affects other teams, when their day-to-day focus is naturally on their
  own?"

- [ ] **Lead communication of complex architecture topics**

  Lead communicating complicated, complex or risky architecture topics
  with technical and non-technical stakeholders, communicate with senior
  stakeholders, adapt your message to your audience, advocate for your
  team, and manage expectations effectively.

  Ask AI: "How would an enterprise architect explain a genuinely complex
  architectural risk to a senior stakeholder without either
  oversimplifying it or losing them in detail?"

- [ ] **Understand the commercial context you work within**

  Understand commercial processes and the right internal contacts within a
  government department, and understand different sourcing strategies and
  when to apply them.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for someone new to enterprise architecture, why
  understanding sourcing strategies matters for architectural decisions."

Ask your manager or a more senior architect which of these your team leans on
most right now, and start there.
