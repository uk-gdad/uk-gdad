# Senior enterprise architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior enterprise architect level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A senior enterprise architect supports lead architects in making sure
strategy is developed, agreed and followed. You network and communicate with
stakeholders across domains or enterprises, identify opportunities for
improvement, support a community or team, use best practice and emerging
technologies, play an active part in delivering the team's objectives
against organisational goals, identify influences or risks, and help
deliver the long-term strategy. Several named skills grow noticeably at
this level — enterprise architecture, making architectural decisions,
problem definition and shaping, strategy design — because you're now
expected to own outcomes, not just support them.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
design decision — describe the actual trade-off and ask what you might be
missing.

- [ ] **Define strategy across teams, and direct its implementation**

  Define strategies or visions across teams that align with organisational
  objectives, direct implementation through roadmaps or plans, define
  architectural principles and patterns, and develop or maintain strategy
  in response to feedback.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between supporting a strategy's
  development and genuinely defining it across several teams?"

- [ ] **Design architecture, and make sure it actually gets used**

  Design an architecture that defines the technology, people, process and
  other elements needed for organisational objectives, and work with
  others across the organisation to make sure your design is actually
  used to achieve them.

  Ask AI: "What tends to stop a well-designed architecture from actually
  being adopted, even when the design itself is sound?"

- [ ] **Make architectural decisions of medium risk, and shape governance**

  Make and guide architectural design decisions of medium risk and
  complexity, identify and address risks that affect multiple teams or
  domains, use architectural governance to manage decisions and risks, and
  contribute to developing that governance.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior enterprise architect decide a risk affects
  multiple teams and genuinely needs escalating, rather than staying with
  one?"

- [ ] **Frame problems of medium complexity, and give teams a shared view**

  Frame a problem of medium complexity, complication or risk so a solution
  can be created, produce architectural representations that give
  different teams a shared understanding throughout the life cycle, and
  describe options so the right delivery method can be chosen.

  Ask AI: "What makes an architectural representation genuinely useful for
  giving several teams a shared understanding, rather than just a diagram
  one person understands?"

- [ ] **Build genuine collaboration across your team**

  Work collaboratively, actively networking with others, adapt feedback so
  it's effective and lasting, use team health checks to spot dynamic
  issues, and help stimulate the right response to them.

  Ask AI: "What makes feedback 'effective and lasting' rather than
  something a colleague hears once and forgets?"

- [ ] **Keep seeing beyond your own goals to the wider organisation**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from enterprise architect
  level: supporting wider organisational objectives, tracking emerging
  issues, and influencing colleagues to solve or mitigate problems.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level awareness of the wider
  organisation add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep leading communication of complex architecture topics**

  This skill also plateaus: leading communication of complex or risky
  architecture topics, communicating with senior stakeholders, and
  adapting your message to the audience.

  Ask AI: "What would leading architecture communication across several
  teams add, beyond communicating complex topics well within one?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding the commercial context you work within**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: understanding commercial processes,
  the right internal contacts, and different sourcing strategies.

  Ask AI: "What would deepening commercial awareness, beyond understanding
  processes and contacts, look like at the next level?"

Ask a lead enterprise architect, or an equivalent principal colleague,
which of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start
there.
