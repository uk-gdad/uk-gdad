# Lead network architect - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead network architect level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead network architect is accountable for the designs and networks
representing their business area — the top of this profession's line. You
act as a point of escalation, actively input into the governance forums that
determine IT strategy, cascade messages, standards and best practice from
other architecture teams to the network architecture team, act as a subject
matter expert for network architects, represent network architects
internally and externally, understand every common network technology
rather than most of them, champion new technologies and solutions, coach
and mentor network architects and associates, and communicate network
concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders as a strategic
thinker.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real escalation or strategic decision you're weighing and ask it to argue
the case you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Shape system design practice at enterprise scale**

  Design systems characterised by high levels of risk, impact and business
  or technical complexity, control system design practice within an
  enterprise or industry architecture, influence industry-based models for
  new technology applications, develop effective implementation and
  procurement strategies, and ensure adherence to relevant technical
  strategies, policies and standards.

  Ask AI: "What changes about network design judgement when you're
  operating at enterprise or industry scale, rather than designing one
  network in isolation?"

- [ ] **Lead teams and departments in infrastructure delivery**

  Lead teams and departments in the design, implementation, administration
  and support of infrastructure solutions and services.

  Ask AI: "What does leading infrastructure delivery across teams and
  departments involve, that goes beyond designing and implementing
  infrastructure well yourself?"

- [ ] **Make governance proportionate, and trusted**

  Make sure governance and assurance is appropriate and proportionate,
  continuously improved and aligned to organisational and government
  needs, guide teams through processes such as service assessments, and
  share your approach so stakeholders trust it to deliver value
  sustainably.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proportionate governance' actually mean for network
  architecture, as opposed to governance that's either too heavy or too
  light for the risk involved?"

- [ ] **Keep coordinating integration across systems**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from network architect
  level: defining the integration build, co-ordinating build activities,
  and supporting integration testing across the whole effort.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level integration judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep diagnosing root causes, not just symptoms**

  This skill also plateaus: breaking a problem down into component parts
  to diagnose root causes, and troubleshooting across different technology
  capabilities.

  Ask AI: "What kind of network problem would genuinely need a lead
  architect's involvement, rather than being handled well by a network
  architect?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
