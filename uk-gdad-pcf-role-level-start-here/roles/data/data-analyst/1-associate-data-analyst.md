# Associate data analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are starting out as an associate data analyst, this page is
for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for,
one step at a time.

## Purpose

A data analyst collects, manages, explores and shares data to support
organisational objectives and deliver real business impact. As an
associate, you support data analysis activities and projects, developing
your skills by working closely with other data professionals: developing
analytical approaches and visualisations under supervision, and learning
how data analysts work alongside the rest of a multidisciplinary team.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation's data, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Understand why coding for analysis matters**

  Explain the importance of coding for analysis, explain the value of
  maintaining documentation of your analytical approach, and design a
  simple analytical approach with support, including writing simple code.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to data analysis. Explain, simply, why writing code for
  analysis is more reliable than doing the same work by hand in a
  spreadsheet."

- [ ] **Learn to prepare and cleanse data under supervision**

  Explain the importance of data models and their uses, identify the right
  channels in your organisation to learn about modelling and quality
  standards, identify data quality issues and suggest possible solutions,
  and prepare and cleanse data under supervision.

  Ask AI: "What does 'preparing and cleansing data' actually involve, and
  why does a small data quality issue matter enough to fix before
  analysis?"

- [ ] **Support statistical and analytical work under supervision**

  Support developing statistical and analytical insights and reports under
  supervision, explain the benefits of basic statistical and analytical
  techniques, and explain why quality assurance and best practice matter
  in producing these outputs.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what quality assurance means for a
  statistical or analytical output, with an example of what could go
  wrong without it."

- [ ] **Develop simple data visualisations under supervision**

  Develop and use an appropriate data visualisation under supervision to
  tell a meaningful story relevant to a set goal, explain the value of
  sharing a story effectively, and explain why inclusive, accessible
  visualisations matter.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data visualisation genuinely inclusive and
  accessible, rather than just visually clear to most people?"

- [ ] **Understand a data project's problem statement and outcome**

  Understand a problem statement and explain the intended outcome of a
  data project, explain the importance of data in solving the problem, and
  explain why adapting your ways of working matters for different project
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate data analyst work out what a data
  project's problem statement is actually asking for, if it's written
  vaguely?"

- [ ] **Understand how data delivers business impact**

  Explain why understanding the organisation's priorities matters,
  contribute to developing data products that deliver business impact, and
  explain how to align data and analysis with business requirements.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate data analyst find out what the
  organisation's actual priorities are, if nobody's explained them
  directly?"

- [ ] **Understand data governance, and how tools relate to a project**

  Explain the importance of data governance policies, and explain how data
  management tools, procedures and methods relate to a specific project.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what data governance means in
  practice, with a concrete example of a policy it might set."

- [ ] **Understand why data ethics and privacy matter**

  Explain the importance of using data ethics and privacy in your work,
  and identify the right channels to discuss ethical issues, with support.

  Ask AI: "What's an example of a data ethics concern an associate data
  analyst might notice in their day-to-day work, and who should they raise
  it with?"

- [ ] **Communicate technical concepts in plain language**

  Explain why it's important to communicate technical concepts in
  non-technical language, and explain the different ways internal and
  external stakeholders need to be communicated with.

  Ask AI: "Give me a technical data concept, and show me two ways to
  explain it — one for a data specialist and one for someone with no
  technical background."

You do not need to master all of this before you start. Ask your manager or
a more senior colleague which of these your team leans on most, and start
there.
