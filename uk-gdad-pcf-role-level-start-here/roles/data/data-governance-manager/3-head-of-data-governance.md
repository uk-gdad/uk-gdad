# Head of data governance - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of data governance level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

The head of data governance works with the chief data officer across the
organisation and government, providing strategic oversight and leadership
in every aspect of data governance and use, and making sure high standards
are maintained in line with regulation and ethical requirements — the top
of this profession's line. You provide strategic oversight of governance,
set and monitor adherence to policies and standards, take accountability
for organisational data risk, act as a senior advisor across government on
data security best practice, maintain long-term relationships with senior
decision-makers, and advocate for better data risk management, literacy and
understanding across the organisation.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Set the data governance strategy, connected to the wider strategy**

  Define the data governance strategy for the organisation and connect it
  with the wider organisational strategy, design and monitor governance
  throughout the data life cycle in a large organisation, set the strategy
  for end-to-end frameworks and compliance, and engage senior stakeholders
  to implement it.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance connect a data governance
  strategy to the organisation's wider strategic goals, rather than
  keeping the two separate?"

- [ ] **Own governance structures across the whole data life cycle**

  Take responsibility for data governance structures and principles,
  ensure they're defined and applied with best practice throughout the
  data life cycle, and direct continuous improvements to optimise
  governance over it.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide ownership of data life cycle
  governance add, beyond optimising it well for one area?"

- [ ] **Set data standards across the organisation and government**

  Create data standards for the organisation, advocate for and oversee
  compliance with them, and decide where standards need setting across the
  organisation and in the wider context of government.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance decide when a data standard
  should be set at organisational level versus pushed for adoption across
  government?"

- [ ] **Oversee data maturity, and report to boards**

  Oversee implementing the organisation's data maturity model, work with
  senior stakeholders to provide data assurance, report assessment
  findings to boards or committees, and communicate improvement plans.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance present a data maturity
  assessment to a board in a way that leads to a real decision, not just
  acknowledgement?"

- [ ] **Champion and lead data management across the organisation**

  Champion and lead data management and governance processes within your
  team and across the organisation, define and lead implementing data
  management responsibilities, define tools and training requirements, and
  enable continuous improvement of practices and compliance.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance get data management
  genuinely embedded across an organisation, rather than followed only
  where it's actively enforced?"

- [ ] **Persuade senior decision-makers that data literacy matters**

  Take responsibility for improving the organisation's data literacy,
  encourage a data-driven culture by strengthening literacy skills among
  all employees, and persuade senior decision-makers that data literacy
  enables effective decision-making at every level.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance persuade a senior
  decision-maker who sees data literacy as a nice-to-have, not a
  priority?"

- [ ] **Horizon-scan for the organisation, and introduce new ways of working**

  Investigate emerging trends in data-related approaches, perform
  horizon-scanning for the organisation, and introduce genuinely
  innovative ways of working.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance distinguish a trend worth
  introducing organisation-wide from one that's interesting but not yet
  proven?"

- [ ] **Be the trusted point of escalation for the hardest risk decisions**

  Act as a point of escalation, be trusted by senior risk owners as an
  expert, and apply risk methodologies at the most complex levels of risk.

  Ask AI: "What builds the kind of trust that makes senior risk owners
  escalate a decision to a head of data governance rather than deciding it
  themselves?"

- [ ] **Lead the design and implementation of strategy**

  Lead the design and implementation of strategy, directing the evaluation
  of strategies and policies to make sure business requirements are being
  met.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between contributing to a governance
  strategy and genuinely leading its design and implementation?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and
  negotiate with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable
  progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior stakeholder
  who has the authority to block a governance decision but no obligation
  to compromise?"

- [ ] **Communicate compelling, complex recommendations**

  Communicate relevant, compelling stories using the most appropriate
  medium, and make complex recommendations at both a tactical and a
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance tailor the same complex
  recommendation differently for a tactical audience and a strategic
  one?"

- [ ] **Represent the community to large audiences**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships, adopt the right
  communication method for stakeholders at every level, moderate difficult
  discussions about high-risk, complex topics under real time pressure,
  and speak on behalf of the community to large audiences inside and
  outside the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data governance prepare to represent their
  community to a large, mostly non-technical audience?"

- [ ] **Own compliance and culture for data ethics across the organisation**

  Oversee compliance with data ethics standards and legislation across the
  organisation, develop and continuously improve an ethical framework,
  build a data ethics culture that influences how others actually act, and
  make sure ethics is applied appropriately when policies and programmes
  are formulated, implemented and evaluated.

  Ask AI: "What builds a genuine data ethics culture across an
  organisation, rather than one that only shows up in compliance
  checklists?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
