# Lead analytics engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead analytics engineer level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead analytics engineer leads the design and deployment of data models
for analysis. You lead and support a team of analytics engineers, work with
stakeholders and teams across the organisation to understand relationships
between data and organisational processes, promote and build understanding
of analytics engineering, review the work of other analytics engineers,
create standards for communication, models and documentation, and define
and improve how the team works. Communicating, data analysis, data
innovation and problem management all sharpen at this level, because
leading a team means your judgement now shapes what other analytics
engineers do, not just your own output.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a team or standards decision — describe the
actual situation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations with senior stakeholders**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of technical and non-technical
  stakeholders, manage active and reactive communication, and support or
  host difficult discussions with the team or with diverse senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead analytics engineer prepare for a difficult
  conversation with a senior stakeholder who disagrees with a modelling
  decision?"

- [ ] **Bring multiple data sources together for analysis**

  Help teams apply a range of data profiling techniques, carry out source
  system analysis from a complex single source, and bring multiple data
  sources together in a conformed model for analysis.

  Ask AI: "What makes combining several data sources into one conformed
  model genuinely harder than modelling one source well?"

- [ ] **Judge when to adopt data innovation, and when to wait**

  Identify areas of innovation in data tools and techniques, and recognise
  the appropriate timing for adopting them.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead analytics engineer decide an emerging data
  tool is ready for adoption, rather than still too immature to depend
  on?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to problems, not just solve them**

  Make sure the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate problems,
  implement solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to a data problem
  involve, beyond diagnosing and fixing it yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep building and reviewing complex data models**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior analytics
  engineer level: building and reviewing complex models, integrating and
  storing data, and improving modelling patterns with other data
  professionals.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level modelling judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep designing and improving metadata repositories**

  This skill also plateaus: designing appropriate repositories, suggesting
  improvements, and advising less experienced team members.

  Ask AI: "What would leading metadata strategy across several teams add,
  beyond designing and improving one repository well?"

- [ ] **Keep designing and building programs of real complexity**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: designing, coding and documenting
  moderate-to-complex programs from agreed specifications, and reviewing
  specifications with others.

  Ask AI: "What would leading programming standards across a team add,
  beyond writing moderate-to-complex programs well yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep defining test conditions, and analysing results**

  This skill also plateaus: reviewing requirements to define test
  conditions, identifying issues and risks, and analysing test results.

  Ask AI: "What would leading testing practice across a team add, beyond
  defining and analysing test conditions well yourself?"

Ask a head of analytics engineering, or an equivalent principal colleague,
which of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start
there.
