# Data engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the data engineer level, this page is
for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A data engineer develops and constructs data products and services, and
integrates them into systems and business processes. At this level, you
deliver designs set by more senior members of the data engineering
community: implementing data flows that connect operational systems to
analytics and business intelligence systems, documenting source-to-target
mappings, re-engineering manual flows so they scale and repeat reliably,
supporting the build of data streaming systems, writing the extract,
transform and load scripts that keep a pipeline performing well, developing
reusable business intelligence reports, and building data that's genuinely
accessible for analysis.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation's data pipelines, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Write and document simple programs under direction**

  Design, code, test, correct and document simple programs or scripts
  under the direction of others — the whole small cycle, not just the
  coding part.

  Ask AI: "Walk me through the full cycle of writing a simple data
  pipeline script: designing it, coding it, testing it, and documenting
  it, so I don't skip a step."

- [ ] **Design and build data products from multiple feeds**

  Design, build and test data products based on feeds from multiple
  systems, using a range of storage technologies or access methods, and
  create products that are repeatable and reusable.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data product genuinely reusable by another team
  later, rather than something only its original builder understands?"

- [ ] **Deliver resilient, scalable data solutions to standard**

  Deliver data solutions in line with agreed organisational standards that
  keep services resilient, scalable and future-proof.

  Ask AI: "Explain, simply, what makes a data integration solution
  'future-proof', with a concrete example of a design choice that helps."

- [ ] **Build and maintain data models for real organisational needs**

  Explain the concepts and principles of data modelling, produce, maintain
  and update data models for your organisation's specific needs, and
  reverse-engineer models from a live system when no model already exists.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for someone new to data engineering, what a data model
  actually is and why reverse-engineering one from a live system is often
  necessary."

- [ ] **Use metadata to support complex analysis**

  Use metadata repositories to complete complex tasks such as data and
  systems integration impact analysis, and maintain a repository to keep
  information accurate and current.

  Ask AI: "Explain, simply, what a metadata repository is for and why
  keeping it accurate matters for tasks like integration impact analysis."

- [ ] **Define test conditions, and analyse results**

  Review requirements and specifications to define test conditions,
  identify issues and risks associated with the work, and analyse and
  report on test activities and results.

  Ask AI: "What makes a test condition genuinely well-defined, as opposed
  to a vague check that something 'looks right'?"

- [ ] **Investigate and resolve data problems**

  Investigate problems in systems, processes and services with an
  understanding of what level a problem sits at — strategic, tactical or
  operational — and contribute to implementing remedies and preventative
  measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a strategic, a tactical and an
  operational data engineering problem, with an example of each?"

- [ ] **Profile data, and present clear insight**

  Undertake data profiling and source system analysis, and present clear
  insights to colleagues to support how the data actually gets used.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data profiling finding genuinely useful to a
  colleague who isn't a data specialist, rather than just technically
  accurate?"

- [ ] **Stay aware of innovation in data tools and uses**

  Show an awareness of opportunities for innovation with new tools and
  uses of data, without needing to champion their adoption yet.

  Ask AI: "How would a data engineer stay aware of emerging data tools and
  techniques without it becoming a full-time pursuit alongside their
  actual job?"

- [ ] **Communicate technical concepts in plain language**

  Explain why it's important to communicate technical concepts in
  non-technical language, and explain the different ways internal and
  external stakeholders need to be communicated with.

  Ask AI: "Give me a technical data engineering concept, and show me two
  ways to explain it — one for a data specialist and one for someone with
  no technical background."

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
