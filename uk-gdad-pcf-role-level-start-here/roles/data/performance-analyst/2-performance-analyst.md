# Performance analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the performance analyst level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A performance analyst has a strong understanding of the sources and
analytical tools needed for the project they're working on. You're
responsible for performance analysis in your area, help the project team
measure and assess outcomes, help plan and ensure delivery, share
information across the project team and community, work independently
while knowing when to seek guidance, adapt your techniques to new contexts
— especially when you're the sole performance analyst on a project — build
a performance measurement framework with the wider team, and stay
responsible for the accuracy and quality of your data and analysis. Most
named skills grow at this level, because you're now the one producing real
outputs, not replicating existing analysis.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own project.

- [ ] **Apply a range of techniques, and present compelling findings**

  Understand and help teams apply a range of techniques to analyse data
  and provide insight, be proactive in presenting compelling findings that
  inform wider decisions, and apply innovative approaches to resolve
  problems.

  Ask AI: "What makes a finding 'compelling' enough to inform a wider
  decision, rather than just technically correct?"

- [ ] **Turn business needs into performance measures, with guidance**

  Participate in discussions determining which performance measurements
  are appropriate, with guidance from more experienced colleagues, and
  demonstrate experience turning business needs and goals into measures.

  Ask AI: "How would a performance analyst turn a vague business goal into
  a measurable performance indicator?"

- [ ] **Manage delivery across product life cycle phases**

  Understand how the needs of the team and product vary across the life
  cycle, make sure the team measures performance appropriate to the
  relevant service standard phase, manage delivery at different phases,
  and plan for future data requirements.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a performance analyst that the team's
  performance measurement needs to change because the product has moved
  to a new phase?"

- [ ] **Take ownership of preparing data, and peer-review outputs**

  Identify appropriate ways to collect, collate and prepare data, decide
  whether data and analysis are accurate and fit for purpose, perform data
  preparation and cleansing with limited guidance, undertake quality
  assurance processes, and peer-review colleagues' outputs.

  Ask AI: "What should a performance analyst look for when peer-reviewing
  a colleague's data analysis, beyond checking the numbers add up?"

- [ ] **Apply core technical concepts to your role**

  Show an understanding of the core technical concepts related to your
  role, and apply them in your work.

  Ask AI: "What core technical concepts does a performance analyst need to
  apply confidently, beyond just being aware they exist?"

- [ ] **Turn user data into outcomes, and collaborate with researchers**

  Use quantitative and qualitative data about users to turn user focus
  into outcomes, deliver analysis that addresses stakeholder needs with
  recommendations, collaborate effectively with user researchers and other
  user-focused professions, and guide others in understanding user
  stories.

  Ask AI: "How would a performance analyst combine quantitative data with
  qualitative user research to reach a conclusion neither could reach
  alone?"

- [ ] **Keep communicating analysis clearly, with support**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from associate level:
  choosing the right medium, shaping communications for the audience, and
  giving tactical recommendations.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would taking more ownership of communicating findings look
  like at the next level?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
