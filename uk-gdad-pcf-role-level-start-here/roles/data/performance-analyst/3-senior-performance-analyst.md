# Senior performance analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior performance analyst level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A senior performance analyst looks to delegate, automate or upskill others
in analysis where possible, provides leadership within their team, and
proactively uses analysis to improve services. You might manage one or more
performance analysts, stay proficient across a wide range of tools, make
sure your team has the skills the head of performance analysis's strategy
calls for, carry out the day-to-day work of the role skilfully — including
setting up measurement frameworks — take on increasingly complex analysis
working with other teams, and actively contribute to the performance
analysis community, forging links with other analytical disciplines. Some
organisations use a "technical specialist" variant of this level, developing
tools and techniques that help other analysts. Most named skills grow at
this level, because leadership and complexity both raise the bar.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing a
framework or delegation decision — describe the actual situation and ask
what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Lead performance measurement framework development**

  Lead developing performance measurement frameworks including key
  performance indicators, demonstrating a deep understanding of business
  goals and turning them into tangible measures.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior performance analyst decide which key
  performance indicators genuinely reflect a business goal, rather than
  just being easy to measure?"

- [ ] **Own data readiness, and communicate its limitations honestly**

  Set up a system to get data ready for use, specify how data should be
  cleansed and prepared, bring data together from different sources, and
  communicate the limitations of the data honestly.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior performance analyst communicate a data
  limitation to a stakeholder without undermining confidence in the whole
  analysis?"

- [ ] **Understand the wider technical landscape, and its limitations**

  Show a deep understanding of the technical concepts required for the
  role and how they fit into the wider technical landscape, and understand
  the limitations of digital technology.

  Ask AI: "What limitations of digital technology should a senior
  performance analyst be upfront about when presenting an analysis based
  on it?"

- [ ] **Apply experience across the whole product life cycle**

  Apply experience in multiple parts of the product life cycle, recognise
  when to move forward and when to stop, recognise the right deliverables
  and engage the right people, work with other Agile disciplines
  throughout, and identify which tools and techniques fit each stage.

  Ask AI: "What evidence would genuinely justify recommending a project
  stop, as opposed to evidence that just makes stopping feel justified?"

- [ ] **Integrate quantitative and qualitative user data**

  Understand the difference between user needs and user desires,
  demonstrate experience meeting needs across a variety of channels,
  integrate digital analytics with qualitative data such as surveys and
  research to develop testable hypotheses, and recommend the best tools
  and methods.

  Ask AI: "What's the practical difference between what users need and
  what users say they want, and how would a senior analyst use both kinds
  of data together?"

- [ ] **Turn complex data into compelling, actionable stories**

  Turn complex data into compelling, clear and actionable stories, share
  data communication skills with your team and across government, present
  complex messages clearly, communicate both negative and positive
  information honestly, and give recommendations at both tactical and
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data story genuinely actionable, rather than just
  clear and well presented?"

- [ ] **Keep applying a range of techniques, and presenting compelling findings**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from performance analyst
  level: applying a range of techniques, presenting compelling findings,
  and applying innovative approaches to resolve problems.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level analytical judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

Ask a lead performance analyst, or an equivalent principal colleague,
which of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start
there.
