# Data ethics lead - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the data ethics lead level, this page is
for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.
The framework publishes only two levels for this role, and this first one
already reads at a genuinely senior register — this is not a role most
people arrive at without other experience already behind them.

## Purpose

A data ethicist assesses the societal effect of technology and data, and
produces recommendations for other data professionals — thinking about
fairness, accountability, the law, moral dilemmas and risk in how
technology, data products and policies get built. You provide research and
expertise on data ethics, enable others to implement best practice through
training, advice and worked examples, communicate to raise awareness and to
listen, convene, advise and mediate across the organisation, and help
people ask questions, voice concerns and discuss genuine ethical dilemmas.
As a data ethics lead, you support the head of data ethics in enabling
people across the organisation to understand data ethics and put it into
practice.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful for pressure-testing an ethical judgement — describe the actual
situation and ask what you might be missing or whose perspective you
haven't considered.

- [ ] **Work with stakeholders to identify and address ethical concerns**

  Work with stakeholders to identify and address ethical and privacy
  concerns, demonstrate and communicate how they fit into the wider
  organisational context, research developments in data ethics and
  privacy, and constructively challenge proposed data ethics policies.

  Ask AI: "What does 'constructively challenging' a proposed data ethics
  policy look like in practice, without it coming across as obstructive?"

- [ ] **Turn research and data into findings that inform decisions**

  Draw together, analyse and evaluate qualitative and quantitative
  information, quickly read and distil complex documents from a range of
  sources, turn research into clear findings, involve colleagues to build
  consensus and challenge assumptions, and help teams integrate ethical
  diagnostics into their project outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What makes an ethical finding genuinely actionable for a
  project team, rather than an accurate but abstract observation?"

- [ ] **Apply social science theory to real data projects**

  Explain social sciences such as anthropology, economics, sociology,
  philosophy, psychology or race theory, apply their theories to inform
  data projects and policies, evaluate and challenge assumptions made in
  data science work, and work with academics and external researchers.

  Ask AI: "How would a data ethics lead use a social science concept like
  algorithmic bias or structural inequality to challenge an assumption in
  a real data project?"

- [ ] **Translate technical concepts for non-technical audiences**

  Expertly translate technical concepts to non-technical audiences,
  demonstrate a good understanding of how technology and data products get
  built, hold meaningful conversations with data science experts on issues
  such as minimising bias, and support data scientists and engineers in
  implementing data ethics directly.

  Ask AI: "What does a data ethics lead need to understand technically
  about how data gets collected, cleansed and reused, without needing to
  be able to build the pipeline themselves?"

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations across the organisation**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of technical and non-technical
  stakeholders, manage active and reactive communication, and support or
  host difficult discussions with the team or with diverse senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a data ethics lead prepare for a difficult
  conversation with a senior stakeholder who disagrees with an ethical
  concern being raised?"

- [ ] **Work with complex risk, and build consensus**

  Work with consequential or complex risks, build consensus between
  services or independent stakeholders, lead others toward good design
  decisions, and apply risk methodologies in proportion to the risk
  involved.

  Ask AI: "How would a data ethics lead build genuine consensus between
  two stakeholders who see an ethical trade-off completely differently?"

- [ ] **Turn ethical principles into practical tools**

  Develop data ethics tools that translate theoretical principles into
  practice, apply product management principles and approaches, adapt to
  new ways of working, capture and translate user needs into deliverables,
  and gather feedback to evaluate and promote what you build.

  Ask AI: "What would a practical data ethics tool actually look like for
  a project team, as opposed to a policy document nobody reads day to
  day?"

- [ ] **Own stakeholder understanding across your team**

  Work with your team to develop and maintain a shared understanding of
  stakeholders, develop and implement communications strategies, identify
  and resolve issues, and build long-term relationships.

  Ask AI: "What does a genuine stakeholder communications strategy contain
  for data ethics work, beyond a list of who to update and how often?"

- [ ] **Bring underrepresented perspectives into your work**

  Incorporate views from underrepresented groups into product and policy
  work through genuine consulting and outreach, engage with the
  organisation's diversity and inclusion plan, and draw on a
  multidisciplinary understanding of bias and discrimination to inform
  your data ethics work.

  Ask AI: "How would a data ethics lead genuinely consult an
  underrepresented group about a data product, rather than consulting
  them as an afterthought?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to ethical problems**

  Make sure the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate them,
  implement solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to an ethical problem
  involve, beyond identifying and raising it yourself?"

Ask the head of data ethics, or an equivalent senior colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
