# Lead digital evaluator - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead digital evaluator level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead digital evaluator plans and leads evaluation activities in larger
teams and for more complex services, usually managing multiple evaluations
and often leading a team of digital evaluators. You influence stakeholders
to secure resources for evaluation, provide guidance on strategy for a
group of digital products and services, and make sure high evaluation
standards are set and met across the wider team. Data ethics, monitoring
across the product life cycle, product and service monitoring, quality
assurance and communication all sharpen at this level, because you're now
setting the standard other evaluators work to, not just meeting it
yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a standards or resourcing decision —
describe the actual situation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Own compliance and culture for data ethics across the organisation**

  Oversee compliance with data ethics standards and legislation across the
  organisation, develop and continuously improve an ethical framework,
  build a data ethics culture that influences how others actually act, and
  make sure ethics is applied appropriately when policies and programmes
  are formulated, implemented and evaluated.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead digital evaluator get data ethics genuinely
  embedded across evaluation practice, rather than followed only where
  it's actively enforced?"

- [ ] **Apply evaluation techniques across the whole product life cycle**

  Apply monitoring and evaluation techniques in multiple parts of the
  product life cycle, recommend when to move forward and when to stop,
  work with other Agile delivery disciplines throughout, and plan and
  proactively engage stakeholders at the right stage.

  Ask AI: "What evidence would genuinely justify recommending a project
  stop, as opposed to evidence that just makes stopping feel justified?"

- [ ] **Get senior support for theories of change, and set best practice**

  Get support from senior stakeholders for developing theories of change,
  provide guidance on how to develop them, and define best practice and
  methodologies for measuring indicators, including economic outcomes, and
  communicating their limitations.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead digital evaluator persuade a senior
  stakeholder who sees a theory of change as an unnecessary extra step?"

- [ ] **Set up multi-team quality assurance, and coach others in it**

  Set up tools and systems for effectively assuring data and analysis
  across multiple teams, use appropriate approaches for verifying and
  validating data, influence senior stakeholders over data and analysis
  approaches, and coach and mentor others in assuring data and analysis.

  Ask AI: "What does multi-team data quality assurance actually require,
  beyond doing quality checks well within one team?"

- [ ] **Communicate compelling, complex recommendations**

  Communicate relevant, compelling stories using the most appropriate
  medium, and make complex recommendations at both a tactical and a
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead digital evaluator tailor the same complex
  recommendation differently for a tactical audience and a strategic
  one?"

- [ ] **Keep evaluating digital services with a wide technique range**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  evaluating digital services using appropriate techniques, and using a
  wide range of analytical approaches.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely lead-level evaluation judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep securing resources, and agreeing evaluation approach**

  This skill also plateaus: influencing stakeholders to secure resources,
  identifying evaluation needs, and agreeing the approach with diverse
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What would leading resourcing decisions across a group of
  products add, beyond securing resources well for one evaluation?"

Ask a head of digital evaluation, or an equivalent principal colleague,
which of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start
there.
