# Senior digital evaluator - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior digital evaluator level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A senior digital evaluator manages the evaluations of products or services,
making sure they follow government guidance and meet standards. You work in
a multidisciplinary team, develop theories of change and evaluation plans
with stakeholders, manage relationships with stakeholders, user researchers
and performance analysts, collect and analyse data through research, assure
the work of other evaluators, and identify the resources evaluation needs.
Most named skills grow at this level, because the qualifiers that softened
earlier expectations — "with support", "some approaches" — mostly drop away.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is often most useful for pressure-testing
an evaluation approach — describe the actual trade-off and ask what you
might be missing.

- [ ] **Evaluate digital services independently, with a wide technique range**

  Evaluate digital services using appropriate techniques including process
  evaluation, impact evaluation, theory of change, experimentation and
  economic evaluation, collect and analyse data from multiple sources, and
  use a wide range of analytical approaches.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior evaluator choose between process evaluation
  and impact evaluation for a specific digital service, rather than
  defaulting to one?"

- [ ] **Secure resources, and agree evaluation approach with stakeholders**

  Influence stakeholders to secure resources for an evaluation project,
  identify evaluation and evidence needs, make sure plans are appropriate
  and proportionate, consider wider policy and practical constraints, and
  agree the evaluation approach with diverse stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior evaluator make the case for the resources an
  evaluation genuinely needs, to a stakeholder who wants it done more
  cheaply?"

- [ ] **Develop theories of change, and build measurement frameworks**

  Develop a theory of change for a product or service through discussion
  with stakeholders, turn theory of change indicators into measurement
  frameworks, and work with performance analysts to build a dashboard
  reporting service analytics and other indicators.

  Ask AI: "What makes a measurement framework genuinely derived from a
  theory of change, rather than a list of whatever's easiest to measure?"

- [ ] **Own data readiness, and communicate its limitations honestly**

  Set up a system to get data ready for use, specify how data should be
  cleansed and prepared, bring data together from different sources, and
  communicate the limitations of the data honestly.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior evaluator communicate a data limitation to a
  stakeholder without undermining confidence in the whole evaluation?"

- [ ] **Turn complex data into compelling, actionable stories**

  Turn complex data into compelling, clear and actionable stories, share
  data communication skills with your team and across government, present
  complex messages clearly, communicate both negative and positive
  information honestly, and give recommendations at both tactical and
  strategic level.

  Ask AI: "What makes a data story genuinely actionable, rather than just
  clear and well presented?"

- [ ] **Address ethical and privacy concerns, and challenge policy**

  Work with stakeholders to identify and address ethical and privacy
  concerns, communicate how ethical issues fit into the wider
  organisational context, research developments in data ethics, and
  constructively challenge proposed data ethics policies.

  Ask AI: "What does 'constructively challenging' a proposed data ethics
  policy look like in practice, without it coming across as obstructive?"

- [ ] **Keep managing evaluation delivery across product life cycle phases**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from digital evaluator
  level: making sure the team measures performance appropriate to the
  phase, and managing evaluation delivery and future data requirements.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level life cycle judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

Ask a lead digital evaluator, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
