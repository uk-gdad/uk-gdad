# Head of data science - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of data science level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

The head of data science has complete oversight of data science within
their organisation — the top of this profession's line. You set direction,
build capability, oversee resourcing, budgeting, professionalism and
outputs, support future IT developments, understand and use a wide range of
data science techniques, lead on ethics, communicate data science and data
ethics effectively to ministers and senior leaders, champion the role of
data science in supporting organisational priorities and cross-profession
working, and represent the department on data science matters. Four skills
carry the same demanding wording as lead level — applied maths, data
ethics, delivering business impact, and developing capability — while data
engineering, data science innovation, programming and understanding product
delivery read more simply here than at lead level. That's the framework's
own choice, and it fits the role: at this level, technical depth sits with
the team you lead, while oversight, direction and representation are yours.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Use advanced techniques to support organisational objectives**

  Identify opportunities to develop statistical insight and models that
  support organisational objectives, critique statistical analyses, use
  techniques such as data mining and predictive analytics through the
  whole data life cycle, and use model outputs to help design services and
  policies.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data science decide which statistical
  finding is significant enough to shape a service or policy decision?"

- [ ] **Own compliance and culture for data ethics across the organisation**

  Oversee compliance with data ethics standards and legislation across the
  organisation, develop and continuously improve an ethical framework,
  build a data ethics culture that influences how others actually act, and
  make sure ethics is applied appropriately when policies and programmes
  are formulated, implemented and evaluated.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data science communicate a genuinely
  difficult data ethics trade-off to ministers or senior leaders?"

- [ ] **Champion the role of data science, and take responsibility for products**

  Champion the role of data science within the organisation, understand
  and champion user research, identify and create opportunities to
  develop products in support of meaningful goals, and take responsibility
  for delivering scalable products with proper maintenance support.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of data science make the case that data
  science should shape a departmental priority, to leaders who don't see
  it as central to their work?"

- [ ] **Lead capability building, and develop deep specialism across the community**

  Act as a leader or technical specialist providing detailed support and
  guidance, set the direction of continuous professional development, keep
  up to date with developments and match them to organisational
  opportunities, and demonstrate in-depth understanding of a wide range of
  techniques plus deep knowledge of at least one specialism.

  Ask AI: "What would building data science capability across an entire
  department add, beyond developing it well within one team?"

- [ ] **Stay involved in engineering collaboration, at oversight level**

  Work with data engineers and data scientists to design and provide
  products for the organisation — the wording here is simpler than at
  lead level, reflecting that hands-on engineering depth now sits with
  the team you lead rather than with you directly.

  Ask AI: "What does a head of data science need to stay across in data
  engineering, to make good oversight decisions without doing the work
  themselves?"

- [ ] **Champion practical data science innovation**

  Demonstrate practical knowledge of data science tools and techniques,
  develop solutions that maximise insight, and identify opportunities for
  data science to improve data practices — again, simpler wording than at
  lead level, because deep technical coaching now sits with your team.

  Ask AI: "How does a head of data science stay credible on innovation
  without being the most technically hands-on person in the room?"

- [ ] **Keep a working knowledge of scripting and shared codebases**

  Write and test scripts and create basic models in one or more
  languages, and collaborate on shared codebases — a simpler expectation
  than at lead level, reflecting where your time is now actually spent.

  Ask AI: "How does a head of data science stay technically credible with
  their team without doing hands-on coding as a regular part of the job?"

- [ ] **Choose delivery methods, and define the minimum viable product**

  Understand the differences between delivery methods such as Agile and
  waterfall and choose the most appropriate one, define the minimum
  viable product and support priority decisions, and work with
  specialists in multidisciplinary teams to deliver smoothly — again
  simpler than the lead-level version, which asks for leading a team
  through the whole delivery life cycle.

  Ask AI: "What oversight role should a head of data science play in
  delivery method decisions that individual teams and their leads are
  best placed to make themselves?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
