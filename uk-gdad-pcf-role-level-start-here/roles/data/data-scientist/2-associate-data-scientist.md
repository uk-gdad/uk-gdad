# Associate data scientist - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the associate data scientist level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

An associate data scientist is still inquisitive, curious about data and
keen to learn, but now understands the basics of software development and
analytical methods well enough to build basic models, implement a range of
data science techniques including machine learning, collaborate on real
solutions, prepare and manipulate data, perform analytics, and present and
communicate effectively. Every named skill grows at this level, because
you're now producing real outputs rather than building foundational
awareness.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own data.

- [ ] **Write and test scripts, and build basic models**

  Write and test scripts and create basic models in one or more languages,
  and collaborate on shared codebases using a variety of methodologies.

  Ask AI: "What does collaborating well on a shared codebase actually
  involve for a data scientist, beyond just writing code that runs?"

- [ ] **Apply analytical methods to reach reliable conclusions**

  Apply analytical methods including exploratory data analysis and
  statistical testing to a specific dataset, use different performance and
  accuracy metrics for model validation, compare statistical methods, and
  access the statistical tools your organisation provides.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate data scientist choose the right
  statistical test for a specific question, rather than defaulting to the
  one they know best?"

- [ ] **Identify engineering support, and use data manipulation tools**

  Identify the right engineering support for a product, recognise
  processes and tools for cleansing and preparing data and put reusable
  checks in place, and use different architectures and data manipulation
  tools.

  Ask AI: "What would tell an associate data scientist that a data
  cleansing process is worth making reusable, rather than a one-off fix?"

- [ ] **Collaborate to identify needs, and deliver data science products**

  Show an understanding of the organisation and the benefits of data
  science, collaborate to identify user needs and develop and deliver data
  science products, and communicate effectively, presenting analysis and
  visualisations tailored to your audience.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate data scientist find out what a
  stakeholder actually needs from a data science product, rather than
  what they initially asked for?"

- [ ] **Link your development to organisational goals**

  Manage your continuous professional development and link your learning
  to objectives and organisational goals, confidently talk about the
  benefits of data science approaches, and demonstrate a good
  understanding of key techniques such as machine learning that you can
  use to build real solutions.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate data scientist choose what to learn
  next, based on what their organisation actually needs rather than
  what's trending?"

- [ ] **Understand delivery methods, and propose the right one**

  Show awareness of the differences between delivery methods such as
  Agile and waterfall, propose the most appropriate one for each product,
  and manage your contribution to fit the work of your wider team.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate data scientist decide which delivery
  method fits a specific data science product, rather than defaulting to
  whatever the team already uses?"

- [ ] **Research new techniques, and spot opportunities for improvement**

  Adopt an inquisitive and curious approach to data, seek out and research
  new data science techniques, ask questions to improve your knowledge of
  data science norms, and see possibilities for improvement and
  innovation.

  Ask AI: "How would an associate data scientist stay aware of emerging
  data science techniques without it becoming a full-time pursuit
  alongside their actual job?"

- [ ] **Apply data ethics and privacy principles, and share risks**

  Apply fundamental principles of data ethics and privacy in your work
  under supervision, and share ethics and privacy risks through the
  appropriate channels.

  Ask AI: "What's an example of a data ethics or privacy risk an associate
  data scientist might spot in a real dataset, and how would they raise
  it?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
