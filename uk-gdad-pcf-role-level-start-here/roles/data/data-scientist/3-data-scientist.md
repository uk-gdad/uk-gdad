# Data scientist - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the data scientist level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A data scientist works independently, with a good understanding of data
science techniques, delivery methods and stages such as minimum viable
products, tools and technologies. You develop complex solutions using a
range of techniques while understanding the ethical considerations
involved, understand the role and benefits of data science within the
organisation, support building capability, collaborate on solutions, prepare
and manipulate data for complex analytics, and present and communicate
effectively. Most named skills grow at this level, because independence
means your own judgement now has to hold up without close supervision.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own models.

- [ ] **Write moderate-to-complex programs, and understand deployment**

  Write moderate-to-complex programs and scripts, and show a good
  understanding of testing methodologies and how to deploy code.

  Ask AI: "What testing considerations matter most for data science code
  specifically, as opposed to general software testing?"

- [ ] **Apply designated techniques to real analysis and prediction**

  Apply designated quantitative techniques such as time series analysis,
  optimisation and simulation to build and embed models, guide others on
  matching data sources to the right techniques, apply appropriate
  techniques to discover new relations and support decision making, and
  use the statistical tools available in your organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a data scientist choose between time series analysis,
  optimisation and simulation for a given problem, rather than defaulting
  to one?"

- [ ] **Work with data engineers to design real products**

  Work with data engineers and data scientists to design and provide
  products for the organisation, rather than handling every part of the
  pipeline alone.

  Ask AI: "What's the right division of labour between a data scientist
  and a data engineer when designing a real data product together?"

- [ ] **Choose delivery methods, and define the minimum viable product**

  Understand the differences between delivery methods such as Agile and
  waterfall and choose the most appropriate one, define the minimum viable
  product and support decisions about priorities, and work with
  specialists in multidisciplinary teams to deliver smoothly.

  Ask AI: "How would a data scientist define a genuinely minimum viable
  version of a data science product, rather than a smaller version of the
  full one?"

- [ ] **Support capability building across the team**

  Manage your continuous professional development linked to organisational
  goals, support capability building across the team and wider
  organisation, confidently talk about the benefits of data science, and
  demonstrate a good understanding of a range of techniques including
  natural language processing.

  Ask AI: "What does supporting a colleague's data science capability
  building actually involve, beyond answering their questions when
  asked?"

- [ ] **Demonstrate practical knowledge, and spot opportunities to improve**

  Demonstrate practical knowledge of data science tools and techniques,
  develop solutions that maximise insight, and identify opportunities for
  data science to improve data practices.

  Ask AI: "What's a good way for a data scientist to identify where data
  science could improve an existing data practice that nobody's
  questioned yet?"

- [ ] **Address ethical and privacy concerns, and challenge policy**

  Work with stakeholders to identify and address ethical and privacy
  concerns, communicate how ethical issues fit into the wider
  organisational context, research developments in data ethics, and
  constructively challenge proposed data ethics policies.

  Ask AI: "What does 'constructively challenging' a proposed data ethics
  policy look like in practice, without it coming across as obstructive?"

- [ ] **Keep collaborating to identify needs and deliver products**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from associate level:
  understanding the organisation's benefits from data science,
  collaborating to identify user needs, and communicating tailored
  analysis to your audience.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely independent-level judgement on delivering
  business impact add beyond what's written here?"

Ask a principal data scientist which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
