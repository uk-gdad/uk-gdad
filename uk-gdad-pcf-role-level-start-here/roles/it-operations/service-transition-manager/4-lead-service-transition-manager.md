# Lead service transition manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead service transition manager
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead service transition manager is responsible for the planning and
co-ordination of resources to ensure services are effectively transitioned
into service operation — the top of this role's line, solely responsible
for co-ordination activities across projects, suppliers and service teams.
Most named skills grow at this level, because sole responsibility for
co-ordination across suppliers asks for more than recommending go-live for
one product at a time.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real cross-supplier co-ordination decision you're weighing and ask it to
argue the case you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Implement availability and capacity standards, and act on issues**

  Ensure the correct implementation of availability and capacity
  management standards and procedures, identify capacity issues and
  implement the required changes, and initiate remedial action.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a lead service transition manager that a
  capacity issue needs remedial action now, rather than being monitored
  for a bit longer?"

- [ ] **Manage complex change, and enforce release discipline**

  Manage high-impact, complex change requests, and make sure release
  policies, procedures and processes are actually applied.

  Ask AI: "What makes a change request genuinely high-impact for service
  transition, and how should that change how a lead manager handles it?"

- [ ] **Build genuine collaboration across suppliers and teams**

  Work collaboratively, actively networking with others, adapt feedback so
  it's effective and lasting, use team health checks to spot dynamic
  issues, and help stimulate the right response to them.

  Ask AI: "What makes feedback 'effective and lasting' rather than
  something a supplier or colleague hears once and forgets?"

- [ ] **Take accountability, and search proactively for problems**

  Take accountability for issues that occur, be proactive in searching for
  potential problems before they surface, and achieve excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What does 'proactively searching for potential problems' look
  like in practice during a service transition, rather than waiting for
  go-live to reveal one?"

- [ ] **Champion service focus across the whole life cycle**

  Maintain focus on the entire life cycle of the service, ensure it's at
  the forefront of readiness activities, understand and communicate
  service benefits to end users, and champion service focus within your
  team, actively recommending its adoption.

  Ask AI: "What does 'championing service focus' within a team involve,
  beyond maintaining it well yourself across one transition?"

- [ ] **Lead on user focus, not just gather it**

  Collaborate with user researchers and represent users internally,
  explain the difference between user needs and desires, champion user
  research so it covers all users, and guide others in understanding the
  user story.

  Ask AI: "What's the practical difference between what users need and
  what they say they want, and how would a lead manager explain that
  difference to a colleague who's confusing the two?"

- [ ] **Keep diagnosing incidents, and finding their resolution**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from service transition
  manager level: diagnosing and prioritising incidents, investigating
  their causes, and finding resolutions.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely lead-level incident judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep investigating patterns behind problems**

  This skill also plateaus: investigating patterns and trends, consulting
  specialists, and determining preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem management strategy across
  suppliers add, beyond investigating patterns well yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding the basics of IT continuity management**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: explaining the basic principles of
  IT standard continuity management processes and procedures.

  Ask AI: "What would applying continuity management principles across
  multiple suppliers look like, beyond explaining them?"

- [ ] **Keep building ITIL knowledge alongside your qualification**

  This skill also plateaus: holding a Level 3 service management
  framework qualification and demonstrating knowledge of ITIL's life
  cycle or capability elements.

  Ask AI: "What would deepening ITIL knowledge, beyond the base
  qualification, look like for a lead service transition manager?"

Ask a peer lead service transition manager, in your organisation or another
part of government, which of these has taken them longest to get right —
that conversation will teach you more than any general reading on
technical leadership.
