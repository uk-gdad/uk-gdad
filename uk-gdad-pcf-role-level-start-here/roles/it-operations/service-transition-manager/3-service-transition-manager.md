# Service transition manager - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the service transition manager level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything
else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it
names.

## Purpose

A service transition manager accepts products, determines whether they
are fit for purpose, and assesses readiness against agreed service
acceptance criteria — making recommendations on go-live, early-life
support and service acceptance, and making sure the acceptance criteria
are understood by the wider IT operations function. Most named skills
grow at this level, because making the go-live recommendation asks for
more than testing a product against criteria someone else owns.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI
tool a specific question rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll
get an answer shaped around your own organisation's transitions.

- [ ] **Maintain secure configuration, and track IT assets**

  Maintain secure configuration and accurate information, control IT
  assets in one or more areas, and verify their location and state.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between tracking asset information as an
  analyst and being responsible for controlling IT assets across an
  area?"

- [ ] **Diagnose incidents, and find their resolution**

  Diagnose and prioritise incidents, investigate their causes, and find
  resolutions rather than just recording and routing them.

  Ask AI: "What's a structured way to diagnose the root cause of an
  incident that surfaces during service transition, rather than fixing
  the first plausible symptom?"

- [ ] **Investigate patterns behind problems, and consult specialists**

  Initiate and monitor actions to investigate patterns and trends behind
  problems, consult specialists where needed, determine the right
  resolution and help implement it, and identify preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between fixing a recurring transition
  fault and preventing the pattern behind it from continuing?"

- [ ] **See the bigger picture across groups of services**

  See the bigger picture by taking groups of services and investigating
  how to get the best out of the underlying services that support them,
  rather than optimising one service at a time.

  Ask AI: "What would a service transition manager look for across a
  group of related services that wouldn't be visible looking at any one
  service in isolation?"

- [ ] **Build ITIL knowledge alongside your qualification**

  Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, and
  demonstrate knowledge of the life cycle or capability elements of ITIL
  (Information Technology Infrastructure Library).

  Ask AI: "Explain, for someone who already has a service management
  qualification, what ITIL's life cycle and capability elements add on
  top of it."

- [ ] **Keep explaining availability and capacity management**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from service readiness
  analyst level: managing service components to make sure they meet
  business needs and key performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely manager-level availability and capacity
  judgement add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep analysing the impact of change before it happens**

  This skill also plateaus: analysing and assessing the impact of change,
  documenting change requests, and actioning approved changes.

  Ask AI: "What would owning the go-live recommendation for a change add,
  beyond assessing its impact well?"

- [ ] **Keep contributing to a well-functioning team**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: contributing to the work of others,
  motivating and empowering the team, and recognising and dealing with
  issues.

  Ask AI: "What would leading a team through a difficult transition add,
  beyond contributing well to it?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding the basics of IT continuity management**

  This skill also plateaus: explaining the basic principles of IT
  standard continuity management processes and procedures.

  Ask AI: "What would applying continuity management principles when
  planning a live transition look like, beyond explaining them?"

- [ ] **Keep owning an issue until it's genuinely resolved**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: owning an issue until a new owner is
  found or it's mitigated or resolved.

  Ask AI: "What would taking accountability for an issue, rather than
  just owning it until resolved, look like at the next level?"

- [ ] **Keep turning user evidence into decisions**

  This skill also plateaus: collating user needs evidence, defining
  fitting research, and using quantitative and qualitative data to turn
  user focus into outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would a service transition manager use user evidence to
  decide whether a service is genuinely ready to go live?"

Ask a lead service transition manager which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
