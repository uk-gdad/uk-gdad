# Service acceptance analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the service acceptance analyst level,
this page is for you: what the role is about, where to find everything
else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it
asks for.

## Purpose

IT service transition managers provide overall planning for service
transitions and co-ordinate the required resources, controlling the
product life cycle of all changes so beneficial changes can be made with
minimum disruption to IT services. As a service acceptance analyst, you
co-ordinate across IT operations and manage the acceptance criteria for
changes within your area, validating changes against those criteria,
gathering evidence and creating materials as necessary.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a foundation for this role,
described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a specific
question rather than searching for a generic guide.

- [ ] **Track and protect assets and components**

  Track, log and correct information to protect assets and components,
  keeping records accurate as things change.

  Ask AI: "Why does keeping asset information accurate matter for
  validating a change against its acceptance criteria?"

- [ ] **Explain availability and capacity management**

  Explain availability and capacity management processes — why a service
  needs to stay up, and why "up" isn't enough if it can't handle the load
  it's given.

  Ask AI: "Explain, simply, what 'availability' and 'capacity' mean for an
  IT service, and why they're treated as separate concerns."

- [ ] **Implement change requests, under supervision**

  Implement change requests, and apply change control procedures under
  supervision.

  Ask AI: "Why do organisations use a formal change control procedure
  instead of just making a fix when it's needed?"

- [ ] **Understand team dynamics, collaboration and feedback**

  Understand the work of others and the importance of team dynamics,
  collaboration and feedback.

  Ask AI: "What does good team collaboration actually look like day to
  day, beyond just being friendly to colleagues?"

- [ ] **Understand the basics of IT continuity management**

  Explain the basic principles of IT standard continuity management
  processes and procedures.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what 'IT continuity management' means,
  and why it's treated separately from change management."

- [ ] **Identify and register an incident properly**

  Identify and register incidents, gathering the required information and
  allocating it to the appropriate channel.

  Ask AI: "What information does a good incident record actually need, and
  why does getting it right at the start matter later on?"

- [ ] **Show awareness of problem resolution, and pass problems on**

  Show an awareness of problem resolution processes, and pass problems on
  to your team when they're beyond what you can resolve yourself.

  Ask AI: "How does a trainee know when to keep trying to solve a problem
  and when to hand it to someone more experienced?"

- [ ] **Investigate a problem, and know its level**

  Investigate problems in systems, processes and services, understanding
  the level of a problem — for example, strategic, tactical or
  operational — and contribute to remedies and preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a strategic, a tactical and an
  operational problem in IT operations, and why does knowing which is
  which change how you respond?"

- [ ] **Build coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  Take inputs and establish coherent frameworks that work, rather than
  leaving acceptance information scattered across different sources.

  Ask AI: "What does 'establishing a coherent framework' mean in practice
  for someone managing acceptance criteria across several changes?"

- [ ] **Build service management framework knowledge**

  Work towards a Level 3 service management framework qualification, so
  your day-to-day practice is grounded in a recognised standard.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what a service management framework
  qualification actually covers, and why organisations value it."

- [ ] **Turn user evidence into decisions**

  Identify and engage with users or stakeholders to collate evidence of
  their needs, understand and define research that actually fits those
  needs, and use both quantitative and qualitative data to turn user
  focus into real outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would a service acceptance analyst use user evidence to
  decide whether a change genuinely meets its acceptance criteria?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
