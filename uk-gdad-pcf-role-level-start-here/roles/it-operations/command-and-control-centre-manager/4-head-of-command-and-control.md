# Head of command and control - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of command and control level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of command and control is responsible for the strategy roadmap,
integration and building the capability of the team — the top of this
profession's line. You make sure all tools are fit for purpose and meet
business requirements, and may still be required to address issues across
IT operations directly. Asset and configuration management, availability
and capacity management, problem management, service management framework
knowledge and user focus all sharpen further at this level, because
owning the roadmap and building capability asks for a wider, more
strategic view than being the escalation point for today's incidents.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real strategic decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case you
haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Own availability and capacity standards, not just meet them**

  Ensure the correct implementation of availability and capacity
  management standards and procedures, identify capacity issues before
  they bite, and initiate remedial action yourself.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between reacting well to a capacity issue
  and genuinely owning availability and capacity management as a
  standard others are held to?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to problems, not just solve them**

  Make sure the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate, implement
  solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to recurring problems
  involve, beyond diagnosing and fixing them yourself?"

- [ ] **Manage configuration items, and service compliance**

  Manage configuration items and related information, and manage service
  compliance and risk.

  Ask AI: "What does managing service compliance and risk add, beyond
  keeping configuration information accurate?"

- [ ] **Build expert-level service management framework knowledge**

  Hold an expert certificate in the service management framework
  qualification, going beyond the foundational level.

  Ask AI: "What does expert-level service management framework knowledge
  add in practice, beyond the Level 3 qualification most colleagues
  hold?"

- [ ] **Set direction on meeting user needs across channels**

  Give direction on which tools or methods to use, demonstrate experience
  meeting user needs across a variety of channels, bring insight into how
  user needs have changed over time, and apply strategic thinking to
  provide the best service for the end user.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of command and control use insight about how
  user needs have changed over time to shape the team's tooling
  roadmap?"

- [ ] **Keep managing complex change, and enforcing release discipline**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from operational control
  manager level: managing high-impact, complex change requests, and
  ensuring release policies are applied.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely head-of-role change judgement add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep building genuine collaboration across your team**

  This skill also plateaus: networking actively, adapting feedback so it
  lasts, and using team health checks to spot and address issues.

  Ask AI: "What would building collaboration culture across the whole
  command and control function add, beyond building it well within one
  team?"

- [ ] **Keep managing runbooks, and testing service continuity**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: managing runbooks and continuity
  processes, and testing them to make sure availability is maintained.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide ownership of continuity strategy
  add, beyond managing and testing runbooks well for one service?"

- [ ] **Keep leading the investigation and resolution of incidents**

  This skill also plateaus: leading the investigation and resolution of
  incidents.

  Ask AI: "What kind of incident would genuinely need a head of command
  and control's direct involvement, rather than being handled well by an
  operational control manager?"

- [ ] **Keep taking accountability, and searching proactively for problems**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: taking accountability for issues,
  searching proactively for problems, and achieving excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide accountability for user outcomes
  add, beyond taking accountability well for your own team's incidents?"

- [ ] **Keep seeing the bigger picture across groups of services**

  This skill also plateaus: taking groups of services and investigating
  how to get the best out of underlying services.

  Ask AI: "What would strategic ownership of service groupings add,
  beyond seeing the bigger picture well within one area?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
