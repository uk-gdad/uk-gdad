# Principal infrastructure operations engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal infrastructure operations
engineer level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal infrastructure operations engineer leads and directs IT
operations specialist teams in monitoring, operating and supporting
technical solutions — the top of this role's line. You develop the
strategic and tactical roadmaps for technologies and services, ensuring
they're future-proofed and the organisation derives maximum value from its
investment, and you own the operational relationships with suppliers.
Asset and configuration management, availability and capacity management,
problem management, service focus, service management framework knowledge,
technical specialism, testing and user focus all grow at this level,
because owning the strategy asks for a wider, more forward-looking view
than managing today's operational service.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real strategic decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case you
haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Manage configuration items, and service compliance**

  Manage configuration items and related information, and manage service
  compliance and risk.

  Ask AI: "What does managing service compliance and risk add, beyond
  keeping configuration information accurate?"

- [ ] **Implement availability and capacity standards, and act on issues**

  Ensure the correct implementation of availability and capacity
  management standards and procedures, identify capacity issues and
  implement the required changes, and initiate remedial action.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a principal engineer that a capacity issue
  needs remedial action now, rather than being monitored for a bit
  longer?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to problems, not just solve them**

  Ensure that the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate problems,
  implement solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to a recurring
  infrastructure problem involve, beyond diagnosing and fixing it
  yourself?"

- [ ] **See the bigger picture across groups of services**

  See the bigger picture by taking groups of services and investigating
  how to get the best out of the underlying services that support them,
  rather than optimising one service at a time.

  Ask AI: "What would a principal engineer look for across a group of
  related services that wouldn't be visible looking at any one service in
  isolation?"

- [ ] **Hold an expert service management framework certificate**

  Show you have an expert certificate in the service management framework
  qualification.

  Ask AI: "What does an expert-level service management framework
  certificate cover, beyond a Level 3 qualification and ITIL knowledge?"

- [ ] **Understand future technology direction, and plan for it**

  Understand the direction of future technologies, and deliver a model to
  support and maintain those technologies alongside any databases that
  coexist in the current environment.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal engineer evaluate whether an emerging
  infrastructure technology is worth planning for now, rather than waiting
  until it's mainstream?"

- [ ] **Manage testing risk, and customer relationships**

  Manage testing activities within development or integration work,
  manage risks and take preventative action when they become
  unacceptable, and manage customer relations.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a principal engineer that a testing risk has
  become unacceptable and needs preventative action, not just
  monitoring?"

- [ ] **Give strategic direction on meeting user needs**

  Give direction on which tools or methods to use, demonstrate experience
  in meeting user needs across a variety of channels, bring insight into
  how those needs have changed over time, and apply strategic thinking to
  provide the best service for the end user.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal engineer decide that user needs have
  genuinely shifted, rather than one team simply having a noisy quarter?"

- [ ] **Keep managing complex change, and enforcing release discipline**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level: managing
  high-impact, complex change requests, and ensuring release policies are
  applied.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely principal-level change judgement add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep interacting with, reading and writing code**

  This skill also plateaus: interacting with, reading and writing code,
  and carrying out baseline repair activities.

  Ask AI: "What kind of code work would genuinely need a principal
  engineer's involvement, rather than being handled well by a lead
  engineer?"

- [ ] **Keep leading the investigation and resolution of incidents**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: leading the investigation and
  resolution of incidents.

  Ask AI: "What kind of incident would genuinely need a principal
  engineer's involvement, rather than being handled well by a lead
  engineer?"

- [ ] **Keep taking accountability, and searching proactively for problems**

  This skill also plateaus: taking accountability for issues, searching
  proactively for problems, and achieving excellent user outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide accountability for user outcomes
  add, beyond taking accountability well for your own team's incidents?"

Ask a peer principal engineer, in your organisation or another part of
government, which of these has taken them longest to get right — that
conversation will teach you more than any general reading on technical
leadership.
