# Associate infrastructure operations engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the associate infrastructure operations
engineer level, this page is for you: what the role is about, where to
find everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for
building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

An infrastructure operations engineer supports, manages and maintains the
core infrastructure that underpins production services. As a trainee, you
work in an established team and support infrastructure operations
engineers in their daily work, under their direction — the basics of
spotting an incident, understanding what a problem actually is, and seeing
a service through the eyes of the people who use it.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a foundation for this role,
described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a specific
question rather than searching for a generic guide.

- [ ] **Identify and register an incident properly**

  Identify and register incidents, gathering the required information and
  allocating it to the appropriate channel.

  Ask AI: "I'm new to infrastructure operations. What information does a
  good incident record actually need, and why does getting it right at the
  start matter later on?"

- [ ] **Investigate a problem, and know its level**

  Investigate problems in systems, processes and services, understanding
  the level of a problem — for example, strategic, tactical or
  operational — and contribute to remedies and preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a strategic, a tactical and an
  operational problem in IT operations, and why does knowing which is
  which change how you respond?"

- [ ] **Show awareness of problem resolution, and pass problems on**

  Show an awareness of problem resolution processes, and pass problems on
  to your team when they're beyond what you can resolve yourself.

  Ask AI: "How does a trainee know when to keep trying to solve a problem
  and when to hand it to someone more experienced?"

- [ ] **Understand user experience analysis**

  Show an awareness or understanding of user experience analysis and its
  principles.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what 'user experience analysis' means
  in an infrastructure operations context, where the users might be other
  engineers rather than the public."

- [ ] **Explain user stories, and why they focus on user needs**

  Explain the purpose of user stories and the focus on user needs.

  Ask AI: "Why do infrastructure teams write user stories at all, when
  most of the work is behind the scenes and not visible to an end user?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
