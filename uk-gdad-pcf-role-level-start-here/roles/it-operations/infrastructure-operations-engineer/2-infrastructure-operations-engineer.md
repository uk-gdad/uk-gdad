# Infrastructure operations engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the infrastructure operations engineer
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
skills it asks for.

## Purpose

An infrastructure operations engineer is responsible for the preparation
and support of IT operations solutions and services, physical or virtual,
according to industry and organisational best practices, standards,
service requirements and key performance indicators throughout the product
life cycle. You create and maintain documentation, perform routine tasks
according to process and checklists, and monitor infrastructure and
applications services, alerting and taking appropriate action. Eight new
skills join at this level, because being responsible for a live service
asks for far more than supporting a team that runs one.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question rather than searching for a generic guide.

- [ ] **Maintain secure configuration, and track IT assets**

  Maintain secure configuration and accurate information, control IT
  assets in one or more areas, and verify their location and state.

  Ask AI: "Why does keeping IT asset information accurate matter for
  responding to incidents quickly?"

- [ ] **Manage service components against business needs**

  Manage service components to make sure they meet business needs and key
  performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "What key performance indicators would an infrastructure
  operations engineer typically be measured against, beyond whether a
  service is simply up or down?"

- [ ] **Implement change requests, under supervision**

  Implement change requests, and apply change control procedures under
  supervision.

  Ask AI: "Why do organisations use a formal change control procedure
  instead of just making a fix when it's needed?"

- [ ] **Understand different scripting tools and software**

  Describe different scripting tools and software that are available and
  currently in use.

  Ask AI: "What kinds of scripting task come up most often in
  infrastructure operations, and what tools are commonly used for them?"

- [ ] **Own an issue until it's genuinely resolved**

  Own an issue until a new owner has been found or the problem has been
  mitigated or resolved, rather than passing it on the moment it gets
  difficult.

  Ask AI: "What does 'owning an issue' mean in practice for an
  infrastructure operations engineer, when the actual fix might belong to
  someone else's team?"

- [ ] **Build coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  Take inputs and establish coherent frameworks that work, rather than
  leaving operational information scattered across different sources.

  Ask AI: "What does 'establishing a coherent framework' mean in practice
  for someone supporting infrastructure operations across several
  services?"

- [ ] **Assist in technical support and routine maintenance**

  Assist in technical support activities, and carry out agreed or routine
  maintenance and administration tasks.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between routine maintenance and the kind
  of technical support activity that needs a more experienced colleague's
  judgement?"

- [ ] **Execute test scripts, and understand testing's role**

  Correctly execute test scripts under supervision, and understand the
  role of testing and how it works.

  Ask AI: "Why does infrastructure operations work get tested the same way
  software does, and what's different about testing an infrastructure
  change?"

- [ ] **Build service management framework knowledge**

  Show you have a Level 3 service management framework qualification.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what a Level 3 service management
  framework qualification actually covers, and why organisations value
  it."

- [ ] **Keep identifying and registering incidents**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from associate level:
  identifying and registering incidents, gathering the required
  information, and allocating it to the appropriate channel.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely infrastructure-operations-engineer-level
  incident judgement add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep investigating problems, and knowing their level**

  This skill also plateaus: investigating problems in systems, processes
  and services, understanding their level, and contributing to remedies
  and preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What would leading problem investigation across a team add,
  beyond investigating problems well yourself?"

- [ ] **Turn user evidence into decisions**

  Identify and engage with users or stakeholders to collate evidence of
  their needs, understand and define research that actually fits those
  needs, and use both quantitative and qualitative data to turn user
  focus into real outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would an infrastructure operations engineer use user
  evidence to decide which of several competing tasks to prioritise?"

Ask a senior infrastructure operations engineer which of these your team
relies on most right now, and start there.
