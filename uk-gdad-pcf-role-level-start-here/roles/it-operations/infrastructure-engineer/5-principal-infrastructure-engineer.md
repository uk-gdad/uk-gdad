# Principal infrastructure engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal infrastructure engineer
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal infrastructure engineer is an accomplished technical leader —
the top of this role's line. You develop strategic and tactical engineering
roadmaps for technologies and services in your area of responsibility,
potentially own the operational relationships with suppliers, and work with
technical architects to translate architectural designs into operations.
Problem management, service focus and systems design all grow at this
level, because owning a technology roadmap asks for more than leading a
team through today's designs.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real strategic decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case you
haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Design systems with high risk and industry-wide impact**

  Design systems characterised by high levels of risk, impact, and
  business or technical complexity, control system design practice within
  an enterprise or industry architecture, and influence industry-based
  models for new technology applications.

  Ask AI: "What does it mean to 'influence industry-based models' for new
  technology, as opposed to designing well within your own organisation?"

- [ ] **Develop implementation and procurement strategies**

  Develop effective implementation and procurement strategies consistent
  with business needs, and ensure adherence to relevant technical
  strategies, policies, standards and practices.

  Ask AI: "What should a principal engineer weigh when developing a
  procurement strategy for infrastructure, beyond technical fit?"

- [ ] **See the bigger picture across groups of services**

  Take groups of services and investigate how to get the best out of the
  underlying services that support them, rather than optimising one
  service at a time.

  Ask AI: "What would a principal engineer look for across a group of
  related services that wouldn't be visible looking at any one service in
  isolation?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to problems, not just solve them**

  Ensure the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate problems,
  implement solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to a recurring
  infrastructure problem involve, beyond diagnosing and fixing it
  yourself?"

- [ ] **Manage testing risk, and customer relationships**

  Manage testing activities within development or integration work,
  manage risks and take preventative action when they become unacceptable,
  and manage customer relations.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a principal engineer that a testing risk has
  become unacceptable and needs preventative action, not just
  monitoring?"

- [ ] **Design and implement infrastructure solutions**

  The framework's own choice, not a gap in your practice: this skill
  returns to design and implementation of infrastructure solutions and
  services — for example, networking and physical infrastructure,
  commercial-off-the-shelf software, open source packages and solutions,
  or virtual and cloud computing — the wording used at senior level,
  rather than continuing the "lead teams and departments" phrasing used at
  lead level.

  Ask AI: "Why might a framework describe a principal engineer's
  infrastructure skill in terms of designing and implementing solutions,
  rather than leading a team through them, when the level below already
  covers leadership?"

- [ ] **Keep interacting with, reading and writing code**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  interacting with, reading and writing code, and carrying out baseline
  repair activities.

  Ask AI: "What kind of code work would genuinely need a principal
  engineer's involvement, rather than being handled well by a lead
  engineer?"

- [ ] **Keep designing solutions with security engineered in**

  This skill also plateaus: designing solutions and services with security
  controls included, specifically engineered to mitigate security
  threats.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide accountability for security-by-
  design add, beyond engineering security into your own designs well?"

- [ ] **Keep applying modern development standards to complex programmes**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: identifying and applying modern
  development standards to support complex projects and programmes, and
  leading others in applying them.

  Ask AI: "What would setting modern development standards across an
  organisation add, beyond applying and teaching them well within one
  team?"

- [ ] **Keep taking accountability, and searching proactively for problems**

  This skill also plateaus: taking accountability for issues, searching
  proactively for problems, and achieving excellent user outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide accountability for user outcomes
  add, beyond taking accountability well for your own team's incidents?"

- [ ] **Keep establishing standards across the service product life cycle**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: establishing standards and procedures
  across a service product life cycle, and managing resources so the
  systems integration function works effectively.

  Ask AI: "What would sponsoring service product life cycle standards at
  board level add, beyond establishing and enforcing them well within your
  own teams?"

- [ ] **Keep using lateral thinking to diagnose root causes**

  This skill also plateaus: using lateral thinking to break a problem down
  into its component parts, troubleshooting across the full range of
  technology capabilities.

  Ask AI: "What would teaching lateral root-cause analysis to a whole
  organisation add, beyond using it well yourself?"

Ask a peer principal engineer, in your organisation or another part of
government, which of these has taken them longest to get right — that
conversation will teach you more than any general reading on technical
leadership.
