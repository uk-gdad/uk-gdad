# Principal application operations engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal application operations
engineer level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find
everything else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the
areas it names.

## Purpose

A principal application operations engineer owns the application strategy
and leads on resourcing, learning and development for their team — the top
of this profession's line. You bring expert technical understanding, are
accountable for vendor and supplier management, and depending on a
project's complexity, size and business impact, might still manage and
maintain certain aspects of an application yourself. Asset and
configuration management, availability and capacity management, problem
management, service focus, service management framework knowledge,
technical specialism and testing all grow at this level, because owning the
strategy asks for a wider, more forward-looking view than leading day-to-day
delivery does.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real strategic decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case you
haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Own availability and capacity standards, not just meet them**

  Ensure the correct implementation of availability and capacity
  management standards and procedures, identify capacity issues before
  they bite, and initiate remedial action yourself.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between reacting well to a capacity issue
  and genuinely owning availability and capacity management as a
  standard others are held to?"

- [ ] **Coordinate the team's response to problems, not just solve them**

  Make sure the right actions are taken to investigate, resolve and
  anticipate problems, and co-ordinate the team to investigate, implement
  solutions and take preventive measures.

  Ask AI: "What does coordinating a team's response to a recurring
  application problem involve, beyond diagnosing and fixing it yourself?"

- [ ] **See the bigger picture across groups of services**

  Take groups of services and investigate how to get the best out of the
  underlying services that support them, rather than optimising one
  service at a time.

  Ask AI: "What would a principal engineer look for across a group of
  related services that wouldn't be visible looking at any one service in
  isolation?"

- [ ] **Build expert-level service management framework knowledge**

  Hold an expert certificate in the service management framework
  qualification, going beyond the foundational level.

  Ask AI: "What does expert-level service management framework knowledge
  add in practice, beyond the Level 3 qualification most colleagues
  hold?"

- [ ] **Understand future technology direction, and plan for it**

  Understand the direction of future technologies, and deliver a model to
  support and maintain those technologies alongside any databases that
  coexist in the current environment.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal engineer evaluate whether an emerging
  technology is worth planning for now, rather than waiting until it's
  mainstream?"

- [ ] **Manage testing risk, and customer relationships**

  Manage testing activities within development or integration work,
  manage risks and take preventative action when they become
  unacceptable, and manage customer relations.

  Ask AI: "What would tell a principal engineer that a testing risk has
  become unacceptable and needs preventative action, not just
  monitoring?"

- [ ] **Manage configuration items, and service compliance**

  Manage configuration items and related information, and manage service
  compliance and risk.

  Ask AI: "What does managing service compliance and risk add, beyond
  keeping configuration information accurate?"

- [ ] **Keep managing complex change, and enforcing release discipline**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level: managing
  high-impact, complex change requests, and ensuring release policies are
  applied.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely principal-level change judgement add beyond what's
  written here?"

- [ ] **Keep leading the investigation and resolution of incidents**

  This skill also plateaus: leading the investigation and resolution of
  incidents.

  Ask AI: "What kind of incident would genuinely need a principal
  engineer's involvement, rather than being handled well by a lead
  engineer?"

- [ ] **Keep taking accountability, and searching proactively for problems**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: taking accountability for issues,
  searching proactively for problems, and achieving excellent user
  outcomes.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide accountability for user outcomes
  add, beyond taking accountability well for your own application?"

- [ ] **Keep leading on user focus**

  This skill also plateaus: collaborating with user researchers,
  representing users internally, and championing research for all users.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal engineer make sure user focus stays
  central to strategic technical decisions, not just delivery-level
  ones?"

Ask a peer principal engineer, in your organisation or another part of
government, which of these has taken them longest to get right — that
conversation will teach you more than any general reading on technical
leadership.
