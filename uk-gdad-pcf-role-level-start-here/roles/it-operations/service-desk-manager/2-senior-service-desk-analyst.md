# Senior service desk analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior service desk analyst level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks
for.

## Purpose

A service desk manager is responsible for managing the first- and
second-line technical support for all departmental IT applications and
services across sites. As a senior service desk analyst, you monitor
inbound calls, support operators in service resolution, intervene in
difficult calls, own and update the script for service desk analysts, and
coach and develop one or more analysts. Problem management and service
management framework knowledge are new at this level, and most other
named skills grow, because supporting a team through difficult calls asks
for more than resolving your own calls well.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question rather than searching for a generic guide.

- [ ] **Deal with complex complaints, using empathy**

  Resolve user requests while maintaining a high level of customer
  service, deal with complex complaints, and use empathy to understand and
  meet customer needs.

  Ask AI: "What's a good approach for a senior analyst to de-escalate a
  complex complaint that a junior colleague couldn't resolve?"

- [ ] **Build coherent frameworks from scattered inputs**

  Take inputs and establish coherent frameworks that work, rather than
  leaving service desk information scattered across different sources.

  Ask AI: "What does 'establishing a coherent framework' mean in practice
  for someone monitoring inbound calls across a whole team?"

- [ ] **Turn user evidence into decisions**

  Identify and engage with users or stakeholders to collate evidence of
  their needs, understand and define research that actually fits those
  needs, and use both quantitative and qualitative data to turn user
  focus into real outcomes.

  Ask AI: "How would a senior service desk analyst use user evidence to
  decide how to update the team's call script?"

- [ ] **Investigate problems, and know their level**

  Investigate problems in systems, processes and services, understanding
  the level of a problem — for example, strategic, tactical or
  operational — and contribute to remedies and preventative measures.

  Ask AI: "What's the difference between a strategic, a tactical and an
  operational problem on a service desk, and why does knowing which is
  which change how you respond?"

- [ ] **Build service management framework knowledge**

  Work towards a Level 3 service management framework qualification, so
  your day-to-day practice is grounded in a recognised standard.

  Ask AI: "Explain, for a beginner, what a service management framework
  qualification actually covers, and why organisations value it."

- [ ] **Keep tracking and protecting assets and components**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from service desk analyst
  level: tracking, logging and correcting information to protect assets
  and components.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely senior-level asset management judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep producing reports, and interpreting data for others**

  This skill also plateaus: producing relevant reports, discussing
  reporting process changes with stakeholders, and interpreting data.

  Ask AI: "What would setting reporting standards across a team add,
  beyond producing good reports yourself?"

- [ ] **Keep owning an issue until it's genuinely resolved**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: owning an issue until a new owner is
  found or it's mitigated or resolved.

  Ask AI: "What would taking accountability for an issue, rather than just
  owning it until resolved, look like at the next level?"

Ask a service desk manager which of these your team relies on most right
now, and start there.
