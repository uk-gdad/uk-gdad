# Technical writer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the technical writer level, this page
is for you: what the role is about, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.
The framework publishes this as the first of only two levels for this
role, and it already reads at a genuinely expert register — this is not a
role most people arrive at as their first job in content or design.

## Purpose

A technical writer takes a user-centred approach to making complicated
technical concepts easier to understand for a specialist audience —
focusing on specialist content and software documentation, and writing for
technologists such as developers, technical architects and technology
leaders. As an expert practitioner with a deep understanding of technology
and the needs of technologists, you take that user-centred approach to
explaining how to use government products and services to a technical
audience, write blog posts for or on behalf of the technical community, and
engage with the cross-government technical writing and content design
communities.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level, described plainly, with a way to go deeper by asking an AI tool a
specific question — the answers you get will be shaped around your own
organisation's technical documentation, which a generic guide never is.

- [ ] **Assure content quality, and coach junior colleagues**

  Show a deep understanding of end-to-end journeys and how content is
  affected within them, identify where fixes are needed, take
  responsibility for assuring junior colleagues' content quality, coach
  and guide them, ensure content is regularly reviewed, and deliver
  through others by directing and critiquing their work.

  Ask AI: "What should a technical writer look for when assuring a junior
  colleague's documentation, beyond checking it's grammatically
  correct?"

- [ ] **Give direction on meeting technical users' needs**

  Give direction on which tools or methods to use, demonstrate experience
  meeting user needs across a variety of channels, bring insight into how
  user needs have changed over time, and apply strategic thinking to
  provide the best service for the end user.

  Ask AI: "How does a technical writer find out what a developer or
  technical architect actually needs from documentation, rather than
  assuming based on their own writing preferences?"

- [ ] **Demonstrate technical knowledge, and collaborate across disciplines**

  Demonstrate knowledge of the technologies used to build and operate
  digital services, and collaborate closely with colleagues in different
  digital disciplines.

  Ask AI: "How deep does a technical writer's own technical knowledge need
  to go to write credible documentation for developers, without needing to
  be a developer themselves?"

- [ ] **Lead the design and implementation of strategy**

  Lead the design and implementation of strategy, directing the
  evaluation of strategies and policies to make sure business
  requirements are being met.

  Ask AI: "What would leading a documentation strategy actually involve
  for a technical writer, as distinct from writing good documentation?"

- [ ] **Own stakeholder understanding across your team**

  Work with your team to develop and maintain a shared understanding of
  stakeholders, develop and implement communications strategies, identify
  and resolve issues, and build long-term relationships.

  Ask AI: "What does a genuine stakeholder communications strategy contain
  for technical documentation, beyond a list of who to update and how
  often?"

- [ ] **Help teams choose the best delivery approach**

  Identify and compare the best processes or delivery methods, including
  measuring and evaluating outcomes, help the team decide the best
  approach, and help teams manage and visualise outcomes while adhering to
  agreed scope and priorities.

  Ask AI: "How would a technical writer compare two plausible approaches
  to documenting a complex technical product, beyond just picking the
  familiar one?"

- [ ] **Understand when prototyping earns its place**

  Understand when to use a specific prototyping technique or method, and
  show your team the value of prototyping.

  Ask AI: "When would prototyping a piece of technical documentation, such
  as a draft structure or a sample page, be worth the time before writing
  it in full?"

Ask a lead technical writer, or an equivalent senior colleague, which of
these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
