# Lead service designer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead service designer level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead service designer is an expert practitioner who influences and
mentors others. You work with service managers and programme directors to
develop design concepts, set direction and assure design quality across
teams, and lead multiple or highly complex services. Leading design,
design communication, evidence-based design and iterative design all
sharpen at this level, because you're now shaping how a wider organisation
approaches service design, not just producing strong individual services
yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a leadership or strategic decision —
describe the actual situation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Lead design work across a team, and mentor other designers**

  Lead and co-ordinate design work in a team, advocate for user-centred
  design with leaders outside digital and data, create an inclusive,
  productive environment for designers, mentor and coach other designers,
  and iterate and improve design processes within your team.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead service designer advocate for user-centred
  design to a policy leader who's never worked with a designer before?"

- [ ] **Gain senior support, and coach effective design communication**

  Gain support for design decisions and strategy from senior leaders,
  communicate design strategy across organisational boundaries, coach
  others in communicating design effectively, and develop more effective
  methods for working in the open.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead service designer gain a senior leader's
  support for a cross-government service redesign they're initially
  sceptical of?"

- [ ] **Embed evidence and hypothesis-driven design across the organisation**

  Embed a practice of hypothesis-driven design in a team, guide the
  organisation in using evidence to improve services, analyse and use
  evidence to improve how the organisation works, and make sure user
  insights are shared and used across the wider organisation and
  government.

  Ask AI: "What does 'embedding hypothesis-driven design' in a team
  actually involve, beyond asking individuals to write hypotheses before
  testing a journey?"

- [ ] **Embed iterative design and agile working across teams**

  Embed the practice of iterative design and Agile working in teams or the
  wider organisation, lead on prototyping complex ideas at any fidelity,
  help teams adapt quickly to changing requirements, and iterate delivery
  processes or team structures.

  Ask AI: "What would embedding iterative design as an organisational
  practice look like, beyond one team prototyping and testing a service
  well?"

- [ ] **Keep designing inclusively, and designing ethically**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  measurably improving inclusive, accessible and sustainable design
  practice, and helping others understand and mitigate the influence of
  privilege and power.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely lead-level inclusive design leadership add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep using risk, opportunity and constraint to shape strategy**

  This skill also plateaus: helping teams understand how design meets
  their goals, using risks and constraints to shape design, and creating
  new patterns and components.

  Ask AI: "What would leading strategic service design decisions across
  several teams add, beyond doing it well within one?"

- [ ] **Keep advising others on running effective design sessions**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: advising on planning and running
  sessions, adapting them for useful outcomes, and working across team or
  profession boundaries.

  Ask AI: "What would coaching several teams on running effective design
  sessions add, beyond advising one team well?"

Ask a head of service design, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
