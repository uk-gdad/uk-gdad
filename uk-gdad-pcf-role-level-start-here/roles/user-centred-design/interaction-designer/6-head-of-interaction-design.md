# Head of interaction design - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of interaction design level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of interaction design is an expert practitioner with broad industry
experience who defines and assures best practice while influencing, leading
and mentoring others — the top of this profession's line. You influence
both design and organisational strategy and priorities, collaborate with
counterpart colleagues across government, and focus on making sure the
right conditions and environment exist for designers to work effectively.
Leading design, designing strategically and designing together all sharpen
at this level, because you're now setting vision and conditions for
interaction design across the organisation, not leading it within a single
team.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Set a vision for interaction design across the whole organisation**

  Set a vision for and lead design across multiple teams or a whole
  organisation, work effectively with senior leaders across the
  organisation or wider public sector, support a culture of learning and
  experimentation, and iterate and measurably improve design maturity
  within the organisation and its partners.

  Ask AI: "What does 'design maturity' mean for how an organisation
  approaches interaction design, and how would a head of interaction
  design measure genuine improvement in it?"

- [ ] **Embed interaction design early in strategy, policy and delivery**

  Work with others to make design part of developing and implementing the
  organisation's strategy, work with leaders to include design in policy,
  technology and delivery processes early enough to inform decisions, and
  enable the use of patterns and components across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What tends to go wrong when interaction design gets involved in
  a policy or technology decision too late, and how would a head of
  interaction design prevent that?"

- [ ] **Run design sessions with the most senior stakeholders**

  Effectively plan and run design sessions that include senior leaders,
  help teams connect and work across organisational boundaries, coach
  other designers in involving stakeholders of any level, and develop more
  effective ways to design together.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of interaction design run a session testing a
  major service flow that genuinely engages senior leaders, rather than
  one they simply attend?"

- [ ] **Keep coaching effective design communication**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level: gaining
  senior support for design decisions, communicating strategy across
  boundaries, and coaching others in communicating design.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead level,
  what would genuinely head-of-role design communication add beyond
  what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep measurably improving inclusive design practice**

  This skill also plateaus: measurably improving inclusive, accessible
  and sustainable design practice, and helping others mitigate the
  influence of privilege and power.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide accountability for inclusive
  interaction design add, beyond improving it well within individual
  teams?"

- [ ] **Keep embedding evidence-driven design across the organisation**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: embedding hypothesis-driven design,
  guiding the organisation in using evidence, and sharing insights across
  government.

  Ask AI: "What would sharing interaction research and insight across
  government, rather than within one organisation, actually require?"

- [ ] **Keep embedding iterative design and Agile working**

  This skill also plateaus: embedding iterative design and Agile working,
  leading complex prototyping, and iterating delivery processes or team
  structures.

  Ask AI: "What would embedding iterative design as a value across an
  entire organisation's culture add, beyond embedding it well in
  individual teams?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
