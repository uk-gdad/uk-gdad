# Lead user researcher - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead user researcher level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A lead user researcher is an expert practitioner, leading and aligning user
research activities across several teams. You make sure teams take a
user-centred, evidence-based approach to service design and delivery, and
develop and assure good user research practice. Research management,
stakeholder relationship management, analysis and synthesis, and user
research methods all sharpen at this level, because you're now setting
organisational standards, not just leading strong research within one
team.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. At this level, an AI tool is most
useful as a sounding board for a standards or programme decision —
describe the actual situation and ask what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Align research programmes to organisational goals**

  Establish and align user research programmes and teams to meet
  organisational goals, synthesise findings from research programmes into
  strategic insights for the organisation's roadmap, set and assure good
  practice research standards including ethics and safeguarding, and
  assure research quality across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead user researcher turn findings from several
  separate research programmes into one strategic insight for an
  organisation's roadmap?"

- [ ] **Direct stakeholder strategy, and negotiate at senior level**

  Direct the stakeholder relationship strategy for your teams, make sure
  stakeholder objectives are set and supported, and influence and
  negotiate with senior stakeholders to resolve issues and enable
  progress.

  Ask AI: "What negotiation approach works best with a senior stakeholder
  who has the authority to block a research programme but no obligation to
  compromise?"

- [ ] **Help the organisation continually improve its research practice**

  Help an organisation adopt a wide range of analysis and synthesis
  techniques, and continually assure, improve and innovate its practices
  to generate clear, valuable findings.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead user researcher tell whether an organisation's
  analysis practices are genuinely improving over time, rather than just
  staying busy?"

- [ ] **Lead a community of practice for research methods**

  Help an organisation adopt a wide range of user research methods, and
  lead a community of practice to help it continually assure, improve and
  innovate its research.

  Ask AI: "What makes a community of practice for user research actually
  useful to its members, rather than a meeting people attend out of
  obligation?"

- [ ] **Keep advocating for agile working across complex products**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  advocating for Agile working, adapting research to product complexity,
  and influencing decisions about priorities.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely lead-level Agile research advocacy add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep advocating for inclusive practice across teams**

  This skill also plateaus: helping teams understand user diversity, and
  advocating for inclusive, accessible service design.

  Ask AI: "What would setting an inclusive research standard across
  several teams add, beyond advocating for it well in one?"

- [ ] **Keep advocating for user research with sceptical stakeholders**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: evaluating approaches for building
  understanding, advocating with sceptical stakeholders, and aligning
  research plans to priorities.

  Ask AI: "What would organisation-wide advocacy for user research add,
  beyond winning over sceptical stakeholders one team at a time?"

Ask a head of user research, or an equivalent principal colleague, which
of these matters most in your organisation right now, and start there.
