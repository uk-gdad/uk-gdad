Welcome. If you are working toward the junior user researcher level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A junior user researcher is embedded in a multidisciplinary team to carry
out user research activities. You have some practical experience but still
need regular guidance and training to produce your best work, working in
combination with a more senior user researcher. Analysis and synthesis, and
user research methods, both deepen at this level, because you're now
expected to plan and conduct research with support, not just describe how
it's done.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. Where you want to go deeper, ask an AI tool a specific question
rather than searching for a generic guide — you'll get an answer shaped
around your own research.

- [ ] **Plan and conduct research, with support**

  Plan and conduct a user research project with support, understand when
  to use basic research methods and how to apply them correctly, and
  effectively involve your team in research activities.

  Ask AI: "What should a junior researcher check before running a
  research session, to make sure the method actually fits the question
  they're trying to answer?"

- [ ] **Apply basic analysis techniques, and present clear findings**

  Understand how to apply basic techniques for analysing research data and
  synthesising findings, effectively involve your team in the process, and
  present clear findings colleagues can understand and use.

  Ask AI: "What makes a research finding 'clear' enough for a colleague
  outside research to act on, rather than just accurate?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding agile working and multidisciplinary roles**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from associate level:
  describing Agile working and the roles in a multidisciplinary digital
  team.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since the level
  below, what would genuinely applying Agile awareness, rather than just
  describing it, look like at this level?"

- [ ] **Keep building awareness of the diversity of service users**

  This skill also plateaus: demonstrating some awareness of user
  diversity, and the need for services to be usable and accessible for
  everyone.

  Ask AI: "What would actively including a more diverse range of
  participants in a research session, rather than just being aware of the
  need to, look like for a junior researcher?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding why a research plan and standards matter**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: explaining the importance of working
  to a research plan, and following standards for user research.

  Ask AI: "What would following a research plan and standards in practice,
  rather than just understanding why they matter, look like day to day?"

- [ ] **Keep learning who your stakeholders are**

  This skill also plateaus: describing who your stakeholders are, and
  what they find important.

  Ask AI: "What would identifying stakeholders independently, rather than
  being told who they are, look like for a junior researcher?"

- [ ] **Keep understanding how services shape user behaviour**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: describing how services and
  technology affect behaviour, and how user-centred practices adapt to
  changing needs.

  Ask AI: "What would a junior researcher's first attempt at applying
  user-centred practice, rather than just describing it, look like?"

Ask your manager or a more senior colleague which of these your team relies on
most right now, and start there.
