# Head of user research - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of user research level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of user research leads user researchers in an organisation, and
attracts and builds talent — the top of this profession's line. You're an
expert practitioner who can define and assure best practice, influence
organisational strategy and priorities, and collaborate with colleagues
across government. Agile research practices, inclusive research and
user-centred practice and advocacy all sharpen further at this level,
because you're now setting standards at organisational and cross-government
scale, not leading research programmes within one organisation.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Help the organisation understand and act on the complete user journey**

  Find ways to help the organisation adopt user-centred practices and
  appreciate the value of understanding users, make sure discussions at
  senior levels or between organisations consider user needs and evolving
  behaviour, and help the organisation understand the complete user
  journey to improve its services.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of user research get senior, non-research
  leaders to genuinely consider user needs in a strategic discussion,
  rather than treating research as an afterthought?"

- [ ] **Set organisational and cross-government standards for agile research**

  Advocate for Agile working at organisational level and with senior
  stakeholders, help organisations adopt Agile research practices, set
  standards for how research insights should support delivery by Agile
  teams, and coach other researchers to adopt Agile ways of working.

  Ask AI: "What does setting an Agile research standard for an entire
  organisation involve, beyond adopting Agile research practices well in
  one team?"

- [ ] **Lead organisational understanding of inclusive research**

  Help organisations understand the diversity of users of government
  services, and guide them to adopt inclusive practices and apply what
  they learn to design and deliver accessible services.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of user research build lasting organisational
  understanding of user diversity, rather than a one-off training session
  that fades?"

- [ ] **Keep aligning research programmes to organisational goals**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  establishing and aligning research programmes, synthesising strategic
  insights, and assuring quality across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since lead
  level, what would genuinely head-of-role research programme leadership
  add beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep directing stakeholder strategy at senior level**

  This skill also plateaus: directing stakeholder relationship strategy,
  ensuring objectives are set and supported, and negotiating with senior
  stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What changes about stakeholder strategy when you're accountable
  for the whole research community's relationships, rather than one
  programme's?"

- [ ] **Keep helping the organisation improve its analysis practice**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: helping the organisation adopt
  analysis and synthesis techniques, and continually improving and
  innovating practice.

  Ask AI: "What would cross-government leadership of analysis practice
  add, beyond improving it well within one organisation?"

- [ ] **Keep leading a community of practice for research methods**

  This skill also plateaus: helping the organisation adopt a wide range of
  methods, and leading a community of practice to improve and innovate
  research.

  Ask AI: "What would leading a cross-government community of practice
  for research methods add, beyond leading one within a single
  organisation?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
