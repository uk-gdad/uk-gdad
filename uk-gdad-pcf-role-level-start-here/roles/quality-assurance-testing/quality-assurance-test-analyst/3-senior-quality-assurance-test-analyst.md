# Senior quality assurance test analyst - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the senior quality assurance test analyst
level, this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything
else this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it
asks for.

## Purpose

A senior quality assurance test analyst influences and guides others in test
analysis, coordinating quality assurance activity across a defined area — and
increasingly, judging the business impact of what testing finds rather than
just reporting it. You are expected to use the right tools and techniques to
analyse products, services, processes and change to catch risk early, help
teams build their own approaches and strategies, and guide others in the
tools and techniques you've already mastered. The shift here is from doing
strong analysis yourself to making sure a defined area of the organisation
can do it too.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is most useful for pressure-testing your own
judgement on a specific, real decision — describe the situation and ask what
you might be missing, rather than asking for general theory you likely
already know.

- [ ] **Lead test analysis across a defined area, not just one piece of work**

  Lead work with stakeholders across teams to determine which quality
  characteristics genuinely add value, judge when an approach needs to
  change based on effort and risk, make sure test needs are addressed early,
  and use more than one technique together to make sense of complex
  information. Coach others in doing the same.

  Ask AI: "What changes about test analysis when you're coordinating it
  across several teams rather than doing it for one, and what tends to go
  wrong first?"

- [ ] **Help shape strategy beyond your own immediate work**

  Work with teams to develop and implement quality testing approaches, plans
  and strategies; contribute to strategy at the organisational level;
  implement ways to capture data that drive continuous improvement; and
  advocate for teams to own their own quality testing activity from early on.

  Ask AI: "What kind of data would show whether a team's quality testing
  approach is actually working, as opposed to just being followed?"

- [ ] **Set, and help others meet, standards for designing and running tests**

  Set up suitable environments, influence which test types and techniques
  get used to catch risk early, lead others through designing, building and
  running tests, contribute to standards for the wider area, and keep
  improving test types and techniques through a structured process rather
  than one-off tweaks.

  Ask AI: "What does a structured process for improving test standards over
  time actually look like, compared with standards that just drift?"

- [ ] **Turn defect patterns into recommendations for a wider area**

  Contribute to standards for defect management, manage and escalate
  dependencies, defects and risks across teams, contribute to mitigation
  plans that span teams, and use defect patterns and trends — not single
  incidents — to make recommendations, with support.

  Ask AI: "How would someone spot a pattern across many defects that points
  to a deeper process problem, rather than treating each one as unrelated?"

- [ ] **Understand where your role's engineering knowledge stops**

  This skill stays at the same explanatory level here as at the levels below
  it: you are expected to explain why testing processes, environments and
  tools matter and to help maintain existing automated tests and tooling —
  the deeper engineering work belongs to the test engineer track, and it's
  worth being clear with yourself about that boundary.

  Ask AI: "In an organisation with both test analyst and test engineer roles,
  where does test analysis stop and test engineering start, and where do
  they need to overlap?"

- [ ] **Handle harder conversations with senior stakeholders**

  Listen to and interpret the needs of a wider range of stakeholders, manage
  the communication that lands on you unplanned as well as what you start,
  and support or host difficult discussions involving diverse senior people.

  Ask AI: "Give me a scenario where two senior stakeholders disagree about
  what a test result means for a release, and suggest how a senior analyst
  might facilitate that toward a decision."

Ask your manager or a lead colleague which of these matters most in your
organisation right now, and start there.
