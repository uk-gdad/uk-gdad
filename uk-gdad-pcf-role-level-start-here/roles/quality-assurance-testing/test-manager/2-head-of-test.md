# Head of test - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of test level, this page is for
you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this repository has
for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

## Purpose

A head of test takes ownership of establishing policy for the test team and
setting the testing strategy — the top of this profession's line, where
there is no level above to check your judgement against. You own continuous
improvement, researching new technology and techniques and deciding which
ones are worth the disruption of adopting; you make decisions that carry real
weight; you build the wider quality assurance and testing community and give
it organisational leadership; and you make sure the teams under you actually
have the capability the organisation needs, not just the headcount. You may
also find yourself advocating for test capability outside government
entirely. At this level, the pathway below is less about acquiring new skills
and more about sharpening judgement you already largely have.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool differently than at earlier
levels: describe a real decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet, so you can test your own reasoning before you commit to
it.

- [ ] **Set policy and strategy for the whole test function**

  Create and manage multiple quality testing plans, approaches and
  strategies; lead and guide multiple teams in adopting them; advocate for
  quality testing to be involved early across the organisation's delivery
  processes; and guide teams in optimising their approach using real data.

  Ask AI: "What separates test policy that shapes real behaviour from policy
  that exists only as a document nobody consults?"

- [ ] **Judge which new technology and techniques are worth adopting**

  Lead work with stakeholders across teams to determine which quality
  characteristics genuinely add value, judge when an approach needs to
  change based on effort and risk, and use multiple techniques to make sense
  of complex, ambiguous information — then decide, at organisational scale,
  what's actually worth changing.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of test decide whether a new testing technique or
  technology is worth the disruption of adopting it organisation-wide, versus
  an interesting idea that isn't ready yet?"

- [ ] **Set the standards the whole organisation designs and runs tests against**

  Set standards and influence organisational decisions for test types,
  techniques, design and execution; coach others in them; advocate for their
  continuous improvement; and make the strategic calls on new or improved
  approaches used across your area.

  Ask AI: "What makes an organisation-wide testing standard survive contact
  with real delivery pressure, rather than being the first thing teams drop
  when a deadline looms?"

- [ ] **Use defect and risk trends to make organisational decisions**

  Lead and coach others in improving test and defect management processes,
  support colleagues assessing the most complex defects the organisation
  faces, and use defect patterns and trends to make tactical and strategic
  recommendations that influence quality processes across the organisation.

  Ask AI: "What's an example of a defect trend that should change
  organisational policy, as opposed to one that should just be fixed and
  left alone?"

- [ ] **Keep a working understanding of engineering, without doing it yourself**

  This skill stays explanatory at this level too: understanding why testing
  processes, environments and tools matter, and supporting the maintenance
  of what already exists, is enough to make good decisions — the deep
  technical work belongs to the engineers and leads you set direction for.

  Ask AI: "What does a head of test need to understand about testing tools
  and automation to make credible decisions about them, without needing to be
  hands-on with any of it?"

- [ ] **Build and represent the QAT community, inside and beyond your organisation**

  Mediate between people and strengthen relationships across the
  organisation, manage stakeholder expectations and moderate difficult,
  high-risk discussions under time pressure, and speak on behalf of, and
  represent, the community to large audiences inside and outside it —
  including, potentially, outside government entirely.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of test build a genuine sense of community across
  test professionals who don't report to each other, rather than a title
  that exists only on an org chart?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
