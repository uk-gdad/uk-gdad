# Principal developer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal developer level, this page
is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

There are two different roles at this level in the framework: a technical
one, covered here, and a management one. If the day-to-day work you're
aiming at is people leadership rather than deep technical practice, the
[principal developer - management](https://uk-gdad.github.io/roles/software-development/software-developer/9-principal-developer-management/)
page describes the difference.

## Purpose

A principal developer leads and plans development across large or multiple
teams — the top of the technical ladder in this role. You're an expert in
many technical areas, or a specialist with very deep knowledge in one, and
you use that knowledge, working with related disciplines, to keep multiple
teams working effectively. You develop capability by facilitating internal
mobility, shaping career paths and recruiting the right talent, making sure
people actually collaborate rather than just coexist, and you identify, test
and champion the adoption of emerging technologies rather than waiting for
them to arrive. Systems design jumps sharply at this level too — from
managing medium complexity to shaping design practice across an entire
enterprise or industry architecture.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is little level above principal in
this role, so use an AI tool as a sounding board rather than a teacher:
describe a real strategic call you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Shape system design practice at enterprise scale**

  Design systems characterised by high levels of risk, impact and business
  or technical complexity, control system design practice within an
  enterprise or industry architecture, influence industry-based models for
  new technology applications, develop implementation and procurement
  strategies consistent with business needs, and ensure adherence to the
  relevant technical strategies, policies and standards.

  Ask AI: "What changes about systems design judgement when you're
  operating at enterprise or industry scale, rather than designing one
  system in isolation?"

- [ ] **Quality-assure security across the organisation's solutions**

  Design, review and quality assure solutions and services to make sure
  they're specifically engineered to mitigate security threats, and lead
  others in using appropriate information security practices.

  Ask AI: "What does quality-assuring security across many teams' solutions
  involve, that goes beyond reviewing each one individually?"

- [ ] **Think about prototyping and service design strategically**

  Use a variety of prototyping methods, share best practice and coach
  others in using them, and look at strategic service design end to end
  rather than one component at a time.

  Ask AI: "What changes about prototyping when you're thinking about a
  service end to end at a strategic level, rather than one component in
  isolation?"

- [ ] **Lead process improvement at organisational scale**

  Analyse current development processes, identify and implement
  optimisation opportunities, lead and develop a team of experts to deliver
  service improvements, and help set the policy and standards changes get
  evaluated against.

  Ask AI: "How does leading process improvement at a principal level differ
  from doing it at a lead level, in terms of scope and what actually
  changes?"

- [ ] **Take technical responsibility for a whole project, not just your part**

  Advise on the right way to apply standards and methods to ensure
  compliance, maintain technical responsibility for all the stages and
  iterations of a software development project, and provide technical
  advice to stakeholders while setting team-based standards for programming
  tools and techniques.

  Ask AI: "What does maintaining technical responsibility for a whole
  project look like at principal level, when you're not the one writing
  most of the code?"

- [ ] **Keep setting development standards for complex, multi-team work**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from lead level:
  identifying and applying modern development standards to support complex
  projects and programmes, and leading others in applying them.

  Ask AI: "How does a principal developer get a development standard
  adopted across teams that don't report into the same structure?"

- [ ] **Keep finding and fixing the hardest service faults**

  This skill also plateaus: identifying, locating and fixing service
  faults.

  Ask AI: "What kind of service fault would genuinely need a principal
  developer's involvement, rather than being handled well by a senior or
  lead developer?"

- [ ] **Keep coordinating integration across systems**

  This skill's wording is unchanged: defining the integration build,
  co-ordinating build activities across systems, and supporting integration
  testing across the whole effort.

  Ask AI: "What does managing systems integration coordination look like at
  an organisational scale, rather than for a single product?"

- [ ] **Keep leading on user focus**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: collaborating with user researchers,
  representing users internally, and guiding others through the user
  story.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal developer make sure user research stays
  central to strategic technical decisions, not just delivery-level ones?"

- [ ] **Keep managing service components against business needs and KPIs**

  This skill's wording plateaus as well, unchanged since senior level:
  managing service components to ensure they meet business needs and key
  performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely principal-level ownership of availability and
  capacity add beyond what's written here?"

Ask a peer principal developer, in your organisation or another part of
government, which of these has taken them longest to get right — that
conversation will teach you more than any general reading on technical
leadership.
