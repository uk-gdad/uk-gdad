# Lead developer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the lead developer level, this page is
for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

There are two different roles at this level in the framework: a technical
one, covered here, and a management one. Both carry the same title and the
same named skills; if the day-to-day work you're aiming at is people
management rather than deep technical leadership, the
[lead developer - management](https://uk-gdad.github.io/roles/software-development/software-developer/7-lead-developer-management/)
page describes the difference in practice.

## Purpose

A lead developer leads development on one or a small number of related
project teams. You guide the way your team works, stay proficient across a
wide range of technical systems, help identify appropriate technology and
decide when software should actually be written, contribute to the wider
developer community, provide technical leadership through coaching and
mentoring, and promote knowledge sharing and the adoption of good practice.
Two skills sharpen clearly at this level — modern development standards and
programming and build — because setting the standard other developers follow
is a different job from meeting it yourself.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. Each one is a requirement of this role
level. At this level, an AI tool is most useful as a sounding board for a
strategic call — describe the trade-off you're weighing across teams and ask
what you might be missing.

- [ ] **Take technical responsibility for a whole project, not just your part**

  Advise on the right way to apply standards and methods to ensure
  compliance, maintain technical responsibility for all the stages and
  iterations of a software development project, and provide technical
  advice to stakeholders while setting team-based standards for programming
  tools and techniques.

  Ask AI: "What does 'maintaining technical responsibility for all stages
  of a project' actually involve day to day, beyond writing code well
  yourself?"

- [ ] **Set development standards for complex, multi-team work**

  Identify and apply modern development standards to support complex
  projects and programmes, and lead others in applying them — the standard
  you set here is one other developers are expected to follow.

  Ask AI: "How does a lead developer introduce a development standard
  across several teams in a way that actually sticks, rather than being
  quietly ignored once attention moves elsewhere?"

- [ ] **Keep finding and fixing service faults**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged from senior level:
  identifying, locating and fixing service faults.

  Ask AI: "What kind of service fault would genuinely need a lead
  developer's involvement, rather than being handled well by someone more
  junior?"

- [ ] **Keep designing systems of real complexity, and reviewing others' designs**

  This skill also plateaus: designing systems characterised by medium
  levels of risk, impact and business or technical complexity, selecting
  the right design standards, and reviewing the systems designs of others.

  Ask AI: "What should a lead developer look for when reviewing a design
  from a team they don't work in day to day?"

- [ ] **Keep coordinating integration across systems**

  This skill's wording is unchanged too: defining the integration build,
  co-ordinating build activities across systems, and supporting integration
  testing across the whole effort.

  Ask AI: "What coordination problems tend to appear when integration spans
  several teams with different priorities, and how would a lead resolve
  them?"

- [ ] **Keep leading on user focus across more than your own team**

  This skill also plateaus: collaborating with user researchers,
  representing users internally, explaining the difference between user
  needs and desires, and guiding others through the user story.

  Ask AI: "How would a lead developer champion user research consistently
  across several teams with different habits and priorities?"

- [ ] **Keep managing service components against business needs and KPIs**

  This skill's wording plateaus here too: managing service components to
  ensure they meet business needs and key performance indicators.

  Ask AI: "What would taking ownership of availability and capacity
  management, beyond just meeting the KPIs, look like for someone leading
  multiple teams?"

- [ ] **Keep contributing to process optimisation under guidance**

  This skill's wording is unchanged: working under guidance to identify
  process optimisation opportunities and contribute to implementing
  proposed solutions.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill is written at a more junior level than some of
  my other responsibilities, what would genuinely leading process
  optimisation look like if I chose to push beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep designing security in from the start**

  This skill's wording also plateaus: designing solutions and services with
  security controls included, specifically engineered to mitigate the
  relevant threats.

  Ask AI: "What security decisions made early tend to have the biggest
  consequences across a whole product line, rather than just one service?"

- [ ] **Keep treating prototyping as a team activity**

  This skill's wording plateaus too: approaching prototyping as something
  the team does together, establishing and iterating design patterns, and
  choosing the right method from several available.

  Ask AI: "How might a lead developer encourage prototyping across several
  teams with different habits, without dictating one rigid method to all of
  them?"

Ask a principal colleague which of these matters most in your organisation
right now, and start there.
