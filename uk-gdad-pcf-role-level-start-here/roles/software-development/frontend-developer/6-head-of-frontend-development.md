# Head of frontend development - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the head of frontend development level,
this page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else
this repository has for it, and a pathway for building the areas it names.

## Purpose

A head of frontend development is an expert practitioner with broad industry
experience — an expert across many technical areas, or a specialist with
very deep knowledge in one. You define and assure best practice while
influencing, leading and mentoring others, influence and develop
organisational strategy and priorities alongside counterpart colleagues
across government, make sure the right conditions and environment exist for
frontend developers to actually work effectively, and build departmental
capability by facilitating internal mobility, shaping career paths and
recruiting the right talent. Most of the named skills below read the same
as at lead level — at the top of a technical ladder, that's often exactly
right, because the real differentiation at this level is organisational
scope, not a longer skill description.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is no level above this one to
measure yourself against, so use an AI tool as a sounding board: describe a
real organisational decision you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Build and grow the whole frontend community, not just resolve its conflicts**

  Solve and unblock issues between teams or departments at the highest
  level, and coach the organisation on team dynamics and conflict
  resolution, while also actively building and growing the wider community
  — a genuinely broader remit than shaping one team's dynamics.

  Ask AI: "What does 'building and growing a professional community' across
  an organisation actually involve, beyond running occasional community
  events?"

- [ ] **Set the strategic direction for prototyping and service design**

  Use a variety of prototyping methods, share best practice and coach
  others in using them, and look at strategic service design end to end
  rather than one component at a time.

  Ask AI: "What changes about prototyping guidance when you're thinking
  about service design end to end across an organisation, rather than one
  team's interface?"

- [ ] **Advocate for web performance as an organisational discipline**

  Advocate for web performance optimisation best practices, coach and guide
  others in applying them, and actively collect data from multiple tools to
  monitor and resolve issues at scale.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of frontend development make web performance a
  genuine organisational priority, rather than something individual teams
  handle inconsistently?"

- [ ] **Keep leading organisational change and resolving the hardest conflicts**

  This skill's wording is unchanged from lead level: changing organisational
  structures towards sustainable designs, leading strategy for the whole
  organisation, making high-risk and high-complexity decisions, and
  building consensus between highly independent stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "What would this kind of organisational leadership look like
  applied across an entire department, rather than the services one lead
  developer is responsible for?"

- [ ] **Keep representing the community to the widest audiences**

  This skill also stays the same: mediating between people, communicating
  with stakeholders at every level, and speaking on behalf of the community
  to large audiences inside and outside government.

  Ask AI: "What's different about representing a whole professional
  community externally, compared with representing one team or service?"

- [ ] **Keep championing accessibility beyond your own organisation**

  This skill's wording is also unchanged: showing a strong understanding of
  accessibility, advocating for users with accessibility needs, and
  championing it to internal and external stakeholders.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of frontend development embed accessibility
  into how an entire department hires, trains and reviews its developers?"

- [ ] **Keep setting technical standards, at departmental scale**

  This skill plateaus too: advising on standards and methods, providing
  technical advice to stakeholders, and setting team-based standards for
  programming tools and techniques.

  Ask AI: "How would technical standard-setting change once it's applied
  across an entire department rather than a handful of teams?"

- [ ] **Keep leading the design and implementation of strategy**

  This skill's wording is unchanged: leading the design and implementation
  of strategy, and directing the evaluation of strategies and policies.

  Ask AI: "What would leading frontend strategy across a whole department
  add, beyond leading it for one programme?"

- [ ] **Keep getting development standards adopted at scale**

  This skill also plateaus: identifying and applying modern development
  standards for complex projects and programmes, and leading others in
  applying them.

  Ask AI: "What's the hardest part of getting a development standard
  genuinely adopted across an entire department, rather than a handful of
  teams?"

- [ ] **Keep owning integration end to end, departmentally**

  This skill plateaus as well: defining the static assets build,
  co-ordinating integration, and taking responsibility for interface and
  network tests.

  Ask AI: "What would departmental-level oversight of integration practice
  look like, as distinct from owning integration for one service?"

- [ ] **Keep representing user needs at the highest level**

  This skill's wording is unchanged too: collaborating with user
  researchers and interaction designers, explaining the difference between
  user needs and desires, and championing research that covers all users.

  Ask AI: "How would a head of frontend development make sure genuine user
  focus survives contact with departmental-level strategic pressure?"

There is no resource list at this level that will tell you what to do next —
the accountability is yours. Ask peers in equivalent roles at other
organisations how they made their hardest recent call, and use it to
stress-test your own thinking rather than to copy their answer.
