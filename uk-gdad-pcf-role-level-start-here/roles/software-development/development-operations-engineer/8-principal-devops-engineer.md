# Principal DevOps engineer - start here

Welcome. If you are working toward the principal DevOps engineer level, this
page is for you: what the role expects, where to find everything else this
repository has for it, and a pathway for building the skills it asks for.

There are two different roles at this level in the framework: a technical
one, covered here, and a management one. If the day-to-day work you're aiming
at is people leadership rather than deep technical practice, the
[principal DevOps engineer - management](https://uk-gdad.github.io/roles/software-development/development-operations-engineer/9-principal-devops-engineer-management/)
page describes the difference.

## Purpose

A principal DevOps engineer leads and plans development across large or
multiple teams, defining the strategic vision for delivery — the top of the
technical ladder in this role. You're expected to identify, test and
champion the adoption of emerging technologies rather than wait for them to
arrive, make sure security, stability and capacity are embedded in
development and deployment as standard rather than as an afterthought, and
help develop your team's capability by recognising high performers, shaping
career paths and recruiting the right talent. You're also expected to spot
skills gaps and important dependencies across technical teams before they
become someone else's crisis. That combination — deep technical judgement
and genuine responsibility for other people's growth — is what separates this
level from lead.

## Learning pathway

Work through these roughly in order. There is little level above principal in
this role, so use an AI tool as a sounding board rather than a teacher:
describe a real strategic call you're weighing and ask it to argue the case
you haven't made yet.

- [ ] **Quality-assure security across the organisation's solutions**

  Design, review and quality assure solutions and services to make sure
  they're specifically engineered to mitigate security threats, and lead
  others in using appropriate information security practices — a step up
  from designing security in yourself, into making sure everyone else does
  too.

  Ask AI: "What does quality-assuring security across many teams' solutions
  involve, that goes beyond reviewing each one individually?"

- [ ] **Think about prototyping and service design strategically**

  Use a variety of prototyping methods, share best practice and coach
  others in using them, and look at strategic service design end to end
  rather than one component at a time.

  Ask AI: "What changes about prototyping when you're thinking about a
  service end to end at a strategic level, rather than one component in
  isolation?"

- [ ] **Lead process improvement at organisational scale**

  Analyse current development processes, identify and implement
  optimisation opportunities, lead and develop a team of experts to deliver
  service improvements, and help set the policy and standards changes get
  evaluated against.

  Ask AI: "How does leading process improvement at a principal level differ
  from doing it at a lead level, in terms of scope and what actually
  changes?"

- [ ] **Own availability and capacity standards across the organisation**

  Ensure the correct implementation of availability and capacity management
  standards and procedures, identify capacity issues early, and initiate
  remedial action.

  Ask AI: "What early warning signs of a capacity problem would a principal
  engineer be expected to catch, that a less senior engineer might miss?"

- [ ] **Set development standards for complex, multi-team work**

  Identify and apply modern development standards to support complex
  projects and programmes, and lead others in applying them.

  Ask AI: "How does a principal engineer get a development standard adopted
  across teams that don't report into the same structure?"

- [ ] **Establish integration standards across a whole product life cycle**

  Establish standards and procedures across a service's product life cycle,
  including its development life cycle, and manage the resources that keep
  systems integration working effectively.

  Ask AI: "What does managing systems integration resources look like at an
  organisational scale, rather than for a single product?"

- [ ] **Take on the hardest faults, and advise others on how to approach them**

  Identify, locate and fix complex service faults, and advise others on
  different methodologies and types of service support.

  Ask AI: "What kind of service fault would genuinely need a principal
  engineer's involvement, rather than being handled well by a senior or
  lead engineer?"

- [ ] **Keep designing systems at a manageable level of complexity**

  This skill's wording plateaus here, unchanged since senior level: design
  systems characterised by medium levels of risk, impact and business or
  technical complexity, and review the designs of others. It's worth being
  deliberately excellent at this rather than assuming seniority alone
  covers it.

  Ask AI: "Given this skill's description hasn't changed since senior
  level, what would genuinely principal-level systems design judgement add
  beyond what's written here?"

- [ ] **Keep building your programming and build practice**

  This skill's expectation is unchanged since the earlier levels:
  collaborating on specifications, then designing, coding, testing and
  documenting programs of medium-to-high complexity to the right standards.

  Ask AI: "How does a principal engineer stay genuinely hands-on with code
  without it crowding out the strategic work only they can do?"

- [ ] **Keep leading on user focus**

  Collaborate with user researchers and represent users internally, explain
  the difference between user needs and user desires, champion user
  research so it covers all users, and guide others in understanding the
  user story.

  Ask AI: "How would a principal engineer make sure user research stays
  central to strategic technical decisions, not just delivery-level ones?"

Ask a peer principal engineer, in your organisation or another part of
government, which of these has taken them longest to get right — that
conversation will teach you more than any general reading on technical
leadership.
