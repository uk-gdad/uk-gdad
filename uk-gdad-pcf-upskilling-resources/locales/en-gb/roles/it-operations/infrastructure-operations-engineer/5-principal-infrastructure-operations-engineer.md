# Principal infrastructure operations engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of principal infrastructure operations engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you lead and direct IT operations specialist teams in monitoring, operating and supporting technical solutions. You develop the strategic and tactical roadmaps for technologies and services and own the operational relationships with suppliers. The resources below emphasise roadmap and future technology judgement, supplier governance, capacity and compliance standards, and leading a specialist capability.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

- [Real-World Engineering Management](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/real-world-engineering-management): Business, technical, and leadership skills for leading specialist teams at scale.

- [Developing a Google SRE Culture](https://www.coursera.org/learn/developing-a-google-sre-culture): How cultural and technical practices rather than tooling purchases produce collaboration.

## Blog posts

- [Embracing Risk, Google SRE Book chapter 3](https://sre.google/sre-book/embracing-risk/): Error budgets, which turn availability targets into negotiated business decisions.

- [The cloud security principles, NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/cloud/the-cloud-security-principles): The assessment framework for cloud providers, which is a procurement instrument as much as a security one.

- [Building On-Call Culture, PagerDuty Ops Guide](https://goingoncall.pagerduty.com/culture/): The conditions that make on-call sustainable, which is a capability and resourcing question you own.

- [Alert Fatigue Is Killing Your NOC Efficiency, Techmonarch](https://techmonarch.com/blog/alert-fatigue-is-killing-your-noc-efficiency-heres-what-actually-works/): Automation removing classes of alert entirely, which is a roadmap decision.

## Research articles

- [Organizational design of IT supplier relationship management: a multiple case study of five client companies](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/jit.2011.30): Strategy, structure, and process for supplier relationship management at organisational level.

- [A Review of the IT Outsourcing Empirical Literature and Future Research Directions](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1057/jit.2010.21): Twenty years of research on outsourcing outcomes, capabilities, and governance.

- [Relational dynamics in information technology outsourcing: An integrative review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/ijmr.12347): 127 empirical studies on what makes outsourcing relationships succeed.

- [A Survey of Time Series Anomaly Detection Methods in the AIOps Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00393): What anomaly detection genuinely does, which is what a tooling evaluation should probe.

- [Infrastructure as Code: Technology Review and Research Challenges](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/389406746_Infrastructure_as_Code_-Technology_Review_and_Research_Challenges): The field and its unresolved problems, useful for judging where the technology actually is.

- [Strategies and Challenges in Cloud-to-Cloud Migration Using Infrastructure as Code](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-78392-0_1): A migration case study, relevant to any roadmap that involves moving platforms.

- [Effectiveness of IT Service Management Capability: Value Co-Creation and Value Facilitation Mechanisms](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333777833_Effectiveness_of_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Value_Co-Creation_and_Value_Facilitation_Mechanisms): Evidence across 256 organisations on how capability creates value.

- [Enhancing reliability in AI inference services: An empirical study on real production incidents](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.07424): 156 high-impact production incidents analysed, with implications for platform governance.

## Professional development tips

- Hold an expert-level certificate in your service management framework, since it is expected at this role level
- Write roadmaps that name what you will decommission, not only what you will adopt
- Judge future technologies against the estate you actually run, not the one a vendor demonstrates
- Deliver a supportable model for new technology alongside the databases and systems that will coexist with it
- Own supplier relationships on evidence and measured outcomes rather than relationship warmth
- Make service compliance and risk visible on a schedule rather than after an audit forces it
- Keep reading and writing code, since baseline repair capability is what makes your technical judgement credible
