# Service desk analyst - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of service desk analyst.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you interact with customers, take calls, resolve queries to the agreed service level, and know when to escalate. The resources below emphasise the customer service craft as much as the technical one: empathy, telephone technique, accurate asset records, and basic service reporting.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Technical Support Fundamentals](https://www.coursera.org/learn/technical-support-fundamentals): Hardware, the internet, software, troubleshooting and customer service. The standard first course for this path.

- [Google IT Support Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-it-support): Troubleshooting and customer service, networking, operating systems, system administration and security, designed for people new to the industry.

- [ITSM Foundations: Optimizing IT Service Management](https://www.coursera.org/learn/itsm-foundations-optimizing-it-service-management): How service requests, incidents and problems are handled in a real service management platform.

## Research articles

- [Sources of Customer Satisfaction and Dissatisfaction with Information Technology Help Desks](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1009794622860): Empirical work on what actually drives satisfaction with IT support.

- [Evaluation of User Support: Factors That Affect User Satisfaction With Helpdesks and Helplines](https://www.academia.edu/17926413/Evaluation_of_User_Support_Factors_That_Affect_User_Satisfaction_With_Helpdesks_and_Helplines): Finds solution quality, consultation experience and tangibles as the real dimensions, and that helpdesk customers judge mostly on the consultation.

- [An investigation of technical support issues influencing user satisfaction](https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc2528/): Finds support method and technician experience level to be the significant variables.

- [Evaluating User Satisfaction of IT Services Through Service Quality Approach](https://www.iieta.org/journals/isi/paper/10.18280/isi.290225): A study finding tangibles and reliability contribute most to positive satisfaction.

- [How Employees' Emotional Labor Promotes Perceived Service Quality: A Dual-Pathway Model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12649272/): How deep acting rather than surface acting produces genuinely better service experiences.

- [Emotion Regulation in Customer Service Roles: Testing a Model of Emotional Labor](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/10928752_Emotion_Regulation_in_Customer_Service_Roles_Testing_a_Model_of_Emotional_Labor): A time-sampling study of call centre employees, which is unusually close to the real job.

- [An empirical study of factors that contribute to the emotional and physical well-being of call centre agents](https://www.academia.edu/68702512/An_empirical_study_of_factors_that_contribute_to_the_emotional_and_physical_well_being_of_call_centre_agents): Evidence on the strain the role carries, which is worth knowing about your own work.

- [Management of perceptions of information technology service quality](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296308002270): How perception of service quality forms, which is what you are actually managing on a call.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical ITSM research from 2012 to 2021.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework for service operations.

## Professional development tips

- Learn what your service level agreement actually promises, including what it excludes
- Take the extra minute to understand what the caller was trying to do, not only what broke
- Write ticket notes for the next person, since they are the main product of your work
- Escalate when the agreed threshold is reached, not when your patience runs out
- Learn how assistive technology works, because supporting it badly excludes people from their jobs
- Notice and report the issues you handle repeatedly, since that is how prevention starts
- Look after yourself between difficult calls, because the emotional load of this role is real and documented
