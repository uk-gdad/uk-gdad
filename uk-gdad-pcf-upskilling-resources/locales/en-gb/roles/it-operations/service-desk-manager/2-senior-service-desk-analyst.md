# Senior service desk analyst - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of senior service desk analyst.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you monitor inbound calls, support operators in resolution and intervene in difficult calls. You own and update the analyst script, coach and develop analysts, and manage availability. The resources below emphasise handling complex complaints, coaching, and the problem investigation that turns repeated calls into prevention.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Professional development tips

- Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, since it is expected at this role level
- Update the script from real calls, and remove the lines that never help
- Coach by listening to calls with the analyst and asking what they noticed, rather than by telling them what you would have said
- Intervene early in a difficult call, since a late rescue costs more than an early one
- Escalate recurring call patterns with numbers attached, because a pattern with data gets acted on
- Manage availability with the emotional load of the work in mind, not only the queue depth
- Feed accessibility scenarios into the script, since these are where scripted support most often fails
