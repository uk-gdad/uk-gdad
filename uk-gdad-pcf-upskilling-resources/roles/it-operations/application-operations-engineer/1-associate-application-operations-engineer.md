# Associate application operations engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of associate application operations engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are a trainee working inside an established team, under the direction of application operations engineers. The resources below emphasise foundations: how services are supported, how to register an incident properly, how problems are escalated rather than absorbed, and why user needs sit behind the work.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Technical Support Fundamentals](https://www.coursera.org/learn/technical-support-fundamentals): An introduction to computer hardware, the internet, software, troubleshooting and customer service. A sensible first course if your technical grounding is uneven.

- [ITSM Foundations: Optimizing IT Service Management](https://www.coursera.org/learn/itsm-foundations-optimizing-it-service-management): Covers core ITSM principles through the ServiceNow platform, including how service requests, incidents and problems are actually handled. No prior ITSM experience assumed.

- [Foundations of Site Reliability Engineering Training](https://www.coursera.org/learn/foundations-of-site-reliability-engineering-training): Builds the vocabulary of modern operations work, covering core SRE principles, reliability culture and how operations teams measure whether a service is healthy.

## Udemy courses

- [IT Support and Service Desk jobs: Crash Course for Beginners](https://www.udemy.com/course/it-support-and-service-desk-part1/): Covers the basic procedures, tools and duties of a support analyst, including using a monitoring system to track servers and respond to alerts.

- [IT Help Desk Ticketing Tool for L1 Support](https://www.udemy.com/course/it-help-desk-ticketing-tool-for-l1-support/): Focused on the ticketing tool itself: recording, tracking and escalating incidents and service requests. Directly relevant to registering incidents and routing them to the right channel.

- [IT service operations and Service Desk management](https://www.udemy.com/course/it-service-operations-and-service-desk-management/): Covers incident and request models, monitoring tools in IT service, types of events and their management.

## Skillsoft courses

- [Event, Incident, and Problem Management: ITIL](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/event-incident-and-problem-management-0fe6c829-bdd7-4921-9a23-d8a7365ae359): Works through monitoring and event management, then incident management, then problem management, which is the sequence you will meet them in practice.

- [ITIL 4 Foundation: The Guiding Principles](https://www.skillsoft.com/course/itil-4-foundation-the-guiding-principles-f6fac874-32d1-4e9f-aae7-4c76077dddfe): The principles underneath the practices, which matters more than memorising process steps when you are learning the discipline.

- [Courses for ITIL Foundation](https://www.skillsoft.com/channel/itil-foundation-2635aa01-2c26-11e7-83d1-dba0327abefc): A channel gathering the ITIL Foundation material, useful if you are working towards a Level 3 service management framework qualification.

## edX courses

- [Introduction to DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering (LinuxFoundationX)](https://www.edx.org/learn/devops/the-linux-foundation-introduction-to-devops-and-site-reliability-engineering): Explains the principles and practices of DevOps and SRE, and why application operations and development are increasingly the same conversation.

- [DevOps, Cloud, and Agile Foundations Professional Certificate (IBM)](https://www.edx.org/professional-certificate/ibm-devops-cloud-and-agile-foundations/): Covers cloud computing characteristics, models and infrastructure alongside agile and DevOps foundations.

- [Introduction to DevOps: Practices and Tools Professional Certificate](https://www.edx.org/professional-certificate/linuxfoundationx-introduction-to-devops-practices-and-tools): Covers containers, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, deployment pipelines and observability systems, which is the toolchain surrounding most modern applications.

## MIT OpenCourseWare courses

- [15.561 Information Technology Essentials](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-561-information-technology-essentials-spring-2005/): Broad coverage of hardware, operating systems, software development tools, relational databases and security, aimed at people with little background in computer technology.

- [6.033 Computer System Engineering](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-033-computer-system-engineering-spring-2018/): Covers controlling complexity, client-server design, operating systems, performance, networks and fault tolerance. The fault tolerance lectures explain why systems fail in the ways you will observe.

- [RES.6-004 Principles of Computer System Design: An Introduction](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-004-principles-of-computer-system-design-an-introduction-spring-2009/): A freely available text on system design principles, including a substantial treatment of faults and how systems tolerate them.

## Blog posts

- [Operate a reliable service, GOV.UK Service Standard point 14](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-14-operate-a-reliable-service): The standard your work is judged against in UK government: minimise downtime, monitor appropriately, and have a proportionate plan to respond.

- [Incident Response, Google SRE Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/incident-response/): The practical chapter on running incident response, including command structure, role separation and communication.

- [PagerDuty Incident Response Documentation](https://response.pagerduty.com/): An openly published incident response process, covering how to prepare new people for on-call and how major incidents are handled.

- [Being On-Call, PagerDuty](https://response.pagerduty.com/oncall/being_oncall/): What being on-call actually involves, written plainly. Read this before your first rotation rather than during it.

- [Building On-Call Culture, PagerDuty Ops Guide](https://goingoncall.pagerduty.com/culture/): Argues that teams need proper onboarding time before joining a rotation, and that people must feel able to act without fear of reprisal.

- [Monitoring Distributed Systems, Google SRE Book chapter 6](https://sre.google/sre-book/monitoring-distributed-systems/): Introduces the four golden signals of latency, traffic, errors and saturation, and what should and should not wake a human.

- [What are the Four Golden Signals and Why Do They Matter?](https://www.groundcover.com/blog/4-golden-signals): A shorter treatment of the same four signals, useful if the Google chapter is heavy going on a first read.

- [A day in the life of a GDS Site Reliability Engineer](https://gds.blog.gov.uk/2022/06/10/a-day-in-the-life-of-a-gds-site-reliability-engineer-sre/): A concrete account of operations work inside government, which is a useful picture of where this career path leads.

- [Incident Management Workflow in IT Ops, Atlassian](https://www.atlassian.com/incident-management/itsm): Walks through the workflow from detection to resolution, showing where a first-line registration step sits in the whole.

- [A Beginner's Guide to CMDB in ITSM, Lansweeper](https://www.lansweeper.com/blog/cybersecurity/a-beginners-guide-to-cmdb-in-itsm-unlocking-efficiency/): Explains configuration items and their interdependencies, which is the map you need before you can diagnose anything.

## Research articles

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical ITSM research published between 2012 and 2021, showing what implementations actually achieve.

- [An IT Service Management Literature Review: Challenges, Benefits, Opportunities and Implementation Practices](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349857851_An_IT_Service_Management_Literature_Review_Challenges_Benefits_Opportunities_and_Implementation_Practices): Analyses 47 articles to extract the benefits, challenges and practices of ITSM implementation.

- [Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and Observations on SRE Process to Make Tasks Easier](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01926v1): A practitioner-oriented account of the SRE process and where its friction points sit.

- [A Survey of Time Series Anomaly Detection Methods in the AIOps Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00393): Explains how monitored KPIs become time series, and how anomalies in them are detected. Useful background for understanding your alerting tooling.

- [Artificial Intelligence for IT Operations (AIOps) Workshop White Paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2101.06054): Maps the AIOps field from resource management through to failure prediction, anomaly detection and remediation.

- [A Comprehensive Survey on Root Cause Analysis in (Micro) Services](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00803v1): Reviews root cause analysis techniques across metrics, traces, logs and multi-model data.

- [Improving the Software Problem Management Process: A Case Study](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11908562_5): A case study of the challenges in a real software problem management process, rather than an idealised description of one.

- [Difficulties in Implementing Effective Problem Management](https://www.ijser.org/paper/Difficulties-in-Implementing-Effective-Problem-Management.html): Examines the barriers that stop problem management working, which explains a lot of what you will observe.

- [Measuring IT Service Management Capability: Scale Development and Empirical Validation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/299616426_Measuring_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Scale_Development_and_Empirical_Validation): Survey-based research on what ITSM capability consists of and how it can be measured.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework for ITIL service operations built from the Lean toolset.

## Professional development tips

- Work towards a Level 3 service management framework qualification early, since it is expected at the next role level
- Register incidents with enough detail that the next person does not have to ask you anything
- Learn the application you support well enough to describe what it does for users, not only how it runs
- Ask to shadow incidents and changes rather than only reading about them
- Escalate early and without embarrassment, because passing a problem on promptly is part of the job at this level
- Keep a personal log of what broke and why, since patterns become visible faster than you expect
- Learn one scripting language well enough to automate your own repetitive checks
