# Application operations engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of application operations engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you maintain services and carry out user and security administration. You monitor applications, are accountable for batch processes, and in some organisations also do end user computing work. The resources below emphasise monitoring, configuration and asset accuracy, change implementation, and executing tests reliably.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) Principles](https://www.coursera.org/learn/site-reliability-engineering-principles): Covers operating reliable systems at scale, combining automation, monitoring, incident response and reliability measurement.

- [Google IT Automation with Python Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-it-automation): Teaches Python, Git and configuration management for automating system administration tasks. The fastest route out of doing routine maintenance by hand.

- [Advanced ITSM with ServiceNow: Optimizing IT Operations](https://www.coursera.org/learn/advanced-itsm-with-servicenow-optimizing-it-operations): Covers incidents, problems, service requests, change management and the configuration management database in a working ITSM platform.

## Udemy courses

- [IT service operations and Service Desk management](https://www.udemy.com/course/it-service-operations-and-service-desk-management/): Covers incident and request models, and building monitoring in IT service including event types and their management.

## Research articles

- [Capacity and availability management by quantitative project management in the IT service industry](https://www.emerald.com/ajq/article-abstract/13/2/163/60850/Capacity-and-availability-management-by): Develops a capacity and availability tracker for IT service delivery from baselined quantitative data.

- [Ensuring Efficient IT Service Management to Increase Information Systems Availability](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/337847919_Ensuring_Efficient_IT_Service_Management_to_Increase_Information_Systems_Availability): Connects service management practice directly to measured availability outcomes.

- [A Survey of Time Series Anomaly Detection Methods in the AIOps Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00393): How monitored KPIs become time series and how anomalies are detected in them.

- [AIOps for log anomaly detection in the era of LLMs: A systematic literature review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2667305325001346): Reviews log anomaly detection, including recent large language model approaches.

- [A Comprehensive Survey on Root Cause Analysis in (Micro) Services](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00803v1): Covers root cause analysis across metrics, traces, logs and multi-model data.

- [Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and Observations on SRE Process to Make Tasks Easier](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01926v1): An account of the SRE process and where practitioners find friction.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical ITSM implementation research from 2012 to 2021.

- [Effectiveness of IT Service Management Capability: Value Co-Creation and Value Facilitation Mechanisms](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333777833_Effectiveness_of_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Value_Co-Creation_and_Value_Facilitation_Mechanisms): A study of 256 organisations finding that ITSM capability works through alignment with the business.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A Lean-derived five-step improvement framework for service operations.

- [Improved Root Cause Analysis Supporting Resilient Production Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278612522001273): Identifies 14 challenges and 17 enablers in root cause analysis, including poor data quality and employee bias.

## Professional development tips

- Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, since it is expected at this role level
- Automate the routine maintenance and administration tasks you repeat, and keep the scripts in version control
- Treat batch process failures as first-class incidents with owners, not as background noise
- Verify asset and configuration records against reality rather than trusting the register
- Learn enough SQL and log querying to answer questions without escalating them
- Execute test scripts precisely and record what actually happened, including what you skipped
- Talk to the users of the application you support, so that user focus is evidence rather than assumption
