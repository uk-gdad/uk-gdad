# Service desk manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of service desk manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you identify emerging issues, act as a knowledge expert, and are responsible for resourcing and developing the team, including preparing and co-ordinating service transition activities. You translate lessons learnt into continual service improvement, oversee the full range of customer service functions, review satisfaction, and investigate which processes can be automated. The resources below emphasise improvement, automation, satisfaction measurement and service transition readiness.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

## Research articles

- [Sources of Customer Satisfaction and Dissatisfaction with Information Technology Help Desks](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1009794622860): What actually drives satisfaction, which should shape what your satisfaction review measures.

- [Evaluation of User Support: Factors That Affect User Satisfaction With Helpdesks and Helplines](https://www.academia.edu/17926413/Evaluation_of_User_Support_Factors_That_Affect_User_Satisfaction_With_Helpdesks_and_Helplines): Finds three quality dimensions rather than the standard SERVQUAL set, which changes survey design.

- [Evaluating User Satisfaction of IT Services Through Service Quality Approach](https://www.iieta.org/journals/isi/paper/10.18280/isi.290225): Finds tangibles and reliability contribute most, which is useful when prioritising improvements.

- [Management of perceptions of information technology service quality](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0148296308002270): How perception forms, which matters when metrics improve and satisfaction does not.

- [How Employees' Emotional Labor Promotes Perceived Service Quality: A Dual-Pathway Model](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12649272/): Deep versus surface acting, which has direct implications for how you train and staff.

- [An empirical study of factors that contribute to the emotional and physical well-being of call centre agents](https://www.academia.edu/68702512/An_empirical_study_of_factors_that_contribute_to_the_emotional_and_physical_well_being_of_call_centre_agents): Evidence on strain, which is a resourcing consideration you own.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical ITSM research, useful when justifying investment.

- [Continual Process Improvement for ITIL Service Operations: A Lean Perspective](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10580530.2019.1587576): A five-step improvement framework you can run directly with your team.

- [Improved Root Cause Analysis Supporting Resilient Production Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278612522001273): Challenges and enablers in root cause analysis, most of which you can address.

- [Measuring IT Service Management Capability: Scale Development and Empirical Validation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/299616426_Measuring_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Scale_Development_and_Empirical_Validation): A validated capability measure, better than a self-assessed maturity workshop.

## Professional development tips

- Measure satisfaction on the dimensions the research says actually matter, not the ones that are easy to collect
- Automate the processes with the highest volume and lowest judgement content first
- Translate lessons learnt into a named change with an owner, or they will not become improvement
- Prepare for service transition by asking what will generate calls, and get answers before go-live
- Resource on demand patterns and on the emotional load of the work, not on headcount precedent
- Develop the team so they can advise project teams, since that is where the next role level sits
- Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, since it is expected at this role level
