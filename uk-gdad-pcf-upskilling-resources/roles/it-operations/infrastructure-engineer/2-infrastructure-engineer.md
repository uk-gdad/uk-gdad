# Infrastructure engineer - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of infrastructure engineer.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you support and maintain infrastructure solutions and services as directed and according to departmental policy, ensure services are delivered and used as required, and work with and support third parties. The resources below emphasise scripting, applied information security, modern development standards, systems design and integration, and structured troubleshooting.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [DevOps Pro: Linux, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and IaC](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/devops-linux-docker-kubernetes-ci-cd-iac): Five courses from Linux administration through to provisioning cloud infrastructure with code, with labs that simulate production.

- [Infrastructure Automation with Terraform](https://www.coursera.org/learn/infrastructure-automation-with-terraform): Automating infrastructure with Terraform across AWS, GCP and Azure.

- [Google IT Automation with Python Professional Certificate](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-it-automation): Python, Git and configuration management for automating administration. The most direct route to the coding and scripting skill at this level.

## Udemy courses

- [The Complete Terraform with Ansible Bootcamp](https://www.udemy.com/course/terraform-and-ansible/): Provisioning with Terraform and configuring with Ansible, which is the common division of labour in practice.

- [AWS Infrastructure as Code With Terraform](https://www.udemy.com/course/learn-devops-infrastructure-automation-with-terraform/): Variables, state, modules, VPC, EC2, RDS and Route 53, then containers and pipeline-driven deployment.

- [Learn DevOps: Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform and Azure DevOps](https://www.udemy.com/course/devops-with-docker-kubernetes-and-azure-devops/): A Microsoft-oriented path, useful if your department is on Azure.

- [The cloud security principles, NCSC](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/cloud/the-cloud-security-principles): The principles used to assess cloud platforms and SaaS across UK government.

- [Secure by Design Principles, UK Government Security](https://www.security.gov.uk/policy-and-guidance/secure-by-design/principles/): Mandatory for departments and arm's length bodies, and directly relevant to how you build.

- [Building, testing and iterating our monitoring and alerting service, GDS](https://technology.blog.gov.uk/2018/11/09/building-testing-and-iterating-our-monitoring-and-alerting-service/): Building shared monitoring on Prometheus, and what was learned iterating it.

- [Release Engineering, Google SRE Book chapter 8](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/): Release as an engineering discipline, which applies to infrastructure changes as much as application code.

- [Operate a reliable service, GOV.UK Service Standard point 14](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-14-operate-a-reliable-service): The reliability expectation your infrastructure has to meet.

- [Use and contribute to open standards, common components and patterns, GOV.UK Service Standard point 13](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-13-use-common-standards-components-patterns): The standard behind re-using platforms rather than building new ones.

## Research articles

- [Infrastructure as Code: Technology Review and Research Challenges](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/389406746_Infrastructure_as_Code_-Technology_Review_and_Research_Challenges): A review of the field and its unresolved problems.

- [Infrastructure as code: A paradigm shift in cloud resource management and deployment automation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/391453229_Infrastructure_as_code_A_paradigm_shifts_in_cloud_resource_management_and_deployment_automation): Empirical outcomes including provisioning time and incident resolution improvements.

- [Leveraging Infrastructure-as-Code Automation to Establish Standardized, Reliable, and Reproducible Cloud Infrastructure](https://zenodo.org/records/19452393): Addresses configuration drift, deployment variability and traceability, which are the real problems IaC is for.

- [Strategies and Challenges in Cloud-to-Cloud Migration Using Infrastructure as Code](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-78392-0_1): A migration case study with honest treatment of the obstacles.

- [A Comprehensive Survey on Root Cause Analysis in (Micro) Services](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00803v1): Root cause analysis across metrics, traces, logs and multi-model data.

- [Improved Root Cause Analysis Supporting Resilient Production Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0278612522001273): The challenges and enablers of root cause analysis, including data quality and bias.

- [A Survey of Time Series Anomaly Detection Methods in the AIOps Domain](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00393): The detection methods behind infrastructure alerting.

- [Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and Observations on SRE Process to Make Tasks Easier](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.01926v1): Practitioner observations on the process and its friction.

- [An empirical study on performance comparisons of different types of DevOps team formations](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/computer-science/articles/10.3389/fcomp.2025.1554299/full): Team structure against delivery performance, relevant to how infrastructure and delivery teams divide work.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical ITSM research from 2012 to 2021.

## Books on Amazon

- [The Practice of Cloud System Administration, Volume 2](https://www.amazon.com/Practice-Cloud-System-Administration-Practices-ebook/dp/B00N7N2CRQ): By Limoncelli, Chalup and Hogan. Distributed and cloud service operations, with case studies from large operators.

- [Infrastructure as Code: Designing and Delivering Dynamic Systems for the Cloud Age](https://www.amazon.com/Infrastructure-Code-Designing-Delivering-Dynamic/dp/109815035X): By Kief Morris. The principles and patterns, independent of any one tool.

## Professional development tips

- Put infrastructure in code and in version control, so that a change is reviewable and a rebuild is possible
- Learn enough of one scripting language to automate your own repeated work, then share it
- Apply the NCSC secure design and cloud security principles when you build, not when you are audited
- Define what integration testing means for your builds, and actually run it
- Support third parties by being specific about interfaces and expectations rather than about effort
- Break a problem into component parts before choosing a tool, since the tool choice usually follows from the decomposition
- Read the systems designs of others, because reviewing designs teaches faster than producing them
