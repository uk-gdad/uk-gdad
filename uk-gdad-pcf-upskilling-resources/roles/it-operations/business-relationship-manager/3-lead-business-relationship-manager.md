# Lead business relationship manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of lead business relationship manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you lead engagement with internal and external stakeholders, may lead a team of business relationship managers, and act as the escalation point when relationships break down. The resources below emphasise directing business analysis, arbitrating between senior stakeholders, facilitating high risk and complex discussions under constrained timelines, and setting the conditions that let others do this work well.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

## Coursera courses

- [Advanced Negotiation and Conflict Resolution for Leaders](https://www.coursera.org/learn/advanced-negotiation-and-conflict-resolution-for-leaders): Frameworks for multi-party negotiation, turning conflict into collaboration, and building influence systematically. The closest match to the arbitration expected at this level.

- [Organizational Leadership](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/organizational-leadership): A specialisation on leading across an organisation rather than within a team, which is the scope of this role.

- [Strategic Technology Management](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/technology-management): A strategic view of technology and innovation, supporting the strategy and policy definition part of the role.

## Research articles

- [Strategic IT alignment: twenty-five years on](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/jit.2014.35): What twenty-five years of alignment research has and has not shown. Worth reading before committing your organisation to a framework.

- [IT-Business Alignment: A Systematic Literature Review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349523359_IT-Business_Alignment_A_Systematic_Literature_Review): A synthesis of the alignment literature and its measurement problems.

- [Relational dynamics in information technology outsourcing: An integrative review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/ijmr.12347): An integrative review of 127 empirical studies on what makes outsourcing relationships work. Directly relevant to external stakeholder leadership.

- [Organizational design of IT supplier relationship management: a multiple case study of five client companies](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/jit.2011.30): Strategy, structure and process for supplier relationship management across five organisations.

- [A Review of the IT Outsourcing Empirical Literature and Future Research Directions](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1057/jit.2010.21): Finds relationship management to be the perennial difficulty across two decades of outsourcing research.

- [Effectiveness of IT Service Management Capability: Value Co-Creation and Value Facilitation Mechanisms](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333777833_Effectiveness_of_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Value_Co-Creation_and_Value_Facilitation_Mechanisms): Evidence across 256 organisations that capability works through business alignment.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of empirical implementation research, useful when challenged on whether any of this works.

- [Strategic Stakeholder Alignment for Organizational Success](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/399827322_Strategic_Stakeholder_Alignment_for_Organizational_Success): Stakeholder alignment as deliberate strategy rather than emergent goodwill.

- [Strategic Alignment of IT and Business Goals: A Critical Analysis of Frameworks, Information Resource Utilization, and Collaborative Governance](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/401041605_STRATEGIC_ALIGNMENT_OF_IT_AND_BUSINESS_GOALS_A_CRITICAL_ANALYSIS_OF_FRAMEWORKS_INFORMATION_RESOURCE_UTILIZATION_AND_COLLABORATIVE_GOVERNANCE): A critical rather than promotional review of alignment frameworks and governance models.

- [Measuring IT Service Management Capability: Scale Development and Empirical Validation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/299616426_Measuring_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Scale_Development_and_Empirical_Validation): A validated measurement instrument, which beats a maturity assertion when reporting upwards.

## Professional development tips

- Identify where new connections are needed rather than only maintaining the ones you inherited
- Arbitrate by making each side's actual objective explicit before proposing anything
- Facilitate high risk discussions with a prepared structure, because constrained timelines punish improvisation
- Direct analysis towards the simplest workable approach, and say plainly when a proposal is more complex than the problem
- Protect your team from escalations that should not reach them, and coach them through the ones that should
- Look across groups of services for the compounding value, since single-relationship wins do not aggregate on their own
- Bring evidence of how user needs have changed over time, because organisations forget faster than users do
