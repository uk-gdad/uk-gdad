# Business relationship manager - upskill resources

This page focuses on learning and growth resources for the work role of business relationship manager.

- The resources include links to online courses, blogs, research articles, videos, and books.

- These help with job upskilling, work training, professional development, and career advancement.

- These are intended to be good starting points, not a comprehensive catalogue.

At this role level you are responsible for consistency in engagement across the business. You prepare documentation to progress new ideas and investments, maintain a view of activity in the relevant systems, and support the continuous improvement of the team's capability. The resources below emphasise stakeholder relationships, investigative business analysis, and communicating how activity meets strategic goals.

We welcome constructive feedback to improve this page and these lists.

- GitHub issues and/or pull requests are welcome.

- The resources are found via AI searches then go through human-in-the-loop proofing.

## Coursera courses

- [Stakeholder Management](https://www.coursera.org/learn/stakeholder-management): Four lessons covering stakeholder identification, analysis, collaboration and change support. Written for project managers, business analysts, change managers and relationship managers.

- [Effective Stakeholder Engagement: A Strategic Approach](https://www.coursera.org/learn/effective-stakeholder-engagement-a-strategic-approach): Treats engagement as something planned rather than reactive, which is exactly what consistency of engagement requires.

- [Relationship Management and Business Development](https://www.coursera.org/learn/relationship-management-and-business-development): Building and managing professional relationships that support long-term growth, aimed at people with a few years of stakeholder engagement experience.

## Udemy courses

- [Stakeholders Relationships Management](https://www.udemy.com/course/stakeholders-relationships-management/): Practical and theoretical aspects of influencing, negotiating and communicating with stakeholders, and sustaining relationships over time.

- [Essentials of Stakeholder Management and Engagement](https://www.udemy.com/course/stakeholder-management-engagement/): Identifying stakeholders, finding win-win outcomes and building a communication plan you can actually maintain.

## Blog posts

- [ITIL 4 Relationship Management: Guide to Building Strategic IT-Business Partnerships](https://itsm.tools/relationship-management/): Explains relationship management as one of the 34 ITIL 4 practices and what it is actually for.

- [Shifting from Business Relationship Management to Relationship Management in ITIL 4](https://www.beyond20.com/blog/relationship-management-in-itil-4/): Argues that good relationship management is a journey across many conversations rather than a transaction.

- [ITIL Business Relationship Management Explained, NovelVista](https://www.novelvista.com/blogs/it-service-management/itil-business-relationship-management): Covers ensuring services meet current and future business needs while delivering measurable value.

- [ITIL Business Relationship Management, the "Hidden" Process](https://flevy.com/blog/itil-business-relationship-management-the-hidden-process/): On maximising IT value by aligning investment with business goals, and why the practice is often invisible.

- [GOV.UK Service Standard](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard): The principles your organisation's digital investment is assessed against, which is the frame for any opportunity you progress.

- [Solve a whole problem for users, GOV.UK Service Standard point 2](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/service-standard/point-2-solve-a-whole-problem): Useful language for the recurring conversation about organisational boundaries versus user journeys.

- [The Digital, Data and Technology Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook/the-digital-data-and-technology-playbook): Government guidance on how digital and technology work should be commissioned and delivered.

- [Digital Assurance Playbook](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-assurance-playbook/digital-assurance-playbook): How digital work is assured in government, which shapes what documentation you need to prepare.

- [Getting approval to spend money on a service, GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/getting-approval-to-spend-money-on-a-service): Digital and technology spend controls were replaced on 1 April 2026. Departments now make most of their own digital and technology spending decisions through their own assurance team, with HM Treasury approval still required above the department's spend limit or for proposals that are novel, contentious or repercussive.

- [Performance Review of Digital Spend: Enabling Strategic Investment and Innovation](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/performance-review-of-digital-spend/performance-review-of-digital-spend-enabling-strategic-investment-and-innovation): The review behind the shift away from central spend controls towards departmental ownership, and the criteria digital investment is judged against.

## Research articles

- [IT-Business Alignment: A Systematic Literature Review](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349523359_IT-Business_Alignment_A_Systematic_Literature_Review): Synthesises the research on aligning IT with business goals, which is the problem this role exists to address.

- [Strategic IT alignment: twenty-five years on](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/jit.2014.35): A Journal of Information Technology retrospective on what a quarter century of alignment research has and has not established.

- [Strategic Alignment: A Model for Organizational Transformation via Information Technology](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/38009338_Strategic_alignment_A_model_for_organizational_transformation_via_information_technology): The Henderson and Venkatraman strategic alignment model, still the reference point for most later work.

- [Investigating business-IT alignment through multi-disciplinary goal concepts](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00766-009-0081-0): Connects business goals to system requirements, which is where relationship work meets analysis work.

- [Strategic Stakeholder Alignment for Organizational Success](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/399827322_Strategic_Stakeholder_Alignment_for_Organizational_Success): On aligning stakeholder interests as a deliberate activity rather than a by-product.

- [Strategic Alignment of IT and Business Goals: A Critical Analysis of Frameworks, Information Resource Utilization, and Collaborative Governance](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/401041605_STRATEGIC_ALIGNMENT_OF_IT_AND_BUSINESS_GOALS_A_CRITICAL_ANALYSIS_OF_FRAMEWORKS_INFORMATION_RESOURCE_UTILIZATION_AND_COLLABORATIVE_GOVERNANCE): A critical look at the alignment frameworks rather than an endorsement of them.

- [Implementation and impacts of IT Service Management in the IT function](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0268401223000099): A systematic review of what ITSM implementations actually achieve, useful when supporting a business case.

- [Effectiveness of IT Service Management Capability: Value Co-Creation and Value Facilitation Mechanisms](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/333777833_Effectiveness_of_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Value_Co-Creation_and_Value_Facilitation_Mechanisms): Finds that ITSM capability works through business alignment, which is direct evidence for your role's value.

- [An IT Service Management Literature Review: Challenges, Benefits, Opportunities and Implementation Practices](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/349857851_An_IT_Service_Management_Literature_Review_Challenges_Benefits_Opportunities_and_Implementation_Practices): Extracts benefits, challenges and practices across 47 studies.

- [Measuring IT Service Management Capability: Scale Development and Empirical Validation](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/299616426_Measuring_IT_Service_Management_Capability_Scale_Development_and_Empirical_Validation): A validated capability measure, useful for continuous improvement activity that is evidenced rather than asserted.

## Professional development tips

- Hold a Level 3 service management framework qualification, since it is expected at this role level
- Keep the record of activity and business knowledge current, because your credibility rests on being the person who knows
- Write documentation that a sceptical reader can act on, not documentation that only survives a friendly audience
- Learn one business analysis technique properly rather than knowing the names of ten
- Own an issue until it is genuinely handed over, and say who has it now
- Ask teams about their ambitions and constraints before you offer them anything
- Practise explaining technical work in the language of the business outcome it serves
